### 12E.1 {#12E.1}

Slander and speaking lies about your brethren is an abomination to God.

[Prov 6:16,19](/bible/proverbs/6#v16)

<details><summary>Proverbs 6:16,19</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/6#v16)</sup> These six things doth the Lord hate: yea, seven are an abomination unto him:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/6#v19)</sup> A false witness that speaketh lies, and he that soweth discord among brethren.

</details>

### 12E.2 {#12E.2}

You shall not go about speaking slander or spreading false reports about others.

[Exod 23:1](/bible/exodus/23#v1); [Lev 19:16](/bible/leviticus/19#v16); [Luke 3:14](/bible/luke/3#v14); [Titus 2:3](/bible/titus/2#v3); [James 4:11](/bible/james/4#v11)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/23#v1)</sup> Thou shalt not raise a false report: put not thine hand with the wicked to be an unrighteous witness.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 19:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/19#v16)</sup> Thou shalt not go up and down as a talebearer among thy people: neither shalt thou stand against the blood of thy neighbour: I am the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 3:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/luke/3#v14)</sup> And the soldiers likewise demanded of him, saying, And what shall we do? And he said unto them, Do violence to no man, neither accuse any falsely; and be content with your wages.

</details>
<details><summary>Titus 2:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/titus/2#v3)</sup> The aged women likewise, that they be in behaviour as becometh holiness, not false accusers, not given to much wine, teachers of good things;

</details>
<details><summary>James 4:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/james/4#v11)</sup> Speak not evil one of another, brethren. He that speaketh evil of his brother, and judgeth his brother, speaketh evil of the law, and judgeth the law: but if thou judge the law, thou art not a doer of the law, but a judge.

</details>

### 12E.3 {#12E.3}

You shall not backbite others.

[Ps 15:1](/bible/psalms/15#v1); [Rom 1:30](/bible/romans/1#v30); [2 Cor 12:20](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v20)

<details><summary>Psalms 15:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/15#v1)</sup> Lord, who shall abide in thy tabernacle? who shall dwell in thy holy hill?

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 1:30</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/romans/1#v30)</sup> Backbiters, haters of God, despiteful, proud, boasters, inventors of evil things, disobedient to parents,

</details>
<details><summary>2 Corinthians 12:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v20)</sup> For I fear, lest, when I come, I shall not find you such as I would, and that I shall be found unto you such as ye would not: lest there be debates, envyings, wraths, strifes, backbitings, whisperings, swellings, tumults:

</details>

### 12E.4 {#12E.4}

A backbiting tongue brings forth an angry countenance (no one will be pleased with a backbiter).

[Prov 25:23](/bible/proverbs/25#v23)

<details><summary>Proverbs 25:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/25#v23)</sup> The north wind driveth away rain: so doth an angry countenance a backbiting tongue.

</details>

### 12E.5 {#12E.5}

A talebearer who spreads scandals causes harm to others.

[Prov 18:8](/bible/proverbs/18#v8); [Prov 26:20,22](/bible/proverbs/26#v20)

<details><summary>Proverbs 18:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/18#v8)</sup> The words of a talebearer are as wounds, and they go down into the innermost parts of the belly.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 26:20,22</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/26#v20)</sup> Where no wood is, there the fire goeth out: so where there is no talebearer, the strife ceaseth.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/26#v22)</sup> The words of a talebearer are as wounds, and they go down into the innermost parts of the belly.

</details>

### 12E.6 {#12E.6}

He who does not backbite and slander with his tongue is near to God.

[Ps 15:1-3](/bible/psalms/15#v1)

<details><summary>Psalms 15:1-3</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/15#v1)</sup> Lord, who shall abide in thy tabernacle? who shall dwell in thy holy hill?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/15#v2)</sup> He that walketh uprightly, and worketh righteousness, and speaketh the truth in his heart.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/15#v3)</sup> He that backbiteth not with his tongue, nor doeth evil to his neighbour, nor taketh up a reproach against his neighbour.

</details>

### 12E.9 {#12E.9}

Slander is a characteristic of the wicked.

[Ps 31:13](/bible/psalms/31#v13); [Ps 50:16,20](/bible/psalms/50#v16); [Jer 6:28](/bible/jeremiah/6#v28)

<details><summary>Psalms 31:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/31#v13)</sup> For I have heard the slander of many: fear was on every side: while they took counsel together against me, they devised to take away my life.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 50:16,20</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/50#v16)</sup> But unto the wicked God saith, What hast thou to do to declare my statutes, or that thou shouldest take my covenant in thy mouth?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/50#v20)</sup> Thou sittest and speakest against thy brother; thou slanderest thine own mother’s son.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 6:28</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/6#v28)</sup> They are all grievous revolters, walking with slanders: they are brass and iron; they are all corrupters.

</details>

### 12E.10 {#12E.10}

Libel and name calling is condemned by Christ.

[Matt 5:22](/bible/matthew/5#v22)

<details><summary>Matthew 5:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/5#v22)</sup> But I say unto you, That whosoever is angry with his brother without a cause shall be in danger of the judgment: and whosoever shall say to his brother, Raca, shall be in danger of the council: but whosoever shall say, Thou fool, shall be in danger of hell fire.

</details>

### 12E.11 {#12E.11}

He that speaks slander is a fool.

[Prov 10:18](/bible/proverbs/10#v18)

<details><summary>Proverbs 10:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/10#v18)</sup> He that hideth hatred with lying lips, and he that uttereth a slander, is a fool.

</details>

### 12E.12 {#12E.12}

<details><summary>Psalms 101:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/101#v5)</sup> Whoso privily slandereth his neighbour, him will I cut off: him that hath an high look and a proud heart will not I suffer.
