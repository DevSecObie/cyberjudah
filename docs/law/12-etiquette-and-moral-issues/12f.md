### 12F.1 {#12F.1}

We are exhorted to be courteous.

[1 Pet 3:8](/bible/1-peter/3#v8)

<details><summary>1 Peter 3:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-peter/3#v8)</sup> Finally, be ye all of one mind, having compassion one of another, love as brethren, be pitiful, be courteous:

</details>

### 12F.2 {#12F.2}

Be peaceable, well behaved, showing courtesy and every consideration to all men.

[Titus 3:2](/bible/titus/3#v2)

<details><summary>Titus 3:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/titus/3#v2)</sup> To speak evil of no man, to be no brawlers, but gentle, shewing all meekness unto all men.

</details>

### 12F.3 {#12F.3}

Brotherly kindness is needed to be fruitful in the knowledge of Jesus Christ.

[2 Pet 1:5-7](/bible/2-peter/1#v5)

<details><summary>2 Peter 1:5-7</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-peter/1#v5)</sup> And beside this, giving all diligence, add to your faith virtue; and to virtue knowledge;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-peter/1#v6)</sup> And to knowledge temperance; and to temperance patience; and to patience godliness;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-peter/1#v7)</sup> And to godliness brotherly kindness; and to brotherly kindness charity.

</details>

### 12F.4 {#12F.4}

We are to show kindness to one another.

[Rom 12:10](/bible/romans/12#v10); [2 Cor 6:6](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v6); [Eph 4:32](/bible/ephesians/4#v32); [Col 3:12](/bible/colossians/3#v12)

<details><summary>Romans 12:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/romans/12#v10)</sup> Be kindly affectioned one to another with brotherly love; in honour preferring one another;

</details>
<details><summary>2 Corinthians 6:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v6)</sup> By pureness, by knowledge, by longsuffering, by kindness, by the Holy Ghost, by love unfeigned,

</details>
<details><summary>Ephesians 4:32</summary>

> <sup>[32](/bible/ephesians/4#v32)</sup> And be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ’s sake hath forgiven you.

</details>
<details><summary>Colossians 3:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/colossians/3#v12)</sup> Put on therefore, as the elect of God, holy and beloved, bowels of mercies, kindness, humbleness of mind, meekness, longsuffering;

</details>
