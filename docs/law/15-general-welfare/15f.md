### 15F.1 {#15F.1}

Poverty is derived from laziness and a lack of work effort.

[Prov 6:10-11](/bible/proverbs/6#v10); [Prov 19:15,24](/bible/proverbs/19#v15); [Prov 20:4](/bible/proverbs/20#v4); [Prov 21:25](/bible/proverbs/21#v25); [Prov 24:30-34](/bible/proverbs/24#v30); [2 Cor 9:6](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v6); [2 Thess 3:8-12](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v8)

<details><summary>Proverbs 6:10-11</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/6#v10)</sup> Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/6#v11)</sup> So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth, and thy want as an armed man.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 19:15,24</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/19#v15)</sup> Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep; and an idle soul shall suffer hunger.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/19#v24)</sup> A slothful man hideth his hand in his bosom, and will not so much as bring it to his mouth again.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 20:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/20#v4)</sup> The sluggard will not plow by reason of the cold; therefore shall he beg in harvest, and have nothing.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 21:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/21#v25)</sup> The desire of the slothful killeth him; for his hands refuse to labour.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 24:30-34</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/24#v30)</sup> I went by the field of the slothful, and by the vineyard of the man void of understanding;
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/24#v31)</sup> And, lo, it was all grown over with thorns, and nettles had covered the face thereof, and the stone wall thereof was broken down.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/24#v32)</sup> Then I saw, and considered it well: I looked upon it, and received instruction.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/proverbs/24#v33)</sup> Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep:
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/proverbs/24#v34)</sup> So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth; and thy want as an armed man.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Corinthians 9:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v6)</sup> But this I say, He which soweth sparingly shall reap also sparingly; and he which soweth bountifully shall reap also bountifully.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Thessalonians 3:8-12</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v8)</sup> Neither did we eat any man’s bread for nought; but wrought with labour and travail night and day, that we might not be chargeable to any of you:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v9)</sup> Not because we have not power, but to make ourselves an ensample unto you to follow us.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v10)</sup> For even when we were with you, this we commanded you, that if any would not work, neither should he eat.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v11)</sup> For we hear that there are some which walk among you disorderly, working not at all, but are busybodies.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v12)</sup> Now them that are such we command and exhort by our Lord Jesus Christ, that with quietness they work, and eat their own bread.

</details>

### 15F.2 {#15F.2}

He who loves pleasure will become a poor man.

[Prov 21:17](/bible/proverbs/21#v17)

<details><summary>Proverbs 21:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/21#v17)</sup> He that loveth pleasure shall be a poor man: he that loveth wine and oil shall not be rich.

</details>

### 15F.3 {#15F.3}

Poverty will come to him who refuses instruction and is unwilling to learn.

[Prov 13:18](/bible/proverbs/13#v18)

<details><summary>Proverbs 13:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/13#v18)</sup> Poverty and shame shall be to him that refuseth instruction: but he that regardeth reproof shall be honoured.

</details>

### 15F.4 {#15F.4}

He who follows empty pursuits will have poverty in plenty.

[Prov 28:19](/bible/proverbs/28#v19)

<details><summary>Proverbs 28:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/28#v19)</sup> He that tilleth his land shall have plenty of bread: but he that followeth after vain persons shall have poverty enough.

</details>

### 15F.5 {#15F.5}

Idle chatter leads only to poverty.

[Prov 14:23](/bible/proverbs/14#v23)

<details><summary>Proverbs 14:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/14#v23)</sup> In all labour there is profit: but the talk of the lips tendeth only to penury.

</details>

### 15F.6 {#15F.6}

Poverty will come to the drunkard and the glutton.

[Prov 23:21](/bible/proverbs/23#v21)

<details><summary>Proverbs 23:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/23#v21)</sup> For the drunkard and the glutton shall come to poverty: and drowsiness shall clothe a man with rags.

</details>

### 15F.7 {#15F.7}

He who oppresses the poor to increase his riches will surely come to poverty.

[Prov 22:16](/bible/proverbs/22#v16)

<details><summary>Proverbs 22:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/22#v16)</sup> He that oppresseth the poor to increase his riches, and he that giveth to the rich, shall surely come to want.

</details>

### 15F.8 {#15F.8}

Poverty caused by love of sleep.

[Prov 6:9-11](/bible/proverbs/6#v9); [Prov 20:13](/bible/proverbs/20#v13)

<details><summary>Proverbs 6:9-11</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/6#v9)</sup> How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard? when wilt thou arise out of thy sleep?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/6#v10)</sup> Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/6#v11)</sup> So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth, and thy want as an armed man.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 20:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/20#v13)</sup> Love not sleep, lest thou come to poverty; open thine eyes, and thou shalt be satisfied with bread.

</details>

[Prov 10:3](/bible/proverbs/10#v3)

<details><summary>Proverbs 10:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/10#v3)</sup> The Lord will not suffer the soul of the righteous to famish: but he casteth away the substance of the wicked.

</details>

[Prov 10:4](/bible/proverbs/10#v4)

<details><summary>Proverbs 10:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/10#v4)</sup> He becometh poor that dealeth with a slack hand: but the hand of the diligent maketh rich.

</details>
