### 15D.1 {#15D.1}

We are not to curse the deaf or put a stumbling block before the blind.

[Lev 19:14](/bible/leviticus/19#v14)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/19#v14)</sup> Thou shalt not curse the deaf, nor put a stumblingblock before the blind, but shalt fear thy God: I am the Lord.

</details>

### 15D.2 {#15D.2}

Cursed be he that causes the blind to wander out of the way.

[Deut 27:18](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v18)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 27:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v18)</sup> Cursed be he that maketh the blind to wander out of the way. And all the people shall say, Amen.

</details>

### 15D.3 {#15D.3}

We are to invite the maimed, the lame and the blind to our feasts.

[Luke 14:13](/bible/luke/14#v13)

<details><summary>Luke 14:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/luke/14#v13)</sup> But when thou makest a feast, call the poor, the maimed, the lame, the blind:

</details>

### 15D.4 {#15D.4}

Show respect for the aged.

[Lev 19:32](/bible/leviticus/19#v32); [1 Tim 5:1](/bible/1-timothy/5#v1)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:32</summary>

> <sup>[32](/bible/leviticus/19#v32)</sup> Thou shalt rise up before the hoary head, and honour the face of the old man, and fear thy God: I am the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 5:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-timothy/5#v1)</sup> Rebuke not an elder, but intreat him as a father; and the younger men as brethren;

</details>

### 15D.5 {#15D.5}

You should support and help the weak.

[Acts 20:35](/bible/acts/20#v35); [Rom 15:1](/bible/romans/15#v1)

<details><summary>Acts 20:35</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/acts/20#v35)</sup> I have shewed you all things, how that so labouring ye ought to support the weak, and to remember the words of the Lord Jesus, how he said, It is more blessed to give than to receive.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 15:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/15#v1)</sup> We then that are strong ought to bear the infirmities of the weak, and not to please ourselves.

</details>
