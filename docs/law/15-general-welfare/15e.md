### 15E.1 {#15E.1}

A small portion of crops are to remain unharvested or unpicked so that they may be left for the poor and fatherless to take.

[Lev 19:9-10](/bible/leviticus/19#v9); [Lev 23:22](/bible/leviticus/23#v22); [Deut 24:19-21](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v19)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:9-10</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/19#v9)</sup> And when ye reap the harvest of your land, thou shalt not wholly reap the corners of thy field, neither shalt thou gather the gleanings of thy harvest.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/19#v10)</sup> And thou shalt not glean thy vineyard, neither shalt thou gather every grape of thy vineyard; thou shalt leave them for the poor and stranger: I am the Lord your God.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 23:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/23#v22)</sup> And when ye reap the harvest of your land, thou shalt not make clean riddance of the corners of thy field when thou reapest, neither shalt thou gather any gleaning of thy harvest: thou shalt leave them unto the poor, and to the stranger: I am the Lord your God.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 24:19-21</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v19)</sup> When thou cuttest down thine harvest in thy field, and hast forgot a sheaf in the field, thou shalt not go again to fetch it: it shall be for the stranger, for the fatherless, and for the widow: that the Lord thy God may bless thee in all the work of thine hands.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v20)</sup> When thou beatest thine olive tree, thou shalt not go over the boughs again: it shall be for the stranger, for the fatherless, and for the widow.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v21)</sup> When thou gatherest the grapes of thy vineyard, thou shalt not glean it afterward: it shall be for the stranger, for the fatherless, and for the widow.

</details>

### 15E.2 {#15E.2}

At the end of three years all of the tithes of the people's increase for the year are to be gathered together and given to and eaten by the stranger, the fatherless and the widow.

[Deut 14:28-29](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v28); [Deut 26:12](/bible/deuteronomy/26#v12)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:28-29</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v28)</sup> At the end of three years thou shalt bring forth all the tithe of thine increase the same year, and shalt lay it up within thy gates:
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v29)</sup> And the Levite, (because he hath no part nor inheritance with thee,) and the stranger, and the fatherless, and the widow, which are within thy gates, shall come, and shall eat and be satisfied; that the Lord thy God may bless thee in all the work of thine hand which thou doest.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 26:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/26#v12)</sup> When thou hast made an end of tithing all the tithes of thine increase the third year, which is the year of tithing, and hast given it unto the Levite, the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow, that they may eat within thy gates, and be filled;

</details>

### 15E.3 {#15E.3}

That of which you have extra is to be shared with the needy.

[Luke 3:11](/bible/luke/3#v11)

<details><summary>Luke 3:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/luke/3#v11)</sup> He answereth and saith unto them, He that hath two coats, let him impart to him that hath none; and he that hath meat, let him do likewise.

</details>

### 15E.4 {#15E.4}

The hungry, when they come to a neighbor's vineyard, may eat their fill of grapes, but shall not put any in their basket.

[Deut 23:24](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v24)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 23:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v24)</sup> When thou comest into thy neighbour’s vineyard, then thou mayest eat grapes thy fill at thine own pleasure; but thou shalt not put any in thy vessel.

</details>

### 15E.5 {#15E.5}

Give to every man that asks and needs, for whatever measure you deal out to others, it will be dealt to you in return.

[Luke 6:30,38](/bible/luke/6#v30)

<details><summary>Luke 6:30,38</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/luke/6#v30)</sup> Give to every man that asketh of thee; and of him that taketh away thy goods ask them not again.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/luke/6#v38)</sup> Give, and it shall be given unto you; good measure, pressed down, and shaken together, and running over, shall men give into your bosom. For with the same measure that ye mete withal it shall be measured to you again.

</details>

### 15E.6 {#15E.6}

The needy may enter their neighbor's grain fields and corn fields and pluck the crop by hand, but shall not cut down the crop with a sickle.

[Deut 23:25](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v25)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 23:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v25)</sup> When thou comest into the standing corn of thy neighbour, then thou mayest pluck the ears with thine hand; but thou shalt not move a sickle unto thy neighbour’s standing corn.

</details>

### 15E.7 {#15E.7}

Individual offerings, donations, and contributions are to be given according to the purpose that one's heart leads, giving to the poor willingly.

[Lev 25:35](/bible/leviticus/25#v35); [Deut 15:7-8](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v7); [2 Cor 9:7](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v7)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:35</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/leviticus/25#v35)</sup> And if thy brother be waxen poor, and fallen in decay with thee; then thou shalt relieve him: yea, though he be a stranger, or a sojourner; that he may live with thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 15:7-8</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v7)</sup> If there be among you a poor man of one of thy brethren within any of thy gates in thy land which the Lord thy God giveth thee, thou shalt not harden thine heart, nor shut thine hand from thy poor brother:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v8)</sup> But thou shalt open thine hand wide unto him, and shalt surely lend him sufficient for his need, in that which he wanteth.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Corinthians 9:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v7)</sup> Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver.

</details>
