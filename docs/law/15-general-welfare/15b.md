### 15B.1 {#15B.1}

A care for the poor is a characteristic of the righteous.

[Ps 112:9](/bible/psalms/112#v9); [Prov 29:7](/bible/proverbs/29#v7); [2 Cor 9:9](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v9); [Gal 2:10](/bible/galatians/2#v10)

<details><summary>Psalms 112:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/112#v9)</sup> He hath dispersed, he hath given to the poor; his righteousness endureth for ever; his horn shall be exalted with honour.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 29:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/29#v7)</sup> The righteous considereth the cause of the poor: but the wicked regardeth not to know it.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Corinthians 9:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v9)</sup> (As it is written, He hath dispersed abroad; he hath given to the poor: his righteousness remaineth for ever.

</details>
<details><summary>Galatians 2:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/galatians/2#v10)</sup> Only they would that we should remember the poor; the same which I also was forward to do.

</details>

### 15B.2 {#15B.2}

We should defend the poor and deliver them from the hand of the wicked.

[Ps 82:3-4](/bible/psalms/82#v3)

<details><summary>Psalms 82:3-4</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/82#v3)</sup> Defend the poor and fatherless: do justice to the afflicted and needy.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/82#v4)</sup> Deliver the poor and needy: rid them out of the hand of the wicked.

</details>

### 15B.3 {#15B.3}

If one of your brethren becomes poor and falls into poverty among you, you are to sustain him, whether he be a foreigner or a countryman.

[Lev 25:35](/bible/leviticus/25#v35)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:35</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/leviticus/25#v35)</sup> And if thy brother be waxen poor, and fallen in decay with thee; then thou shalt relieve him: yea, though he be a stranger, or a sojourner; that he may live with thee.

</details>

### 15B.4 {#15B.4}

Do not harden your heart towards the poor but lend to him sufficient to cover his need.

[Deut 15:7-10](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v7)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 15:7-10</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v7)</sup> If there be among you a poor man of one of thy brethren within any of thy gates in thy land which the Lord thy God giveth thee, thou shalt not harden thine heart, nor shut thine hand from thy poor brother:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v8)</sup> But thou shalt open thine hand wide unto him, and shalt surely lend him sufficient for his need, in that which he wanteth.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v9)</sup> Beware that there be not a thought in thy wicked heart, saying, The seventh year, the year of release, is at hand; and thine eye be evil against thy poor brother, and thou givest him nought; and he cry unto the Lord against thee, and it be sin unto thee.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v10)</sup> Thou shalt surely give him, and thine heart shall not be grieved when thou givest unto him: because that for this thing the Lord thy God shall bless thee in all thy works, and in all that thou puttest thine hand unto.

</details>

### 15B.5 {#15B.5}

You are not to mock the poor.

[Prov 17:5](/bible/proverbs/17#v5)

<details><summary>Proverbs 17:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/17#v5)</sup> Whoso mocketh the poor reproacheth his Maker: and he that is glad at calamities shall not be unpunished.

</details>

### 15B.6 {#15B.6}

You shall not oppress the poor.

[Deut 24:14](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v14); [Job 24:3-4,10](/bible/job/24#v3); [Ps 10:2](/bible/psalms/10#v2); [Isa 10:1-2](/bible/isaiah/10#v1); [Zech 7:10](/bible/zechariah/7#v10)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 24:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v14)</sup> Thou shalt not oppress an hired servant that is poor and needy, whether he be of thy brethren, or of thy strangers that are in thy land within thy gates:

</details>
<details><summary>Job 24:3-4,10</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/job/24#v3)</sup> They drive away the ass of the fatherless, they take the widow’s ox for a pledge.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/24#v4)</sup> They turn the needy out of the way: the poor of the earth hide themselves together.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/24#v10)</sup> They cause him to go naked without clothing, and they take away the sheaf from the hungry;

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 10:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/10#v2)</sup> The wicked in his pride doth persecute the poor: let them be taken in the devices that they have imagined.

</details>
<details><summary>Isaiah 10:1-2</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/10#v1)</sup> Woe unto them that decree unrighteous decrees, and that write grievousness which they have prescribed;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/10#v2)</sup> To turn aside the needy from judgment, and to take away the right from the poor of my people, that widows may be their prey, and that they may rob the fatherless!

</details>
<details><summary>Zechariah 7:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/zechariah/7#v10)</sup> And oppress not the widow, nor the fatherless, the stranger, nor the poor; and let none of you imagine evil against his brother in your heart.

</details>

### 15B.7 {#15B.7}

He who oppresses the poor reproaches his Maker, but he who honors his Maker has mercy towards the needy.

[Prov 14:21,31](/bible/proverbs/14#v21)

<details><summary>Proverbs 14:21,31</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/14#v21)</sup> He that despiseth his neighbour sinneth: but he that hath mercy on the poor, happy is he.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/14#v31)</sup> He that oppresseth the poor reproacheth his Maker: but he that honoureth him hath mercy on the poor.

</details>
