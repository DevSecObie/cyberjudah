### 15A.1 {#15A.1}

We are to be charitable to the poor and hungry.

[Deut 15:7-8](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v7); [Matt 25:40-45](/bible/matthew/25#v40); [1 Cor 13:1](/bible/1-corinthians/13#v1); [1 Cor 14:1](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v1); [2 Pet 1:7](/bible/2-peter/1#v7); [1 John 3:17](/bible/1-john/3#v17)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 15:7-8</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v7)</sup> If there be among you a poor man of one of thy brethren within any of thy gates in thy land which the Lord thy God giveth thee, thou shalt not harden thine heart, nor shut thine hand from thy poor brother:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v8)</sup> But thou shalt open thine hand wide unto him, and shalt surely lend him sufficient for his need, in that which he wanteth.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 25:40-45</summary>

> <sup>[40](/bible/matthew/25#v40)</sup> And the King shall answer and say unto them, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/25#v41)</sup> Then shall he say also unto them on the left hand, Depart from me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire, prepared for the devil and his angels:
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/matthew/25#v42)</sup> For I was an hungred, and ye gave me no meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me no drink:
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/matthew/25#v43)</sup> I was a stranger, and ye took me not in: naked, and ye clothed me not: sick, and in prison, and ye visited me not.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/matthew/25#v44)</sup> Then shall they also answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, or athirst, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and did not minister unto thee?
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/matthew/25#v45)</sup> Then shall he answer them, saying, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to me.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 13:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/13#v1)</sup> Though I speak with the tongues of men and of angels, and have not charity, I am become as sounding brass, or a tinkling cymbal.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 14:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v1)</sup> Follow after charity, and desire spiritual gifts, but rather that ye may prophesy.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Peter 1:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-peter/1#v7)</sup> And to godliness brotherly kindness; and to brotherly kindness charity.

</details>
<details><summary>1 John 3:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-john/3#v17)</sup> But whoso hath this world’s good, and seeth his brother have need, and shutteth up his bowels of compassion from him, how dwelleth the love of God in him?

</details>

### 15A.2 {#15A.2}

Acts of uncharitableness towards the poor are condemned.

[Prov 29:7](/bible/proverbs/29#v7); [Isa 32:7](/bible/isaiah/32#v7); [James 2:15-16](/bible/james/2#v15)

<details><summary>Proverbs 29:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/29#v7)</sup> The righteous considereth the cause of the poor: but the wicked regardeth not to know it.

</details>
<details><summary>Isaiah 32:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/32#v7)</sup> The instruments also of the churl are evil: he deviseth wicked devices to destroy the poor with lying words, even when the needy speaketh right.

</details>
<details><summary>James 2:15-16</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/james/2#v15)</sup> If a brother or sister be naked, and destitute of daily food,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/james/2#v16)</sup> And one of you say unto them, Depart in peace, be ye warmed and filled; notwithstanding ye give them not those things which are needful to the body; what doth it profit?

</details>

### 15A.3 {#15A.3}

If because of food your brother is hurt, you are no longer walking charitably.

[Rom 14:15](/bible/romans/14#v15)

<details><summary>Romans 14:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/romans/14#v15)</sup> But if thy brother be grieved with thy meat, now walkest thou not charitably. Destroy not him with thy meat, for whom Christ died.

</details>

### 15A.4 {#15A.4}

The love of God does not dwell in those that are uncharitable.

[1 John 3:17](/bible/1-john/3#v17)

<details><summary>1 John 3:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-john/3#v17)</sup> But whoso hath this world’s good, and seeth his brother have need, and shutteth up his bowels of compassion from him, how dwelleth the love of God in him?

</details>

### 15A.5 {#15A.5}

It is more blessed to give than to receive.

[Acts 20:35](/bible/acts/20#v35)

<details><summary>Acts 20:35</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/acts/20#v35)</sup> I have shewed you all things, how that so labouring ye ought to support the weak, and to remember the words of the Lord Jesus, how he said, It is more blessed to give than to receive.

</details>

### 15A.6 {#15A.6}

Every man is to give according to the purpose that God has placed in his heart.

[2 Cor 9:7](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v7)

<details><summary>2 Corinthians 9:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v7)</sup> Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver.

</details>

### 15A.7 {#15A.7}

Do not neglect to show hospitality to strangers, for by so doing some have entertained angels without knowing it.

[Heb 13:2](/bible/hebrews/13#v2)

<details><summary>Hebrews 13:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/hebrews/13#v2)</sup> Be not forgetful to entertain strangers: for thereby some have entertained angels unawares.

</details>

### 15A.8 {#15A.8}

Charity and love is to be sought above all things.

[Col 3:14](/bible/colossians/3#v14)

<details><summary>Colossians 3:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/colossians/3#v14)</sup> And above all these things put on charity, which is the bond of perfectness.

</details>

### 15A.9 {#15A.9}

We are to be hospitable towards one another without grudging.

[Rom 12:13](/bible/romans/12#v13); [1 Tim 5:10](/bible/1-timothy/5#v10); [1 Pet 4:9](/bible/1-peter/4#v9)

<details><summary>Romans 12:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/12#v13)</sup> Distributing to the necessity of saints; given to hospitality.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 5:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-timothy/5#v10)</sup> Well reported of for good works; if she have brought up children, if she have lodged strangers, if she have washed the saints’ feet, if she have relieved the afflicted, if she have diligently followed every good work.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Peter 4:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-peter/4#v9)</sup> Use hospitality one to another without grudging.

</details>

Give not grudgingly or of necessity, for God loves a cheerful giver.

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v7)</sup> Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver.

<details><summary>1 Peter 4:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-peter/4#v9)</sup> Use hospitality one to another without grudging.

</details>

### 15A.11 {#15A.11}

Aid and hospitality is to be given to the poor.

[Isa 58:7](/bible/isaiah/58#v7); [Luke 14:13](/bible/luke/14#v13)

<details><summary>Isaiah 58:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/58#v7)</sup> Is it not to deal thy bread to the hungry, and that thou bring the poor that are cast out to thy house? when thou seest the naked, that thou cover him; and that thou hide not thyself from thine own flesh?

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 14:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/luke/14#v13)</sup> But when thou makest a feast, call the poor, the maimed, the lame, the blind:

</details>

### 15A.12 {#15A.12}

The righteous man gives his bread to the hungry, and covers the naked with clothing, but the unrighteous man who oppresses the poor and needy shall not live.

[Ezek 18:5,7,12,13](/bible/ezekiel/18#v5)

<details><summary>Ezekiel 18:5,7,12,13</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/18#v5)</sup> But if a man be just, and do that which is lawful and right,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/18#v7)</sup> And hath not oppressed any, but hath restored to the debtor his pledge, hath spoiled none by violence, hath given his bread to the hungry, and hath covered the naked with a garment;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/18#v12)</sup> Hath oppressed the poor and needy, hath spoiled by violence, hath not restored the pledge, and hath lifted up his eyes to the idols, hath committed abomination,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/18#v13)</sup> Hath given forth upon usury, and hath taken increase: shall he then live? he shall not live: he hath done all these abominations; he shall surely die; his blood shall be upon him.

</details>

### 15A.13 {#15A.13}

Charity shall cover a multitude of sins.

[1 Pet 4:8](/bible/1-peter/4#v8)

<details><summary>1 Peter 4:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-peter/4#v8)</sup> And above all things have fervent charity among yourselves: for charity shall cover the multitude of sins.

</details>

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 10:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v24)</sup> Let no man seek his own, but every man another’s wealth.

</details>

<details><summary>Proverbs 21:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/21#v13)</sup> Whoso stoppeth his ears at the cry of the poor, he also shall cry himself, but shall not be heard.

</details>

<details><summary>Matthew 5:42</summary>
