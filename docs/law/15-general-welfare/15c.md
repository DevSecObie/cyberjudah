### 15C.1 {#15C.1}

We should be of aid and help to the fatherless and widows with their afflictions.

[Deut 14:29](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v29); [Job 24:21](/bible/job/24#v21); [Job 29:12-13](/bible/job/29#v12); [James 1:27](/bible/james/1#v27); [1 Tim 5:16](/bible/1-timothy/5#v16)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:29</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v29)</sup> And the Levite, (because he hath no part nor inheritance with thee,) and the stranger, and the fatherless, and the widow, which are within thy gates, shall come, and shall eat and be satisfied; that the Lord thy God may bless thee in all the work of thine hand which thou doest.

</details>
<details><summary>Job 24:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/job/24#v21)</sup> He evil entreateth the barren that beareth not: and doeth not good to the widow.

</details>
<details><summary>Job 29:12-13</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/job/29#v12)</sup> Because I delivered the poor that cried, and the fatherless, and him that had none to help him.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/29#v13)</sup> The blessing of him that was ready to perish came upon me: and I caused the widow’s heart to sing for joy.

</details>
<details><summary>James 1:27</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/james/1#v27)</sup> Pure religion and undefiled before God and the Father is this, To visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and to keep himself unspotted from the world.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 5:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/1-timothy/5#v16)</sup> If any man or woman that believeth have widows, let them relieve them, and let not the church be charged; that it may relieve them that are widows indeed.

</details>

### 15C.2 {#15C.2}

Do not take a widow's raiment in pledge.

[Deut 24:17](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v17)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 24:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not pervert the judgment of the stranger, nor of the fatherless; nor take a widow’s raiment to pledge:

</details>

### 15C.3 {#15C.3}

You shall not afflict or oppress any widow or fatherless child.

[Exod 22:22-3](/bible/exodus/22#v22); [Deut 27:19](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v19); [Jer 7:6-7](/bible/jeremiah/7#v6); [Jer 22:3](/bible/jeremiah/22#v3); [Zech 7:10](/bible/zechariah/7#v10)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:22-3</summary>



</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 27:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v19)</sup> Cursed be he that perverteth the judgment of the stranger, fatherless, and widow. And all the people shall say, Amen.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 7:6-7</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/7#v6)</sup> If ye oppress not the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow, and shed not innocent blood in this place, neither walk after other gods to your hurt:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/7#v7)</sup> Then will I cause you to dwell in this place, in the land that I gave to your fathers, for ever and ever.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 22:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/22#v3)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Execute ye judgment and righteousness, and deliver the spoiled out of the hand of the oppressor: and do no wrong, do no violence to the stranger, the fatherless, nor the widow, neither shed innocent blood in this place.

</details>
<details><summary>Zechariah 7:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/zechariah/7#v10)</sup> And oppress not the widow, nor the fatherless, the stranger, nor the poor; and let none of you imagine evil against his brother in your heart.

</details>

### 15C.4 {#15C.4}

We should defend the fatherless and needy and plead for the widow.

[Ps 82:3](/bible/psalms/82#v3); [Isa 1:17](/bible/isaiah/1#v17)

<details><summary>Psalms 82:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/82#v3)</sup> Defend the poor and fatherless: do justice to the afflicted and needy.

</details>
<details><summary>Isaiah 1:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/1#v17)</sup> Learn to do well; seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow.

</details>

### 15C.5 {#15C.5}

Curses and Punishments will befall those who neglect or cause affliction to the fatherless and widows.

[Deut 27:19](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v19); [Job 31:16-23](/bible/job/31#v16); [Mal 3:5](/bible/malachi/3#v5)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 27:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v19)</sup> Cursed be he that perverteth the judgment of the stranger, fatherless, and widow. And all the people shall say, Amen.

</details>
<details><summary>Job 31:16-23</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/job/31#v16)</sup> If I have withheld the poor from their desire, or have caused the eyes of the widow to fail;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/31#v17)</sup> Or have eaten my morsel myself alone, and the fatherless hath not eaten thereof;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/job/31#v18)</sup> (For from my youth he was brought up with me, as with a father, and I have guided her from my mother’s womb;)
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/job/31#v19)</sup> If I have seen any perish for want of clothing, or any poor without covering;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/job/31#v20)</sup> If his loins have not blessed me, and if he were not warmed with the fleece of my sheep;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/job/31#v21)</sup> If I have lifted up my hand against the fatherless, when I saw my help in the gate:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/job/31#v22)</sup> Then let mine arm fall from my shoulder blade, and mine arm be broken from the bone.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/job/31#v23)</sup> For destruction from God was a terror to me, and by reason of his highness I could not endure.

</details>
<details><summary>Malachi 3:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/malachi/3#v5)</sup> And I will come near to you to judgment; and I will be a swift witness against the sorcerers, and against the adulterers, and against false swearers, and against those that oppress the hireling in his wages, the widow, and the fatherless, and that turn aside the stranger from his right, and fear not me, saith the Lord of hosts.

</details>

</details>

Honor widows who are really widows (those 60 years old and up and been the wife of one man).

<details><summary>1 Timothy 5:3,9</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-timothy/5#v3)</sup> Honour widows that are widows indeed.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-timothy/5#v9)</sup> Let not a widow be taken into the number under threescore years old, having been the wife of one man,

</details>
