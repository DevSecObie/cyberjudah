### 2E.2 {#2E.2}

God is faithful to those that love Him, and keeps His covenant and mercy with them (God acknowledges those who love Him).

[Deut 7:9](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v9); [1 Cor 8:3](/bible/1-corinthians/8#v3)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 7:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v9)</sup> Know therefore that the Lord thy God, he is God, the faithful God, which keepeth covenant and mercy with them that love him and keep his commandments to a thousand generations;

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 8:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/8#v3)</sup> But if any man love God, the same is known of him.

</details>

</details>

>

<details><summary>John 14:15,21-24</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/john/14#v15)</sup> If ye love me, keep my commandments.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/john/14#v21)</sup> He that hath my commandments, and keepeth them, he it is that loveth me: and he that loveth me shall be loved of my Father, and I will love him, and will manifest myself to him.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/john/14#v22)</sup> Judas saith unto him, not Iscariot, Lord, how is it that thou wilt manifest thyself unto us, and not unto the world?
>

### 2E.11 {#2E.11}

We are not to allow anything to separate us from the love of God which is in Jesus Christ -not tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword, or principalities or powers.

[Rom 8:35-39](/bible/romans/8#v35)

<details><summary>Romans 8:35-39</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/romans/8#v35)</sup> Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword?
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/romans/8#v36)</sup> As it is written, For thy sake we are killed all the day long; we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/romans/8#v37)</sup> Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him that loved us.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/romans/8#v38)</sup> For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come,
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/romans/8#v39)</sup> Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

</details>
