### 2C.1 {#2C.1}

To fear God is a primary duty of man.

[Eccl 12:13](/bible/ecclesiastes/12#v13)

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 12:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/ecclesiastes/12#v13)</sup> Let us hear the conclusion of the whole matter: Fear God, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man.

</details>

</details>
<details><summary>Joshua 4:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/joshua/4#v24)</sup> That all the people of the earth might know the hand of the Lord, that it is mighty: that ye might fear the Lord your God for ever.
