### 2M.1 {#2M.1}

[1 Chr 16:28-29](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v28); [Ps 22:23](/bible/psalms/22#v23); [Ps 86:9](/bible/psalms/86#v9); [Isa 42:12](/bible/isaiah/42#v12); [Isa 43:21](/bible/isaiah/43#v21); [Matt 15:31](/bible/matthew/15#v31); [Acts 4:21](/bible/acts/4#v21); [Acts 11:18](/bible/acts/11#v18); [Rev 5:13](/bible/revelation/5#v13); [Rev 14:7](/bible/revelation/14#v7)

### 2M.9 {#2M.9}

Punishment for not giving glory unto the name of God.

[Dan 5:23](/bible/daniel/5#v23); [Mal 2:2](/bible/malachi/2#v2); [Acts 12:23](/bible/acts/12#v23); [Rom 1:21](/bible/romans/1#v21)

<details><summary>Daniel 5:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/daniel/5#v23)</sup> But hast lifted up thyself against the Lord of heaven; and they have brought the vessels of his house before thee, and thou, and thy lords, thy wives, and thy concubines, have drunk wine in them; and thou hast praised the gods of silver, and gold, of brass, iron, wood, and stone, which see not, nor hear, nor know: and the God in whose hand thy breath is, and whose are all thy ways, hast thou not glorified:

</details>
<details><summary>Malachi 2:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/malachi/2#v2)</sup> If ye will not hear, and if ye will not lay it to heart, to give glory unto my name, saith the Lord of hosts, I will even send a curse upon you, and I will curse your blessings: yea, I have cursed them already, because ye do not lay it to heart.

</details>
<details><summary>Acts 12:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/acts/12#v23)</sup> And immediately the angel of the Lord smote him, because he gave not God the glory: and he was eaten of worms, and gave up the ghost.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 1:21</summary>
