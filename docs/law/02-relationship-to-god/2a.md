### 2A.1 {#2A.1}

Access to God is by Christ.

[John 14:6-7](/bible/john/14#v6); [Rom 5:1-2](/bible/romans/5#v1); [Eph 2:12-13,18](/bible/ephesians/2#v12); [Heb 7:19,25](/bible/hebrews/7#v19); [Heb 10:19](/bible/hebrews/10#v19); [1 Pet 3:18](/bible/1-peter/3#v18)

<details><summary>John 14:6-7</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/john/14#v6)</sup> Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/john/14#v7)</sup> If ye had known me, ye should have known my Father also: and from henceforth ye know him, and have seen him.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 5:1-2</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/5#v1)</sup> Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/5#v2)</sup> By whom also we have access by faith into this grace wherein we stand, and rejoice in hope of the glory of God.

</details>
<details><summary>Ephesians 2:12-13,18</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/ephesians/2#v12)</sup> That at that time ye were without Christ, being aliens from the commonwealth of Israel, and strangers from the covenants of promise, having no hope, and without God in the world:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ephesians/2#v13)</sup> But now in Christ Jesus ye who sometimes were far off are made nigh by the blood of Christ.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ephesians/2#v18)</sup> For through him we both have access by one Spirit unto the Father.

</details>
<details><summary>Hebrews 7:19,25</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/hebrews/7#v19)</sup> For the law made nothing perfect, but the bringing in of a better hope did; by the which we draw nigh unto God.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/hebrews/7#v25)</sup> Wherefore he is able also to save them to the uttermost that come unto God by him, seeing he ever liveth to make intercession for them.

</details>
<details><summary>Hebrews 10:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/hebrews/10#v19)</sup> Having therefore, brethren, boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus,

</details>
<details><summary>1 Peter 3:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/1-peter/3#v18)</sup> For Christ also hath once suffered for sins, the just for the unjust, that he might bring us to God, being put to death in the flesh, but quickened by the Spirit:

</details>

### 2A.6 {#2A.6}

If you seek God, He will be found by you; but if you forsake Him, He will cast you off.

[Deut 4:27-29](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v27); [1 Chr 28:9](/bible/1-chronicles/28#v9)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 4:27-29</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v27)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter you among the nations, and ye shall be left few in number among the heathen, whither the Lord shall lead you.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v28)</sup> And there ye shall serve gods, the work of men’s hands, wood and stone, which neither see, nor hear, nor eat, nor smell.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v29)</sup> But if from thence thou shalt seek the Lord thy God, thou shalt find him, if thou seek him with all thy heart and with all thy soul.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Chronicles 28:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/28#v9)</sup> And thou, Solomon my son, know thou the God of thy father, and serve him with a perfect heart and with a willing mind: for the Lord searcheth all hearts, and understandeth all the imaginations of the thoughts: if thou seek him, he will be found of thee; but if thou forsake him, he will cast thee off for ever.

</details>
