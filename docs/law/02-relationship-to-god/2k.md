### 2K.1 {#2K.1}

You are not to take the name of God in vain.

[Exod 20:7](/bible/exodus/20#v7); [Deut 5:11](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v11)

<details><summary>Exodus 20:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/20#v7)</sup> Thou shalt not take the name of the Lord thy God in vain; for the Lord will not hold him guiltless that taketh his name in vain.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 5:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v11)</sup> Thou shalt not take the name of the Lord thy God in vain: for the Lord will not hold him guiltless that taketh his name in vain.

</details>

### 2K.2 {#2K.2}

Cursing or blasphemy against God forbidden.

[Col 3:8](/bible/colossians/3#v8); [Lev 18:21](/bible/leviticus/18#v21); [Lev 19:12](/bible/leviticus/19#v12); [Lev 24:15-16](/bible/leviticus/24#v15); [Ps 109:17-18](/bible/psalms/109#v17)

<details><summary>Colossians 3:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/colossians/3#v8)</sup> But now ye also put off all these; anger, wrath, malice, blasphemy, filthy communication out of your mouth.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 18:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/18#v21)</sup> And thou shalt not let any of thy seed pass through the fire to Molech, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy God: I am the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 19:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/19#v12)</sup> And ye shall not swear by my name falsely, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy God: I am the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 24:15-16</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/24#v15)</sup> And thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel, saying, Whosoever curseth his God shall bear his sin.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/24#v16)</sup> And he that blasphemeth the name of the Lord, he shall surely be put to death, and all the congregation shall certainly stone him: as well the stranger, as he that is born in the land, when he blasphemeth the name of the Lord, shall be put to death.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 109:17-18</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/109#v17)</sup> As he loved cursing, so let it come unto him: as he delighted not in blessing, so let it be far from him.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/109#v18)</sup> As he clothed himself with cursing like as with his garment, so let it come into his bowels like water, and like oil into his bones.

</details>

### 2K.3 {#2K.3}

We should give no occasion for blasphemy to occur.

[2 Sam 12:14](/bible/2-samuel/12#v14); [1 Tim 6:1](/bible/1-timothy/6#v1)

<details><summary>2 Samuel 12:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-samuel/12#v14)</sup> Howbeit, because by this deed thou hast given great occasion to the enemies of the Lord to blaspheme, the child also that is born unto thee shall surely die.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 6:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-timothy/6#v1)</sup> Let as many servants as are under the yoke count their own masters worthy of all honour, that the name of God and his doctrine be not blasphemed.

</details>

### 2K.4 {#2K.4}

Blasphemy against the Holy Spirit never to be forgiven.

[Matt 12:31-32](/bible/matthew/12#v31); [Mark 3:29](/bible/mark/3#v29); [Luke 12:10](/bible/luke/12#v10)

<details><summary>Matthew 12:31-32</summary>

> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/12#v31)</sup> Wherefore I say unto you, All manner of sin and blasphemy shall be forgiven unto men: but the blasphemy against the Holy Ghost shall not be forgiven unto men.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/12#v32)</sup> And whosoever speaketh a word against the Son of man, it shall be forgiven him: but whosoever speaketh against the Holy Ghost, it shall not be forgiven him, neither in this world, neither in the world to come.

</details>
<details><summary>Mark 3:29</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/mark/3#v29)</sup> But he that shall blaspheme against the Holy Ghost hath never forgiveness, but is in danger of eternal damnation:

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 12:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/luke/12#v10)</sup> And whosoever shall speak a word against the Son of man, it shall be forgiven him: but unto him that blasphemeth against the Holy Ghost it shall not be forgiven.

</details>

### 2K.5 {#2K.5}

Whoever blasphemes the name of the LORD shall surely be put to death.

[Lev 24:16,23](/bible/leviticus/24#v16); [1 Kgs 21:10,13](/bible/1-kings/21#v10)

<details><summary>Leviticus 24:16,23</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/24#v16)</sup> And he that blasphemeth the name of the Lord, he shall surely be put to death, and all the congregation shall certainly stone him: as well the stranger, as he that is born in the land, when he blasphemeth the name of the Lord, shall be put to death.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/24#v23)</sup> And Moses spake to the children of Israel, that they should bring forth him that had cursed out of the camp, and stone him with stones. And the children of Israel did as the Lord commanded Moses.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Kings 21:10,13</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-kings/21#v10)</sup> And set two men, sons of Belial, before him, to bear witness against him, saying, Thou didst blaspheme God and the king. And then carry him out, and stone him, that he may die.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-kings/21#v13)</sup> And there came in two men, children of Belial, and sat before him: and the men of Belial witnessed against him, even against Naboth, in the presence of the people, saying, Naboth did blaspheme God and the king. Then they carried him forth out of the city, and stoned him with stones, that he died.

</details>

### 2K.6 {#2K.6}

Pray to keep yourself from profaning God's name.

[Prov 30:9](/bible/proverbs/30#v9)

<details><summary>Proverbs 30:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/30#v9)</sup> Lest I be full, and deny thee, and say, Who is the Lord? or lest I be poor, and steal, and take the name of my God in vain.

</details>

### 2K.7 {#2K.7}

One blasphemes the name of God by breaking the law which he preaches others to keep.

[Rom 2:21-24](/bible/romans/2#v21)

<details><summary>Romans 2:21-24</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/romans/2#v21)</sup> Thou therefore which teachest another, teachest thou not thyself? thou that preachest a man should not steal, dost thou steal?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/romans/2#v22)</sup> Thou that sayest a man should not commit adultery, dost thou commit adultery? thou that abhorrest idols, dost thou commit sacrilege?
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/romans/2#v23)</sup> Thou that makest thy boast of the law, through breaking the law dishonourest thou God?
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/romans/2#v24)</sup> For the name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles through you, as it is written.

</details>

### 2K.8 {#2K.8}

The nation suffers from swearing.

[Jer 23:10](/bible/jeremiah/23#v10); [Hos 4:2-3](/bible/hosea/4#v2)

<details><summary>Jeremiah 23:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/23#v10)</sup> For the land is full of adulterers; for because of swearing the land mourneth; the pleasant places of the wilderness are dried up, and their course is evil, and their force is not right.

</details>
<details><summary>Hosea 4:2-3</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/4#v2)</sup> By swearing, and lying, and killing, and stealing, and committing adultery, they break out, and blood toucheth blood.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/4#v3)</sup> Therefore shall the land mourn, and every one that dwelleth therein shall languish, with the beasts of the field, and with the fowls of heaven; yea, the fishes of the sea also shall be taken away.

</details>

It is a foolish people who blaspheme God's name.

[Ps 74:18](/bible/psalms/74#v18)

<details><summary>Psalms 74:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/74#v18)</sup> Remember this, that the enemy hath reproached, O Lord, and that the foolish people have blasphemed thy name.

</details>
