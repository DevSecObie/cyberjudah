### 2P.2 {#2P.2}

<details><summary>Job 4:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/job/4#v8)</sup> Even as I have seen, they that plow iniquity, and sow wickedness, reap the same.

</details>

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/91#v8)</sup> Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked.

</details>

### 2P.5 {#2P.5}

God chastises and punishes only His sons who He loves, for if you receive not chastisement, then are you bastards and not sons.

[Ps 89:30-32](/bible/psalms/89#v30); [Prov 3:11-12](/bible/proverbs/3#v11); [Heb 12:8](/bible/hebrews/12#v8)

<details><summary>Psalms 89:30-32</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/psalms/89#v30)</sup> If his children forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments;
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/psalms/89#v31)</sup> If they break my statutes, and keep not my commandments;
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/psalms/89#v32)</sup> Then will I visit their transgression with the rod, and their iniquity with stripes.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 3:11-12</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/3#v11)</sup> My son, despise not the chastening of the Lord; neither be weary of his correction:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/3#v12)</sup> For whom the Lord loveth he correcteth; even as a father the son in whom he delighteth.

</details>
<details><summary>Hebrews 12:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/hebrews/12#v8)</sup> But if ye be without chastisement, whereof all are partakers, then are ye bastards, and not sons.

</details>
