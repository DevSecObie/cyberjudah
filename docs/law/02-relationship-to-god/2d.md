[Ps 115:4-8](/bible/psalms/115#v4); [Isa 2:20](/bible/isaiah/2#v20); [Isa 42:17](/bible/isaiah/42#v17); [Isa 44:9,17-19](/bible/isaiah/44#v9); [Jer 10:14-15](/bible/jeremiah/10#v14); [Jer 11:12-14](/bible/jeremiah/11#v12); [Jer 16:20](/bible/jeremiah/16#v20); [Rom 1:21-25](/bible/romans/1#v21)

<details><summary>Psalms 115:4-8</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/115#v4)</sup> Their idols are silver and gold, the work of men’s hands.
>

>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/115#v6)</sup> They have ears, but they hear not: noses have they, but they smell not:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/115#v7)</sup> They have hands, but they handle not: feet have they, but they walk not: neither speak they through their throat.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/115#v8)</sup> They that make them are like unto them; so is every one that trusteth in them.

</details>

</details>

> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/10#v15)</sup> They are vanity, and the work of errors: in the time of their visitation they shall perish.

### 2D.11 {#2D.11}

We are not to acknowledge the gods of heathen people by making mention of their name or by swearing by them.

[Exod 23:13](/bible/exodus/23#v13); [Josh 23:7](/bible/joshua/23#v7)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/23#v13)</sup> And in all things that I have said unto you be circumspect: and make no mention of the name of other gods, neither let it be heard out of thy mouth.

</details>
<details><summary>Joshua 23:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/23#v7)</sup> That ye come not among these nations, these that remain among you; neither make mention of the name of their gods, nor cause to swear by them, neither serve them, nor bow yourselves unto them:

</details>

### 2D.12 {#2D.12}

<details><summary>Exodus 22:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/22#v20)</sup> He that sacrificeth unto any god, save unto the Lord only, he shall be utterly destroyed.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 17:2-5</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v2)</sup> If there be found among you, within any of thy gates which the Lord thy God giveth thee, man or woman, that hath wrought wickedness in the sight of the Lord thy God, in transgressing his covenant,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v3)</sup> And hath gone and served other gods, and worshipped them, either the sun, or moon, or any of the host of heaven, which I have not commanded;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v4)</sup> And it be told thee, and thou hast heard of it, and enquired diligently, and, behold, it be true, and the thing certain, that such abomination is wrought in Israel:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v5)</sup> Then shalt thou bring forth that man or that woman, which have committed that wicked thing, unto thy gates, even that man or that woman, and shalt stone them with stones, till they die.

</details>

### 2D.14 {#2D.14}

Do not set up idols in your heart, for it will cause you to stumble into iniquity.

[Ezek 14:3,4,7](/bible/ezekiel/14#v3)

<details><summary>Ezekiel 14:3,4,7</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/14#v3)</sup> Son of man, these men have set up their idols in their heart, and put the stumblingblock of their iniquity before their face: should I be enquired of at all by them?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/14#v4)</sup> Therefore speak unto them, and say unto them, Thus saith the Lord God; Every man of the house of Israel that setteth up his idols in his heart, and putteth the stumblingblock of his iniquity before his face, and cometh to the prophet; I the Lord will answer him that cometh according to the multitude of his idols;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/14#v7)</sup> For every one of the house of Israel, or of the stranger that sojourneth in Israel, which separateth himself from me, and setteth up his idols in his heart, and putteth the stumblingblock of his iniquity before his face, and cometh to a prophet to enquire of him concerning me; I the Lord will answer him by myself:

</details>
