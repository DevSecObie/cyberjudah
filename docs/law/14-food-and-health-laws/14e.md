### 14E.1 {#14E.1}

Fast observed on occasions of judgments of God.

[Joel 1:14](/bible/joel/1#v14); [Joel 2:12](/bible/joel/2#v12)

<details><summary>Joel 1:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/joel/1#v14)</sup> Sanctify ye a fast, call a solemn assembly, gather the elders and all the inhabitants of the land into the house of the Lord your God, and cry unto the Lord,

</details>
<details><summary>Joel 2:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/joel/2#v12)</sup> Therefore also now, saith the Lord, turn ye even to me with all your heart, and with fasting, and with weeping, and with mourning:

</details>

### 14E.2 {#14E.2}

Fasting is not to be made a subject of display.

[Matt 6:16-18](/bible/matthew/6#v16)

<details><summary>Matthew 6:16-18</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/6#v16)</sup> Moreover when ye fast, be not, as the hypocrites, of a sad countenance: for they disfigure their faces, that they may appear unto men to fast. Verily I say unto you, They have their reward.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/6#v17)</sup> But thou, when thou fastest, anoint thine head, and wash thy face;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/6#v18)</sup> That thou appear not unto men to fast, but unto thy Father which is in secret: and thy Father, which seeth in secret, shall reward thee openly.

</details>

### 14E.3 {#14E.3}

Fasting is to be unto God.

[Zech 7:5](/bible/zechariah/7#v5); [Matt 6:18](/bible/matthew/6#v18)

<details><summary>Zechariah 7:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/zechariah/7#v5)</sup> Speak unto all the people of the land, and to the priests, saying, When ye fasted and mourned in the fifth and seventh month, even those seventy years, did ye at all fast unto me, even to me?

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 6:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/6#v18)</sup> That thou appear not unto men to fast, but unto thy Father which is in secret: and thy Father, which seeth in secret, shall reward thee openly.

</details>

### 14E.4 {#14E.4}

Fasting for private afflictions.

[2 Sam 12:16](/bible/2-samuel/12#v16)

<details><summary>2 Samuel 12:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/2-samuel/12#v16)</sup> David therefore besought God for the child; and David fasted, and went in, and lay all night upon the earth.

</details>

### 14E.5 {#14E.5}

You shall 'afflict your souls' (fast) on the Day of Atonement.

[Lev 16:29,31](/bible/leviticus/16#v29); [Lev 23:27,29](/bible/leviticus/23#v27)

<details><summary>Leviticus 16:29,31</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/leviticus/16#v29)</sup> And this shall be a statute for ever unto you: that in the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month, ye shall afflict your souls, and do no work at all, whether it be one of your own country, or a stranger that sojourneth among you:
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/leviticus/16#v31)</sup> It shall be a sabbath of rest unto you, and ye shall afflict your souls, by a statute for ever.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 23:27,29</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/23#v27)</sup> Also on the tenth day of this seventh month there shall be a day of atonement: it shall be an holy convocation unto you; and ye shall afflict your souls, and offer an offering made by fire unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/leviticus/23#v29)</sup> For whatsoever soul it be that shall not be afflicted in that same day, he shall be cut off from among his people.

</details>

### 14E.6 {#14E.6}

Fasting accompanied by confession of sin.

[1 Sam 7:6](/bible/1-samuel/7#v6); [Neh 9:1-2](/bible/nehemiah/9#v1)

<details><summary>1 Samuel 7:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-samuel/7#v6)</sup> And they gathered together to Mizpeh, and drew water, and poured it out before the Lord, and fasted on that day, and said there, We have sinned against the Lord. And Samuel judged the children of Israel in Mizpeh.

</details>
<details><summary>Nehemiah 9:1-2</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/nehemiah/9#v1)</sup> Now in the twenty and fourth day of this month the children of Israel were assembled with fasting, and with sackclothes, and earth upon them.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/nehemiah/9#v2)</sup> And the seed of Israel separated themselves from all strangers, and stood and confessed their sins, and the iniquities of their fathers.

</details>

### 14E.7 {#14E.7}

Public fasts proclaimed.

[1 Kgs 21:9](/bible/1-kings/21#v9); [Ezra 8:21](/bible/ezra/8#v21); [Zech 8:19](/bible/zechariah/8#v19)

<details><summary>1 Kings 21:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-kings/21#v9)</sup> And she wrote in the letters, saying, Proclaim a fast, and set Naboth on high among the people:

</details>
<details><summary>Ezra 8:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/ezra/8#v21)</sup> Then I proclaimed a fast there, at the river of Ahava, that we might afflict ourselves before our God, to seek of him a right way for us, and for our little ones, and for all our substance.

</details>
<details><summary>Zechariah 8:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/zechariah/8#v19)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts; The fast of the fourth month, and the fast of the fifth, and the fast of the seventh, and the fast of the tenth, shall be to the house of Judah joy and gladness, and cheerful feasts; therefore love the truth and peace.

</details>
