### 14A.1 {#14A.1}

Whatever animals that have divided or cloven hoofs and chews its cud you may eat.

[Lev 11:2](/bible/leviticus/11#v2); [Deut 14:6](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v6)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/11#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, These are the beasts which ye shall eat among all the beasts that are on the earth.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v6)</sup> And every beast that parteth the hoof, and cleaveth the cleft into two claws, and cheweth the cud among the beasts, that ye shall eat.

</details>

### 14A.2 {#14A.2}

Fishes that have fins and scales may be eaten (Bass, croppie, flounder, cod, haddock, pike, salmon, sunfish, mackerel, snapper, trout, perch, smelt, tuna, etc.).

[Lev 11:9](/bible/leviticus/11#v9); [Deut 14:9](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v9)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/11#v9)</sup> These shall ye eat of all that are in the waters: whatsoever hath fins and scales in the waters, in the seas, and in the rivers, them shall ye eat.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v9)</sup> These ye shall eat of all that are in the waters: all that have fins and scales shall ye eat:

</details>

### 14A.3 {#14A.3}

All clean birds can be eaten (e.g., chicken, turkey, pheasant, quail, grouse, partridge, etc.).

[Deut 14:11,20](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v11)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:11,20</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v11)</sup> Of all clean birds ye shall eat.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v20)</sup> But of all clean fowls ye may eat.

</details>

### 14A.4 {#14A.4}

Insects-locust, bald locust, grasshoppers.

[Lev 11:22](/bible/leviticus/11#v22)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/11#v22)</sup> Even these of them ye may eat; the locust after his kind, and the bald locust after his kind, and the beetle after his kind, and the grasshopper after his kind.

</details>

### 14A.5 {#14A.5}

If a clean animal dies it is considered unclean until evening.

[Lev 11:39-30](/bible/leviticus/11#v39)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:39-30</summary>



</details>

### 14A.6 {#14A.6}

Animals which you can eat--ox, sheep, goats, deer, roebuck, gazelle, fallow deer, wild goat, antelope, buffalo, caribou, elk, ibex, wild ox, moose, chamois.

[Deut 12:15,22](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v15); [Deut 14:4-6](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v4)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 12:15,22</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v15)</sup> Notwithstanding thou mayest kill and eat flesh in all thy gates, whatsoever thy soul lusteth after, according to the blessing of the Lord thy God which he hath given thee: the unclean and the clean may eat thereof, as of the roebuck, and as of the hart.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v22)</sup> Even as the roebuck and the hart is eaten, so thou shalt eat them: the unclean and the clean shall eat of them alike.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:4-6</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v4)</sup> These are the beasts which ye shall eat: the ox, the sheep, and the goat,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v5)</sup> The hart, and the roebuck, and the fallow deer, and the wild goat, and the pygarg, and the wild ox, and the chamois.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v6)</sup> And every beast that parteth the hoof, and cleaveth the cleft into two claws, and cheweth the cud among the beasts, that ye shall eat.

</details>
