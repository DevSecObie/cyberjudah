### 14D.1 {#14D.1}

You shall not eat anything which dies of itself.

[Deut 14:21](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v21)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v21)</sup> Ye shall not eat of any thing that dieth of itself: thou shalt give it unto the stranger that is in thy gates, that he may eat it; or thou mayest sell it unto an alien: for thou art an holy people unto the Lord thy God. Thou shalt not seethe a kid in his mother’s milk.

</details>

### 14D.2 {#14D.2}

That which dies of itself may be given or sold to foreigners.

[Deut 14:21](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v21)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v21)</sup> Ye shall not eat of any thing that dieth of itself: thou shalt give it unto the stranger that is in thy gates, that he may eat it; or thou mayest sell it unto an alien: for thou art an holy people unto the Lord thy God. Thou shalt not seethe a kid in his mother’s milk.

</details>

### 14D.3 {#14D.3}

One who eats any animal that died of itself shall wash his clothes and bathe, and will be unclean until evening; otherwise he will bear his iniquity.

[Lev 17:15-16](/bible/leviticus/17#v15)

<details><summary>Leviticus 17:15-16</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/17#v15)</sup> And every soul that eateth that which died of itself, or that which was torn with beasts, whether it be one of your own country, or a stranger, he shall both wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the even: then shall he be clean.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/17#v16)</sup> But if he wash them not, nor bathe his flesh; then he shall bear his iniquity.

</details>

### 14D.4 {#14D.4}

You shall eat no manner of fat from any animal.

[Lev 3:17](/bible/leviticus/3#v17); [Lev 7:23-25](/bible/leviticus/7#v23)

<details><summary>Leviticus 3:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/3#v17)</sup> It shall be a perpetual statute for your generations throughout all your dwellings, that ye eat neither fat nor blood.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 7:23-25</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/7#v23)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, Ye shall eat no manner of fat, of ox, or of sheep, or of goat.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/7#v24)</sup> And the fat of the beast that dieth of itself, and the fat of that which is torn with beasts, may be used in any other use: but ye shall in no wise eat of it.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/leviticus/7#v25)</sup> For whosoever eateth the fat of the beast, of which men offer an offering made by fire unto the Lord, even the soul that eateth it shall be cut off from his people.

</details>
