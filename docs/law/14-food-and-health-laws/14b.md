### 14B.1 {#14B.1}

Camel (does not have a divided hoof).

[Lev 11:4](/bible/leviticus/11#v4); [Deut 14:7](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v7)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/11#v4)</sup> Nevertheless these shall ye not eat of them that chew the cud, or of them that divide the hoof: as the camel, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v7)</sup> Nevertheless these ye shall not eat of them that chew the cud, or of them that divide the cloven hoof; as the camel, and the hare, and the coney: for they chew the cud, but divide not the hoof; therefore they are unclean unto you.

</details>

### 14B.2 {#14B.2}

Rabbits, hares, and rock hyrax (does not have a divided hoof).

[Lev 11:5-6](/bible/leviticus/11#v5); [Deut 14:7](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v7)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:5-6</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/11#v5)</sup> And the coney, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/11#v6)</sup> And the hare, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v7)</sup> Nevertheless these ye shall not eat of them that chew the cud, or of them that divide the cloven hoof; as the camel, and the hare, and the coney: for they chew the cud, but divide not the hoof; therefore they are unclean unto you.

</details>

### 14B.3 {#14B.3}

Cattle which either do not chew the cud or divide the hoof (horse, donkey, quagga, llama, etc.).

[Lev 11:4](/bible/leviticus/11#v4); [Deut 14:7](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v7)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/11#v4)</sup> Nevertheless these shall ye not eat of them that chew the cud, or of them that divide the hoof: as the camel, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v7)</sup> Nevertheless these ye shall not eat of them that chew the cud, or of them that divide the cloven hoof; as the camel, and the hare, and the coney: for they chew the cud, but divide not the hoof; therefore they are unclean unto you.

</details>

### 14B.4 {#14B.4}

Swine or hog (does not chew the cud).

[Lev 11:7](/bible/leviticus/11#v7); [Deut 14:8](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v8)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/11#v7)</sup> And the swine, though he divide the hoof, and be clovenfooted, yet he cheweth not the cud; he is unclean to you.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v8)</sup> And the swine, because it divideth the hoof, yet cheweth not the cud, it is unclean unto you: ye shall not eat of their flesh, nor touch their dead carcase.

</details>

### 14B.5 {#14B.5}

Creeping things-weasel, mouse, tortoise, turtle, sand reptile, ferret, chameleon, gecko, frogs, toads, snakes, crocodile, lizard, squirrel, raccoon, mole.

[Lev 11:29-31](/bible/leviticus/11#v29)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:29-31</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/leviticus/11#v29)</sup> These also shall be unclean unto you among the creeping things that creep upon the earth; the weasel, and the mouse, and the tortoise after his kind,
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/leviticus/11#v30)</sup> And the ferret, and the chameleon, and the lizard, and the snail, and the mole.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/leviticus/11#v31)</sup> These are unclean to you among all that creep: whosoever doth touch them, when they be dead, shall be unclean until the even.

</details>

### 14B.6 {#14B.6}

All water life that does not have fins and scales are not to be eaten (e.g., shrimp, lobster, snails, clams, shark, shell fish, eels, catfish, scallop, octopus, etc.).

[Lev 11:10-12](/bible/leviticus/11#v10); [Deut 14:10](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v10)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:10-12</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/11#v10)</sup> And all that have not fins and scales in the seas, and in the rivers, of all that move in the waters, and of any living thing which is in the waters, they shall be an abomination unto you:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/11#v11)</sup> They shall be even an abomination unto you; ye shall not eat of their flesh, but ye shall have their carcases in abomination.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/11#v12)</sup> Whatsoever hath no fins nor scales in the waters, that shall be an abomination unto you.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v10)</sup> And whatsoever hath not fins and scales ye may not eat; it is unclean unto you.

</details>

### 14B.7 {#14B.7}

Every creeping thing that flies is unclean.

[Deut 14:19](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v19)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v19)</sup> And every creeping thing that flieth is unclean unto you: they shall not be eaten.

</details>

Unclean birds-Eagle, buzzard, osprey, vultures, kite, ravens, owl, night hawk, ostrich, hawk, little owl, cormorant, great owl, falcon, swan, pelican, stork, herons, hoopoe, bats (and the eggs of unclean birds).

[Lev 11:13-19](/bible/leviticus/11#v13); [Deut 14:12-18](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v12)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:13-19</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/11#v13)</sup> And these are they which ye shall have in abomination among the fowls; they shall not be eaten, they are an abomination: the eagle, and the ossifrage, and the ospray,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/11#v14)</sup> And the vulture, and the kite after his kind;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/11#v15)</sup> Every raven after his kind;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/11#v16)</sup> And the owl, and the night hawk, and the cuckow, and the hawk after his kind,

> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/11#v17)</sup> And the little owl, and the cormorant, and the great owl,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/11#v18)</sup> And the swan, and the pelican, and the gier eagle,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/11#v19)</sup> And the stork, the heron after her kind, and the lapwing, and the bat.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 14:12-18</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v12)</sup> But these are they of which ye shall not eat: the eagle, and the ossifrage, and the ospray,

>
> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v16)</sup> The little owl, and the great owl, and the swan,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v17)</sup> And the pelican, and the gier eagle, and the cormorant,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v18)</sup> And the stork, and the heron after her kind, and the lapwing, and the bat.

</details>

### 14B.9 {#14B.9}

All winged insects that walk on all fours.

[Lev 11:20](/bible/leviticus/11#v20)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/11#v20)</sup> All fowls that creep, going upon all four, shall be an abomination unto you.

</details>

### 14B.10 {#14B.10}

Whatever crawls upon its belly or is multi-footed among all creeping things that creep on the earth.

[Lev 11:42](/bible/leviticus/11#v42)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:42</summary>

> <sup>[42](/bible/leviticus/11#v42)</sup> Whatsoever goeth upon the belly, and whatsoever goeth upon all four, or whatsoever hath more feet among all creeping things that creep upon the earth, them ye shall not eat; for they are an abomination.

</details>

### 14B.11 {#14B.11}

Whatever goes on its paws, among all kinds of animals that go on all fours, are unclean (e.g., dogs, cats, coyote, bears, foxes, etc.).

[Lev 11:27](/bible/leviticus/11#v27)

<details><summary>Leviticus 11:27</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/11#v27)</sup> And whatsoever goeth upon his paws, among all manner of beasts that go on all four, those are unclean unto you: whoso toucheth their carcase shall be unclean until the even.

</details>
