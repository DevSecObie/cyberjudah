[Lev 13:52,55,57](/bible/leviticus/13#v52)

> <sup>[55](/bible/leviticus/13#v55)</sup> And the priest shall look on the plague, after that it is washed: and, behold, if the plague have not changed his colour, and the plague be not spread; it is unclean; thou shalt burn it in the fire; it is fret inward, whether it be bare within or without.

> <sup>[57](/bible/leviticus/13#v57)</sup> And if it appear still in the garment, either in the warp, or in the woof, or in any thing of skin; it is a spreading plague: thou shalt burn that wherein the plague is with fire.
