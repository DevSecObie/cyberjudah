### 6B.1 {#6B.1}

Children are to obey and listen to their parents.

[Gen 28:7](/bible/genesis/28#v7); [Prov 1:8,9](/bible/proverbs/1#v8); [Prov 6:20](/bible/proverbs/6#v20); [Prov 13:1](/bible/proverbs/13#v1); [Prov 13:22](/bible/proverbs/13#v22); [Prov 23:22](/bible/proverbs/23#v22); [Rom 1:30](/bible/romans/1#v30); [Eph 6:1](/bible/ephesians/6#v1); [Col 3:20](/bible/colossians/3#v20)

<details><summary>Genesis 28:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/28#v7)</sup> And that Jacob obeyed his father and his mother, and was gone to Padan–aram;

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 1:8,9</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/1#v8)</sup> My son, hear the instruction of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/1#v9)</sup> For they shall be an ornament of grace unto thy head, and chains about thy neck.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 6:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/6#v20)</sup> My son, keep thy father’s commandment, and forsake not the law of thy mother:

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 13:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/13#v1)</sup> A wise son heareth his father’s instruction: but a scorner heareth not rebuke.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 13:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/13#v22)</sup> A good man leaveth an inheritance to his children’s children: and the wealth of the sinner is laid up for the just.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 23:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/23#v22)</sup> Hearken unto thy father that begat thee, and despise not thy mother when she is old.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 1:30</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/romans/1#v30)</sup> Backbiters, haters of God, despiteful, proud, boasters, inventors of evil things, disobedient to parents,

</details>
<details><summary>Ephesians 6:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/ephesians/6#v1)</sup> Children, obey your parents in the Lord: for this is right.

</details>
<details><summary>Colossians 3:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/colossians/3#v20)</sup> Children, obey your parents in all things: for this is well pleasing unto the Lord.

### 6B.2 {#6B.2}

Children to honor their father and mother.

[Exod 20:12](/bible/exodus/20#v12); [Deut 5:16](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v16); [Matt 15:4](/bible/matthew/15#v4); [Mark 10:19](/bible/mark/10#v19); [Luke 18:20](/bible/luke/18#v20); [Eph 6:2](/bible/ephesians/6#v2)

<details><summary>Exodus 20:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/20#v12)</sup> Honour thy father and thy mother: that thy days may be long upon the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 5:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v16)</sup> Honour thy father and thy mother, as the Lord thy God hath commanded thee; that thy days may be prolonged, and that it may go well with thee, in the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 15:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/15#v4)</sup> For God commanded, saying, Honour thy father and mother: and, He that curseth father or mother, let him die the death.

</details>
<details><summary>Mark 10:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/mark/10#v19)</sup> Thou knowest the commandments, Do not commit adultery, Do not kill, Do not steal, Do not bear false witness, Defraud not, Honour thy father and mother.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 18:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/luke/18#v20)</sup> Thou knowest the commandments, Do not commit adultery, Do not kill, Do not steal, Do not bear false witness, Honour thy father and thy mother.

</details>
<details><summary>Ephesians 6:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/ephesians/6#v2)</sup> Honour thy father and mother; (which is the first commandment with promise;)

</details>

### 6B.3 {#6B.3}

> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/19#v3)</sup> Ye shall fear every man his mother, and his father, and keep my sabbaths: I am the Lord your God.

</details>

### 6B.8 {#6B.8}

Children are not to curse their parents.

[Lev 20:9](/bible/leviticus/20#v9)

<details><summary>Leviticus 20:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/20#v9)</sup> For every one that curseth his father or his mother shall be surely put to death: he hath cursed his father or his mother; his blood shall be upon him.

</details>
