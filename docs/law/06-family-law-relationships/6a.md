### 6A.4 {#6A.4}

Parents should sternly chastise and punish their children to put them on a righteous course - For he who spares the rod hates his son, but he who loves his son disciplines him diligently.

[Prov 3:12](/bible/proverbs/3#v12); [Prov 13:24](/bible/proverbs/13#v24); [Prov 19:18](/bible/proverbs/19#v18); [Prov 22:15](/bible/proverbs/22#v15); [Prov 23:13-14](/bible/proverbs/23#v13); [Prov 29:15,17](/bible/proverbs/29#v15); [Heb 12:7](/bible/hebrews/12#v7)

<details><summary>Proverbs 3:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/3#v12)</sup> For whom the Lord loveth he correcteth; even as a father the son in whom he delighteth.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 13:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/13#v24)</sup> He that spareth his rod hateth his son: but he that loveth him chasteneth him betimes.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 19:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/19#v18)</sup> Chasten thy son while there is hope, and let not thy soul spare for his crying.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 22:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/22#v15)</sup> Foolishness is bound in the heart of a child; but the rod of correction shall drive it far from him.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 23:13-14</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/23#v13)</sup> Withhold not correction from the child: for if thou beatest him with the rod, he shall not die.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/23#v14)</sup> Thou shalt beat him with the rod, and shalt deliver his soul from hell.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 29:15,17</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/29#v15)</sup> The rod and reproof give wisdom: but a child left to himself bringeth his mother to shame.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/29#v17)</sup> Correct thy son, and he shall give thee rest; yea, he shall give delight unto thy soul.

</details>
<details><summary>Hebrews 12:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/12#v7)</sup> If ye endure chastening, God dealeth with you as with sons; for what son is he whom the father chasteneth not?

</details>
