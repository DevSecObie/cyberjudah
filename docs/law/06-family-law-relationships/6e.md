### 6E.1 {#6E.1}

Firstborn son is to receive the father's birthright.

[Gen 15:4](/bible/genesis/15#v4); [Gen 25:5](/bible/genesis/25#v5); [1 Chr 5:1](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v1); [Matt 21:37-38](/bible/matthew/21#v37)

<details><summary>Genesis 15:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/15#v4)</sup> And, behold, the word of the Lord came unto him, saying, This shall not be thine heir; but he that shall come forth out of thine own bowels shall be thine heir.

</details>
<details><summary>Genesis 25:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/25#v5)</sup> And Abraham gave all that he had unto Isaac.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Chronicles 5:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v1)</sup> Now the sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel, (for he was the firstborn; but, forasmuch as he defiled his father’s bed, his birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph the son of Israel: and the genealogy is not to be reckoned after the birthright.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 21:37-38</summary>

> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/21#v37)</sup> But last of all he sent unto them his son, saying, They will reverence my son.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/21#v38)</sup> But when the husbandmen saw the son, they said among themselves, This is the heir; come, let us kill him, and let us seize on his inheritance.

</details>

### 6E.4 {#6E.4}

Birthright of the firstborn can be sold to another son .

[Gen 25:31-34](/bible/genesis/25#v31); [Heb 12:16](/bible/hebrews/12#v16)

<details><summary>Genesis 25:31-34</summary>

> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/25#v31)</sup> And Jacob said, Sell me this day thy birthright.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/genesis/25#v32)</sup> And Esau said, Behold, I am at the point to die: and what profit shall this birthright do to me?
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/genesis/25#v33)</sup> And Jacob said, Swear to me this day; and he sware unto him: and he sold his birthright unto Jacob.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/genesis/25#v34)</sup> Then Jacob gave Esau bread and pottage of lentiles; and he did eat and drink, and rose up, and went his way: thus Esau despised his birthright.

### 6E.5 {#6E.5}

<details><summary>Genesis 25:5-6</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/25#v5)</sup> And Abraham gave all that he had unto Isaac.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/25#v6)</sup> But unto the sons of the concubines, which Abraham had, Abraham gave gifts, and sent them away from Isaac his son, while he yet lived, eastward, unto the east country.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 21:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v17)</sup> But he shall acknowledge the son of the hated for the firstborn, by giving him a double portion of all that he hath: for he is the beginning of his strength; the right of the firstborn is his.

</details>

>
> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v16)</sup> Then it shall be, when he maketh his sons to inherit that which he hath, that he may not make the son of the beloved firstborn before the son of the hated, which is indeed the firstborn:

### 6E.11 {#6E.11}

If a man has no children his inheritance is given to his brothers.

[Num 27:9](/bible/numbers/27#v9)

<details><summary>Numbers 27:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/27#v9)</sup> And if he have no daughter, then ye shall give his inheritance unto his brethren.

### 6E.12 {#6E.12}

If a man has no children or brothers then his inheritance is given to his father's brethren or his nearest family kin.

[Num 27:10-11](/bible/numbers/27#v10)

<details><summary>Numbers 27:10-11</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/27#v10)</sup> And if he have no brethren, then ye shall give his inheritance unto his father’s brethren.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/27#v11)</sup> And if his father have no brethren, then ye shall give his inheritance unto his kinsman that is next to him of his family, and he shall possess it: and it shall be unto the children of Israel a statute of judgment, as the Lord commanded Moses.

</details>

### 6E.14 {#6E.14}

When a man dies having no children and his widow marries her husband's brother, the son she bears by him shall succeed in the name of the brother which is dead and shall have his birthright and inheritance.

[Deut 25:5-6](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v5)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 25:5-6</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v5)</sup> If brethren dwell together, and one of them die, and have no child, the wife of the dead shall not marry without unto a stranger: her husband’s brother shall go in unto her, and take her to him to wife, and perform the duty of an husband’s brother unto her.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v6)</sup> And it shall be, that the firstborn which she beareth shall succeed in the name of his brother which is dead, that his name be not put out of Israel.

</details>

### 6E.15 {#6E.15}

A will and testament is in force after a man is dead, for it is never in force while the one who made it lives.

[Heb 9:17](/bible/hebrews/9#v17)

<details><summary>Hebrews 9:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/hebrews/9#v17)</sup> For a testament is of force after men are dead: otherwise it is of no strength at all while the testator liveth.

</details>

### 6E.16 {#6E.16}

A heir, as long as he is a child, is under guardians until the time prescribed by his father that he is to inherit.

[Gal 4:1-2](/bible/galatians/4#v1)

<details><summary>Galatians 4:1-2</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/galatians/4#v1)</sup> Now I say, That the heir, as long as he is a child, differeth nothing from a servant, though he be lord of all;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/galatians/4#v2)</sup> But is under tutors and governors until the time appointed of the father.

</details>
