### 21A.1 {#21A.1}

You are not to oppress the hired servant that is poor and needy, whether he be of thy brethren or a stranger, but shall give him his due wages.

[Deut 24:14-15](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v14); [Jer 22:13](/bible/jeremiah/22#v13)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 24:14-15</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v14)</sup> Thou shalt not oppress an hired servant that is poor and needy, whether he be of thy brethren, or of thy strangers that are in thy land within thy gates:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v15)</sup> At his day thou shalt give him his hire, neither shall the sun go down upon it; for he is poor, and setteth his heart upon it: lest he cry against thee unto the Lord, and it be sin unto thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 22:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/22#v13)</sup> Woe unto him that buildeth his house by unrighteousness, and his chambers by wrong; that useth his neighbour’s service without wages, and giveth him not for his work;

</details>

### 21A.2 {#21A.2}

The laborer is worthy of his hire.

[Luke 10:7](/bible/luke/10#v7); [1 Tim 5:18](/bible/1-timothy/5#v18)

<details><summary>Luke 10:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/luke/10#v7)</sup> And in the same house remain, eating and drinking such things as they give: for the labourer is worthy of his hire. Go not from house to house.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 5:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/1-timothy/5#v18)</sup> For the scripture saith, Thou shalt not muzzle the ox that treadeth out the corn. And, The labourer is worthy of his reward.

</details>

### 21A.3 {#21A.3}

Dutiful servants are to be honored.

[Prov 27:18](/bible/proverbs/27#v18)

<details><summary>Proverbs 27:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/27#v18)</sup> Whoso keepeth the fig tree shall eat the fruit thereof: so he that waiteth on his master shall be honoured.

</details>

### 21A.4 {#21A.4}

The wages of a hired man are not to be detained but paid to him the same day.

[Lev 19:13](/bible/leviticus/19#v13); [Deut 24:15](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v15)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/19#v13)</sup> Thou shalt not defraud thy neighbour, neither rob him: the wages of him that is hired shall not abide with thee all night until the morning.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 24:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v15)</sup> At his day thou shalt give him his hire, neither shall the sun go down upon it; for he is poor, and setteth his heart upon it: lest he cry against thee unto the Lord, and it be sin unto thee.

</details>

### 21A.5 {#21A.5}

Masters are to give to their servants that which is just and fair.

[Col 4:1](/bible/colossians/4#v1)

<details><summary>Colossians 4:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/colossians/4#v1)</sup> Masters, give unto your servants that which is just and equal; knowing that ye also have a Master in heaven.

</details>

### 21A.6 {#21A.6}

Masters are to treat servants well and not to threaten them.

[Eph 6:9](/bible/ephesians/6#v9)

<details><summary>Ephesians 6:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/ephesians/6#v9)</sup> And, ye masters, do the same things unto them, forbearing threatening: knowing that your Master also is in heaven; neither is there respect of persons with him.

</details>

### 21A.7 {#21A.7}

God will avenge those who keep back the wages of their hirelings through fraud and exploitation.

[Mal 3:5](/bible/malachi/3#v5); [James 5:4](/bible/james/5#v4)

<details><summary>Malachi 3:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/malachi/3#v5)</sup> And I will come near to you to judgment; and I will be a swift witness against the sorcerers, and against the adulterers, and against false swearers, and against those that oppress the hireling in his wages, the widow, and the fatherless, and that turn aside the stranger from his right, and fear not me, saith the Lord of hosts.

</details>
<details><summary>James 5:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/james/5#v4)</sup> Behold, the hire of the labourers who have reaped down your fields, which is of you kept back by fraud, crieth: and the cries of them which have reaped are entered into the ears of the Lord of sabaoth.

</details>

### 21A.8 {#21A.8}

You shall not rule over a servant with rigor or severity, but you shall fear your God.

[Lev 25:43](/bible/leviticus/25#v43)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:43</summary>

> <sup>[43](/bible/leviticus/25#v43)</sup> Thou shalt not rule over him with rigour; but shalt fear thy God.

</details>

### 21A.9 {#21A.9}

When a servant departs from your service, you shall not let him go away emptyhanded, you shall supply him liberally from your estate.

[Deut 15:13-14](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v13)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 15:13-14</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v13)</sup> And when thou sendest him out free from thee, thou shalt not let him go away empty:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v14)</sup> Thou shalt furnish him liberally out of thy flock, and out of thy floor, and out of thy winepress: of that wherewith the Lord thy God hath blessed thee thou shalt give unto him.

</details>
