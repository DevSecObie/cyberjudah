### 21B.1 {#21B.1}

Servants and employees are not to work on the Sabbath.

[Exod 20:10](/bible/exodus/20#v10); [Deut 5:12-14](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v12)

<details><summary>Exodus 20:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/20#v10)</sup> But the seventh day is the sabbath of the Lord thy God: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates:

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 5:12-14</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v12)</sup> Keep the sabbath day to sanctify it, as the Lord thy God hath commanded thee.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v13)</sup> Six days thou shalt labour, and do all thy work:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v14)</sup> But the seventh day is the sabbath of the Lord thy God: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, nor thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thine ox, nor thine ass, nor any of thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates; that thy manservant and thy maidservant may rest as well as thou.

</details>

### 21B.2 {#21B.2}

A faithful servant should be given authority and a part of the inheritance.

[Gen 41:40-43](/bible/genesis/41#v40); [Prov 17:2](/bible/proverbs/17#v2)

<details><summary>Genesis 41:40-43</summary>

> <sup>[40](/bible/genesis/41#v40)</sup> Thou shalt be over my house, and according unto thy word shall all my people be ruled: only in the throne will I be greater than thou.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/genesis/41#v41)</sup> And Pharaoh said unto Joseph, See, I have set thee over all the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/genesis/41#v42)</sup> And Pharaoh took off his ring from his hand, and put it upon Joseph’s hand, and arrayed him in vestures of fine linen, and put a gold chain about his neck;
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/genesis/41#v43)</sup> And he made him to ride in the second chariot which he had; and they cried before him, Bow the knee: and he made him ruler over all the land of Egypt.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 17:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/17#v2)</sup> A wise servant shall have rule over a son that causeth shame, and shall have part of the inheritance among the brethren.

</details>

### 21B.3 {#21B.3}

No man can serve two masters.

[Matt 6:24](/bible/matthew/6#v24); [Luke 16:13](/bible/luke/16#v13)

<details><summary>Matthew 6:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/6#v24)</sup> No man can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other; or else he will hold to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 16:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/luke/16#v13)</sup> No servant can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other; or else he will hold to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon.

</details>

### 21B.4 {#21B.4}

Your own brethren are not to be compelled to serve as a slave but shall be treated as a hired servant.

[Lev 25:39-40,42](/bible/leviticus/25#v39)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:39-40,42</summary>

> <sup>[39](/bible/leviticus/25#v39)</sup> And if thy brother that dwelleth by thee be waxen poor, and be sold unto thee; thou shalt not compel him to serve as a bondservant:
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/leviticus/25#v40)</sup> But as an hired servant, and as a sojourner, he shall be with thee, and shall serve thee unto the year of jubile:
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/leviticus/25#v42)</sup> For they are my servants, which I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: they shall not be sold as bondmen.

</details>

### 21B.5 {#21B.5}

Do not accuse a servant to his master, lest he curse you and you be found guilty.

[Prov 30:10](/bible/proverbs/30#v10)

<details><summary>Proverbs 30:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/30#v10)</sup> Accuse not a servant unto his master, lest he curse thee, and thou be found guilty.

</details>

### 21B.6 {#21B.6}

A maidservant betrothed to her master can be redeemed but cannot be sold to a foreign nation.

[Exod 21:8](/bible/exodus/21#v8)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/21#v8)</sup> If she please not her master, who hath betrothed her to himself, then shall he let her be redeemed: to sell her unto a strange nation he shall have no power, seeing he hath dealt deceitfully with her.

</details>

### 21B.7 {#21B.7}

Those of your race can be in service to you for only six years and are to be freed in the seventh year.

[Exod 21:2](/bible/exodus/21#v2); [Deut 15:12](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v12)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/21#v2)</sup> If thou buy an Hebrew servant, six years he shall serve: and in the seventh he shall go out free for nothing.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 15:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v12)</sup> And if thy brother, an Hebrew man, or an Hebrew woman, be sold unto thee, and serve thee six years; then in the seventh year thou shalt let him go free from thee.

</details>

### 21B.8 {#21B.8}

If a maid-servant is betrothed to the master's son she shall be treated according to the custom of daughters.

[Exod 21:9](/bible/exodus/21#v9)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/21#v9)</sup> And if he have betrothed her unto his son, he shall deal with her after the manner of daughters.

</details>

### 21B.9 {#21B.9}

A servant, when free from his service, shall be allowed to depart with that which he had when he went into service.

[Exod 21:3-4](/bible/exodus/21#v3)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:3-4</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/21#v3)</sup> If he came in by himself, he shall go out by himself: if he were married, then his wife shall go out with him.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/21#v4)</sup> If his master have given him a wife, and she have born him sons or daughters; the wife and her children shall be her master’s, and he shall go out by himself.

</details>

### 21B.10 {#21B.10}

Do not become slaves of men.

[1 Cor 7:23](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v23)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 7:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v23)</sup> Ye are bought with a price; be not ye the servants of men.

</details>

### 21B.11 {#21B.11}

If a servant does not want his freedom at the end of six years but desires to remain with his master, he then shall be in servitude to him for life.

[Exod 21:5-6](/bible/exodus/21#v5); [Deut 15:16-17](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v16)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:5-6</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/21#v5)</sup> And if the servant shall plainly say, I love my master, my wife, and my children; I will not go out free:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/21#v6)</sup> Then his master shall bring him unto the judges; he shall also bring him to the door, or unto the door post; and his master shall bore his ear through with an aul; and he shall serve him for ever.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 15:16-17</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v16)</sup> And it shall be, if he say unto thee, I will not go away from thee; because he loveth thee and thine house, because he is well with thee;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v17)</sup> Then thou shalt take an aul, and thrust it through his ear unto the door, and he shall be thy servant for ever. And also unto thy maidservant thou shalt do likewise.

</details>

### 21B.12 {#21B.12}

A servant is not above or greater than his master.

[Matt 10:24](/bible/matthew/10#v24); [Luke 6:40](/bible/luke/6#v40); [John 13:16](/bible/john/13#v16); [John 15:20](/bible/john/15#v20)

<details><summary>Matthew 10:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/10#v24)</sup> The disciple is not above his master, nor the servant above his lord.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 6:40</summary>

> <sup>[40](/bible/luke/6#v40)</sup> The disciple is not above his master: but every one that is perfect shall be as his master.

</details>
<details><summary>John 13:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/john/13#v16)</sup> Verily, verily, I say unto you, The servant is not greater than his lord; neither he that is sent greater than he that sent him.

</details>
<details><summary>John 15:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/john/15#v20)</sup> Remember the word that I said unto you, The servant is not greater than his lord. If they have persecuted me, they will also persecute you; if they have kept my saying, they will keep yours also.

</details>

### 21B.13 {#21B.13}

Those who are slaves of foreigners can be treated and bought as slaves (but not free men).

[Lev 25:44-46](/bible/leviticus/25#v44)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:44-46</summary>

> <sup>[44](/bible/leviticus/25#v44)</sup> Both thy bondmen, and thy bondmaids, which thou shalt have, shall be of the heathen that are round about you; of them shall ye buy bondmen and bondmaids.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/leviticus/25#v45)</sup> Moreover of the children of the strangers that do sojourn among you, of them shall ye buy, and of their families that are with you, which they begat in your land: and they shall be your possession.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/leviticus/25#v46)</sup> And ye shall take them as an inheritance for your children after you, to inherit them for a possession; they shall be your bondmen for ever: but over your brethren the children of Israel, ye shall not rule one over another with rigour.

</details>

### 21B.14 {#21B.14}

A brethren who is poor and sells himself into service to a foreigner can be redeemed at any time by one of his brethren, or if he is able, may redeem himself.

[Lev 25:47-49](/bible/leviticus/25#v47)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:47-49</summary>

> <sup>[47](/bible/leviticus/25#v47)</sup> And if a sojourner or stranger wax rich by thee, and thy brother that dwelleth by him wax poor, and sell himself unto the stranger or sojourner by thee, or to the stock of the stranger’s family:
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/leviticus/25#v48)</sup> After that he is sold he may be redeemed again; one of his brethren may redeem him:
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/leviticus/25#v49)</sup> Either his uncle, or his uncle’s son, may redeem him, or any that is nigh of kin unto him of his family may redeem him; or if he be able, he may redeem himself.

</details>

### 21B.15 {#21B.15}

A fugitive slave should not be turned over to his master but is allowed to live among you.

[Deut 23:15-16](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v15)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 23:15-16</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v15)</sup> Thou shalt not deliver unto his master the servant which is escaped from his master unto thee:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v16)</sup> He shall dwell with thee, even among you, in that place which he shall choose in one of thy gates, where it liketh him best: thou shalt not oppress him.

</details>

### 21B.16 {#21B.16}

If a man strikes his servant and he dies at his hand, he shall be punished.

[Exod 21:20-21](/bible/exodus/21#v20)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:20-21</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/21#v20)</sup> And if a man smite his servant, or his maid, with a rod, and he die under his hand; he shall be surely punished.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/21#v21)</sup> Notwithstanding, if he continue a day or two, he shall not be punished: for he is his money.

</details>

### 21B.17 {#21B.17}

If a servant is injured by his master he is then to be set free.

[Exod 21:26-27](/bible/exodus/21#v26)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:26-27</summary>

> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/21#v26)</sup> And if a man smite the eye of his servant, or the eye of his maid, that it perish; he shall let him go free for his eye’s sake.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/exodus/21#v27)</sup> And if he smite out his manservant’s tooth, or his maidservant’s tooth; he shall let him go free for his tooth’s sake.

</details>
