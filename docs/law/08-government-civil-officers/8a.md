### 8A.6 {#8A.6}

Rulers are not to be a terror to good behavior, but rather to the wicked and evil doers.

[Prov 20:26](/bible/proverbs/20#v26); [Rom 13:3-4](/bible/romans/13#v3)

<details><summary>Proverbs 20:26</summary>
