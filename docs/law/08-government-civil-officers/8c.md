### 8C.1 {#8C.1}

Judges were originally established by God.

[Judg 2:16,18](/bible/judges/2#v16); [2 Sam 7:11](/bible/2-samuel/7#v11)

<details><summary>Judges 2:16,18</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/judges/2#v16)</sup> Nevertheless the Lord raised up judges, which delivered them out of the hand of those that spoiled them.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/judges/2#v18)</sup> And when the Lord raised them up judges, then the Lord was with the judge, and delivered them out of the hand of their enemies all the days of the judge: for it repented the Lord because of their groanings by reason of them that oppressed them and vexed them.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Samuel 7:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-samuel/7#v11)</sup> And as since the time that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel, and have caused thee to rest from all thine enemies. Also the Lord telleth thee that he will make thee an house.

</details>

### 8C.2 {#8C.2}

Judges shall justify the righteous and condemn the wicked.

[Deut 25:1-3](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v1)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 25:1-3</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v1)</sup> If there be a controversy between men, and they come unto judgment, that the judges may judge them; then they shall justify the righteous, and condemn the wicked.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v2)</sup> And it shall be, if the wicked man be worthy to be beaten, that the judge shall cause him to lie down, and to be beaten before his face, according to his fault, by a certain number.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v3)</sup> Forty stripes he may give him, and not exceed: lest, if he should exceed, and beat him above these with many stripes, then thy brother should seem vile unto thee.

</details>

### 8C.3 {#8C.3}

Judges shall righteously judge the causes and transgressions between persons with just judgment.

[Lev 19:15](/bible/leviticus/19#v15); [Deut 1:16](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v16); [Deut 16:18](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v18); [1 Sam 2:25](/bible/1-samuel/2#v25); [2 Sam 7:11](/bible/2-samuel/7#v11); [2 Chr 19:5](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v5); [Ps 82:2-4](/bible/psalms/82#v2); [Prov 31:9](/bible/proverbs/31#v9)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/19#v15)</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment: thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor honour the person of the mighty: but in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 1:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v16)</sup> And I charged your judges at that time, saying, Hear the causes between your brethren, and judge righteously between every man and his brother, and the stranger that is with him.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 16:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v18)</sup> Judges and officers shalt thou make thee in all thy gates, which the Lord thy God giveth thee, throughout thy tribes: and they shall judge the people with just judgment.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Samuel 2:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/1-samuel/2#v25)</sup> If one man sin against another, the judge shall judge him: but if a man sin against the Lord, who shall intreat for him? Notwithstanding they hearkened not unto the voice of their father, because the Lord would slay them.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Samuel 7:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-samuel/7#v11)</sup> And as since the time that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel, and have caused thee to rest from all thine enemies. Also the Lord telleth thee that he will make thee an house.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Chronicles 19:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v5)</sup> And he set judges in the land throughout all the fenced cities of Judah, city by city,

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 82:2-4</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/82#v2)</sup> How long will ye judge unjustly, and accept the persons of the wicked? Selah.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/82#v3)</sup> Defend the poor and fatherless: do justice to the afflicted and needy.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/82#v4)</sup> Deliver the poor and needy: rid them out of the hand of the wicked.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 31:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/31#v9)</sup> Open thy mouth, judge righteously, and plead the cause of the poor and needy.

</details>

### 8C.4 {#8C.4}

Judges are not to be partial or respect persons in judgment, but shall equally judge the small as well as the great.

[Exod 23:31](/bible/exodus/23#v31); [Deut 1:17](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v17); [Lev 19:15](/bible/leviticus/19#v15); [2 Chr 19:7](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v7); [Prov 18:5](/bible/proverbs/18#v5); [Prov 24:23](/bible/proverbs/24#v23); [James 2:9](/bible/james/2#v9)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:31</summary>

> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/23#v31)</sup> And I will set thy bounds from the Red sea even unto the sea of the Philistines, and from the desert unto the river: for I will deliver the inhabitants of the land into your hand; and thou shalt drive them out before thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 1:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v17)</sup> Ye shall not respect persons in judgment; but ye shall hear the small as well as the great; ye shall not be afraid of the face of man; for the judgment is God’s: and the cause that is too hard for you, bring it unto me, and I will hear it.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 19:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/19#v15)</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment: thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor honour the person of the mighty: but in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Chronicles 19:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v7)</sup> Wherefore now let the fear of the Lord be upon you; take heed and do it: for there is no iniquity with the Lord our God, nor respect of persons, nor taking of gifts.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 18:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/18#v5)</sup> It is not good to accept the person of the wicked, to overthrow the righteous in judgment.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 24:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/24#v23)</sup> These things also belong to the wise. It is not good to have respect of persons in judgment.

</details>
<details><summary>James 2:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/james/2#v9)</sup> But if ye have respect to persons, ye commit sin, and are convinced of the law as transgressors.

</details>

### 8C.5 {#8C.5}

Judges are not to take a gift or bribe.

[2 Chr 19:7](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v7)

<details><summary>2 Chronicles 19:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v7)</sup> Wherefore now let the fear of the Lord be upon you; take heed and do it: for there is no iniquity with the Lord our God, nor respect of persons, nor taking of gifts.

</details>

### 8C.6 {#8C.6}

Judges are to judge the people at all times.

[Exod 18:22,26](/bible/exodus/18#v22)

<details><summary>Exodus 18:22,26</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/18#v22)</sup> And let them judge the people at all seasons: and it shall be, that every great matter they shall bring unto thee, but every small matter they shall judge: so shall it be easier for thyself, and they shall bear the burden with thee.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/18#v26)</sup> And they judged the people at all seasons: the hard causes they brought unto Moses, but every small matter they judged themselves.

</details>

### 8C.7 {#8C.7}

Judges and magistrates are to know the laws of God.

[Ezra 7:25](/bible/ezra/7#v25)

<details><summary>Ezra 7:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/ezra/7#v25)</sup> And thou, Ezra, after the wisdom of thy God, that is in thine hand, set magistrates and judges, which may judge all the people that are beyond the river, all such as know the laws of thy God; and teach ye them that know them not.

</details>

### 8C.8 {#8C.8}

Judges judge not for man but for God.

[Deut 1:17](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v17); [2 Chr 19:6](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v6)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 1:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v17)</sup> Ye shall not respect persons in judgment; but ye shall hear the small as well as the great; ye shall not be afraid of the face of man; for the judgment is God’s: and the cause that is too hard for you, bring it unto me, and I will hear it.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Chronicles 19:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v6)</sup> And said to the judges, Take heed what ye do: for ye judge not for man, but for the Lord, who is with you in the judgment.

</details>

### 8C.9 {#8C.9}

The judge that justifies the wicked and condemns the just is an abomination to God.

[Prov 17:15](/bible/proverbs/17#v15)

<details><summary>Proverbs 17:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/17#v15)</sup> He that justifieth the wicked, and he that condemneth the just, even they both are abomination to the Lord.

</details>

### 8C.10 {#8C.10}

Wisdom and knowledge is needed and should be prayed for in order to judge between good and bad.

[1 Kgs 3:9](/bible/1-kings/3#v9); [2 Chr 1:10](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v10); [James 1:5](/bible/james/1#v5)

<details><summary>1 Kings 3:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-kings/3#v9)</sup> Give therefore thy servant an understanding heart to judge thy people, that I may discern between good and bad: for who is able to judge this thy so great a people?

</details>
<details><summary>2 Chronicles 1:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v10)</sup> Give me now wisdom and knowledge, that I may go out and come in before this people: for who can judge this thy people, that is so great?

</details>
<details><summary>James 1:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/james/1#v5)</sup> If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.

</details>

### 8C.11 {#8C.11}

Judges are to judge matters according to the laws, statutes, commandments, and judgments of God.

[Exod 18:16](/bible/exodus/18#v16); [2 Chr 19:10](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v10)

<details><summary>Exodus 18:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/18#v16)</sup> When they have a matter, they come unto me; and I judge between one and another, and I do make them know the statutes of God, and his laws.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Chronicles 19:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v10)</sup> And what cause soever shall come to you of your brethren that dwell in their cities, between blood and blood, between law and commandment, statutes and judgments, ye shall even warn them that they trespass not against the Lord, and so wrath come upon you, and upon your brethren: this do, and ye shall not trespass.

</details>
