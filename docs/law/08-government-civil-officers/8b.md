### 8B.1 {#8B.1}

The powers of government are appointed by God and are in His hand.

[Prov 8:15-16](/bible/proverbs/8#v15); [Prov 21:1](/bible/proverbs/21#v1); [Rom 13:1](/bible/romans/13#v1)

<details><summary>Proverbs 8:15-16</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/8#v15)</sup> By me kings reign, and princes decree justice.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/8#v16)</sup> By me princes rule, and nobles, even all the judges of the earth.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 21:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/21#v1)</sup> The king’s heart is in the hand of the Lord, as the rivers of water: he turneth it whithersoever he will.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 13:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/13#v1)</sup> Let every soul be subject unto the higher powers. For there is no power but of God: the powers that be are ordained of God.

</details>

### 8B.2 {#8B.2}

A distribution of power and levels of authority are to be established-rulers of thousands, and rulers of hundreds, rulers of fifties, and rulers of tens.

[Exod 18:21,25](/bible/exodus/18#v21); [Deut 1:13-15](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v13)

<details><summary>Exodus 18:21,25</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/18#v21)</sup> Moreover thou shalt provide out of all the people able men, such as fear God, men of truth, hating covetousness; and place such over them, to be rulers of thousands, and rulers of hundreds, rulers of fifties, and rulers of tens:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/18#v25)</sup> And Moses chose able men out of all Israel, and made them heads over the people, rulers of thousands, rulers of hundreds, rulers of fifties, and rulers of tens.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 1:13-15</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v13)</sup> Take you wise men, and understanding, and known among your tribes, and I will make them rulers over you.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v14)</sup> And ye answered me, and said, The thing which thou hast spoken is good for us to do.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v15)</sup> So I took the chief of your tribes, wise men, and known, and made them heads over you, captains over thousands, and captains over hundreds, and captains over fifties, and captains over tens, and officers among your tribes.

</details>

### 8B.3 {#8B.3}

Judges, magistrates and officers are to be appointed in all towns and cities.

[Deut 16:18](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v18); [2 Chr 19:5](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v5); [Ezra 7:25](/bible/ezra/7#v25)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 16:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v18)</sup> Judges and officers shalt thou make thee in all thy gates, which the Lord thy God giveth thee, throughout thy tribes: and they shall judge the people with just judgment.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Chronicles 19:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v5)</sup> And he set judges in the land throughout all the fenced cities of Judah, city by city,

</details>
<details><summary>Ezra 7:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/ezra/7#v25)</sup> And thou, Ezra, after the wisdom of thy God, that is in thine hand, set magistrates and judges, which may judge all the people that are beyond the river, all such as know the laws of thy God; and teach ye them that know them not.

</details>

### 8B.4 {#8B.4}

The less wicked a government is, the more the righteous will prosper.

[Prov 28:12,28](/bible/proverbs/28#v12)

<details><summary>Proverbs 28:12,28</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/28#v12)</sup> When righteous men do rejoice, there is great glory: but when the wicked rise, a man is hidden.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/28#v28)</sup> When the wicked rise, men hide themselves: but when they perish, the righteous increase.

</details>

### 8B.5 {#8B.5}

Government is to be for the punishment of the evildoers, and for the praise of them that do well.

[Rom 13:3](/bible/romans/13#v3); [1 Pet 2:14](/bible/1-peter/2#v14)

<details><summary>Romans 13:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/romans/13#v3)</sup> For rulers are not a terror to good works, but to the evil. Wilt thou then not be afraid of the power? do that which is good, and thou shalt have praise of the same:

</details>
<details><summary>1 Peter 2:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-peter/2#v14)</sup> Or unto governors, as unto them that are sent by him for the punishment of evildoers, and for the praise of them that do well.

</details>
