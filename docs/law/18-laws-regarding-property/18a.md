### 18A.2 {#18A.2}

Land can be sold but not permanently. You shall grant a redemption for all the land in your possession (the original owner has the right to buy back land he sold).

[Lev 25:23-24](/bible/leviticus/25#v23)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:23-24</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/25#v23)</sup> The land shall not be sold for ever: for the land is mine; for ye are strangers and sojourners with me.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/25#v24)</sup> And in all the land of your possession ye shall grant a redemption for the land.

</details>

### 18A.3 {#18A.3}

In the year of the Jubilee (every fiftieth year) land is to be returned to the person, or his rightful heirs or family, that originally possessed the land.

[Lev 25:10,13-17,28,31,33-34](/bible/leviticus/25#v10)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:10,13-17,28,31,33-34</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/25#v10)</sup> And ye shall hallow the fiftieth year, and proclaim liberty throughout all the land unto all the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a jubile unto you; and ye shall return every man unto his possession, and ye shall return every man unto his family.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/25#v13)</sup> In the year of this jubile ye shall return every man unto his possession.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/25#v14)</sup> And if thou sell ought unto thy neighbour, or buyest ought of thy neighbour’s hand, ye shall not oppress one another:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/25#v15)</sup> According to the number of years after the jubile thou shalt buy of thy neighbour, and according unto the number of years of the fruits he shall sell unto thee:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/25#v16)</sup> According to the multitude of years thou shalt increase the price thereof, and according to the fewness of years thou shalt diminish the price of it: for according to the number of the years of the fruits doth he sell unto thee.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/25#v17)</sup> Ye shall not therefore oppress one another; but thou shalt fear thy God: for I am the Lord your God.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/leviticus/25#v28)</sup> But if he be not able to restore it to him, then that which is sold shall remain in the hand of him that hath bought it until the year of jubile: and in the jubile it shall go out, and he shall return unto his possession.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/leviticus/25#v31)</sup> But the houses of the villages which have no wall round about them shall be counted as the fields of the country: they may be redeemed, and they shall go out in the jubile.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/leviticus/25#v33)</sup> And if a man purchase of the Levites, then the house that was sold, and the city of his possession, shall go out in the year of jubile: for the houses of the cities of the Levites are their possession among the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/leviticus/25#v34)</sup> But the field of the suburbs of their cities may not be sold; for it is their perpetual possession.

</details>

### 18A.4 {#18A.4}

If a house in the city is sold, it can be redeemed (bought back) within a year. After that the house is unredeemable and shall belong permanently to him who bought it. It will not return to the seller in the Jubilee.

[Lev 25:29-30](/bible/leviticus/25#v29)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:29-30</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/leviticus/25#v29)</sup> And if a man sell a dwelling house in a walled city, then he may redeem it within a whole year after it is sold; within a full year may he redeem it.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/leviticus/25#v30)</sup> And if it be not redeemed within the space of a full year, then the house that is in the walled city shall be established for ever to him that bought it throughout his generations: it shall not go out in the jubile.

</details>

### 18A.5 {#18A.5}

Houses in the rural or country regions may be redeemed at any time, and they shall be released in the Jubilee.

[Lev 25:31](/bible/leviticus/25#v31)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:31</summary>

> <sup>[31](/bible/leviticus/25#v31)</sup> But the houses of the villages which have no wall round about them shall be counted as the fields of the country: they may be redeemed, and they shall go out in the jubile.

</details>

### 18A.6 {#18A.6}

If one becomes poor and is unable to redeem his land, a kinsmen of his may redeem for him what he had sold.

[Lev 25:25](/bible/leviticus/25#v25); [Ruth 4:3-4](/bible/ruth/4#v3); [Jer 32:7](/bible/jeremiah/32#v7)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/leviticus/25#v25)</sup> If thy brother be waxen poor, and hath sold away some of his possession, and if any of his kin come to redeem it, then shall he redeem that which his brother sold.

</details>

### 18A.7 {#18A.7}

If one has no means by which to pay the purchaser of his property the redemption money, then what was sold shall remain in the hand of him who bought it until the Year of Jubilee.

[Lev 25:28](/bible/leviticus/25#v28)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:28</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/leviticus/25#v28)</sup> But if he be not able to restore it to him, then that which is sold shall remain in the hand of him that hath bought it until the year of jubile: and in the jubile it shall go out, and he shall return unto his possession.

</details>

### 18A.14 {#18A.14}

>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/22#v6)</sup> If fire break out, and catch in thorns, so that the stacks of corn, or the standing corn, or the field, be consumed therewith; he that kindled the fire shall surely make restitution.

</details>
