### 18B.1 {#18B.1}

You shall not steal another's property.

[Exod 20:15](/bible/exodus/20#v15); [Lev 19:11,13](/bible/leviticus/19#v11); [Ps 62:10](/bible/psalms/62#v10); [Prov 21:7](/bible/proverbs/21#v7); [Amos 3:10](/bible/amos/3#v10); [Deut 5:19](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v19); [Matt 19:18](/bible/matthew/19#v18); [Mark 10:19](/bible/mark/10#v19); [Luke 18:20](/bible/luke/18#v20); [Rom 13:9](/bible/romans/13#v9); [1 Cor 6:10](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v10); [1 Pet 4:15](/bible/1-peter/4#v15)

### 18B.4 {#18B.4}

Theft proceeds from the heart and defiles a man.

[Matt 15:19-20](/bible/matthew/15#v19)

<details><summary>Matthew 15:19-20</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/15#v19)</sup> For out of the heart proceed evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false witness, blasphemies:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/15#v20)</sup> These are the things which defile a man: but to eat with unwashen hands defileth not a man.

</details>

### 18B.5 {#18B.5}

One who is judged to have stolen property is to repay double to the owner.

[Exod 22:7-9](/bible/exodus/22#v7)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:7-9</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/22#v7)</sup> If a man shall deliver unto his neighbour money or stuff to keep, and it be stolen out of the man’s house; if the thief be found, let him pay double.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/22#v8)</sup> If the thief be not found, then the master of the house shall be brought unto the judges, to see whether he have put his hand unto his neighbour’s goods.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/22#v9)</sup> For all manner of trespass, whether it be for ox, for ass, for sheep, for raiment, or for any manner of lost thing, which another challengeth to be his, the cause of both parties shall come before the judges; and whom the judges shall condemn, he shall pay double unto his neighbour.

</details>

### 18B.6 {#18B.6}

If one strikes a thief found breaking in a building and he dies, there shall be no guilt upon him for murder.

[Exod 22:2](/bible/exodus/22#v2)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/22#v2)</sup> If a thief be found breaking up, and be smitten that he die, there shall no blood be shed for him.

</details>

### 18B.7 {#18B.7}

We are exhorted to stop theft in the land by having a thief labor with his hands.

[Eph 4:28](/bible/ephesians/4#v28)

<details><summary>Ephesians 4:28</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/ephesians/4#v28)</sup> Let him that stole steal no more: but rather let him labour, working with his hands the thing which is good, that he may have to give to him that needeth.

</details>

### 18B.8 {#18B.8}

We should not despise one who steals due to hunger, though he must restore sevenfold of what he took.

[Prov 6:30](/bible/proverbs/6#v30)

<details><summary>Proverbs 6:30</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/6#v30)</sup> Men do not despise a thief, if he steal to satisfy his soul when he is hungry;

</details>
