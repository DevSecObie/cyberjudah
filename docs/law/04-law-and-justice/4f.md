### 4F.1 {#4F.1}

You are not to twist, distort or pervert justice.

[Deut 16:19](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v19)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 16:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v19)</sup> Thou shalt not wrest judgment; thou shalt not respect persons, neither take a gift: for a gift doth blind the eyes of the wise, and pervert the words of the righteous.

</details>

### 4F.2 {#4F.2}

You shall not be unjust but shall follow that which is just and righteous in matters of law and judgment.

[Lev 19:15,35](/bible/leviticus/19#v15); [Deut 16:20](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v20); [Jer 22:3](/bible/jeremiah/22#v3)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:15,35</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/19#v15)</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment: thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor honour the person of the mighty: but in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/leviticus/19#v35)</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment, in meteyard, in weight, or in measure.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 16:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v20)</sup> That which is altogether just shalt thou follow, that thou mayest live, and inherit the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 22:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/22#v3)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Execute ye judgment and righteousness, and deliver the spoiled out of the hand of the oppressor: and do no wrong, do no violence to the stranger, the fatherless, nor the widow, neither shed innocent blood in this place.

</details>

### 4F.3 {#4F.3}

You are not to be partial towards anyone or a respecter of persons in exacting justice, whether they be poor or rich, small or great.

[Exod 23:3](/bible/exodus/23#v3); [Lev 19:15](/bible/leviticus/19#v15); [Deut 16:19](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v19); [2 Chr 19:7](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v7); [Prov 28:21](/bible/proverbs/28#v21)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/23#v3)</sup> Neither shalt thou countenance a poor man in his cause.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 19:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/19#v15)</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment: thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor honour the person of the mighty: but in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 16:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v19)</sup> Thou shalt not wrest judgment; thou shalt not respect persons, neither take a gift: for a gift doth blind the eyes of the wise, and pervert the words of the righteous.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Chronicles 19:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v7)</sup> Wherefore now let the fear of the Lord be upon you; take heed and do it: for there is no iniquity with the Lord our God, nor respect of persons, nor taking of gifts.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 28:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/28#v21)</sup> To have respect of persons is not good: for for a piece of bread that man will transgress.

</details>

### 4F.4 {#4F.4}

Judgment to be quickly executed upon those violating the law of God or the law of the land.

[Ezra 7:26](/bible/ezra/7#v26); [Jer 21:12](/bible/jeremiah/21#v12)

<details><summary>Ezra 7:26</summary>

> <sup>[26](/bible/ezra/7#v26)</sup> And whosoever will not do the law of thy God, and the law of the king, let judgment be executed speedily upon him, whether it be unto death, or to banishment, or to confiscation of goods, or to imprisonment.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 21:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/21#v12)</sup> O house of David, thus saith the Lord; Execute judgment in the morning, and deliver him that is spoiled out of the hand of the oppressor, lest my fury go out like fire, and burn that none can quench it, because of the evil of your doings.

</details>

### 4F.5 {#4F.5}

Seek good, and not evil; hate the evil, and love the good, and establish justice in your courts.

[Amos 5:14-15](/bible/amos/5#v14)

<details><summary>Amos 5:14-15</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/amos/5#v14)</sup> Seek good, and not evil, that ye may live: and so the Lord, the God of hosts, shall be with you, as ye have spoken.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/amos/5#v15)</sup> Hate the evil, and love the good, and establish judgment in the gate: it may be that the Lord God of hosts will be gracious unto the remnant of Joseph.

</details>

### 4F.6 {#4F.6}

[Exod 23:7](/bible/exodus/23#v7)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:7</summary>

### 4F.7 {#4F.7}

<details><summary>Luke 16:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/luke/16#v10)</sup> He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much: and he that is unjust in the least is unjust also in much.

</details>

### 4F.8 {#4F.8}

You shall do no injustice regarding the measurement of length, weight or volume, but shall adhere to a just and standard measurement.

[Lev 19:35-36](/bible/leviticus/19#v35); [Ps 82:2](/bible/psalms/82#v2)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:35-36</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/leviticus/19#v35)</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment, in meteyard, in weight, or in measure.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/leviticus/19#v36)</sup> Just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin, shall ye have: I am the Lord your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 82:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/82#v2)</sup> How long will ye judge unjustly, and accept the persons of the wicked? Selah.

</details>
