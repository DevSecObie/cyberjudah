### 4E.1 {#4E.1}

You shall not bear false witness against another nor raise a false report.

[Exod 20:16](/bible/exodus/20#v16); [Exod 23:1](/bible/exodus/23#v1); [Deut 5:20](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v20); [Luke 3:14](/bible/luke/3#v14); [Luke 18:20](/bible/luke/18#v20)

<details><summary>Exodus 20:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/20#v16)</sup> Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour.

</details>
<details><summary>Exodus 23:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/23#v1)</sup> Thou shalt not raise a false report: put not thine hand with the wicked to be an unrighteous witness.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 5:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v20)</sup> Neither shalt thou bear false witness against thy neighbour.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 3:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/luke/3#v14)</sup> And the soldiers likewise demanded of him, saying, And what shall we do? And he said unto them, Do violence to no man, neither accuse any falsely; and be content with your wages.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 18:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/luke/18#v20)</sup> Thou knowest the commandments, Do not commit adultery, Do not kill, Do not steal, Do not bear false witness, Honour thy father and thy mother.

</details>

### 4E.2 {#4E.2}

A faithful witness speaks the truth and saves lives, but a false witness speaks lies.

[Prov 14:5,25](/bible/proverbs/14#v5)

<details><summary>Proverbs 14:5,25</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/14#v5)</sup> A faithful witness will not lie: but a false witness will utter lies.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/14#v25)</sup> A true witness delivereth souls: but a deceitful witness speaketh lies.

</details>

### 4E.3 {#4E.3}

One witness shall not condemn a man for any iniquity or crime he commits.

[Num 35:30](/bible/numbers/35#v30); [Deut 17:6](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v6); [Deut 19:15](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v15)

<details><summary>Numbers 35:30</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/35#v30)</sup> Whoso killeth any person, the murderer shall be put to death by the mouth of witnesses: but one witness shall not testify against any person to cause him to die.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 17:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v6)</sup> At the mouth of two witnesses, or three witnesses, shall he that is worthy of death be put to death; but at the mouth of one witness he shall not be put to death.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 19:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v15)</sup> One witness shall not rise up against a man for any iniquity, or for any sin, in any sin that he sinneth: at the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses, shall the matter be established.

</details>

### 4E.5 {#4E.5}

False witness is an abomination and condemned.

[Prov 6:16,19](/bible/proverbs/6#v16); [Prov 12:17](/bible/proverbs/12#v17); [Prov 25:18](/bible/proverbs/25#v18); [Jer 7:9-10](/bible/jeremiah/7#v9)

<details><summary>Proverbs 6:16,19</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/6#v16)</sup> These six things doth the Lord hate: yea, seven are an abomination unto him:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/6#v19)</sup> A false witness that speaketh lies, and he that soweth discord among brethren.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 12:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/12#v17)</sup> He that speaketh truth sheweth forth righteousness: but a false witness deceit.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 25:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/25#v18)</sup> A man that beareth false witness against his neighbour is a maul, and a sword, and a sharp arrow.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 7:9-10</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/7#v9)</sup> Will ye steal, murder, and commit adultery, and swear falsely, and burn incense unto Baal, and walk after other gods whom ye know not;
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/7#v10)</sup> And come and stand before me in this house, which is called by my name, and say, We are delivered to do all these abominations?

</details>

### 4E.7 {#4E.7}

A false witness is to be punished.

<details><summary>Proverbs 19:5,9</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/19#v5)</sup> A false witness shall not be unpunished, and he that speaketh lies shall not escape.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/19#v9)</sup> A false witness shall not be unpunished, and he that speaketh lies shall perish.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 21:28</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/21#v28)</sup> A false witness shall perish: but the man that heareth speaketh constantly.

</details>
<details><summary>Zechariah 5:3-4</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/zechariah/5#v3)</sup> Then said he unto me, This is the curse that goeth forth over the face of the whole earth: for every one that stealeth shall be cut off as on this side according to it; and every one that sweareth shall be cut off as on that side according to it.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/zechariah/5#v4)</sup> I will bring it forth, saith the Lord of hosts, and it shall enter into the house of the thief, and into the house of him that sweareth falsely by my name: and it shall remain in the midst of his house, and shall consume it with the timber thereof and the stones thereof.

</details>

> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/23#v2)</sup> Thou shalt not follow a multitude to do evil; neither shalt thou speak in a cause to decline after many to wrest judgment:

</details>

### 4E.10 {#4E.10}

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 17:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v6)</sup> At the mouth of two witnesses, or three witnesses, shall he that is worthy of death be put to death; but at the mouth of one witness he shall not be put to death.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 19:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v15)</sup> One witness shall not rise up against a man for any iniquity, or for any sin, in any sin that he sinneth: at the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses, shall the matter be established.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 18:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/18#v16)</sup> But if he will not hear thee, then take with thee one or two more, that in the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.

</details>
<details><summary>John 8:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/john/8#v17)</sup> It is also written in your law, that the testimony of two men is true.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Corinthians 13:1</summary>

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 5:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/1-timothy/5#v19)</sup> Against an elder receive not an accusation, but before two or three witnesses.

</details>
<details><summary>Hebrews 10:28</summary>

### 4E.12 {#4E.12}

If someone gives contrary testimony, then the judges shall make diligent inquisition between both men to see if the witness is a false witness.

[Deut 19:16-18](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v16)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 19:16-18</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v16)</sup> If a false witness rise up against any man to testify against him that which is wrong;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v17)</sup> Then both the men, between whom the controversy is, shall stand before the Lord, before the priests and the judges, which shall be in those days;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v18)</sup> And the judges shall make diligent inquisition: and, behold, if the witness be a false witness, and hath testified falsely against his brother;

</details>

### 4E.13 {#4E.13}

If a witness is found to be a false witness by testifying falsely against his brother, then he shall receive the punishment that the accused brethren would have received if guilty.

[Deut 19:18-19](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v18)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 19:18-19</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v18)</sup> And the judges shall make diligent inquisition: and, behold, if the witness be a false witness, and hath testified falsely against his brother;
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v19)</sup> Then shall ye do unto him, as he had thought to have done unto his brother: so shalt thou put the evil away from among you.

</details>

### 4E.14 {#4E.14}

When a person who is a witness sins when he has taken an oath, about an event he saw or knew, if he does not tell it, then he will bear his guilt.

[Lev 5:1](/bible/leviticus/5#v1)

<details><summary>Leviticus 5:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/5#v1)</sup> And if a soul sin, and hear the voice of swearing, and is a witness, whether he hath seen or known of it; if he do not utter it, then he shall bear his iniquity.

</details>

[Prov 19:28](/bible/proverbs/19#v28)

<details><summary>Proverbs 19:28</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/19#v28)</sup> An ungodly witness scorneth judgment: and the mouth of the wicked devoureth iniquity.

</details>
