### 4D.1 {#4D.1}

If you swear or take an oath you shall do so in God's name.

[Gen 24:3](/bible/genesis/24#v3); [Deut 6:13](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v13); [Deut 10:20](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v20)

<details><summary>Genesis 24:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/24#v3)</sup> And I will make thee swear by the Lord, the God of heaven, and the God of the earth, that thou shalt not take a wife unto my son of the daughters of the Canaanites, among whom I dwell:

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 6:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v13)</sup> Thou shalt fear the Lord thy God, and serve him, and shalt swear by his name.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 10:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v20)</sup> Thou shalt fear the Lord thy God; him shalt thou serve, and to him shalt thou cleave, and swear by his name.

</details>

### 4D.2 {#4D.2}

You shall make no false or deceitful oath or covenant.

[Ps 24:4](/bible/psalms/24#v4); [Jer 7:9](/bible/jeremiah/7#v9); [Zech 8:17](/bible/zechariah/8#v17); [Hos 10:4](/bible/hosea/10#v4)

<details><summary>Psalms 24:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/24#v4)</sup> He that hath clean hands, and a pure heart; who hath not lifted up his soul unto vanity, nor sworn deceitfully.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 7:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/7#v9)</sup> Will ye steal, murder, and commit adultery, and swear falsely, and burn incense unto Baal, and walk after other gods whom ye know not;

</details>
<details><summary>Zechariah 8:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/zechariah/8#v17)</sup> And let none of you imagine evil in your hearts against his neighbour; and love no false oath: for all these are things that I hate, saith the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>Hosea 10:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/10#v4)</sup> They have spoken words, swearing falsely in making a covenant: thus judgment springeth up as hemlock in the furrows of the field.

</details>

### 4D.3 {#4D.3}

You shall not swear by God's name falsely.

[Lev 19:12](/bible/leviticus/19#v12)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/19#v12)</sup> And ye shall not swear by my name falsely, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy God: I am the Lord.

</details>

### 4D.4 {#4D.4}

When one fully performs all the terms of an oath or contract, he is clear of all obligation to it.

[Gen 24:8](/bible/genesis/24#v8)

<details><summary>Genesis 24:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/24#v8)</sup> And if the woman will not be willing to follow thee, then thou shalt be clear from this my oath: only bring not my son thither again.

</details>

### 4D.5 {#4D.5}

If a man makes a vow to God or swears an oath to bind himself by contract or binding obligation, he shall not violate his word but shall do all that he stated.

[Num 30:2](/bible/numbers/30#v2); [Josh 9:20](/bible/joshua/9#v20); [Judg 11:30-36](/bible/judges/11#v30); [2 Sam 15:7-9](/bible/2-samuel/15#v7)

<details><summary>Numbers 30:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/30#v2)</sup> If a man vow a vow unto the Lord, or swear an oath to bind his soul with a bond; he shall not break his word, he shall do according to all that proceedeth out of his mouth.

</details>
<details><summary>Joshua 9:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/joshua/9#v20)</sup> This we will do to them; we will even let them live, lest wrath be upon us, because of the oath which we sware unto them.

</details>
<details><summary>Judges 11:30-36</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/judges/11#v30)</sup> And Jephthah vowed a vow unto the Lord, and said, If thou shalt without fail deliver the children of Ammon into mine hands,
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/judges/11#v31)</sup> Then it shall be, that whatsoever cometh forth of the doors of my house to meet me, when I return in peace from the children of Ammon, shall surely be the Lord’s, and I will offer it up for a burnt offering.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/judges/11#v32)</sup> So Jephthah passed over unto the children of Ammon to fight against them; and the Lord delivered them into his hands.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/judges/11#v33)</sup> And he smote them from Aroer, even till thou come to Minnith, even twenty cities, and unto the plain of the vineyards, with a very great slaughter. Thus the children of Ammon were subdued before the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/judges/11#v34)</sup> And Jephthah came to Mizpeh unto his house, and, behold, his daughter came out to meet him with timbrels and with dances: and she was his only child; beside her he had neither son nor daughter.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/judges/11#v35)</sup> And it came to pass, when he saw her, that he rent his clothes, and said, Alas, my daughter! thou hast brought me very low, and thou art one of them that trouble me: for I have opened my mouth unto the Lord, and I cannot go back.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/judges/11#v36)</sup> And she said unto him, My father, if thou hast opened thy mouth unto the Lord, do to me according to that which hath proceeded out of thy mouth; forasmuch as the Lord hath taken vengeance for thee of thine enemies, even of the children of Ammon.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Samuel 15:7-9</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-samuel/15#v7)</sup> And it came to pass after forty years, that Absalom said unto the king, I pray thee, let me go and pay my vow, which I have vowed unto the Lord, in Hebron.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-samuel/15#v8)</sup> For thy servant vowed a vow while I abode at Geshur in Syria, saying, If the Lord shall bring me again indeed to Jerusalem, then I will serve the Lord.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-samuel/15#v9)</sup> And the king said unto him, Go in peace. So he arose, and went to Hebron.

</details>

### 4D.6 {#4D.6}

You are to keep your word.

[Deut 23:23](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v23)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 23:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v23)</sup> That which is gone out of thy lips thou shalt keep and perform; even a freewill offering, according as thou hast vowed unto the Lord thy God, which thou hast promised with thy mouth.

</details>

### 4D.7 {#4D.7}

When you make a vow unto God, you shall not delay to pay it, for God will require you to keep your word.

[Deut 23:21](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v21); [Ps 65:1](/bible/psalms/65#v1); [Ps 66:13](/bible/psalms/66#v13); [Ps 76:11](/bible/psalms/76#v11); [Ps 116:18](/bible/psalms/116#v18); [Eccl 5:4-5](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v4)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 23:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v21)</sup> When thou shalt vow a vow unto the Lord thy God, thou shalt not slack to pay it: for the Lord thy God will surely require it of thee; and it would be sin in thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 65:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/65#v1)</sup> Praise waiteth for thee, O God, in Sion: and unto thee shall the vow be performed.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 66:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/66#v13)</sup> I will go into thy house with burnt offerings: I will pay thee my vows,

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 76:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/76#v11)</sup> Vow, and pay unto the Lord your God: let all that be round about him bring presents unto him that ought to be feared.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 116:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/116#v18)</sup> I will pay my vows unto the Lord now in the presence of all his people,

</details>
<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 5:4-5</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v4)</sup> When thou vowest a vow unto God, defer not to pay it; for he hath no pleasure in fools: pay that which thou hast vowed.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v5)</sup> Better is it that thou shouldest not vow, than that thou shouldest vow and not pay.

</details>

### 4D.8 {#4D.8}

If you refrain from vowing there is no sin in not performing what you said you would do.

[Deut 23:22](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v22)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 23:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v22)</sup> But if thou shalt forbear to vow, it shall be no sin in thee.

</details>

### 4D.9 {#4D.9}

If a young woman, who is under her father's house, makes a vow and her father hears it but says nothing then all her vows shall stand.

[Num 30:3-4](/bible/numbers/30#v3)

<details><summary>Numbers 30:3-4</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/30#v3)</sup> If a woman also vow a vow unto the Lord, and bind herself by a bond, being in her father’s house in her youth;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/30#v4)</sup> And her father hear her vow, and her bond wherewith she hath bound her soul, and her father shall hold his peace at her: then all her vows shall stand, and every bond wherewith she hath bound her soul shall stand.

</details>

### 4D.10 {#4D.10}

A father can cancel the obligations of a vow or contract by his daughter who is in his house.

[Num 30:5](/bible/numbers/30#v5)

<details><summary>Numbers 30:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/30#v5)</sup> But if her father disallow her in the day that he heareth; not any of her vows, or of her bonds wherewith she hath bound her soul, shall stand: and the Lord shall forgive her, because her father disallowed her.

</details>

### 4D.11 {#4D.11}

A husband can allow or annul the vows or contracts made by his wife.

[Num 30:6-8](/bible/numbers/30#v6)

<details><summary>Numbers 30:6-8</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/30#v6)</sup> And if she had at all an husband, when she vowed, or uttered ought out of her lips, wherewith she bound her soul;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/30#v7)</sup> And her husband heard it, and held his peace at her in the day that he heard it: then her vows shall stand, and her bonds wherewith she bound her soul shall stand.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/30#v8)</sup> But if her husband disallowed her on the day that he heard it; then he shall make her vow which she vowed, and that which she uttered with her lips, wherewith she bound her soul, of none effect: and the Lord shall forgive her.

</details>

### 4D.12 {#4D.12}

A vow of a widow or divorced woman shall stand against her.

[Num 30:9](/bible/numbers/30#v9)

<details><summary>Numbers 30:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/30#v9)</sup> But every vow of a widow, and of her that is divorced, wherewith they have bound their souls, shall stand against her.

</details>

### 4D.13 {#4D.13}

If a divorced woman makes a vow while in her husband's house, her husband can affirm or annul the vow.

[Num 30:10-14](/bible/numbers/30#v10)

<details><summary>Numbers 30:10-14</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/30#v10)</sup> And if she vowed in her husband’s house, or bound her soul by a bond with an oath;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/30#v11)</sup> And her husband heard it, and held his peace at her, and disallowed her not: then all her vows shall stand, and every bond wherewith she bound her soul shall stand.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/30#v12)</sup> But if her husband hath utterly made them void on the day he heard them; then whatsoever proceeded out of her lips concerning her vows, or concerning the bond of her soul, shall not stand: her husband hath made them void; and the Lord shall forgive her.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/30#v13)</sup> Every vow, and every binding oath to afflict the soul, her husband may establish it, or her husband may make it void.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/30#v14)</sup> But if her husband altogether hold his peace at her from day to day; then he establisheth all her vows, or all her bonds, which are upon her: he confirmeth them, because he held his peace at her in the day that he heard them.

</details>

### 4D.14 {#4D.14}

We should avoid making any oaths so that you may not fall under judgment.

[Matt 5:34-37](/bible/matthew/5#v34); [James 5:12](/bible/james/5#v12)

<details><summary>Matthew 5:34-37</summary>

> <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/5#v34)</sup> But I say unto you, Swear not at all; neither by heaven; for it is God’s throne:
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/5#v35)</sup> Nor by the earth; for it is his footstool: neither by Jerusalem; for it is the city of the great King.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/5#v36)</sup> Neither shalt thou swear by thy head, because thou canst not make one hair white or black.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/5#v37)</sup> But let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay: for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil.

</details>
<details><summary>James 5:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/james/5#v12)</sup> But above all things, my brethren, swear not, neither by heaven, neither by the earth, neither by any other oath: but let your yea be yea; and your nay, nay; lest ye fall into condemnation.

</details>

### 4D.15 {#4D.15}

God will judge and punish false swearers.

[Zech 5:4](/bible/zechariah/5#v4); [Mal 3:5](/bible/malachi/3#v5)

<details><summary>Zechariah 5:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/zechariah/5#v4)</sup> I will bring it forth, saith the Lord of hosts, and it shall enter into the house of the thief, and into the house of him that sweareth falsely by my name: and it shall remain in the midst of his house, and shall consume it with the timber thereof and the stones thereof.

</details>
<details><summary>Malachi 3:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/malachi/3#v5)</sup> And I will come near to you to judgment; and I will be a swift witness against the sorcerers, and against the adulterers, and against false swearers, and against those that oppress the hireling in his wages, the widow, and the fatherless, and that turn aside the stranger from his right, and fear not me, saith the Lord of hosts.

</details>

### 4D.16 {#4D.16}

If a person swears thoughtlessly with his lips to do evil or to do good, and whatever he pronounced by the oath is hidden or unknown to him, when he comes to know it, then he shall be guilty in these matters and shall confess them.

[Lev 5:4-5](/bible/leviticus/5#v4)

<details><summary>Leviticus 5:4-5</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/5#v4)</sup> Or if a soul swear, pronouncing with his lips to do evil, or to do good, whatsoever it be that a man shall pronounce with an oath, and it be hid from him; when he knoweth of it, then he shall be guilty in one of these.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/5#v5)</sup> And it shall be, when he shall be guilty in one of these things, that he shall confess that he hath sinned in that thing:

</details>
