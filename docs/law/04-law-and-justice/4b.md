### 4B.1 {#4B.1}

The law is to be revealed to the people; it is not to be kept secret, or made obscure, or hidden in any manner.

[Deut 29:29](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v29); [Deut 30:11](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v11)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 29:29</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v29)</sup> The secret things belong unto the Lord our God: but those things which are revealed belong unto us and to our children for ever, that we may do all the words of this law.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 30:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v11)</sup> For this commandment which I command thee this day, it is not hidden from thee, neither is it far off.

</details>

### 4B.3 {#4B.3}

Every seven years, on the year of release, the law is to be read before all the people that they may learn and observe all the words of the law.

[Deut 31:10-13](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v10)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 31:10-13</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v10)</sup> And Moses commanded them, saying, At the end of every seven years, in the solemnity of the year of release, in the feast of tabernacles,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v11)</sup> When all Israel is come to appear before the Lord thy God in the place which he shall choose, thou shalt read this law before all Israel in their hearing.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v12)</sup> Gather the people together, men, and women, and children, and thy stranger that is within thy gates, that they may hear, and that they may learn, and fear the Lord your God, and observe to do all the words of this law:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v13)</sup> And that their children, which have not known any thing, may hear, and learn to fear the Lord your God, as long as ye live in the land whither ye go over Jordan to possess it.

</details>
