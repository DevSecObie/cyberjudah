### 4C.1 {#4C.1}

You are not to take a gift or money offered as a bribe.

[Deut 16:19](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v19); [2 Chr 19:7](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v7); [Isa 33:15](/bible/isaiah/33#v15)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 16:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v19)</sup> Thou shalt not wrest judgment; thou shalt not respect persons, neither take a gift: for a gift doth blind the eyes of the wise, and pervert the words of the righteous.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Chronicles 19:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v7)</sup> Wherefore now let the fear of the Lord be upon you; take heed and do it: for there is no iniquity with the Lord our God, nor respect of persons, nor taking of gifts.

</details>
<details><summary>Isaiah 33:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/33#v15)</sup> He that walketh righteously, and speaketh uprightly; he that despiseth the gain of oppressions, that shaketh his hands from holding of bribes, that stoppeth his ears from hearing of blood, and shutteth his eyes from seeing evil;

</details>

### 4C.2 {#4C.2}

Bribery condemned as being wicked and perverting justice and judgment.

[1 Sam 8:3](/bible/1-samuel/8#v3); [Job 15:34](/bible/job/15#v34); [Ps 26:9-10](/bible/psalms/26#v9); [Prov 17:23](/bible/proverbs/17#v23); [Eccl 7:7](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v7); [Isa 1:23](/bible/isaiah/1#v23); [Isa 5:23](/bible/isaiah/5#v23); [Ezek 13:19](/bible/ezekiel/13#v19); [Amos 5:12](/bible/amos/5#v12); [Matt 26:14-15](/bible/matthew/26#v14)

<details><summary>1 Samuel 8:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-samuel/8#v3)</sup> And his sons walked not in his ways, but turned aside after lucre, and took bribes, and perverted judgment.

</details>
<details><summary>Job 15:34</summary>

> <sup>[34](/bible/job/15#v34)</sup> For the congregation of hypocrites shall be desolate, and fire shall consume the tabernacles of bribery.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 26:9-10</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/26#v9)</sup> Gather not my soul with sinners, nor my life with bloody men:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/26#v10)</sup> In whose hands is mischief, and their right hand is full of bribes.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 17:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/17#v23)</sup> A wicked man taketh a gift out of the bosom to pervert the ways of judgment.

</details>
<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 7:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v7)</sup> Surely oppression maketh a wise man mad; and a gift destroyeth the heart.

</details>
<details><summary>Isaiah 1:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/1#v23)</sup> Thy princes are rebellious, and companions of thieves: every one loveth gifts, and followeth after rewards: they judge not the fatherless, neither doth the cause of the widow come unto them.

</details>
<details><summary>Isaiah 5:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/5#v23)</sup> Which justify the wicked for reward, and take away the righteousness of the righteous from him!

</details>
<details><summary>Ezekiel 13:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/13#v19)</sup> And will ye pollute me among my people for handfuls of barley and for pieces of bread, to slay the souls that should not die, and to save the souls alive that should not live, by your lying to my people that hear your lies?

</details>
<details><summary>Amos 5:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/amos/5#v12)</sup> For I know your manifold transgressions and your mighty sins: they afflict the just, they take a bribe, and they turn aside the poor in the gate from their right.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 26:14-15</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/26#v14)</sup> Then one of the twelve, called Judas Iscariot, went unto the chief priests,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/26#v15)</sup> And said unto them, What will ye give me, and I will deliver him unto you? And they covenanted with him for thirty pieces of silver.

</details>

### 4C.3 {#4C.3}

He who takes a bribe against the innocent may not dwell in the LORD's holy nation.

[Ps 15:1,5](/bible/psalms/15#v1)

<details><summary>Psalms 15:1,5</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/15#v1)</sup> Lord, who shall abide in thy tabernacle? who shall dwell in thy holy hill?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/15#v5)</sup> He that putteth not out his money to usury, nor taketh reward against the innocent. He that doeth these things shall never be moved.

</details>

### 4C.4 {#4C.4}

You shall not take a bribe, for a bribe blinds the wise and subverts the cause of the just.

[Exod 23:8](/bible/exodus/23#v8)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/23#v8)</sup> And thou shalt take no gift: for the gift blindeth the wise, and perverteth the words of the righteous.

</details>

### 4C.5 {#4C.5}

Those in government who take bribes overthrows the justice and stability established in the land.

[Prov 29:4](/bible/proverbs/29#v4)

<details><summary>Proverbs 29:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/29#v4)</sup> The king by judgment establisheth the land: but he that receiveth gifts overthroweth it.

</details>

### 4C.6 {#4C.6}

They are cursed who take reward against the innocent.

[Deut 27:25](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v25); [Ps 15:5](/bible/psalms/15#v5)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 27:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v25)</sup> Cursed be he that taketh reward to slay an innocent person. And all the people shall say, Amen.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 15:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/15#v5)</sup> He that putteth not out his money to usury, nor taketh reward against the innocent. He that doeth these things shall never be moved.

</details>

### 4C.7 {#4C.7}

He that hates gifts is blessed.

[Prov 15:27](/bible/proverbs/15#v27)

<details><summary>Proverbs 15:27</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/15#v27)</sup> He that is greedy of gain troubleth his own house; but he that hateth gifts shall live.

</details>

### 4C.8 {#4C.8}

It is iniquity for judges to pronounce judgment for a bribe or reward.

[Mic 3:10-11](/bible/micah/3#v10)

<details><summary>Micah 3:10-11</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/micah/3#v10)</sup> They build up Zion with blood, and Jerusalem with iniquity.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/micah/3#v11)</sup> The heads thereof judge for reward, and the priests thereof teach for hire, and the prophets thereof divine for money: yet will they lean upon the Lord, and say, Is not the Lord among us? none evil can come upon us.

</details>
