### 4A.1 {#4A.1}

The laws, statutes, commandments and judgments of God are to be kept throughout the land.

[Lev 18:4-5](/bible/leviticus/18#v4); [Deut 5:32-33](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v32); [Ezek 20:19](/bible/ezekiel/20#v19)

<details><summary>Leviticus 18:4-5</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/18#v4)</sup> Ye shall do my judgments, and keep mine ordinances, to walk therein: I am the Lord your God.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/18#v5)</sup> Ye shall therefore keep my statutes, and my judgments: which if a man do, he shall live in them: I am the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 5:32-33</summary>

> <sup>[32](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v32)</sup> Ye shall observe to do therefore as the Lord your God hath commanded you: ye shall not turn aside to the right hand or to the left.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v33)</sup> Ye shall walk in all the ways which the Lord your God hath commanded you, that ye may live, and that it may be well with you, and that ye may prolong your days in the land which ye shall possess.

</details>
<details><summary>Ezekiel 20:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/20#v19)</sup> I am the Lord your God; walk in my statutes, and keep my judgments, and do them;

</details>

### 4A.2 {#4A.2}

There is to be one law applied to the citizen and the stranger.

[Exod 12:49](/bible/exodus/12#v49); [Lev 24:22](/bible/leviticus/24#v22); [Num 9:14](/bible/numbers/9#v14)

<details><summary>Exodus 12:49</summary>

> <sup>[49](/bible/exodus/12#v49)</sup> One law shall be to him that is homeborn, and unto the stranger that sojourneth among you.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 24:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/24#v22)</sup> Ye shall have one manner of law, as well for the stranger, as for one of your own country: for I am the Lord your God.

</details>
<details><summary>Numbers 9:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/9#v14)</sup> And if a stranger shall sojourn among you, and will keep the passover unto the Lord; according to the ordinance of the passover, and according to the manner thereof, so shall he do: ye shall have one ordinance, both for the stranger, and for him that was born in the land.

</details>

<details><summary>Isaiah 33:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/33#v22)</sup> For the Lord is our judge, the Lord is our lawgiver, the Lord is our king; he will save us.

</details>
<details><summary>James 4:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/james/4#v12)</sup> There is one lawgiver, who is able to save and to destroy: who art thou that judgest another?

### 4A.5 {#4A.5}

You shall not follow the laws and ordinances of other nations in your land.

[Lev 18:3](/bible/leviticus/18#v3); [Mic 6:16](/bible/micah/6#v16); [2 Kgs 17:19-20](/bible/2-kings/17#v19)

<details><summary>Leviticus 18:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/18#v3)</sup> After the doings of the land of Egypt, wherein ye dwelt, shall ye not do: and after the doings of the land of Canaan, whither I bring you, shall ye not do: neither shall ye walk in their ordinances.

</details>
<details><summary>Micah 6:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/micah/6#v16)</sup> For the statutes of Omri are kept, and all the works of the house of Ahab, and ye walk in their counsels; that I should make thee a desolation, and the inhabitants thereof an hissing: therefore ye shall bear the reproach of my people.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Kings 17:19-20</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/2-kings/17#v19)</sup> Also Judah kept not the commandments of the Lord their God, but walked in the statutes of Israel which they made.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-kings/17#v20)</sup> And the Lord rejected all the seed of Israel, and afflicted them, and delivered them into the hand of spoilers, until he had cast them out of his sight.

</details>

### 4A.6 {#4A.6}

He who does any thing defiantly and with contempt for the law, that person shall be cut off from among his people.

[Num 15:29-31](/bible/numbers/15#v29)

<details><summary>Numbers 15:29-31</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/15#v29)</sup> Ye shall have one law for him that sinneth through ignorance, both for him that is born among the children of Israel, and for the stranger that sojourneth among them.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/15#v30)</sup> But the soul that doeth ought presumptuously, whether he be born in the land, or a stranger, the same reproacheth the Lord; and that soul shall be cut off from among his people.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/15#v31)</sup> Because he hath despised the word of the Lord, and hath broken his commandment, that soul shall utterly be cut off; his iniquity shall be upon him.

</details>
