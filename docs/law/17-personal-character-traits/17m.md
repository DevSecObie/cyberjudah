### 17M.1 {#17M.1}

You are not to personally avenge or bear any grudge against your brethren.

[Lev 19:18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18); [Prov 24:29](/bible/proverbs/24#v29)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18)</sup> Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 24:29</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/24#v29)</sup> Say not, I will do so to him as he hath done to me: I will render to the man according to his work.

</details>

### 17M.2 {#17M.2}

Repay to no man evil for evil.

[Rom 12:17](/bible/romans/12#v17); [1 Thess 5:15](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v15); [1 Pet 3:9](/bible/1-peter/3#v9)

<details><summary>Romans 12:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/romans/12#v17)</sup> Recompense to no man evil for evil. Provide things honest in the sight of all men.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 5:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v15)</sup> See that none render evil for evil unto any man; but ever follow that which is good, both among yourselves, and to all men.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Peter 3:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-peter/3#v9)</sup> Not rendering evil for evil, or railing for railing: but contrariwise blessing; knowing that ye are thereunto called, that ye should inherit a blessing.

</details>

### 17M.3 {#17M.3}

Avenge not yourselves, for vengeance is in God's hands.

[Deut 32:35](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v35); [Ps 18:47](/bible/psalms/18#v47); [Ps 94:1](/bible/psalms/94#v1); [Jer 46:9-10](/bible/jeremiah/46#v9); [Ezek 25:12-17](/bible/ezekiel/25#v12); [Nah 1:2](/bible/nahum/1#v2); [Luke 18:7](/bible/luke/18#v7); [Rom 12:19](/bible/romans/12#v19); [1 Thess 4:6](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v6); [Heb 10:30](/bible/hebrews/10#v30)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 32:35</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v35)</sup> To me belongeth vengeance, and recompence; their foot shall slide in due time: for the day of their calamity is at hand, and the things that shall come upon them make haste.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 18:47</summary>

> <sup>[47](/bible/psalms/18#v47)</sup> It is God that avengeth me, and subdueth the people under me.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 94:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/94#v1)</sup> O Lord God, to whom vengeance belongeth; O God, to whom vengeance belongeth, shew thyself.

</details>

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/46#v10)</sup> For this is the day of the Lord God of hosts, a day of vengeance, that he may avenge him of his adversaries: and the sword shall devour, and it shall be satiate and made drunk with their blood: for the Lord God of hosts hath a sacrifice in the north country by the river Euphrates.

>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/25#v14)</sup> And I will lay my vengeance upon Edom by the hand of my people Israel: and they shall do in Edom according to mine anger and according to my fury; and they shall know my vengeance, saith the Lord God.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/25#v15)</sup> Thus saith the Lord God; Because the Philistines have dealt by revenge, and have taken vengeance with a despiteful heart, to destroy it for the old hatred;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/25#v16)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I will stretch out mine hand upon the Philistines, and I will cut off the Cherethims, and destroy the remnant of the sea coast.

</details>

<details><summary>Hebrews 10:30</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/hebrews/10#v30)</sup> For we know him that hath said, Vengeance belongeth unto me, I will recompense, saith the Lord. And again, The Lord shall judge his people.

</details>

### 17M.4 {#17M.4}

Trust in God instead of taking revenge.

[Prov 20:22](/bible/proverbs/20#v22)

<details><summary>Proverbs 20:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/20#v22)</sup> Say not thou, I will recompense evil; but wait on the Lord, and he shall save thee.

</details>

### 17M.5 {#17M.5}

God will repay with affliction and tribulation those who persecute and afflict you because of your faith.

[2 Thess 1:4-9](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v4)

<details><summary>2 Thessalonians 1:4-9</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v4)</sup> So that we ourselves glory in you in the churches of God for your patience and faith in all your persecutions and tribulations that ye endure:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v5)</sup> Which is a manifest token of the righteous judgment of God, that ye may be counted worthy of the kingdom of God, for which ye also suffer:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v6)</sup> Seeing it is a righteous thing with God to recompense tribulation to them that trouble you;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v7)</sup> And to you who are troubled rest with us, when the Lord Jesus shall be revealed from heaven with his mighty angels,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v8)</sup> In flaming fire taking vengeance on them that know not God, and that obey not the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v9)</sup> Who shall be punished with everlasting destruction from the presence of the Lord, and from the glory of his power;

</details>

### 17M.6 {#17M.6}

We should keep others from taking revenge.

[1 Sam 24:4-7](/bible/1-samuel/24#v4); [1 Sam 26:9](/bible/1-samuel/26#v9)

<details><summary>1 Samuel 24:4-7</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-samuel/24#v4)</sup> And the men of David said unto him, Behold the day of which the Lord said unto thee, Behold, I will deliver thine enemy into thine hand, that thou mayest do to him as it shall seem good unto thee. Then David arose, and cut off the skirt of Saul’s robe privily.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-samuel/24#v5)</sup> And it came to pass afterward, that David’s heart smote him, because he had cut off Saul’s skirt.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-samuel/24#v6)</sup> And he said unto his men, The Lord forbid that I should do this thing unto my master, the Lord’s anointed, to stretch forth mine hand against him, seeing he is the anointed of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-samuel/24#v7)</sup> So David stayed his servants with these words, and suffered them not to rise against Saul. But Saul rose up out of the cave, and went on his way.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Samuel 26:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-samuel/26#v9)</sup> And David said to Abishai, Destroy him not: for who can stretch forth his hand against the Lord’s anointed, and be guiltless?

</details>

### 17M.8 {#17M.8}

We should pray to God for His vengeance against those that unjustly inflict us.

[Jer 11:19-20](/bible/jeremiah/11#v19); [Jer 20:10-12](/bible/jeremiah/20#v10)

<details><summary>Jeremiah 11:19-20</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/11#v19)</sup> But I was like a lamb or an ox that is brought to the slaughter; and I knew not that they had devised devices against me, saying, Let us destroy the tree with the fruit thereof, and let us cut him off from the land of the living, that his name may be no more remembered.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/11#v20)</sup> But, O Lord of hosts, that judgest righteously, that triest the reins and the heart, let me see thy vengeance on them: for unto thee have I revealed my cause.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 20:10-12</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/20#v10)</sup> For I heard the defaming of many, fear on every side. Report, say they, and we will report it. All my familiars watched for my halting, saying, Peradventure he will be enticed, and we shall prevail against him, and we shall take our revenge on him.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/20#v11)</sup> But the Lord is with me as a mighty terrible one: therefore my persecutors shall stumble, and they shall not prevail: they shall be greatly ashamed; for they shall not prosper: their everlasting confusion shall never be forgotten.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/20#v12)</sup> But, O Lord of hosts, that triest the righteous, and seest the reins and the heart, let me see thy vengeance on them: for unto thee have I opened my cause.

</details>
