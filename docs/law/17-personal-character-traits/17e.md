### 17E.1 {#17E.1}

Patience, long-suffering and temperance exhorted and commended.

[Eccl 7:8](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v8); [Eph 4:2](/bible/ephesians/4#v2); [Col 1:11](/bible/colossians/1#v11); [Col 3:12](/bible/colossians/3#v12); [1 Thess 5:14](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v14); [2 Tim 3:10](/bible/2-timothy/3#v10); [Titus 2:2](/bible/titus/2#v2); [1 Pet 1:6](/bible/1-peter/1#v6); [Rev 2:2-3](/bible/revelation/2#v2)

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 7:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v8)</sup> Better is the end of a thing than the beginning thereof: and the patient in spirit is better than the proud in spirit.

</details>
<details><summary>Ephesians 4:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/ephesians/4#v2)</sup> With all lowliness and meekness, with longsuffering, forbearing one another in love;

</details>
<details><summary>Colossians 1:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/colossians/1#v11)</sup> Strengthened with all might, according to his glorious power, unto all patience and longsuffering with joyfulness;

</details>
<details><summary>Colossians 3:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/colossians/3#v12)</sup> Put on therefore, as the elect of God, holy and beloved, bowels of mercies, kindness, humbleness of mind, meekness, longsuffering;

</details>
<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 5:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v14)</sup> Now we exhort you, brethren, warn them that are unruly, comfort the feebleminded, support the weak, be patient toward all men.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Timothy 3:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-timothy/3#v10)</sup> But thou hast fully known my doctrine, manner of life, purpose, faith, longsuffering, charity, patience,

</details>
<details><summary>Titus 2:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/titus/2#v2)</sup> That the aged men be sober, grave, temperate, sound in faith, in charity, in patience.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Peter 1:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-peter/1#v6)</sup> Wherein ye greatly rejoice, though now for a season, if need be, ye are in heaviness through manifold temptations:

</details>
<details><summary>Revelation 2:2-3</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/2#v2)</sup> I know thy works, and thy labour, and thy patience, and how thou canst not bear them which are evil: and thou hast tried them which say they are apostles, and are not, and hast found them liars:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/revelation/2#v3)</sup> And hast borne, and hast patience, and for my name’s sake hast laboured, and hast not fainted.

</details>

### 17E.2 {#17E.2}

Rashness and quick-temperedness exalts folly.

[Prov 14:29](/bible/proverbs/14#v29)

<details><summary>Proverbs 14:29</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/14#v29)</sup> He that is slow to wrath is of great understanding: but he that is hasty of spirit exalteth folly.

</details>

### 17E.3 {#17E.3}

Trials of Christians leads to patience.

[Rom 5:3-4](/bible/romans/5#v3); [James 1:3-4](/bible/james/1#v3)

<details><summary>Romans 5:3-4</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/romans/5#v3)</sup> And not only so, but we glory in tribulations also: knowing that tribulation worketh patience;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/5#v4)</sup> And patience, experience; and experience, hope:

</details>
<details><summary>James 1:3-4</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/james/1#v3)</sup> Knowing this, that the trying of your faith worketh patience.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/james/1#v4)</sup> But let patience have her perfect work, that ye may be perfect and entire, wanting nothing.

</details>

### 17E.4 {#17E.4}

When you do what is right and suffer for it, if you patiently endure it, this is commendable before God.

[1 Pet 2:20](/bible/1-peter/2#v20)

<details><summary>1 Peter 2:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/1-peter/2#v20)</sup> For what glory is it, if, when ye be buffeted for your faults, ye shall take it patiently? but if, when ye do well, and suffer for it, ye take it patiently, this is acceptable with God.

</details>

### 17E.5 {#17E.5}

Be not rash with what you speak and do.

[Eccl 5:2](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v2); [Acts 19:36](/bible/acts/19#v36)

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 5:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v2)</sup> Be not rash with thy mouth, and let not thine heart be hasty to utter any thing before God: for God is in heaven, and thou upon earth: therefore let thy words be few.

</details>
<details><summary>Acts 19:36</summary>

> <sup>[36](/bible/acts/19#v36)</sup> Seeing then that these things cannot be spoken against, ye ought to be quiet, and to do nothing rashly.

</details>

<details><summary>Psalms 37:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/37#v7)</sup> Rest in the Lord, and wait patiently for him: fret not thyself because of him who prospereth in his way, because of the man who bringeth wicked devices to pass.

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>2 Thessalonians 3:5</summary>
