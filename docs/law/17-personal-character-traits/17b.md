### 17B.1 {#17B.1}

We are not to be envious of one another.

[Prov 3:31](/bible/proverbs/3#v31); [Rom 1:28-29](/bible/romans/1#v28); [Rom 13:13](/bible/romans/13#v13); [2 Cor 12:20](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v20); [Gal 5:26](/bible/galatians/5#v26)

<details><summary>Proverbs 3:31</summary>

> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/3#v31)</sup> Envy thou not the oppressor, and choose none of his ways.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 1:28-29</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/romans/1#v28)</sup> And even as they did not like to retain God in their knowledge, God gave them over to a reprobate mind, to do those things which are not convenient;
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/romans/1#v29)</sup> Being filled with all unrighteousness, fornication, wickedness, covetousness, maliciousness; full of envy, murder, debate, deceit, malignity; whisperers,

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 13:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/13#v13)</sup> Let us walk honestly, as in the day; not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and envying.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Corinthians 12:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v20)</sup> For I fear, lest, when I come, I shall not find you such as I would, and that I shall be found unto you such as ye would not: lest there be debates, envyings, wraths, strifes, backbitings, whisperings, swellings, tumults:

</details>
<details><summary>Galatians 5:26</summary>

> <sup>[26](/bible/galatians/5#v26)</sup> Let us not be desirous of vain glory, provoking one another, envying one another.

</details>

### 17B.3 {#17B.3}

Envy is a work of the flesh.

[Gal 5:19,21](/bible/galatians/5#v19); [James 4:5](/bible/james/4#v5); [1 Cor 3:3](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v3)

<details><summary>Galatians 5:19,21</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/galatians/5#v19)</sup> Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are these; Adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/galatians/5#v21)</sup> Envyings, murders, drunkenness, revellings, and such like: of the which I tell you before, as I have also told you in time past, that they which do such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God.

</details>
<details><summary>James 4:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/james/4#v5)</sup> Do ye think that the scripture saith in vain, The spirit that dwelleth in us lusteth to envy?

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 3:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v3)</sup> For ye are yet carnal: for whereas there is among you envying, and strife, and divisions, are ye not carnal, and walk as men?

</details>

### 17B.4 {#17B.4}

It is vain to be envious of the good deeds of other.

[Eccl 4:4](/bible/ecclesiastes/4#v4)

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 4:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/ecclesiastes/4#v4)</sup> Again, I considered all travail, and every right work, that for this a man is envied of his neighbour. This is also vanity and vexation of spirit.

</details>

</details>
