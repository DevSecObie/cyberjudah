### 17G.1 {#17G.1}

Pride is a sin and is hateful to God.

[Prov 6:16-17](/bible/proverbs/6#v16); [Prov 16:5](/bible/proverbs/16#v5); [Prov 21:4,24](/bible/proverbs/21#v4); [Isa 23:9](/bible/isaiah/23#v9); [Jer 50:31-32](/bible/jeremiah/50#v31)

<details><summary>Proverbs 6:16-17</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/6#v16)</sup> These six things doth the Lord hate: yea, seven are an abomination unto him:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/6#v17)</sup> A proud look, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood,

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 16:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/16#v5)</sup> Every one that is proud in heart is an abomination to the Lord: though hand join in hand, he shall not be unpunished.

</details>

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/21#v4)</sup> An high look, and a proud heart, and the plowing of the wicked, is sin.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/21#v24)</sup> Proud and haughty scorner is his name, who dealeth in proud wrath.

</details>
<details><summary>Isaiah 23:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/23#v9)</sup> The Lord of hosts hath purposed it, to stain the pride of all glory, and to bring into contempt all the honourable of the earth.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 50:31-32</summary>

> <sup>[31](/bible/jeremiah/50#v31)</sup> Behold, I am against thee, O thou most proud, saith the Lord God of hosts: for thy day is come, the time that I will visit thee.
