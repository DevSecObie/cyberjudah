### 17L.1 {#17L.1}

He that wishes one well but does not give to him what he needs exhibits selfishness.

[James 2:15-16](/bible/james/2#v15); [1 John 3:17](/bible/1-john/3#v17)

<details><summary>James 2:15-16</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/james/2#v15)</sup> If a brother or sister be naked, and destitute of daily food,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/james/2#v16)</sup> And one of you say unto them, Depart in peace, be ye warmed and filled; notwithstanding ye give them not those things which are needful to the body; what doth it profit?

</details>
<details><summary>1 John 3:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-john/3#v17)</sup> But whoso hath this world’s good, and seeth his brother have need, and shutteth up his bowels of compassion from him, how dwelleth the love of God in him?

</details>

### 17L.2 {#17L.2}

We should not desire to please ourselves.

[Rom 15:1](/bible/romans/15#v1); [2 Tim 3:2](/bible/2-timothy/3#v2)

<details><summary>Romans 15:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/15#v1)</sup> We then that are strong ought to bear the infirmities of the weak, and not to please ourselves.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Timothy 3:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/2-timothy/3#v2)</sup> For men shall be lovers of their own selves, covetous, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy,

</details>

### 17L.3 {#17L.3}

A servant of God is to act for the benefit or profit of others.

[Isa 56:11](/bible/isaiah/56#v11); [1 Cor 10:33](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v33)

<details><summary>Isaiah 56:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/56#v11)</sup> Yea, they are greedy dogs which can never have enough, and they are shepherds that cannot understand: they all look to their own way, every one for his gain, from his quarter.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 10:33</summary>

> <sup>[33](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v33)</sup> Even as I please all men in all things, not seeking mine own profit, but the profit of many, that they may be saved.

</details>

### 17L.5 {#17L.5}

Let us not please ourselves, but let every one of us please his neighbor.

[Rom 15:1-3](/bible/romans/15#v1)

<details><summary>Romans 15:1-3</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/15#v1)</sup> We then that are strong ought to bear the infirmities of the weak, and not to please ourselves.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/15#v2)</sup> Let every one of us please his neighbour for his good to edification.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/romans/15#v3)</sup> For even Christ pleased not himself; but, as it is written, The reproaches of them that reproached thee fell on me.

</details>

### 17L.6 {#17L.6}

No one should merely seek or look out for his own interest, but also for the interests of others.

[1 Cor 10:24](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v24); [Phil 2:4](/bible/philippians/2#v4)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 10:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v24)</sup> Let no man seek his own, but every man another’s wealth.

</details>
<details><summary>Philippians 2:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/philippians/2#v4)</sup> Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others.

</details>

### 17L.7 {#17L.7}

The selfish shall receive punishment according to their ways.

[Prov 21:13](/bible/proverbs/21#v13)

<details><summary>Proverbs 21:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/21#v13)</sup> Whoso stoppeth his ears at the cry of the poor, he also shall cry himself, but shall not be heard.

</details>
