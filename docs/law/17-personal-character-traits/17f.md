### 17F.1 {#17F.1}

Circumcise the foreskin of your heart, and be no more stiff-necked.

[Deut 10:16](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v16)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 10:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v16)</sup> Circumcise therefore the foreskin of your heart, and be no more stiffnecked.

</details>

### 17F.2 {#17F.2}

Stubbornness towards the words of the LORD is as iniquity and idolatry.

[1 Sam 15:23](/bible/1-samuel/15#v23)

<details><summary>1 Samuel 15:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/1-samuel/15#v23)</sup> For rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry. Because thou hast rejected the word of the Lord, he hath also rejected thee from being king.

</details>

### 17F.3 {#17F.3}

Speak not with a stiff neck.

[Ps 75:5](/bible/psalms/75#v5)

<details><summary>Psalms 75:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/75#v5)</sup> Lift not up your horn on high: speak not with a stiff neck.

</details>

### 17F.4 {#17F.4}

He who hardens his neck after much reproof will suddenly be destroyed beyond remedy.

[Prov 29:1](/bible/proverbs/29#v1)

<details><summary>Proverbs 29:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/29#v1)</sup> He, that being often reproved hardeneth his neck, shall suddenly be destroyed, and that without remedy.

</details>

### 17F.5 {#17F.5}

Those who are stiff-necked reject God and his law and become vain by following heathen ways.

[2 Kgs 17:14-17](/bible/2-kings/17#v14)

<details><summary>2 Kings 17:14-17</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-kings/17#v14)</sup> Notwithstanding they would not hear, but hardened their necks, like to the neck of their fathers, that did not believe in the Lord their God.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-kings/17#v15)</sup> And they rejected his statutes, and his covenant that he made with their fathers, and his testimonies which he testified against them; and they followed vanity, and became vain, and went after the heathen that were round about them, concerning whom the Lord had charged them, that they should not do like them.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-kings/17#v16)</sup> And they left all the commandments of the Lord their God, and made them molten images, even two calves, and made a grove, and worshipped all the host of heaven, and served Baal.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-kings/17#v17)</sup> And they caused their sons and their daughters to pass through the fire, and used divination and enchantments, and sold themselves to do evil in the sight of the Lord, to provoke him to anger.

</details>

<details><summary>2 Timothy 3:4</summary>

</details>

### 17F.7 {#17F.7}

Be not stiff-necked as your fathers were, but yield yourselves to the LORD.

[2 Chr 30:8](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v8)

<details><summary>2 Chronicles 30:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v8)</sup> Now be ye not stiffnecked, as your fathers were, but yield yourselves unto the Lord, and enter into his sanctuary, which he hath sanctified for ever: and serve the Lord your God, that the fierceness of his wrath may turn away from you.

</details>
