### 17C.1 {#17C.1}

Thou shall not covet thy neighbor's possessions.

[Exod 20:17](/bible/exodus/20#v17); [Deut 5:21](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v21); [Rom 7:7](/bible/romans/7#v7); [Rom 13:9](/bible/romans/13#v9)

<details><summary>Exodus 20:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/20#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour’s house, thou shalt not covet thy neighbour’s wife, nor his manservant, nor his maidservant, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor any thing that is thy neighbour’s.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 5:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v21)</sup> Neither shalt thou desire thy neighbour’s wife, neither shalt thou covet thy neighbour’s house, his field, or his manservant, or his maidservant, his ox, or his ass, or any thing that is thy neighbour’s.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 7:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/romans/7#v7)</sup> What shall we say then? Is the law sin? God forbid. Nay, I had not known sin, but by the law: for I had not known lust, except the law had said, Thou shalt not covet.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 13:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/romans/13#v9)</sup> For this, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not kill, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness, Thou shalt not covet; and if there be any other commandment, it is briefly comprehended in this saying, namely, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

</details>

### 17C.2 {#17C.2}

We are not to have a primary desire for earthly things.

[Matt 6:25](/bible/matthew/6#v25); [Luke 12:22,29](/bible/luke/12#v22); [Luke 21:34](/bible/luke/21#v34); [John 6:27](/bible/john/6#v27); [Phil 3:19](/bible/philippians/3#v19)

<details><summary>Matthew 6:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/6#v25)</sup> Therefore I say unto you, Take no thought for your life, what ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink; nor yet for your body, what ye shall put on. Is not the life more than meat, and the body than raiment?

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 12:22,29</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/luke/12#v22)</sup> And he said unto his disciples, Therefore I say unto you, Take no thought for your life, what ye shall eat; neither for the body, what ye shall put on.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/luke/12#v29)</sup> And seek not ye what ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink, neither be ye of doubtful mind.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 21:34</summary>

> <sup>[34](/bible/luke/21#v34)</sup> And take heed to yourselves, lest at any time your hearts be overcharged with surfeiting, and drunkenness, and cares of this life, and so that day come upon you unawares.

</details>
<details><summary>John 6:27</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/john/6#v27)</sup> Labour not for the meat which perisheth, but for that meat which endureth unto everlasting life, which the Son of man shall give unto you: for him hath God the Father sealed.

</details>
<details><summary>Philippians 3:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/philippians/3#v19)</sup> Whose end is destruction, whose God is their belly, and whose glory is in their shame, who mind earthly things.)

</details>

> <sup>[14](/bible/luke/8#v14)</sup> And that which fell among thorns are they, which, when they have heard, go forth, and are choked with cares and riches and pleasures of this life, and bring no fruit to perfection.

</details>

>

>
> <sup>[20](/bible/luke/14#v20)</sup> And another said, I have married a wife, and therefore I cannot come.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/luke/14#v21)</sup> So that servant came, and shewed his lord these things. Then the master of the house being angry said to his servant, Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city, and bring in hither the poor, and the maimed, and the halt, and the blind.

</details>
