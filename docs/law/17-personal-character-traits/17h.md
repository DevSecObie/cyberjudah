### 17H.1 {#17H.1}

[1 Cor 3:3](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v3); [1 Cor 10:6](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v6); [1 Pet 2:11](/bible/1-peter/2#v11); [Jude 1:16](/bible/jude/1#v16)

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v3)</sup> For ye are yet carnal: for whereas there is among you envying, and strife, and divisions, are ye not carnal, and walk as men?

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 10:6</summary>

[Rom 8:7-8](/bible/romans/8#v7); [Col 2:18](/bible/colossians/2#v18); [James 4:4](/bible/james/4#v4)

<details><summary>Romans 8:7-8</summary>

>
> <sup>[8](/bible/romans/8#v8)</sup> So then they that are in the flesh cannot please God.

</details>
<details><summary>Colossians 2:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/colossians/2#v18)</sup> Let no man beguile you of your reward in a voluntary humility and worshipping of angels, intruding into those things which he hath not seen, vainly puffed up by his fleshly mind,
