### 17J.1 {#17J.1}

Slothfulness brings one into bondage.

[Prov 12:24](/bible/proverbs/12#v24)

<details><summary>Proverbs 12:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/12#v24)</sup> The hand of the diligent shall bear rule: but the slothful shall be under tribute.

</details>

### 17J.2 {#17J.2}

Slothfulness magnifies obstacles.

[Prov 10:26](/bible/proverbs/10#v26); [Prov 15:19](/bible/proverbs/15#v19); [Prov 22:13](/bible/proverbs/22#v13)

<details><summary>Proverbs 10:26</summary>

> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/10#v26)</sup> As vinegar to the teeth, and as smoke to the eyes, so is the sluggard to them that send him.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 15:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/15#v19)</sup> The way of the slothful man is as an hedge of thorns: but the way of the righteous is made plain.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 22:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/22#v13)</sup> The slothful man saith, There is a lion without, I shall be slain in the streets.

</details>

### 17J.3 {#17J.3}

[2 Thess 3:10](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v10)

<details><summary>2 Thessalonians 3:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v10)</sup> For even when we were with you, this we commanded you, that if any would not work, neither should he eat.

</details>

### 17J.4 {#17J.4}

Slothfulness brings hardship and destruction.

[Prov 21:25](/bible/proverbs/21#v25)

<details><summary>Proverbs 21:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/21#v25)</sup> The desire of the slothful killeth him; for his hands refuse to labour.

</details>

### 17J.5 {#17J.5}

Slothfulness and idleness leads to want, poverty and hunger.

[Prov 6:6-11](/bible/proverbs/6#v6); [Prov 10:4](/bible/proverbs/10#v4); [Prov 13:4](/bible/proverbs/13#v4); [Prov 18:9](/bible/proverbs/18#v9); [Prov 19:15](/bible/proverbs/19#v15); [Prov 24:30-34](/bible/proverbs/24#v30); [Eccl 10:18](/bible/ecclesiastes/10#v18)

<details><summary>Proverbs 6:6-11</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/6#v6)</sup> Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/6#v7)</sup> Which having no guide, overseer, or ruler,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/6#v8)</sup> Provideth her meat in the summer, and gathereth her food in the harvest.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/6#v9)</sup> How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard? when wilt thou arise out of thy sleep?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/6#v10)</sup> Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/6#v11)</sup> So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth, and thy want as an armed man.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 10:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/10#v4)</sup> He becometh poor that dealeth with a slack hand: but the hand of the diligent maketh rich.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 13:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/13#v4)</sup> The soul of the sluggard desireth, and hath nothing: but the soul of the diligent shall be made fat.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 18:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/18#v9)</sup> He also that is slothful in his work is brother to him that is a great waster.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 19:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/19#v15)</sup> Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep; and an idle soul shall suffer hunger.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 24:30-34</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/24#v30)</sup> I went by the field of the slothful, and by the vineyard of the man void of understanding;
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/24#v31)</sup> And, lo, it was all grown over with thorns, and nettles had covered the face thereof, and the stone wall thereof was broken down.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/24#v32)</sup> Then I saw, and considered it well: I looked upon it, and received instruction.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/proverbs/24#v33)</sup> Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep:
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/proverbs/24#v34)</sup> So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth; and thy want as an armed man.

</details>
<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 10:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/ecclesiastes/10#v18)</sup> By much slothfulness the building decayeth; and through idleness of the hands the house droppeth through.

</details>

### 17J.6 {#17J.6}

Exhortations against slothfulness.

[Prov 6:4](/bible/proverbs/6#v4); [Prov 26:13-16](/bible/proverbs/26#v13); [Rom 13:11](/bible/romans/13#v11); [1 Thess 5:6](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v6); [Heb 6:12](/bible/hebrews/6#v12)

<details><summary>Proverbs 6:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/6#v4)</sup> Give not sleep to thine eyes, nor slumber to thine eyelids.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 26:13-16</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/26#v13)</sup> The slothful man saith, There is a lion in the way; a lion is in the streets.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/26#v14)</sup> As the door turneth upon his hinges, so doth the slothful upon his bed.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/26#v15)</sup> The slothful hideth his hand in his bosom; it grieveth him to bring it again to his mouth.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/26#v16)</sup> The sluggard is wiser in his own conceit than seven men that can render a reason.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 13:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/romans/13#v11)</sup> And that, knowing the time, that now it is high time to awake out of sleep: for now is our salvation nearer than when we believed.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 5:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v6)</sup> Therefore let us not sleep, as do others; but let us watch and be sober.

</details>
<details><summary>Hebrews 6:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/6#v12)</sup> That ye be not slothful, but followers of them who through faith and patience inherit the promises.

</details>
