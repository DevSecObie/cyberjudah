### 17A.1 {#17A.1}

Anger causes strife and transgression.

[Prov 15:18](/bible/proverbs/15#v18); [Prov 29:8,22](/bible/proverbs/29#v8); [Prov 30:33](/bible/proverbs/30#v33)

<details><summary>Proverbs 15:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/15#v18)</sup> A wrathful man stirreth up strife: but he that is slow to anger appeaseth strife.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 29:8,22</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/29#v8)</sup> Scornful men bring a city into a snare: but wise men turn away wrath.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/29#v22)</sup> An angry man stirreth up strife, and a furious man aboundeth in transgression.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 30:33</summary>

> <sup>[33](/bible/proverbs/30#v33)</sup> Surely the churning of milk bringeth forth butter, and the wringing of the nose bringeth forth blood: so the forcing of wrath bringeth forth strife.

</details>

### 17A.2 {#17A.2}

Be not hasty in your spirit to be angry (be slow to anger).

[Eccl 7:9](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v9); [Prov 15:18](/bible/proverbs/15#v18); [Prov 16:32](/bible/proverbs/16#v32); [James 1:19-20](/bible/james/1#v19)

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 7:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v9)</sup> Be not hasty in thy spirit to be angry: for anger resteth in the bosom of fools.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 15:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/15#v18)</sup> A wrathful man stirreth up strife: but he that is slow to anger appeaseth strife.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 16:32</summary>

> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/16#v32)</sup> He that is slow to anger is better than the mighty; and he that ruleth his spirit than he that taketh a city.

</details>
<details><summary>James 1:19-20</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/james/1#v19)</sup> Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/james/1#v20)</sup> For the wrath of man worketh not the righteousness of God.

</details>

### 17A.3 {#17A.3}

Anger without cause is condemned.

[Matt 5:22](/bible/matthew/5#v22)

<details><summary>Matthew 5:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/5#v22)</sup> But I say unto you, That whosoever is angry with his brother without a cause shall be in danger of the judgment: and whosoever shall say to his brother, Raca, shall be in danger of the council: but whosoever shall say, Thou fool, shall be in danger of hell fire.

</details>

### 17A.4 {#17A.4}

Wrath is characteristic of fools.

[Prov 12:16](/bible/proverbs/12#v16); [Prov 14:16,29](/bible/proverbs/14#v16); [Prov 27:3](/bible/proverbs/27#v3); [Eccl 7:9](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v9)

<details><summary>Proverbs 12:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/12#v16)</sup> A fool’s wrath is presently known: but a prudent man covereth shame.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 14:16,29</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/14#v16)</sup> A wise man feareth, and departeth from evil: but the fool rageth, and is confident.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/14#v29)</sup> He that is slow to wrath is of great understanding: but he that is hasty of spirit exalteth folly.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 27:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/27#v3)</sup> A stone is heavy, and the sand weighty; but a fool’s wrath is heavier than them both.

</details>
<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 7:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v9)</sup> Be not hasty in thy spirit to be angry: for anger resteth in the bosom of fools.

</details>

### 17A.5 {#17A.5}

[Ps 4:4](/bible/psalms/4#v4); [Eph 4:26](/bible/ephesians/4#v26)

<details><summary>Psalms 4:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/4#v4)</sup> Stand in awe, and sin not: commune with your own heart upon your bed, and be still. Selah.

</details>
<details><summary>Ephesians 4:26</summary>

> <sup>[26](/bible/ephesians/4#v26)</sup> Be ye angry, and sin not: let not the sun go down upon your wrath:

</details>

<details><summary>Genesis 49:7</summary>

<details><summary>Job 5:2</summary>

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 19:19</summary>

</details>
