### 9D.1 {#9D.1}

Six years you shall sow the land and reap its produce, but the seventh year you shall let the land lie fallow.

[Exod 23:10-11](/bible/exodus/23#v10); [Lev 25:2-4](/bible/leviticus/25#v2)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:10-11</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/23#v10)</sup> And six years thou shalt sow thy land, and shalt gather in the fruits thereof:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/23#v11)</sup> But the seventh year thou shalt let it rest and lie still; that the poor of thy people may eat: and what they leave the beasts of the field shall eat. In like manner thou shalt deal with thy vineyard, and with thy oliveyard.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 25:2-4</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/25#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye come into the land which I give you, then shall the land keep a sabbath unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/25#v3)</sup> Six years thou shalt sow thy field, and six years thou shalt prune thy vineyard, and gather in the fruit thereof;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/25#v4)</sup> But in the seventh year shall be a sabbath of rest unto the land, a sabbath for the Lord: thou shalt neither sow thy field, nor prune thy vineyard.

</details>

### 9D.2 {#9D.2}

Every seventh year to be a Sabbath of rest for the land, you shall neither sow your field nor prune your vineyard.

[Lev 25:4](/bible/leviticus/25#v4)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/25#v4)</sup> But in the seventh year shall be a sabbath of rest unto the land, a sabbath for the Lord: thou shalt neither sow thy field, nor prune thy vineyard.

</details>

### 9D.3 {#9D.3}

In the year of the land Sabbath you are not to harvest those crops which grow by its own accord.

[Lev 25:5](/bible/leviticus/25#v5)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/25#v5)</sup> That which groweth of its own accord of thy harvest thou shalt not reap, neither gather the grapes of thy vine undressed: for it is a year of rest unto the land.

</details>

### 9D.4 {#9D.4}

The food which grows of itself can be taken by the poor to eat during the land Sabbath.

[Exod 23:11](/bible/exodus/23#v11)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/23#v11)</sup> But the seventh year thou shalt let it rest and lie still; that the poor of thy people may eat: and what they leave the beasts of the field shall eat. In like manner thou shalt deal with thy vineyard, and with thy oliveyard.

</details>

### 9D.5 {#9D.5}

Food requirement for the year of the land Sabbath and following year will be provided by God with an abundant crop in the sixth year.

[Lev 25:20-22](/bible/leviticus/25#v20)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:20-22</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/25#v20)</sup> And if ye shall say, What shall we eat the seventh year? behold, we shall not sow, nor gather in our increase:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/25#v21)</sup> Then I will command my blessing upon you in the sixth year, and it shall bring forth fruit for three years.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/25#v22)</sup> And ye shall sow the eighth year, and eat yet of old fruit until the ninth year; until her fruits come in ye shall eat of the old store.

</details>

### 9D.6 {#9D.6}

The land and its fruits are holy to God, and therefore should not be abused.

[Lev 27:30](/bible/leviticus/27#v30); [Ezek 45:1,4](/bible/ezekiel/45#v1); [Zech 2:12](/bible/zechariah/2#v12)

<details><summary>Leviticus 27:30</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/leviticus/27#v30)</sup> And all the tithe of the land, whether of the seed of the land, or of the fruit of the tree, is the Lord’s: it is holy unto the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>Ezekiel 45:1,4</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/45#v1)</sup> Moreover, when ye shall divide by lot the land for inheritance, ye shall offer an oblation unto the Lord, an holy portion of the land: the length shall be the length of five and twenty thousand reeds, and the breadth shall be ten thousand. This shall be holy in all the borders thereof round about.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/45#v4)</sup> The holy portion of the land shall be for the priests the ministers of the sanctuary, which shall come near to minister unto the Lord: and it shall be a place for their houses, and an holy place for the sanctuary.

</details>
<details><summary>Zechariah 2:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/zechariah/2#v12)</sup> And the Lord shall inherit Judah his portion in the holy land, and shall choose Jerusalem again.

</details>
