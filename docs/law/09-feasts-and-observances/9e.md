### 9E.1 {#9E.1}

A Jubilee is to be observed at the end of every seven land Sabbaths (every fifty years).

[Lev 25:8](/bible/leviticus/25#v8)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/25#v8)</sup> And thou shalt number seven sabbaths of years unto thee, seven times seven years; and the space of the seven sabbaths of years shall be unto thee forty and nine years.

</details>

### 9E.2 {#9E.2}

The fiftieth year or the Jubilee is to be hallowed and to be a year of liberty and release throughout the land.

[Lev 25:10](/bible/leviticus/25#v10)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/25#v10)</sup> And ye shall hallow the fiftieth year, and proclaim liberty throughout all the land unto all the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a jubile unto you; and ye shall return every man unto his possession, and ye shall return every man unto his family.

</details>

### 9E.3 {#9E.3}

You shall not sow or reap on the Jubilee year but eat the increase gathered in previous years.

[Lev 25:11-12,18-22](/bible/leviticus/25#v11)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:11-12,18-22</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/25#v11)</sup> A jubile shall that fiftieth year be unto you: ye shall not sow, neither reap that which groweth of itself in it, nor gather the grapes in it of thy vine undressed.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/25#v12)</sup> For it is the jubile; it shall be holy unto you: ye shall eat the increase thereof out of the field.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/25#v18)</sup> Wherefore ye shall do my statutes, and keep my judgments, and do them; and ye shall dwell in the land in safety.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/25#v19)</sup> And the land shall yield her fruit, and ye shall eat your fill, and dwell therein in safety.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/25#v20)</sup> And if ye shall say, What shall we eat the seventh year? behold, we shall not sow, nor gather in our increase:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/25#v21)</sup> Then I will command my blessing upon you in the sixth year, and it shall bring forth fruit for three years.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/25#v22)</sup> And ye shall sow the eighth year, and eat yet of old fruit until the ninth year; until her fruits come in ye shall eat of the old store.

</details>

### 9E.4 {#9E.4}

The Jubilee to be holy to you.

[Lev 25:12](/bible/leviticus/25#v12)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/25#v12)</sup> For it is the jubile; it shall be holy unto you: ye shall eat the increase thereof out of the field.

</details>

### 9E.5 {#9E.5}

On the year of the Jubilee you shall return to every man his property, the field shall return to the one from whom it was purchased, to whom the possession of the land belongs.

[Lev 25:10,13](/bible/leviticus/25#v10); [Lev 27:24](/bible/leviticus/27#v24)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:10,13</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/25#v10)</sup> And ye shall hallow the fiftieth year, and proclaim liberty throughout all the land unto all the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a jubile unto you; and ye shall return every man unto his possession, and ye shall return every man unto his family.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/25#v13)</sup> In the year of this jubile ye shall return every man unto his possession.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 27:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/27#v24)</sup> In the year of the jubile the field shall return unto him of whom it was bought, even to him to whom the possession of the land did belong.

</details>

### 9E.6 {#9E.6}

All those in service to another are to be released from their contract or indenture of service, and allowed to return to his family.

[Lev 25:10,40-41](/bible/leviticus/25#v10)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:10,40-41</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/25#v10)</sup> And ye shall hallow the fiftieth year, and proclaim liberty throughout all the land unto all the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a jubile unto you; and ye shall return every man unto his possession, and ye shall return every man unto his family.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/leviticus/25#v40)</sup> But as an hired servant, and as a sojourner, he shall be with thee, and shall serve thee unto the year of jubile:
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/leviticus/25#v41)</sup> And then shall he depart from thee, both he and his children with him, and shall return unto his own family, and unto the possession of his fathers shall he return.

</details>

### 9E.7 {#9E.7}

The Jubilee to start and be proclaimed on the Day of Atonement.

[Lev 25:9](/bible/leviticus/25#v9)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/25#v9)</sup> Then shalt thou cause the trumpet of the jubile to sound on the tenth day of the seventh month, in the day of atonement shall ye make the trumpet sound throughout all your land.

</details>
