### 9B.1 {#9B.1}

Anointing (inauguration ceremony) for new civil leader coming into office.

[1 Sam 10:1](/bible/1-samuel/10#v1); [1 Sam 16:12-13](/bible/1-samuel/16#v12); [1 Kgs 1:39](/bible/1-kings/1#v39)

<details><summary>1 Samuel 10:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-samuel/10#v1)</sup> Then Samuel took a vial of oil, and poured it upon his head, and kissed him, and said, Is it not because the Lord hath anointed thee to be captain over his inheritance?

</details>
<details><summary>1 Samuel 16:12-13</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-samuel/16#v12)</sup> And he sent, and brought him in. Now he was ruddy, and withal of a beautiful countenance, and goodly to look to. And the Lord said, Arise, anoint him: for this is he.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-samuel/16#v13)</sup> Then Samuel took the horn of oil, and anointed him in the midst of his brethren: and the Spirit of the Lord came upon David from that day forward. So Samuel rose up, and went to Ramah.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Kings 1:39</summary>

> <sup>[39](/bible/1-kings/1#v39)</sup> And Zadok the priest took an horn of oil out of the tabernacle, and anointed Solomon. And they blew the trumpet; and all the people said, God save king Solomon.

</details>

### 9B.2 {#9B.2}

The first born of man or animal to be dedicated to God.

[Exod 13:2,12](/bible/exodus/13#v2); [Exod 34:19-20](/bible/exodus/34#v19)

<details><summary>Exodus 13:2,12</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/13#v2)</sup> Sanctify unto me all the firstborn, whatsoever openeth the womb among the children of Israel, both of man and of beast: it is mine.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/13#v12)</sup> That thou shalt set apart unto the Lord all that openeth the matrix, and every firstling that cometh of a beast which thou hast; the males shall be the Lord’s.

</details>
<details><summary>Exodus 34:19-20</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/34#v19)</sup> All that openeth the matrix is mine; and every firstling among thy cattle, whether ox or sheep, that is male.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/34#v20)</sup> But the firstling of an ass thou shalt redeem with a lamb: and if thou redeem him not, then shalt thou break his neck. All the firstborn of thy sons thou shalt redeem. And none shall appear before me empty.

</details>

### 9B.3 {#9B.3}

Three times in a year all males shall appear before the God of Israel, and make an offering to provide for the feasts.

[Exod 23:14-17](/bible/exodus/23#v14); [Exod 34:23](/bible/exodus/34#v23); [Deut 16:16](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v16)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:14-17</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/23#v14)</sup> Three times thou shalt keep a feast unto me in the year.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/23#v15)</sup> Thou shalt keep the feast of unleavened bread: (thou shalt eat unleavened bread seven days, as I commanded thee, in the time appointed of the month Abib; for in it thou camest out from Egypt: and none shall appear before me empty:)
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/23#v16)</sup> And the feast of harvest, the firstfruits of thy labours, which thou hast sown in the field: and the feast of ingathering, which is in the end of the year, when thou hast gathered in thy labours out of the field.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/23#v17)</sup> Three times in the year all thy males shall appear before the Lord God.

</details>
<details><summary>Exodus 34:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/34#v23)</sup> Thrice in the year shall all your men children appear before the Lord God, the God of Israel.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 16:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v16)</sup> Three times in a year shall all thy males appear before the Lord thy God in the place which he shall choose; in the feast of unleavened bread, and in the feast of weeks, and in the feast of tabernacles: and they shall not appear before the Lord empty:

</details>

### 9B.4 {#9B.4}

Be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins.

[Acts 2:38](/bible/acts/2#v38); [Acts 8:12](/bible/acts/8#v12)

<details><summary>Acts 2:38</summary>

> <sup>[38](/bible/acts/2#v38)</sup> Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.

</details>
<details><summary>Acts 8:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/acts/8#v12)</sup> But when they believed Philip preaching the things concerning the kingdom of God, and the name of Jesus Christ, they were baptized, both men and women.

</details>

### 9B.5 {#9B.5}

Feasts to be published and proclaimed in all the cities and towns in the land.

[Neh 8:15](/bible/nehemiah/8#v15)

<details><summary>Nehemiah 8:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/nehemiah/8#v15)</sup> And that they should publish and proclaim in all their cities, and in Jerusalem, saying, Go forth unto the mount, and fetch olive branches, and pine branches, and myrtle branches, and palm branches, and branches of thick trees, to make booths, as it is written.

</details>

### 9B.6 {#9B.6}

Partaking of Communion in remembrance of Christ and in celebration of the Lord's supper.

[Matt 26:26-29](/bible/matthew/26#v26); [Mark 14:22-25](/bible/mark/14#v22); [Luke 22:17-19](/bible/luke/22#v17); [1 Cor 10:16](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v16); [1 Cor 11:23-26](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v23)

<details><summary>Matthew 26:26-29</summary>

> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/26#v26)</sup> And as they were eating, Jesus took bread, and blessed it, and brake it, and gave it to the disciples, and said, Take, eat; this is my body.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/26#v27)</sup> And he took the cup, and gave thanks, and gave it to them, saying, Drink ye all of it;
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/26#v28)</sup> For this is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many for the remission of sins.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/26#v29)</sup> But I say unto you, I will not drink henceforth of this fruit of the vine, until that day when I drink it new with you in my Father’s kingdom.

</details>
<details><summary>Mark 14:22-25</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/mark/14#v22)</sup> And as they did eat, Jesus took bread, and blessed, and brake it, and gave to them, and said, Take, eat: this is my body.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/mark/14#v23)</sup> And he took the cup, and when he had given thanks, he gave it to them: and they all drank of it.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/mark/14#v24)</sup> And he said unto them, This is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/mark/14#v25)</sup> Verily I say unto you, I will drink no more of the fruit of the vine, until that day that I drink it new in the kingdom of God.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 22:17-19</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/luke/22#v17)</sup> And he took the cup, and gave thanks, and said, Take this, and divide it among yourselves:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/luke/22#v18)</sup> For I say unto you, I will not drink of the fruit of the vine, until the kingdom of God shall come.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/luke/22#v19)</sup> And he took bread, and gave thanks, and brake it, and gave unto them, saying, This is my body which is given for you: this do in remembrance of me.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 10:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v16)</sup> The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not the communion of the blood of Christ? The bread which we break, is it not the communion of the body of Christ?

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 11:23-26</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v23)</sup> For I have received of the Lord that which also I delivered unto you, That the Lord Jesus the same night in which he was betrayed took bread:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v24)</sup> And when he had given thanks, he brake it, and said, Take, eat: this is my body, which is broken for you: this do in remembrance of me.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v25)</sup> After the same manner also he took the cup, when he had supped, saying, This cup is the new testament in my blood: this do ye, as oft as ye drink it, in remembrance of me.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v26)</sup> For as often as ye eat this bread, and drink this cup, ye do shew the Lord’s death till he come.

</details>
