### 22C.1 {#22C.1}

Those who have built a new house and have not yet dedicated it may be deferred from service.

[Deut 20:5](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v5)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 20:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v5)</sup> And the officers shall speak unto the people, saying, What man is there that hath built a new house, and hath not dedicated it? let him go and return to his house, lest he die in the battle, and another man dedicate it.

</details>

### 22C.2 {#22C.2}

Those who have planted a crop but have not yet reaped its fruits can be deferred from service.

[Deut 20:6](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v6)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 20:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v6)</sup> And what man is he that hath planted a vineyard, and hath not yet eaten of it? let him also go and return unto his house, lest he die in the battle, and another man eat of it.

</details>

### 22C.3 {#22C.3}

Those that are fearful of battle or are faint-hearted are to be exempt from service.

[Deut 20:8](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v8)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 20:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v8)</sup> And the officers shall speak further unto the people, and they shall say, What man is there that is fearful and fainthearted? let him go and return unto his house, lest his brethren’s heart faint as well as his heart.

</details>

### 22C.4 {#22C.4}

A man that has been married less than one year is not to go to war or be charged with any military duties.

[Deut 20:7](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v7); [Deut 24:5](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v5)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 20:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v7)</sup> And what man is there that hath betrothed a wife, and hath not taken her? let him go and return unto his house, lest he die in the battle, and another man take her.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 24:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v5)</sup> When a man hath taken a new wife, he shall not go out to war, neither shall he be charged with any business: but he shall be free at home one year, and shall cheer up his wife which he hath taken.

</details>

### 22C.5 {#22C.5}

Ministers are not eligible for military service.

[Num 1:47](/bible/numbers/1#v47)

<details><summary>Numbers 1:47</summary>

> <sup>[47](/bible/numbers/1#v47)</sup> But the Levites after the tribe of their fathers were not numbered among them.

</details>
