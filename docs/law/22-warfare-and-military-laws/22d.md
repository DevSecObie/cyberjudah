### 22D.1 {#22D.1}

When faced with the alternative of battle we are not to be afraid of strong enemies.

[Deut 20:1](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v1); [Josh 11:6](/bible/joshua/11#v6); [Ps 27:3](/bible/psalms/27#v3)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 20:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v1)</sup> When thou goest out to battle against thine enemies, and seest horses, and chariots, and a people more than thou, be not afraid of them: for the Lord thy God is with thee, which brought thee up out of the land of Egypt.

</details>
<details><summary>Joshua 11:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/11#v6)</sup> And the Lord said unto Joshua, Be not afraid because of them: for to morrow about this time will I deliver them up all slain before Israel: thou shalt hough their horses, and burn their chariots with fire.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 27:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/27#v3)</sup> Though an host should encamp against me, my heart shall not fear: though war should rise against me, in this will I be confident.

</details>

### 22D.2 {#22D.2}

Enemies are to be given an opportunity to surrender in peace before engaging in battle.

[Deut 20:10](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v10)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 20:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v10)</sup> When thou comest nigh unto a city to fight against it, then proclaim peace unto it.

</details>

### 22D.3 {#22D.3}

Enemies that surrender peaceable should be put under tribute but not killed.

[Deut 20:11](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v11)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 20:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v11)</sup> And it shall be, if it make thee answer of peace, and open unto thee, then it shall be, that all the people that is found therein shall be tributaries unto thee, and they shall serve thee.

</details>

### 22D.4 {#22D.4}

If an enemy city or territory will not surrender in peace, but wills to war against us, we are then to go to war against it and besieged the it.

[Deut 20:12](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v12); [Josh 11:19-20](/bible/joshua/11#v19)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 20:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v12)</sup> And if it will make no peace with thee, but will make war against thee, then thou shalt besiege it:

</details>
<details><summary>Joshua 11:19-20</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/joshua/11#v19)</sup> There was not a city that made peace with the children of Israel, save the Hivites the inhabitants of Gibeon: all other they took in battle.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/joshua/11#v20)</sup> For it was of the Lord to harden their hearts, that they should come against Israel in battle, that he might destroy them utterly, and that they might have no favour, but that he might destroy them, as the Lord commanded Moses.

</details>

### 22D.5 {#22D.5}

We are not to destroy enemies taken captive or conquered but rather provide for them.

[2 Kgs 6:22-23](/bible/2-kings/6#v22)

<details><summary>2 Kings 6:22-23</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/2-kings/6#v22)</sup> And he answered, Thou shalt not smite them: wouldest thou smite those whom thou hast taken captive with thy sword and with thy bow? set bread and water before them, that they may eat and drink, and go to their master.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-kings/6#v23)</sup> And he prepared great provision for them: and when they had eaten and drunk, he sent them away, and they went to their master. So the bands of Syria came no more into the land of Israel.

</details>

### 22D.8 {#22D.8}

We are not to have pity or mercy on heathen or ungodly enemies, but are to destroy all those who God shall deliver to us.

[Deut 7:2,16](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v2)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 7:2,16</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v2)</sup> And when the Lord thy God shall deliver them before thee; thou shalt smite them, and utterly destroy them; thou shalt make no covenant with them, nor shew mercy unto them:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v16)</sup> And thou shalt consume all the people which the Lord thy God shall deliver thee; thine eye shall have no pity upon them: neither shalt thou serve their gods; for that will be a snare unto thee.

</details>

### 22D.10 {#22D.10}

All males of heathen nations are to be killed.

[Num 31:7,17](/bible/numbers/31#v7); [Deut 20:13](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v13); [Josh 11:14](/bible/joshua/11#v14)

<details><summary>Numbers 31:7,17</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/31#v7)</sup> And they warred against the Midianites, as the Lord commanded Moses; and they slew all the males.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/31#v17)</sup> Now therefore kill every male among the little ones, and kill every woman that hath known man by lying with him.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 20:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v13)</sup> And when the Lord thy God hath delivered it into thine hands, thou shalt smite every male thereof with the edge of the sword:

</details>
<details><summary>Joshua 11:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/joshua/11#v14)</sup> And all the spoil of these cities, and the cattle, the children of Israel took for a prey unto themselves; but every man they smote with the edge of the sword, until they had destroyed them, neither left they any to breathe.

</details>
