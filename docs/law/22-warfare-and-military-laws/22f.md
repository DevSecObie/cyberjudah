### 22F.1 {#22F.1}

There is a time for war and a time for peace.

[Eccl 3:8](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v8)

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 3:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v8)</sup> A time to love, and a time to hate; a time of war, and a time of peace.

</details>

### 22F.2 {#22F.2}

If you walk in God's statutes and keep His commandments, and do them, He will give peace in the land, and none shall make you afraid.

[Lev 26:3,6](/bible/leviticus/26#v3); [Ps 119:165](/bible/psalms/119#v165); [Prov 3:1-2](/bible/proverbs/3#v1); [Prov 16:7](/bible/proverbs/16#v7); [Isa 32:17-18](/bible/isaiah/32#v17); [Isa 48:18](/bible/isaiah/48#v18)

<details><summary>Leviticus 26:3,6</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/26#v3)</sup> If ye walk in my statutes, and keep my commandments, and do them;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/26#v6)</sup> And I will give peace in the land, and ye shall lie down, and none shall make you afraid: and I will rid evil beasts out of the land, neither shall the sword go through your land.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 119:165</summary>

> <sup>[165](/bible/psalms/119#v165)</sup> Great peace have they which love thy law: and nothing shall offend them.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 3:1-2</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/3#v1)</sup> My son, forget not my law; but let thine heart keep my commandments:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/3#v2)</sup> For length of days, and long life, and peace, shall they add to thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 16:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/16#v7)</sup> When a man’s ways please the Lord, he maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him.

</details>
<details><summary>Isaiah 32:17-18</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/32#v17)</sup> And the work of righteousness shall be peace; and the effect of righteousness quietness and assurance for ever.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/32#v18)</sup> And my people shall dwell in a peaceable habitation, and in sure dwellings, and in quiet resting places;

</details>
<details><summary>Isaiah 48:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/48#v18)</sup> O that thou hadst hearkened to my commandments! then had thy peace been as a river, and thy righteousness as the waves of the sea:

</details>

### 22F.3 {#22F.3}

Victory against enemies in war is from God.

[Deut 7:23](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v23); [Deut 20:4](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v4); [Josh 10:8-11](/bible/joshua/10#v8)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 7:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v23)</sup> But the Lord thy God shall deliver them unto thee, and shall destroy them with a mighty destruction, until they be destroyed.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 20:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v4)</sup> For the Lord your God is he that goeth with you, to fight for you against your enemies, to save you.

</details>
<details><summary>Joshua 10:8-11</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/10#v8)</sup> And the Lord said unto Joshua, Fear them not: for I have delivered them into thine hand; there shall not a man of them stand before thee.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/10#v9)</sup> Joshua therefore came unto them suddenly, and went up from Gilgal all night.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/joshua/10#v10)</sup> And the Lord discomfited them before Israel, and slew them with a great slaughter at Gibeon, and chased them along the way that goeth up to Beth–horon, and smote them to Azekah, and unto Makkedah.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/joshua/10#v11)</sup> And it came to pass, as they fled from before Israel, and were in the going down to Beth–horon, that the Lord cast down great stones from heaven upon them unto Azekah, and they died: they were more which died with hailstones than they whom the children of Israel slew with the sword.

</details>

</details>

>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/26#v3)</sup> Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/26#v12)</sup> Lord, thou wilt ordain peace for us: for thou also hast wrought all our works in us.

### 22F.5 {#22F.5}

The fear of God in our enemies can prevent war.

[2 Chr 17:10](/bible/2-chronicles/17#v10); [2 Chr 20:29-30](/bible/2-chronicles/20#v29)

<details><summary>2 Chronicles 17:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/17#v10)</sup> And the fear of the Lord fell upon all the kingdoms of the lands that were round about Judah, so that they made no war against Jehoshaphat.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Chronicles 20:29-30</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/2-chronicles/20#v29)</sup> And the fear of God was on all the kingdoms of those countries, when they had heard that the Lord fought against the enemies of Israel.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/2-chronicles/20#v30)</sup> So the realm of Jehoshaphat was quiet: for his God gave him rest round about.

</details>

### 22F.10 {#22F.10}

<details><summary>Exodus 23:22,27</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/23#v22)</sup> But if thou shalt indeed obey his voice, and do all that I speak; then I will be an enemy unto thine enemies, and an adversary unto thine adversaries.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/exodus/23#v27)</sup> I will send my fear before thee, and will destroy all the people to whom thou shalt come, and I will make all thine enemies turn their backs unto thee.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Kings 8:46-50</summary>

> <sup>[46](/bible/1-kings/8#v46)</sup> If they sin against thee, (for there is no man that sinneth not,) and thou be angry with them, and deliver them to the enemy, so that they carry them away captives unto the land of the enemy, far or near;
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/1-kings/8#v47)</sup> Yet if they shall bethink themselves in the land whither they were carried captives, and repent, and make supplication unto thee in the land of them that carried them captives, saying, We have sinned, and have done perversely, we have committed wickedness;
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/1-kings/8#v48)</sup> And so return unto thee with all their heart, and with all their soul, in the land of their enemies, which led them away captive, and pray unto thee toward their land, which thou gavest unto their fathers, the city which thou hast chosen, and the house which I have built for thy name:
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/1-kings/8#v49)</sup> Then hear thou their prayer and their supplication in heaven thy dwelling place, and maintain their cause,
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/1-kings/8#v50)</sup> And forgive thy people that have sinned against thee, and all their transgressions wherein they have transgressed against thee, and give them compassion before them who carried them captive, that they may have compassion on them:

<details><summary>2 Chronicles 6:24-25,36-39</summary>

> <sup>[36](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v36)</sup> If they sin against thee, (for there is no man which sinneth not,) and thou be angry with them, and deliver them over before their enemies, and they carry them away captives unto a land far off or near;
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v37)</sup> Yet if they bethink themselves in the land whither they are carried captive, and turn and pray unto thee in the land of their captivity, saying, We have sinned, we have done amiss, and have dealt wickedly;
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v38)</sup> If they return to thee with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their captivity, whither they have carried them captives, and pray toward their land, which thou gavest unto their fathers, and toward the city which thou hast chosen, and toward the house which I have built for thy name:
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v39)</sup> Then hear thou from the heavens, even from thy dwelling place, their prayer and their supplications, and maintain their cause, and forgive thy people which have sinned against thee.

</details>
