### 19D.1 {#19D.1}

Death for murder.

[Exod 21:12](/bible/exodus/21#v12); [Lev 24:21](/bible/leviticus/24#v21); [Num 35:16-21,309](/bible/numbers/35#v16); [Ezra 7:26](/bible/ezra/7#v26)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/21#v12)</sup> He that smiteth a man, so that he die, shall be surely put to death.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 24:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/24#v21)</sup> And he that killeth a beast, he shall restore it: and he that killeth a man, he shall be put to death.

</details>
<details><summary>Numbers 35:16-21,309</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/35#v16)</sup> And if he smite him with an instrument of iron, so that he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/35#v17)</sup> And if he smite him with throwing a stone, wherewith he may die, and he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/35#v18)</sup> Or if he smite him with an hand weapon of wood, wherewith he may die, and he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/35#v19)</sup> The revenger of blood himself shall slay the murderer: when he meeteth him, he shall slay him.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/35#v20)</sup> But if he thrust him of hatred, or hurl at him by laying of wait, that he die;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/35#v21)</sup> Or in enmity smite him with his hand, that he die: he that smote him shall surely be put to death; for he is a murderer: the revenger of blood shall slay the murderer, when he meeteth him.

</details>
<details><summary>Ezra 7:26</summary>

> <sup>[26](/bible/ezra/7#v26)</sup> And whosoever will not do the law of thy God, and the law of the king, let judgment be executed speedily upon him, whether it be unto death, or to banishment, or to confiscation of goods, or to imprisonment.

</details>

### 19D.2 {#19D.2}

Scourging or whipping (not to exceed forty stripes).

[Lev 19:20](/bible/leviticus/19#v20); [Deut 25:1-3](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v1)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/19#v20)</sup> And whosoever lieth carnally with a woman, that is a bondmaid, betrothed to an husband, and not at all redeemed, nor freedom given her; she shall be scourged; they shall not be put to death, because she was not free.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 25:1-3</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v1)</sup> If there be a controversy between men, and they come unto judgment, that the judges may judge them; then they shall justify the righteous, and condemn the wicked.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v2)</sup> And it shall be, if the wicked man be worthy to be beaten, that the judge shall cause him to lie down, and to be beaten before his face, according to his fault, by a certain number.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v3)</sup> Forty stripes he may give him, and not exceed: lest, if he should exceed, and beat him above these with many stripes, then thy brother should seem vile unto thee.

</details>

### 19D.10 {#19D.10}

Compensation and restitution for lost time, property damage or injury.

[Exod 21:18-19,22,32-36](/bible/exodus/21#v18); [Exod 22:12-15](/bible/exodus/22#v12)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:18-19,22,32-36</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/21#v18)</sup> And if men strive together, and one smite another with a stone, or with his fist, and he die not, but keepeth his bed:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/21#v19)</sup> If he rise again, and walk abroad upon his staff, then shall he that smote him be quit: only he shall pay for the loss of his time, and shall cause him to be thoroughly healed.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/21#v22)</sup> If men strive, and hurt a woman with child, so that her fruit depart from her, and yet no mischief follow: he shall be surely punished, according as the woman’s husband will lay upon him; and he shall pay as the judges determine.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/exodus/21#v32)</sup> If the ox shall push a manservant or a maidservant; he shall give unto their master thirty shekels of silver, and the ox shall be stoned.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/exodus/21#v33)</sup> And if a man shall open a pit, or if a man shall dig a pit, and not cover it, and an ox or an ass fall therein;
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/exodus/21#v34)</sup> The owner of the pit shall make it good, and give money unto the owner of them; and the dead beast shall be his.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/exodus/21#v35)</sup> And if one man’s ox hurt another’s, that he die; then they shall sell the live ox, and divide the money of it; and the dead ox also they shall divide.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/exodus/21#v36)</sup> Or if it be known that the ox hath used to push in time past, and his owner hath not kept him in; he shall surely pay ox for ox; and the dead shall be his own.

</details>
<details><summary>Exodus 22:12-15</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/22#v12)</sup> And if it be stolen from him, he shall make restitution unto the owner thereof.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/22#v13)</sup> If it be torn in pieces, then let him bring it for witness, and he shall not make good that which was torn.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/22#v14)</sup> And if a man borrow ought of his neighbour, and it be hurt, or die, the owner thereof being not with it, he shall surely make it good.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/22#v15)</sup> But if the owner thereof be with it, he shall not make it good: if it be an hired thing, it came for his hire.

</details>
