### 19C.1 {#19C.1}

The restitution and punishment should be according to the crime--Life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, wound for wound, stripe for stripe.

[Exod 21:23-25](/bible/exodus/21#v23); [Lev 24:17-20](/bible/leviticus/24#v17); [Deut 19:21](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v21)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:23-25</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/21#v23)</sup> And if any mischief follow, then thou shalt give life for life,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/21#v24)</sup> Eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/21#v25)</sup> Burning for burning, wound for wound, stripe for stripe.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 24:17-20</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/24#v17)</sup> And he that killeth any man shall surely be put to death.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/24#v18)</sup> And he that killeth a beast shall make it good; beast for beast.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/24#v19)</sup> And if a man cause a blemish in his neighbour; as he hath done, so shall it be done to him;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/24#v20)</sup> Breach for breach, eye for eye, tooth for tooth: as he hath caused a blemish in a man, so shall it be done to him again.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 19:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v21)</sup> And thine eye shall not pity; but life shall go for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.

</details>

### 19C.2 {#19C.2}

Stolen livestock which is slaughtered is to be repaid four or five times.

[Exod 22:1](/bible/exodus/22#v1)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/22#v1)</sup> If a man shall steal an ox, or a sheep, and kill it, or sell it; he shall restore five oxen for an ox, and four sheep for a sheep.

</details>

### 19C.3 {#19C.3}

Stolen livestock which is found alive in the thief's possession is to be repaid double.

[Exod 22:4](/bible/exodus/22#v4)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/22#v4)</sup> If the theft be certainly found in his hand alive, whether it be ox, or ass, or sheep; he shall restore double.

</details>

### 19C.4 {#19C.4}

If one man's animal kills another man's animal, then the live animal is to be sold and the money equally divided between them.

[Exod 21:35](/bible/exodus/21#v35)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:35</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/exodus/21#v35)</sup> And if one man’s ox hurt another’s, that he die; then they shall sell the live ox, and divide the money of it; and the dead ox also they shall divide.

</details>

### 19C.5 {#19C.5}

If property or money is left in another's possession and is stolen, but the thief is not caught, then the judges will determine if the items were stolen by the one in possession of the goods. If stolen by the one in possession then he will pay double.

[Exod 22:7-9](/bible/exodus/22#v7)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:7-9</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/22#v7)</sup> If a man shall deliver unto his neighbour money or stuff to keep, and it be stolen out of the man’s house; if the thief be found, let him pay double.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/22#v8)</sup> If the thief be not found, then the master of the house shall be brought unto the judges, to see whether he have put his hand unto his neighbour’s goods.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/22#v9)</sup> For all manner of trespass, whether it be for ox, for ass, for sheep, for raiment, or for any manner of lost thing, which another challengeth to be his, the cause of both parties shall come before the judges; and whom the judges shall condemn, he shall pay double unto his neighbour.

</details>

### 19C.6 {#19C.6}

Fines are to be assessed by judges for some personal injuries.

[Exod 21:22](/bible/exodus/21#v22)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/21#v22)</sup> If men strive, and hurt a woman with child, so that her fruit depart from her, and yet no mischief follow: he shall be surely punished, according as the woman’s husband will lay upon him; and he shall pay as the judges determine.

</details>

### 19C.7 {#19C.7}

If a man causes injury or blemish to another, he shall have the same injury done to him.

[Lev 24:19-20](/bible/leviticus/24#v19); [Deut 19:21](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v21)

<details><summary>Leviticus 24:19-20</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/24#v19)</sup> And if a man cause a blemish in his neighbour; as he hath done, so shall it be done to him;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/24#v20)</sup> Breach for breach, eye for eye, tooth for tooth: as he hath caused a blemish in a man, so shall it be done to him again.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 19:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v21)</sup> And thine eye shall not pity; but life shall go for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.

</details>

### 19C.8 {#19C.8}

If a thief is caught breaking in a building and is killed, the killer is not guilty of the crime of murder.

[Exod 22:2](/bible/exodus/22#v2)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/22#v2)</sup> If a thief be found breaking up, and be smitten that he die, there shall no blood be shed for him.

</details>

### 19C.9 {#19C.9}

If two men are quarreling and one injures the other, he shall compensate the injured party for his loss of time and shall provide for him until he is completely healed.

[Exod 21:18-19](/bible/exodus/21#v18)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:18-19</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/21#v18)</sup> And if men strive together, and one smite another with a stone, or with his fist, and he die not, but keepeth his bed:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/21#v19)</sup> If he rise again, and walk abroad upon his staff, then shall he that smote him be quit: only he shall pay for the loss of his time, and shall cause him to be thoroughly healed.

</details>

### 19C.10 {#19C.10}

A thief shall pay double for stolen property.

[Exod 22:7,9](/bible/exodus/22#v7)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:7,9</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/22#v7)</sup> If a man shall deliver unto his neighbour money or stuff to keep, and it be stolen out of the man’s house; if the thief be found, let him pay double.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/22#v9)</sup> For all manner of trespass, whether it be for ox, for ass, for sheep, for raiment, or for any manner of lost thing, which another challengeth to be his, the cause of both parties shall come before the judges; and whom the judges shall condemn, he shall pay double unto his neighbour.

</details>

### 19C.11 {#19C.11}

Restitution is required for acts done out of ignorance.

[Lev 5:15-16](/bible/leviticus/5#v15)

<details><summary>Leviticus 5:15-16</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/5#v15)</sup> If a soul commit a trespass, and sin through ignorance, in the holy things of the Lord; then he shall bring for his trespass unto the Lord a ram without blemish out of the flocks, with thy estimation by shekels of silver, after the shekel of the sanctuary, for a trespass offering:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/5#v16)</sup> And he shall make amends for the harm that he hath done in the holy thing, and shall add the fifth part thereto, and give it unto the priest: and the priest shall make an atonement for him with the ram of the trespass offering, and it shall be forgiven him.

</details>

### 19C.12 {#19C.12}

If a man delivers an animal to his neighbor to keep for him and it is injured or dies while in his possession, he shall make full restitution . But if the owner is with the animal, he shall not make restitution.

[Exod 22:14](/bible/exodus/22#v14)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/22#v14)</sup> And if a man borrow ought of his neighbour, and it be hurt, or die, the owner thereof being not with it, he shall surely make it good.

</details>

### 19C.13 {#19C.13}

If a man lets his animal loose so that it grazes in another man's field, he shall make restitution from the best of his own field.

[Exod 22:5](/bible/exodus/22#v5)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/22#v5)</sup> If a man shall cause a field or vineyard to be eaten, and shall put in his beast, and shall feed in another man’s field; of the best of his own field, and of the best of his own vineyard, shall he make restitution.

</details>

### 19C.14 {#19C.14}

Whoever kills another's animal shall make it good, animal for animal.

[Lev 24:18,21](/bible/leviticus/24#v18)

<details><summary>Leviticus 24:18,21</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/24#v18)</sup> And he that killeth a beast shall make it good; beast for beast.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/24#v21)</sup> And he that killeth a beast, he shall restore it: and he that killeth a man, he shall be put to death.

</details>

### 19C.15 {#19C.15}

If one gains property or money by way of a false report, lying, deceit, or extortion, he shall make full restitution plus an additional fifth part of its value.

[Lev 6:2-5](/bible/leviticus/6#v2)

<details><summary>Leviticus 6:2-5</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/6#v2)</sup> If a soul sin, and commit a trespass against the Lord, and lie unto his neighbour in that which was delivered him to keep, or in fellowship, or in a thing taken away by violence, or hath deceived his neighbour;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/6#v3)</sup> Or have found that which was lost, and lieth concerning it, and sweareth falsely; in any of all these that a man doeth, sinning therein:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/6#v4)</sup> Then it shall be, because he hath sinned, and is guilty, that he shall restore that which he took violently away, or the thing which he hath deceitfully gotten, or that which was delivered him to keep, or the lost thing which he found,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/6#v5)</sup> Or all that about which he hath sworn falsely; he shall even restore it in the principal, and shall add the fifth part more thereto, and give it unto him to whom it appertaineth, in the day of his trespass offering.

</details>

### 19C.16 {#19C.16}

If a man starts a fire which causes damages, he is to make restitution for all that is destroyed by the fire.

[Exod 22:6](/bible/exodus/22#v6)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/22#v6)</sup> If fire break out, and catch in thorns, so that the stacks of corn, or the standing corn, or the field, be consumed therewith; he that kindled the fire shall surely make restitution.

</details>

### 19C.17 {#19C.17}

If a pregnant woman is inadvertently struck and as a result gives birth prematurely, yet no lasting harm follows, the one who struck her is to be fined as the woman's husband may demand of him and he shall pay as the judges decide. But if any lasting harm follows, then you shall give life for life.

[Exod 21:22-23](/bible/exodus/21#v22)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:22-23</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/21#v22)</sup> If men strive, and hurt a woman with child, so that her fruit depart from her, and yet no mischief follow: he shall be surely punished, according as the woman’s husband will lay upon him; and he shall pay as the judges determine.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/21#v23)</sup> And if any mischief follow, then thou shalt give life for life,

</details>

### 19C.18 {#19C.18}

If a thief is unable to make restitution for his theft, then he shall be put in servitude to pay what he owes with his labor.

[Exod 22:3](/bible/exodus/22#v3)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/22#v3)</sup> If the sun be risen upon him, there shall be blood shed for him; for he should make full restitution; if he have nothing, then he shall be sold for his theft.

</details>
