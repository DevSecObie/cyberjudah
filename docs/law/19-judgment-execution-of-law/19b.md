### 19B.1 {#19B.1}

The aim of judgments in civil disputes should be to justify the righteous and condemn the wicked.

[Deut 25:1](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v1); [2 Chr 6:22-23](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v22); [Prov 17:15](/bible/proverbs/17#v15); [Prov 18:5](/bible/proverbs/18#v5)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 25:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v1)</sup> If there be a controversy between men, and they come unto judgment, that the judges may judge them; then they shall justify the righteous, and condemn the wicked.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Chronicles 6:22-23</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v22)</sup> If a man sin against his neighbour, and an oath be laid upon him to make him swear, and the oath come before thine altar in this house;
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v23)</sup> Then hear thou from heaven, and do, and judge thy servants, by requiting the wicked, by recompensing his way upon his own head; and by justifying the righteous, by giving him according to his righteousness.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 17:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/17#v15)</sup> He that justifieth the wicked, and he that condemneth the just, even they both are abomination to the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 18:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/18#v5)</sup> It is not good to accept the person of the wicked, to overthrow the righteous in judgment.

</details>

### 19B.2 {#19B.2}

Judgments are to adhere to a just and standard system of weights, measurements, and balances.

[Lev 19:35-36](/bible/leviticus/19#v35)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:35-36</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/leviticus/19#v35)</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment, in meteyard, in weight, or in measure.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/leviticus/19#v36)</sup> Just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin, shall ye have: I am the Lord your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt.

</details>

### 19B.3 {#19B.3}

Execute true judgment between man and man.

[Ezek 18:8](/bible/ezekiel/18#v8); [Zech 7:9](/bible/zechariah/7#v9)

<details><summary>Ezekiel 18:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/18#v8)</sup> He that hath not given forth upon usury, neither hath taken any increase, that hath withdrawn his hand from iniquity, hath executed true judgment between man and man,

</details>
<details><summary>Zechariah 7:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/zechariah/7#v9)</sup> Thus speaketh the Lord of hosts, saying, Execute true judgment, and shew mercy and compassions every man to his brother:

</details>

### 19B.4 {#19B.4}

The man who will act presumptuously and will not listen to the proper authorities or the judges, should be put to death.

[Deut 17:12-13](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v12); [Num 15:30-31](/bible/numbers/15#v30)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 17:12-13</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v12)</sup> And the man that will do presumptuously, and will not hearken unto the priest that standeth to minister there before the Lord thy God, or unto the judge, even that man shall die: and thou shalt put away the evil from Israel.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v13)</sup> And all the people shall hear, and fear, and do no more presumptuously.

</details>
<details><summary>Numbers 15:30-31</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/15#v30)</sup> But the soul that doeth ought presumptuously, whether he be born in the land, or a stranger, the same reproacheth the Lord; and that soul shall be cut off from among his people.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/15#v31)</sup> Because he hath despised the word of the Lord, and hath broken his commandment, that soul shall utterly be cut off; his iniquity shall be upon him.

</details>

### 19B.5 {#19B.5}

Controversies are to be judged according to God's judgments.

[Ezek 44:24](/bible/ezekiel/44#v24)

<details><summary>Ezekiel 44:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/ezekiel/44#v24)</sup> And in controversy they shall stand in judgment; and they shall judge it according to my judgments: and they shall keep my laws and my statutes in all mine assemblies; and they shall hallow my sabbaths.

</details>

### 19B.6 {#19B.6}

Judge not according to the outward appearance of things.

[1 Sam 16:7](/bible/1-samuel/16#v7); [Matt 23:27-28](/bible/matthew/23#v27); [John 7:24](/bible/john/7#v24); [2 Cor 10:7](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v7)

<details><summary>1 Samuel 16:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-samuel/16#v7)</sup> But the Lord said unto Samuel, Look not on his countenance, or on the height of his stature; because I have refused him: for the Lord seeth not as man seeth; for man looketh on the outward appearance, but the Lord looketh on the heart.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 23:27-28</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/23#v27)</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/23#v28)</sup> Even so ye also outwardly appear righteous unto men, but within ye are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.

</details>
<details><summary>John 7:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/john/7#v24)</sup> Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Corinthians 10:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v7)</sup> Do ye look on things after the outward appearance? If any man trust to himself that he is Christ’s, let him of himself think this again, that, as he is Christ’s, even so are we Christ’s.

</details>

### 19B.7 {#19B.7}

Ignorance of a crime (or breaking a law unintentionally) does not excuse the crime or eliminate punishment.

[Lev 4:27-28](/bible/leviticus/4#v27); [Lev 5:15-19](/bible/leviticus/5#v15); [Num 15:27-28](/bible/numbers/15#v27)

<details><summary>Leviticus 4:27-28</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/4#v27)</sup> And if any one of the common people sin through ignorance, while he doeth somewhat against any of the commandments of the Lord concerning things which ought not to be done, and be guilty;
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/leviticus/4#v28)</sup> Or if his sin, which he hath sinned, come to his knowledge: then he shall bring his offering, a kid of the goats, a female without blemish, for his sin which he hath sinned.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 5:15-19</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/5#v15)</sup> If a soul commit a trespass, and sin through ignorance, in the holy things of the Lord; then he shall bring for his trespass unto the Lord a ram without blemish out of the flocks, with thy estimation by shekels of silver, after the shekel of the sanctuary, for a trespass offering:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/5#v16)</sup> And he shall make amends for the harm that he hath done in the holy thing, and shall add the fifth part thereto, and give it unto the priest: and the priest shall make an atonement for him with the ram of the trespass offering, and it shall be forgiven him.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/5#v17)</sup> And if a soul sin, and commit any of these things which are forbidden to be done by the commandments of the Lord; though he wist it not, yet is he guilty, and shall bear his iniquity.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/5#v18)</sup> And he shall bring a ram without blemish out of the flock, with thy estimation, for a trespass offering, unto the priest: and the priest shall make an atonement for him concerning his ignorance wherein he erred and wist it not, and it shall be forgiven him.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/5#v19)</sup> It is a trespass offering: he hath certainly trespassed against the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>Numbers 15:27-28</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/15#v27)</sup> And if any soul sin through ignorance, then he shall bring a she goat of the first year for a sin offering.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/15#v28)</sup> And the priest shall make an atonement for the soul that sinneth ignorantly, when he sinneth by ignorance before the Lord, to make an atonement for him; and it shall be forgiven him.

</details>

### 19B.8 {#19B.8}

One who does anything unintentionally or out of ignorance is to be given a lighter sentence than a person who does anything defiantly or with outright contempt of the law.

[Num 15:29-31](/bible/numbers/15#v29); [Luke 12:47-48](/bible/luke/12#v47)

<details><summary>Numbers 15:29-31</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/15#v29)</sup> Ye shall have one law for him that sinneth through ignorance, both for him that is born among the children of Israel, and for the stranger that sojourneth among them.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/15#v30)</sup> But the soul that doeth ought presumptuously, whether he be born in the land, or a stranger, the same reproacheth the Lord; and that soul shall be cut off from among his people.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/15#v31)</sup> Because he hath despised the word of the Lord, and hath broken his commandment, that soul shall utterly be cut off; his iniquity shall be upon him.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 12:47-48</summary>

> <sup>[47](/bible/luke/12#v47)</sup> And that servant, which knew his lord’s will, and prepared not himself, neither did according to his will, shall be beaten with many stripes.
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/luke/12#v48)</sup> But he that knew not, and did commit things worthy of stripes, shall be beaten with few stripes. For unto whomsoever much is given, of him shall be much required: and to whom men have committed much, of him they will ask the more.

</details>

### 19B.9 {#19B.9}

Fathers shall not be put to death for the crimes of their children, neither shall the children be put to death for the crimes of their father.

[Deut 24:16](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v16); [2 Kgs 14:6](/bible/2-kings/14#v6); [2 Chr 25:4](/bible/2-chronicles/25#v4); [Jer 31:29-30](/bible/jeremiah/31#v29); [Ezek 18:20](/bible/ezekiel/18#v20)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 24:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v16)</sup> The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, neither shall the children be put to death for the fathers: every man shall be put to death for his own sin.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Kings 14:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-kings/14#v6)</sup> But the children of the murderers he slew not: according unto that which is written in the book of the law of Moses, wherein the Lord commanded, saying, The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, nor the children be put to death for the fathers; but every man shall be put to death for his own sin.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Chronicles 25:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/25#v4)</sup> But he slew not their children, but did as it is written in the law in the book of Moses, where the Lord commanded, saying, The fathers shall not die for the children, neither shall the children die for the fathers, but every man shall die for his own sin.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 31:29-30</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/31#v29)</sup> In those days they shall say no more, The fathers have eaten a sour grape, and the children’s teeth are set on edge.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/31#v30)</sup> But every one shall die for his own iniquity: every man that eateth the sour grape, his teeth shall be set on edge.

</details>
<details><summary>Ezekiel 18:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/18#v20)</sup> The soul that sinneth, it shall die. The son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, neither shall the father bear the iniquity of the son: the righteousness of the righteous shall be upon him, and the wickedness of the wicked shall be upon him.

</details>

### 19B.10 {#19B.10}

You shall not be afraid in any man's presence, for the judgment is God's.

[Deut 1:17](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v17)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 1:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v17)</sup> Ye shall not respect persons in judgment; but ye shall hear the small as well as the great; ye shall not be afraid of the face of man; for the judgment is God’s: and the cause that is too hard for you, bring it unto me, and I will hear it.

</details>

### 19B.11 {#19B.11}

Do not show partiality in judgment.

[Exod 23:3](/bible/exodus/23#v3); [Lev 19:15](/bible/leviticus/19#v15); [Deut 16:19](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v19); [Prov 24:23](/bible/proverbs/24#v23)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/23#v3)</sup> Neither shalt thou countenance a poor man in his cause.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 19:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/19#v15)</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment: thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor honour the person of the mighty: but in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 16:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v19)</sup> Thou shalt not wrest judgment; thou shalt not respect persons, neither take a gift: for a gift doth blind the eyes of the wise, and pervert the words of the righteous.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 24:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/24#v23)</sup> These things also belong to the wise. It is not good to have respect of persons in judgment.

</details>
