### 20B.1 {#20B.1}

The office of priesthood is not to be filled by one who has recently become a Christian.

[1 Tim 3:6](/bible/1-timothy/3#v6)

<details><summary>1 Timothy 3:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-timothy/3#v6)</sup> Not a novice, lest being lifted up with pride he fall into the condemnation of the devil.

</details>

### 20B.2 {#20B.2}

Ministers are not to profane the name of their God.

[Lev 21:6](/bible/leviticus/21#v6)

<details><summary>Leviticus 21:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/21#v6)</sup> They shall be holy unto their God, and not profane the name of their God: for the offerings of the Lord made by fire, and the bread of their God, they do offer: therefore they shall be holy.

</details>

### 20B.3 {#20B.3}

Priests are not to defile or profane themselves.

[Lev 21:1-4,15](/bible/leviticus/21#v1)

<details><summary>Leviticus 21:1-4,15</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/21#v1)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Speak unto the priests the sons of Aaron, and say unto them, There shall none be defiled for the dead among his people:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/21#v2)</sup> But for his kin, that is near unto him, that is, for his mother, and for his father, and for his son, and for his daughter, and for his brother,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/21#v3)</sup> And for his sister a virgin, that is nigh unto him, which hath had no husband; for her may he be defiled.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/21#v4)</sup> But he shall not defile himself, being a chief man among his people, to profane himself.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/21#v15)</sup> Neither shall he profane his seed among his people: for I the Lord do sanctify him.

</details>

### 20B.4 {#20B.4}

Priests are to be males of at least thirty years of age.

[2 Chr 31:16](/bible/2-chronicles/31#v16)

<details><summary>2 Chronicles 31:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/31#v16)</sup> Beside their genealogy of males, from three years old and upward, even unto every one that entereth into the house of the Lord, his daily portion for their service in their charges according to their courses;

</details>

### 20B.5 {#20B.5}

Priests are not to marry a whore, widow, divorced woman or profane woman, but shall marry a virgin of his own people. Lev, 21:7,13-14;

[Ezek 44:22](/bible/ezekiel/44#v22)

<details><summary>Ezekiel 44:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/44#v22)</sup> Neither shall they take for their wives a widow, nor her that is put away: but they shall take maidens of the seed of the house of Israel, or a widow that had a priest before.

</details>

### 20B.6 {#20B.6}

A minister must be blameless, vigilant, sober, just, holy, self-control led, of good behavior, given to hospitality, and able to teach and exhort.

[1 Tim 3:2](/bible/1-timothy/3#v2); [Titus 1:7-9](/bible/titus/1#v7)

<details><summary>1 Timothy 3:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/3#v2)</sup> A bishop then must be blameless, the husband of one wife, vigilant, sober, of good behaviour, given to hospitality, apt to teach;

</details>
<details><summary>Titus 1:7-9</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/titus/1#v7)</sup> For a bishop must be blameless, as the steward of God; not selfwilled, not soon angry, not given to wine, no striker, not given to filthy lucre;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/titus/1#v8)</sup> But a lover of hospitality, a lover of good men, sober, just, holy, temperate;
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/titus/1#v9)</sup> Holding fast the faithful word as he hath been taught, that he may be able by sound doctrine both to exhort and to convince the gainsayers.

</details>

### 20B.7 {#20B.7}

A minister must not be addicted to alcohol, violent or quick-tempered, greedy for money, quarrelsome, or covetous.

[1 Tim 3:3](/bible/1-timothy/3#v3); [Titus 1:7-8](/bible/titus/1#v7)

<details><summary>1 Timothy 3:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-timothy/3#v3)</sup> Not given to wine, no striker, not greedy of filthy lucre; but patient, not a brawler, not covetous;

</details>
<details><summary>Titus 1:7-8</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/titus/1#v7)</sup> For a bishop must be blameless, as the steward of God; not selfwilled, not soon angry, not given to wine, no striker, not given to filthy lucre;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/titus/1#v8)</sup> But a lover of hospitality, a lover of good men, sober, just, holy, temperate;

</details>

### 20B.8 {#20B.8}

Must be of a good reputation outside the church.

[1 Tim 3:7](/bible/1-timothy/3#v7)

<details><summary>1 Timothy 3:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-timothy/3#v7)</sup> Moreover he must have a good report of them which are without; lest he fall into reproach and the snare of the devil.

</details>

### 20B.9 {#20B.9}

Priests are not to make themselves bald, shave off the corner of their beard or make any cuttings in their flesh.

[Lev 21:5](/bible/leviticus/21#v5)

<details><summary>Leviticus 21:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/21#v5)</sup> They shall not make baldness upon their head, neither shall they shave off the corner of their beard, nor make any cuttings in their flesh.

</details>

### 20B.10 {#20B.10}

Priests are not to let their hair grow long.

[Ezek 44:20](/bible/ezekiel/44#v20)

<details><summary>Ezekiel 44:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/44#v20)</sup> Neither shall they shave their heads, nor suffer their locks to grow long; they shall only poll their heads.

</details>

### 20B.11 {#20B.11}

A priest shall not profane his posterity among his people.

[Lev 21:15](/bible/leviticus/21#v15)

<details><summary>Leviticus 21:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/21#v15)</sup> Neither shall he profane his seed among his people: for I the Lord do sanctify him.

</details>

### 20B.12 {#20B.12}

A priest shall not eat anything that died of itself or has been torn in pieces.

[Ezek 44:31](/bible/ezekiel/44#v31)

<details><summary>Ezekiel 44:31</summary>

> <sup>[31](/bible/ezekiel/44#v31)</sup> The priests shall not eat of any thing that is dead of itself, or torn, whether it be fowl or beast.

</details>

### 20B.13 {#20B.13}

Disabilities and deformities not allowed for the priesthood: No blemish, blind man, lame, disfigured face, deformed limb, having a broken foot or hand, a hunchback or dwarf, a blemish in his eye, having eczema, or scabs, or crushed testicles.

[Lev 21:16-23](/bible/leviticus/21#v16)

<details><summary>Leviticus 21:16-23</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/21#v16)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/21#v17)</sup> Speak unto Aaron, saying, Whosoever he be of thy seed in their generations that hath any blemish, let him not approach to offer the bread of his God.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/21#v18)</sup> For whatsoever man he be that hath a blemish, he shall not approach: a blind man, or a lame, or he that hath a flat nose, or any thing superfluous,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/21#v19)</sup> Or a man that is brokenfooted, or brokenhanded,
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/21#v20)</sup> Or crookbackt, or a dwarf, or that hath a blemish in his eye, or be scurvy, or scabbed, or hath his stones broken;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/21#v21)</sup> No man that hath a blemish of the seed of Aaron the priest shall come nigh to offer the offerings of the Lord made by fire: he hath a blemish; he shall not come nigh to offer the bread of his God.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/21#v22)</sup> He shall eat the bread of his God, both of the most holy, and of the holy.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/21#v23)</sup> Only he shall not go in unto the vail, nor come nigh unto the altar, because he hath a blemish; that he profane not my sanctuaries: for I the Lord do sanctify them.

</details>

### 20B.14 {#20B.14}

Priests to be of an unpolluted genealogy.

[Ezra 2:61-62](/bible/ezra/2#v61)

<details><summary>Ezra 2:61-62</summary>

> <sup>[61](/bible/ezra/2#v61)</sup> And of the children of the priests: the children of Habaiah, the children of Koz, the children of Barzillai; which took a wife of the daughters of Barzillai the Gileadite, and was called after their name:
>
> <sup>[62](/bible/ezra/2#v62)</sup> These sought their register among those that were reckoned by genealogy, but they were not found: therefore were they, as polluted, put from the priesthood.

</details>

### 20B.15 {#20B.15}

A stranger (alien) who comes near the priest's office or the tabernacle shall be put to death.

[Num 3:10,38](/bible/numbers/3#v10)

<details><summary>Numbers 3:10,38</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/3#v10)</sup> And thou shalt appoint Aaron and his sons, and they shall wait on their priest’s office: and the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/numbers/3#v38)</sup> But those that encamp before the tabernacle toward the east, even before the tabernacle of the congregation eastward, shall be Moses, and Aaron and his sons, keeping the charge of the sanctuary for the charge of the children of Israel; and the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death.

</details>

### 20B.16 {#20B.16}

Ministers should not use flattery.

[1 Thess 2:5](/bible/1-thessalonians/2#v5)

<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 2:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-thessalonians/2#v5)</sup> For neither at any time used we flattering words, as ye know, nor a cloke of covetousness; God is witness:

</details>

### 20B.17 {#20B.17}

A minister must be one who rules well his own house.

[1 Tim 3:4-5](/bible/1-timothy/3#v4)

<details><summary>1 Timothy 3:4-5</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-timothy/3#v4)</sup> One that ruleth well his own house, having his children in subjection with all gravity;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-timothy/3#v5)</sup> (For if a man know not how to rule his own house, how shall he take care of the church of God?)

</details>

### 20B.18 {#20B.18}

A priest's daughter that plays the whore is to be burnt with fire.

[Lev 21:9](/bible/leviticus/21#v9)

<details><summary>Leviticus 21:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/21#v9)</sup> And the daughter of any priest, if she profane herself by playing the whore, she profaneth her father: she shall be burnt with fire.

</details>

### 20B.19 {#20B.19}

Pastors should hold fast to sound words.

[2 Tim 1:13](/bible/2-timothy/1#v13); [Titus 1:9](/bible/titus/1#v9)

<details><summary>2 Timothy 1:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/2-timothy/1#v13)</sup> Hold fast the form of sound words, which thou hast heard of me, in faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.

</details>
<details><summary>Titus 1:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/titus/1#v9)</sup> Holding fast the faithful word as he hath been taught, that he may be able by sound doctrine both to exhort and to convince the gainsayers.

</details>

### 20B.20 {#20B.20}

Ministers should mourn for the release of heathen control and rule over the nation.

[Joel 2:17](/bible/joel/2#v17)

<details><summary>Joel 2:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/joel/2#v17)</sup> Let the priests, the ministers of the Lord, weep between the porch and the altar, and let them say, Spare thy people, O Lord, and give not thine heritage to reproach, that the heathen should rule over them: wherefore should they say among the people, Where is their God?

</details>
