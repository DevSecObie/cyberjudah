### 20D.1 {#20D.1}

No one who is in any way unclean should enter the house of the LORD.

[2 Chr 23:19](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v19)

<details><summary>2 Chronicles 23:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v19)</sup> And he set the porters at the gates of the house of the Lord, that none which was unclean in any thing should enter in.

</details>

### 20D.2 {#20D.2}

No one who is emasculated, or has his male organ cut off , shall enter the assembly of the LORD.

[Deut 23:1](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v1)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 23:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v1)</sup> He that is wounded in the stones, or hath his privy member cut off, shall not enter into the congregation of the Lord.

</details>

### 20D.3 {#20D.3}

A bastard (mongrel) shall not enter into the congregation of the LORD; even to his tenth generation.

[Deut 23:2](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v2)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 23:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v2)</sup> A bastard shall not enter into the congregation of the Lord; even to his tenth generation shall he not enter into the congregation of the Lord.

</details>

### 20D.4 {#20D.4}

No Ammonite or Moabite (those of another race). shall enter the assembly of the LORD; none of their descendants, even to the tenth generation, shall ever enter the assembly of the LORD.

[Deut 23:3](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v3); [Neh 13:1](/bible/nehemiah/13#v1)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 23:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v3)</sup> An Ammonite or Moabite shall not enter into the congregation of the Lord; even to their tenth generation shall they not enter into the congregation of the Lord for ever:

</details>
<details><summary>Nehemiah 13:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/nehemiah/13#v1)</sup> On that day they read in the book of Moses in the audience of the people; and therein was found written, that the Ammonite and the Moabite should not come into the congregation of God for ever;

</details>

### 20D.5 {#20D.5}

Women are to keep silence in the churches: for it is not permitted for them to speak, but they may speak through their husbands.

[1 Cor 14:34-35](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v34)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 14:34-35</summary>

> <sup>[34](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v34)</sup> Let your women keep silence in the churches: for it is not permitted unto them to speak; but they are commanded to be under obedience, as also saith the law.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v35)</sup> And if they will learn any thing, let them ask their husbands at home: for it is a shame for women to speak in the church.

</details>

### 20D.6 {#20D.6}

Women are not to teach in the church.

[1 Tim 2:11-12](/bible/1-timothy/2#v11)

<details><summary>1 Timothy 2:11-12</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-timothy/2#v11)</sup> Let the woman learn in silence with all subjection.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-timothy/2#v12)</sup> But I suffer not a woman to teach, nor to usurp authority over the man, but to be in silence.

</details>

### 20D.7 {#20D.7}

You shall not plant a grove of any trees near the alter of the LORD your God.

[Deut 16:21](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v21)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 16:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v21)</sup> Thou shalt not plant thee a grove of any trees near unto the altar of the Lord thy God, which thou shalt make thee.

</details>

### 20D.8 {#20D.8}

Each member of the church or assembly of God is to use their gifts for the edification of the church.

[1 Cor 14:5,12](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v5)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 14:5,12</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v5)</sup> I would that ye all spake with tongues, but rather that ye prophesied: for greater is he that prophesieth than he that speaketh with tongues, except he interpret, that the church may receive edifying.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v12)</sup> Even so ye, forasmuch as ye are zealous of spiritual gifts, seek that ye may excel to the edifying of the church.

</details>

### 20D.9 {#20D.9}

Give no offense to the church of God.

[1 Cor 10:32](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v32)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 10:32</summary>

> <sup>[32](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v32)</sup> Give none offence, neither to the Jews, nor to the Gentiles, nor to the church of God:

</details>

### 20D.10 {#20D.10}

A church is not to have those within it who hold to the doctrine of Balaam.

[Rev 2:14](/bible/revelation/2#v14)

<details><summary>Revelation 2:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/revelation/2#v14)</sup> But I have a few things against thee, because thou hast there them that hold the doctrine of Balaam, who taught Balac to cast a stumblingblock before the children of Israel, to eat things sacrificed unto idols, and to commit fornication.

</details>
