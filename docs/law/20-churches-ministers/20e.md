### 20E.1 {#20E.1}

A tenth part of all increase (not all wealth owned) is to be given as a tithe.

[Gen 14:20](/bible/genesis/14#v20); [Gen 28:22](/bible/genesis/28#v22); [Num 18:21](/bible/numbers/18#v21); [Heb 7:2,4](/bible/hebrews/7#v2)

### 20E.3 {#20E.3}

We are to honor God by bringing the choice first fruits of our land into the house of God, so He may bring prosperity to us.

[Exod 23:19](/bible/exodus/23#v19); [Mal 3:10-12](/bible/malachi/3#v10); [Prov 3:9-10](/bible/proverbs/3#v9)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/23#v19)</sup> The first of the firstfruits of thy land thou shalt bring into the house of the Lord thy God. Thou shalt not seethe a kid in his mother’s milk.

</details>
<details><summary>Malachi 3:10-12</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/malachi/3#v10)</sup> Bring ye all the tithes into the storehouse, that there may be meat in mine house, and prove me now herewith, saith the Lord of hosts, if I will not open you the windows of heaven, and pour you out a blessing, that there shall not be room enough to receive it.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/malachi/3#v11)</sup> And I will rebuke the devourer for your sakes, and he shall not destroy the fruits of your ground; neither shall your vine cast her fruit before the time in the field, saith the Lord of hosts.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/malachi/3#v12)</sup> And all nations shall call you blessed: for ye shall be a delightsome land, saith the Lord of hosts.

</details>
