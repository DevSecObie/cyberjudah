### 20C.2 {#20C.2}

A church body should labor with perseverance for the sake of Christ's name.

[Rev 2:3](/bible/revelation/2#v3)

<details><summary>Revelation 2:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/revelation/2#v3)</sup> And hast borne, and hast patience, and for my name’s sake hast laboured, and hast not fainted.

</details>

### 20C.3 {#20C.3}

The church is to assist only those widows that are not in the care of anyone.

[1 Tim 5:16](/bible/1-timothy/5#v16)

<details><summary>1 Timothy 5:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/1-timothy/5#v16)</sup> If any man or woman that believeth have widows, let them relieve them, and let not the church be charged; that it may relieve them that are widows indeed.

</details>

### 20C.4 {#20C.4}

A church body is not to be lukewarm in its beliefs, but be either hot or cold regarding them.

[Rev 3:15-16](/bible/revelation/3#v15)

<details><summary>Revelation 3:15-16</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/revelation/3#v15)</sup> I know thy works, that thou art neither cold nor hot: I would thou wert cold or hot.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/revelation/3#v16)</sup> So then because thou art lukewarm, and neither cold nor hot, I will spue thee out of my mouth.

</details>

### 20C.5 {#20C.5}

The church is to make known the wisdom of God to civil leaders.

[Eph 3:10](/bible/ephesians/3#v10)

<details><summary>Ephesians 3:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/ephesians/3#v10)</sup> To the intent that now unto the principalities and powers in heavenly places might be known by the church the manifold wisdom of God,

</details>

</details>

### 20C.7 {#20C.7}

Christ is the head of the church.

[Eph 5:23,29-30](/bible/ephesians/5#v23)

<details><summary>Ephesians 5:23,29-30</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/ephesians/5#v23)</sup> For the husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church: and he is the saviour of the body.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/ephesians/5#v29)</sup> For no man ever yet hated his own flesh; but nourisheth and cherisheth it, even as the Lord the church:
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/ephesians/5#v30)</sup> For we are members of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones.

</details>

### 20C.8 {#20C.8}

It is an abomination to do evil practices in God's house.

[2 Chr 36:14](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v14); [Jer 7:30](/bible/jeremiah/7#v30)

<details><summary>2 Chronicles 36:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v14)</sup> Moreover all the chief of the priests, and the people, transgressed very much after all the abominations of the heathen; and polluted the house of the Lord which he had hallowed in Jerusalem.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 7:30</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/7#v30)</sup> For the children of Judah have done evil in my sight, saith the Lord: they have set their abominations in the house which is called by my name, to pollute it.

</details>
