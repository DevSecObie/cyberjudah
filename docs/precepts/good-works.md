**[Titus 1:16](/bible/titus/1#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/titus/1#v16)</sup> They profess that they know God; but in works they deny him, being abominable, and disobedient, and unto every good work reprobate.

**[Titus 3:8](/bible/titus/3#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/titus/3#v8)</sup> This is a faithful saying, and these things I will that thou affirm constantly, that they which have believed in God might be careful to maintain good works. These things are good and profitable unto men.

**[1 Timothy 5:25](/bible/1-timothy/5#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/1-timothy/5#v25)</sup> Likewise also the good works of some are manifest beforehand; and they that are otherwise cannot be hid.

**[James 2:26](/bible/james/2#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/james/2#v26)</sup> For as the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead also.

**[Psalms 78:7](/bible/psalms/78#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/78#v7)</sup> That they might set their hope in God, and not forget the works of God, but keep his commandments:

**[2 Esdras 7:24](/bible/2-esdras/7#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/2-esdras/7#v24)</sup> But his law have they despised, and denied his covenants; in his statutes have they not been faithful, and have not performed his works.

**[2 Esdras 9:7](/bible/2-esdras/9#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-esdras/9#v7)</sup> And every one that shall be saved, and shall be able to escape by his works, and by faith, whereby ye have believed,

**[Sirach 16:12](/bible/sirach/16#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/16#v12)</sup> As his mercy is great, so is his correction also: he judgeth a man according to his works

**[Matthew 5:16](/bible/matthew/5#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/5#v16)</sup> Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.

**[Acts 9:36](/bible/acts/9#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/acts/9#v36)</sup> Now there was at Joppa a certain disciple named Tabitha, which by interpretation is called Dorcas: this woman was full of good works and almsdeeds which she did.

**[Revelation 20:12](/bible/revelation/20#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/20#v12)</sup> And I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life: and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works.
