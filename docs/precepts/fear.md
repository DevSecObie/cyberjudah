**[Wisdom of Solomon 17:12](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/17#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/17#v12)</sup> For fear is nothing else but a betraying of the succours which reason offereth.

**[2 Timothy 1:7](/bible/2-timothy/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-timothy/1#v7)</sup> For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.

**[Romans 8:15](/bible/romans/8#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/romans/8#v15)</sup> For ye have not received the spirit of bondage again to fear; but ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father.

**[Deuteronomy 20:8](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v8)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 19-22](/study/deuteronomy/19-22)

> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v8)</sup> And the officers shall speak further unto the people, and they shall say, What man is there that is fearful and fainthearted? let him go and return unto his house, lest his brethren’s heart faint as well as his heart.

**[Joshua 1:9](/bible/joshua/1#v9)** · taught in [Joshua 1-4](/study/joshua/1-4)

> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/1#v9)</sup> Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the Lord thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest.

**[Psalms 34:4](/bible/psalms/34#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/34#v4)</sup> I sought the Lord, and he heard me, and delivered me from all my fears.
