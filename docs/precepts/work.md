**[1 Thessalonians 4:11](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v11)</sup> And that ye study to be quiet, and to do your own business, and to work with your own hands, as we commanded you;

**[2 Thessalonians 3:10](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v10)** (key)

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v10)</sup> For even when we were with you, this we commanded you, that if any would not work, neither should he eat.

**[Ephesians 4:28](/bible/ephesians/4#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/ephesians/4#v28)</sup> Let him that stole steal no more: but rather let him labour, working with his hands the thing which is good, that he may have to give to him that needeth.

**[Proverbs 10:4](/bible/proverbs/10#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/10#v4)</sup> He becometh poor that dealeth with a slack hand: but the hand of the diligent maketh rich.

**[Sirach 7:15](/bible/sirach/7#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/7#v15)</sup> Hate not laborious work, neither husbandry, which the most High hath ordained.

**[Sirach 38:25](/bible/sirach/38#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/38#v25)</sup> How can he get wisdom that holdeth the plough, and that glorieth in the goad, that driveth oxen, and is occupied in their labours, and whose talk is of bullocks?
