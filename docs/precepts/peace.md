**[Romans 12:18](/bible/romans/12#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/romans/12#v18)</sup> If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men.

**[James 3:18](/bible/james/3#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/james/3#v18)</sup> And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace of them that make peace.

**[Psalms 34:14](/bible/psalms/34#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/34#v14)</sup> Depart from evil, and do good; seek peace, and pursue it.

**[Psalms 37:37](/bible/psalms/37#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/psalms/37#v37)</sup> Mark the perfect man, and behold the upright: for the end of that man is peace.

**[Ephesians 4:3](/bible/ephesians/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/ephesians/4#v3)</sup> Endeavouring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.

**[2 Corinthians 13:11](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v11)</sup> Finally, brethren, farewell. Be perfect, be of good comfort, be of one mind, live in peace; and the God of love and peace shall be with you.

**[Proverbs 16:7](/bible/proverbs/16#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/16#v7)</sup> When a man’s ways please the Lord, he maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him.

**[Isaiah 26:3](/bible/isaiah/26#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/26#v3)</sup> Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.

**[Sirach 28:9](/bible/sirach/28#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/28#v9)</sup> A sinful man disquieteth friends, and maketh debate among them that be at peace.

**[Matthew 5:9](/bible/matthew/5#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/5#v9)</sup> Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God.
