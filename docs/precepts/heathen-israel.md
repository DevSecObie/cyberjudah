**[Galatians 3:8](/bible/galatians/3#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/galatians/3#v8)</sup> And the scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the heathen through faith, preached before the gospel unto Abraham, saying, In thee shall all nations be blessed.

**[2 Esdras 2:34](/bible/2-esdras/2#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/2-esdras/2#v34)</sup> And therefore I say unto you, O ye heathen, that hear and understand, look for your Shepherd, he shall give you everlasting rest; for he is nigh at hand, that shall come in the end of the world.

**[Ezekiel 20:32](/bible/ezekiel/20#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/ezekiel/20#v32)</sup> And that which cometh into your mind shall not be at all, that ye say, We will be as the heathen, as the families of the countries, to serve wood and stone.

**[Ezekiel 25:8](/bible/ezekiel/25#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/25#v8)</sup> Thus saith the Lord God; Because that Moab and Seir do say, Behold, the house of Judah is like unto all the heathen;
