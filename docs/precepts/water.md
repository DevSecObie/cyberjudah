**[1 Peter 3:21](/bible/1-peter/3#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/1-peter/3#v21)</sup> The like figure whereunto even baptism doth also now save us (not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the answer of a good conscience toward God,) by the resurrection of Jesus Christ:

> <sup>[26](/bible/ephesians/5#v26)</sup> That he might sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word,

> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/2#v22)</sup> For though thou wash thee with nitre, and take thee much soap, yet thine iniquity is marked before me, saith the Lord God.

> <sup>[5](/bible/john/3#v5)</sup> Jesus answered, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God.

> <sup>[3](/bible/john/15#v3)</sup> Now ye are clean through the word which I have spoken unto you.
