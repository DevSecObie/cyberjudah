**[Micah 3:8](/bible/micah/3#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/micah/3#v8)</sup> But truly I am full of power by the spirit of the Lord, and of judgment, and of might, to declare unto Jacob his transgression, and to Israel his sin.

**[Exodus 18:26](/bible/exodus/18#v26)** · taught in [Exodus 18-21](/study/exodus/18-21)

> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/18#v26)</sup> And they judged the people at all seasons: the hard causes they brought unto Moses, but every small matter they judged themselves.

**[Deuteronomy 1:16](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v16)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 1-2](/study/deuteronomy/1-2)

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v16)</sup> And I charged your judges at that time, saying, Hear the causes between your brethren, and judge righteously between every man and his brother, and the stranger that is with him.

**[Deuteronomy 19:18](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v18)** (key) · taught in [Deuteronomy 19-22](/study/deuteronomy/19-22)

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v18)</sup> And the judges shall make diligent inquisition: and, behold, if the witness be a false witness, and hath testified falsely against his brother;

**[Leviticus 19:15](/bible/leviticus/19#v15)** · taught in [Leviticus 18-21](/study/leviticus/18-21)

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/19#v15)</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment: thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor honour the person of the mighty: but in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour.

**[Galatians 6:1](/bible/galatians/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/galatians/6#v1)</sup> Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted.

**[1 Corinthians 2:15](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v15)</sup> But he that is spiritual judgeth all things, yet he himself is judged of no man.

**[Proverbs 18:13](/bible/proverbs/18#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/18#v13)</sup> He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him.

**[Proverbs 28:5](/bible/proverbs/28#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/28#v5)</sup> Evil men understand not judgment: but they that seek the Lord understand all things.

**[Sirach 11:7](/bible/sirach/11#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/11#v7)</sup> Blame not before thou hast examined the truth: understand first, and then rebuke.

**[Sirach 31:15](/bible/sirach/31#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/31#v15)</sup> Judge not thy neighbour by thyself: and be discreet in every point.

**[Sirach 38:33](/bible/sirach/38#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/sirach/38#v33)</sup> They shall not be sought for in publick counsel, nor sit high in the congregation: they shall not sit on the judges’ seat, nor understand the sentence of judgment: they cannot declare justice and judgment; and they shall not be found where parables are spoken.

**[Sirach 45:26](/bible/sirach/45#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/45#v26)</sup> God give you wisdom in your heart to judge his people in righteousness, that their good things be not abolished, and that their glory may endure for ever.

**[Matthew 18:16](/bible/matthew/18#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/18#v16)</sup> But if he will not hear thee, then take with thee one or two more, that in the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.

**[John 7:24](/bible/john/7#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/john/7#v24)</sup> Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment.
