**[Obadiah 1:18](/bible/obadiah/1#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/obadiah/1#v18)</sup> And the house of Jacob shall be a fire, and the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau for stubble, and they shall kindle in them, and devour them; and there shall not be any remaining of the house of Esau; for the Lord hath spoken it.

**[Obadiah 1:21](/bible/obadiah/1#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/obadiah/1#v21)</sup> And saviours shall come up on mount Zion to judge the mount of Esau; and the kingdom shall be the Lord’s.

**[Malachi 1:4](/bible/malachi/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/malachi/1#v4)</sup> Whereas Edom saith, We are impoverished, but we will return and build the desolate places; thus saith the Lord of hosts, They shall build, but I will throw down; and they shall call them, The border of wickedness, and, The people against whom the Lord hath indignation for ever.

**[Genesis 25:26](/bible/genesis/25#v26)** · taught in [Genesis 24-27](/study/genesis/24-27)

> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/25#v26)</sup> And after that came his brother out, and his hand took hold on Esau’s heel; and his name was called Jacob: and Isaac was threescore years old when she bare them.

**[Isaiah 34:5](/bible/isaiah/34#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/34#v5)</sup> For my sword shall be bathed in heaven: behold, it shall come down upon Idumea, and upon the people of my curse, to judgment.

**[2 Esdras 6:9](/bible/2-esdras/6#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-esdras/6#v9)</sup> For Esau is the end of the world, and Jacob is the beginning of it that followeth.

**[Jeremiah 49:10](/bible/jeremiah/49#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/49#v10)</sup> But I have made Esau bare, I have uncovered his secret places, and he shall not be able to hide himself: his seed is spoiled, and his brethren, and his neighbours, and he is not.
