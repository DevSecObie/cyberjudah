**[James 1:7](/bible/james/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/james/1#v7)</sup> For let not that man think that he shall receive any thing of the Lord.

**[James 4:3](/bible/james/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/james/4#v3)</sup> Ye ask, and receive not, because ye ask amiss, that ye may consume it upon your lusts.

**[Proverbs 15:29](/bible/proverbs/15#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/15#v29)</sup> The Lord is far from the wicked: but he heareth the prayer of the righteous.

**[Proverbs 28:9](/bible/proverbs/28#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/28#v9)</sup> He that turneth away his ear from hearing the law, even his prayer shall be abomination.

**[John 9:31](/bible/john/9#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/john/9#v31)</sup> Now we know that God heareth not sinners: but if any man be a worshipper of God, and doeth his will, him he heareth.
