**[Judith 10:3](/bible/judith/10#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/judith/10#v3)</sup> And pulled off the sackcloth which she had on, and put off the garments of her widowhood, and washed her body all over with water, and anointed herself with precious ointment, and braided the hair of her head, and put on a tire upon it, and put on her garments of gladness, wherewith she was clad during the life of Manasses her husband.

**[1 Timothy 2:9](/bible/1-timothy/2#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-timothy/2#v9)</sup> In like manner also, that women adorn themselves in modest apparel, with shamefacedness and sobriety; not with broided hair, or gold, or pearls, or costly array;

**[1 Peter 3:3](/bible/1-peter/3#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-peter/3#v3)</sup> Whose adorning let it not be that outward adorning of plaiting the hair, and of wearing of gold, or of putting on of apparel;

**[Proverbs 7:10](/bible/proverbs/7#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/7#v10)</sup> And, behold, there met him a woman with the attire of an harlot, and subtil of heart.
