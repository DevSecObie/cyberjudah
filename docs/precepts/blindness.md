> <sup>[8](/bible/romans/11#v8)</sup> (According as it is written, God hath given them the spirit of slumber, eyes that they should not see, and ears that they should not hear;) unto this day.

**[Romans 11:25](/bible/romans/11#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/romans/11#v25)</sup> For I would not, brethren, that ye should be ignorant of this mystery, lest ye should be wise in your own conceits; that blindness in part is happened to Israel, until the fulness of the Gentiles be come in.

> <sup>[18](/bible/ephesians/4#v18)</sup> Having the understanding darkened, being alienated from the life of God through the ignorance that is in them, because of the blindness of their heart:
