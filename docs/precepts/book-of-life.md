**[Philippians 4:3](/bible/philippians/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/philippians/4#v3)</sup> And I intreat thee also, true yokefellow, help those women which laboured with me in the gospel, with Clement also, and with other my fellowlabourers, whose names are in the book of life.

**[Exodus 32:33](/bible/exodus/32#v33)** · taught in [Exodus 30-33](/study/exodus/30-33)

> <sup>[33](/bible/exodus/32#v33)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Whosoever hath sinned against me, him will I blot out of my book.

**[Daniel 12:1](/bible/daniel/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/12#v1)</sup> And at that time shall Michael stand up, the great prince which standeth for the children of thy people: and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation even to that same time: and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book.

**[Sirach 41:11](/bible/sirach/41#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/41#v11)</sup> The mourning of men is about their bodies: but an ill name of sinners shall be blotted out.

**[Revelation 3:5](/bible/revelation/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/revelation/3#v5)</sup> He that overcometh, the same shall be clothed in white raiment; and I will not blot out his name out of the book of life, but I will confess his name before my Father, and before his angels.

**[Revelation 20:12](/bible/revelation/20#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/20#v12)</sup> And I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life: and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works.

**[Revelation 20:15](/bible/revelation/20#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/revelation/20#v15)</sup> And whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire.

**[Revelation 21:27](/bible/revelation/21#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/revelation/21#v27)</sup> And there shall in no wise enter into it any thing that defileth, neither whatsoever worketh abomination, or maketh a lie: but they which are written in the Lamb’s book of life.
