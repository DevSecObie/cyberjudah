**[1 John 3:15](/bible/1-john/3#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-john/3#v15)</sup> Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer: and ye know that no murderer hath eternal life abiding in him.

**[Leviticus 19:17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)** · taught in [Leviticus 18-21](/study/leviticus/18-21)

> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him.

**[Proverbs 10:18](/bible/proverbs/10#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/10#v18)</sup> He that hideth hatred with lying lips, and he that uttereth a slander, is a fool.
