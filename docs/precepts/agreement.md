**[Amos 3:3](/bible/amos/3#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/amos/3#v3)</sup> Can two walk together, except they be agreed?

**[2 Corinthians 6:16](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v16)</sup> And what agreement hath the temple of God with idols? for ye are the temple of the living God; as God hath said, I will dwell in them, and walk in them; and I will be their God, and they shall be my people.

**[Sirach 13:17](/bible/sirach/13#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/13#v17)</sup> What fellowship hath the wolf with the lamb? so the sinner with the godly.

**[Sirach 25:1](/bible/sirach/25#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/25#v1)</sup> In three things I was beautified, and stood up beautiful both before God and men: the unity of brethren, the love of neighbours, a man and a wife that agree together.
