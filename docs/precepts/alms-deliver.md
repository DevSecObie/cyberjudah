**[Tobit 4:10](/bible/tobit/4#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/tobit/4#v10)</sup> Because that alms do deliver from death, and suffereth not to come into darkness.

**[Tobit 12:9](/bible/tobit/12#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/tobit/12#v9)</sup> For alms doth deliver from death, and shall purge away all sin. Those that exercise alms and righteousness shall be filled with life:

**[Tobit 14:11](/bible/tobit/14#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/tobit/14#v11)</sup> Wherefore now, my son, consider what alms doeth, and how righteousness doth deliver. When he had said these things, he gave up the ghost in the bed, being an hundred and eight and fifty years old; and he buried him honourably.

**[Sirach 3:30](/bible/sirach/3#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/sirach/3#v30)</sup> Water will quench a flaming fire; and alms maketh an atonement for sins.

**[Sirach 12:3](/bible/sirach/12#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/12#v3)</sup> There can no good come to him that is always occupied in evil, nor to him that giveth no alms.

**[Sirach 29:12](/bible/sirach/29#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/29#v12)</sup> Shut up alms in thy storehouses: and it shall deliver thee from all affliction.

**[Sirach 40:24](/bible/sirach/40#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/40#v24)</sup> Brethren and help are against time of trouble: but alms shall deliver more than them both.

**[Matthew 6:4](/bible/matthew/6#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/6#v4)</sup> That thine alms may be in secret: and thy Father which seeth in secret himself shall reward thee openly.

**[Acts 9:36](/bible/acts/9#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/acts/9#v36)</sup> Now there was at Joppa a certain disciple named Tabitha, which by interpretation is called Dorcas: this woman was full of good works and almsdeeds which she did.

**[Acts 10:2](/bible/acts/10#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/acts/10#v2)</sup> A devout man, and one that feared God with all his house, which gave much alms to the people, and prayed to God alway.

**[Acts 10:4](/bible/acts/10#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/acts/10#v4)</sup> And when he looked on him, he was afraid, and said, What is it, Lord? And he said unto him, Thy prayers and thine alms are come up for a memorial before God.
