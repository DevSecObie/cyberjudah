**[Romans 13:14](/bible/romans/13#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/romans/13#v14)</sup> But put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make not provision for the flesh, to fulfil the lusts thereof.

**[James 1:12](/bible/james/1#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/james/1#v12)</sup> Blessed is the man that endureth temptation: for when he is tried, he shall receive the crown of life, which the Lord hath promised to them that love him.

**[1 Corinthians 10:13](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v13)</sup> There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able; but will with the temptation also make a way to escape, that ye may be able to bear it.

**[Sirach 2:1](/bible/sirach/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/2#v1)</sup> My son, if thou come to serve the Lord, prepare thy soul for temptation.

**[Sirach 33:1](/bible/sirach/33#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/33#v1)</sup> There shall no evil happen unto him that feareth the Lord; but in temptation even again he will deliver him.

**[Matthew 6:13](/bible/matthew/6#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/6#v13)</sup> And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.

**[Matthew 26:41](/bible/matthew/26#v41)**

> <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/26#v41)</sup> Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.
