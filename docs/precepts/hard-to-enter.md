**[2 Esdras 7:7](/bible/2-esdras/7#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-esdras/7#v7)</sup> The entrance thereof is narrow, and is set in a dangerous place to fall, like as if there were a fire on the right hand, and on the left a deep water:

**[Matthew 7:14](/bible/matthew/7#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/7#v14)</sup> Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it.

**[Luke 13:24](/bible/luke/13#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/luke/13#v24)</sup> Strive to enter in at the strait gate: for many, I say unto you, will seek to enter in, and shall not be able.

**[Acts 14:22](/bible/acts/14#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/acts/14#v22)</sup> Confirming the souls of the disciples, and exhorting them to continue in the faith, and that we must through much tribulation enter into the kingdom of God.
