**[Colossians 1:2](/bible/colossians/1#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/colossians/1#v2)</sup> To the saints and faithful brethren in Christ which are at Colosse: Grace be unto you, and peace, from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**[Romans 1:7](/bible/romans/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/romans/1#v7)</sup> To all that be in Rome, beloved of God, called to be saints: Grace to you and peace from God our Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**[Psalms 50:5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)</sup> Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.

**[Ephesians 1:1](/bible/ephesians/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ephesians/1#v1)</sup> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, to the saints which are at Ephesus, and to the faithful in Christ Jesus:

**[Psalms 148:14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)</sup> He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him. Praise ye the Lord.

**[1 Corinthians 1:2](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v2)</sup> Unto the church of God which is at Corinth, to them that are sanctified in Christ Jesus, called to be saints, with all that in every place call upon the name of Jesus Christ our Lord, both theirs and ours:

**[2 Corinthians 1:1](/bible/2-corinthians/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-corinthians/1#v1)</sup> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, and Timothy our brother, unto the church of God which is at Corinth, with all the saints which are in all Achaia:

**[Revelation 13:10](/bible/revelation/13#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/revelation/13#v10)</sup> He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity: he that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword. Here is the patience and the faith of the saints.

**[Revelation 14:12](/bible/revelation/14#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/14#v12)</sup> Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus.
