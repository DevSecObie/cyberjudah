**[1 John 3:10](/bible/1-john/3#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-john/3#v10)</sup> In this the children of God are manifest, and the children of the devil: whosoever doeth not righteousness is not of God, neither he that loveth not his brother.

**[Romans 12:10](/bible/romans/12#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/romans/12#v10)</sup> Be kindly affectioned one to another with brotherly love; in honour preferring one another;

**[Leviticus 19:17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)** · taught in [Leviticus 18-21](/study/leviticus/18-21)

> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him.

**[1 Thessalonians 4:9](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v9)</sup> But as touching brotherly love ye need not that I write unto you: for ye yourselves are taught of God to love one another.

**[Hebrews 13:1](/bible/hebrews/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/13#v1)</sup> Let brotherly love continue.

**[Sirach 25:1](/bible/sirach/25#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/25#v1)</sup> In three things I was beautified, and stood up beautiful both before God and men: the unity of brethren, the love of neighbours, a man and a wife that agree together.
