**[Wisdom of Solomon 13:3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v3)</sup> With whose beauty if they being delighted took them to be gods; let them know how much better the Lord of them is: for the first author of beauty hath created them.

**[Judith 10:7](/bible/judith/10#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/judith/10#v7)</sup> And when they saw her, that her countenance was altered, and her apparel was changed, they wondered at her beauty very greatly, and said unto her.

**[Lamentations 2:15](/bible/lamentations/2#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/lamentations/2#v15)</sup> All that pass by clap their hands at thee; they hiss and wag their head at the daughter of Jerusalem, saying, Is this the city that men call The perfection of beauty, The joy of the whole earth?

**[Psalms 50:2](/bible/psalms/50#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/50#v2)</sup> Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God hath shined.

**[Ezekiel 16:14](/bible/ezekiel/16#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/16#v14)</sup> And thy renown went forth among the heathen for thy beauty: for it was perfect through my comeliness, which I had put upon thee, saith the Lord God.
