**[Wisdom of Solomon 8:5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v5)</sup> If riches be a possession to be desired in this life; what is richer than wisdom, that worketh all things?

**[Baruch 3:9](/bible/baruch/3#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/baruch/3#v9)</sup> Hear, Israel, the commandments of life: give ear to understand wisdom.

**[James 1:5](/bible/james/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/james/1#v5)</sup> If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.

**[Psalms 111:10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)</sup> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do his commandments: his praise endureth for ever.

**[1 Kings 4:29](/bible/1-kings/4#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/1-kings/4#v29)</sup> And God gave Solomon wisdom and understanding exceeding much, and largeness of heart, even as the sand that is on the sea shore.

**[Job 28:28](/bible/job/28#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/job/28#v28)</sup> And unto man he said, Behold, the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom; and to depart from evil is understanding.

**[1 Corinthians 1:24](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v24)</sup> But unto them which are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the power of God, and the wisdom of God.

**[Proverbs 4:7](/bible/proverbs/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/4#v7)</sup> Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.

**[Proverbs 8:12](/bible/proverbs/8#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/8#v12)</sup> I wisdom dwell with prudence, and find out knowledge of witty inventions.

**[Sirach 1:26](/bible/sirach/1#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/1#v26)</sup> If thou desire wisdom, keep the commandments, and the Lord shall give her unto thee.

**[Sirach 6:37](/bible/sirach/6#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/sirach/6#v37)</sup> Let thy mind be upon the ordinances of the Lord and meditate continually in his commandments: he shall establish thine heart, and give thee wisdom at thine owns desire.

**[Sirach 11:15](/bible/sirach/11#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/11#v15)</sup> Wisdom, knowledge, and understanding of the law, are of the Lord: love, and the way of good works, are from him.

**[Sirach 19:20](/bible/sirach/19#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/19#v20)</sup> The fear of the Lord is all wisdom; and in all wisdom is the performance of the law, and the knowledge of his omnipotency.

**[Sirach 21:11](/bible/sirach/21#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/21#v11)</sup> He that keepeth the law of the Lord getteth the understanding thereof: and the perfection of the fear of the Lord is wisdom.

**[Sirach 38:25](/bible/sirach/38#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/38#v25)</sup> How can he get wisdom that holdeth the plough, and that glorieth in the goad, that driveth oxen, and is occupied in their labours, and whose talk is of bullocks?

**[Sirach 39:1](/bible/sirach/39#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/39#v1)</sup> But he that giveth his mind to the law of the most High, and is occupied in the meditation thereof, will seek out the wisdom of all the ancient, and be occupied in prophecies.
