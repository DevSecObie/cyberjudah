**[Isaiah 5:7](/bible/isaiah/5#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/5#v7)</sup> For the vineyard of the Lord of hosts is the house of Israel, and the men of Judah his pleasant plant: and he looked for judgment, but behold oppression; for righteousness, but behold a cry.

**[Matthew 20:1](/bible/matthew/20#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/20#v1)</sup> For the kingdom of heaven is like unto a man that is an householder, which went out early in the morning to hire labourers into his vineyard.

**[John 15:5](/bible/john/15#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/john/15#v5)</sup> I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing.
