**[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

**[Deuteronomy 32:7](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v7)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 31-34](/study/deuteronomy/31-34)

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v7)</sup> Remember the days of old, consider the years of many generations: ask thy father, and he will shew thee; thy elders, and they will tell thee.

**[Job 8:8](/bible/job/8#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/job/8#v8)</sup> For enquire, I pray thee, of the former age, and prepare thyself to the search of their fathers:

**[Sirach 2:10](/bible/sirach/2#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/2#v10)</sup> Look at the generations of old, and see; did ever any trust in the Lord, and was confounded? or did any abide in his fear, and was forsaken? or whom did he ever despise, that called upon him?

**[1 Maccabees 2:51](/bible/1-maccabees/2#v51)**

> <sup>[51](/bible/1-maccabees/2#v51)</sup> Call to remembrance what acts our fathers did in their time; so shall ye receive great honour and an everlasting name.
