**[Genesis 3:15](/bible/genesis/3#v15)** · taught in [Genesis 1-4](/study/genesis/1-4)

**[Leviticus 15:16](/bible/leviticus/15#v16)** · taught in [Leviticus 14-15](/study/leviticus/14-15)

> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/15#v16)</sup> And if any man’s seed of copulation go out from him, then he shall wash all his flesh in water, and be unclean until the even.

> <sup>[16](/bible/galatians/3#v16)</sup> Now to Abraham and his seed were the promises made. He saith not, And to seeds, as of many; but as of one, And to thy seed, which is Christ.

> <sup>[16](/bible/hebrews/2#v16)</sup> For verily he took not on him the nature of angels; but he took on him the seed of Abraham.

> <sup>[12](/bible/tobit/4#v12)</sup> Beware of all whoredom, my son, and chiefly take a wife of the seed of thy fathers, and take not a strange woman to wife, which is not of thy father’s tribe: for we are the children of the prophets, Noe, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob: remember, my son, that our fathers from the beginning, even that they all married wives of their own kindred, and were blessed in their children, and their seed shall inherit the land.
