**[Prayer of Manasseh 1:1](/bible/prayer-of-manasseh/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/prayer-of-manasseh/1#v1)</sup> O Lord, Almighty God of our fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and of their righteous seed;

**[Romans 9:7](/bible/romans/9#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/romans/9#v7)</sup> Neither, because they are the seed of Abraham, are they all children: but, In Isaac shall thy seed be called.

**[Genesis 17:19](/bible/genesis/17#v19)** · taught in [Genesis 17-20](/study/genesis/17-20)

> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/17#v19)</sup> And God said, Sarah thy wife shall bear thee a son indeed; and thou shalt call his name Isaac: and I will establish my covenant with him for an everlasting covenant, and with his seed after him.

**[Genesis 21:12](/bible/genesis/21#v12)** · taught in [Genesis 21-24](/study/genesis/21-24)

> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/21#v12)</sup> And God said unto Abraham, Let it not be grievous in thy sight because of the lad, and because of thy bondwoman; in all that Sarah hath said unto thee, hearken unto her voice; for in Isaac shall thy seed be called.

**[Galatians 3:29](/bible/galatians/3#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/galatians/3#v29)</sup> And if ye be Christ’s, then are ye Abraham’s seed, and heirs according to the promise.

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/105#v6)</sup> O ye seed of Abraham his servant, ye children of Jacob his chosen.

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v13)</sup> O ye seed of Israel his servant, ye children of Jacob, his chosen ones.

> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/41#v8)</sup> But thou, Israel, art my servant, Jacob whom I have chosen, the seed of Abraham my friend.

**[1 Esdras 8:70](/bible/1-esdras/8#v70)**

> <sup>[70](/bible/1-esdras/8#v70)</sup> For both they and their sons have married with their daughters, and the holy seed is mixed with the strange people of the land; and from the beginning of this matter the rulers and the great men have been partakers of this iniquity.
