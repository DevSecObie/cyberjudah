**[2 John 1:6](/bible/2-john/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-john/1#v6)</sup> And this is love, that we walk after his commandments. This is the commandment, That, as ye have heard from the beginning, ye should walk in it.

**[1 John 5:3](/bible/1-john/5#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-john/5#v3)</sup> For this is the love of God, that we keep his commandments: and his commandments are not grievous.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 6:18](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v18)</sup> And love is the keeping of her laws; and the giving heed unto her laws is the assurance of incorruption;

**[Romans 13:9](/bible/romans/13#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/romans/13#v9)</sup> For this, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not kill, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness, Thou shalt not covet; and if there be any other commandment, it is briefly comprehended in this saying, namely, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

**[Proverbs 8:17](/bible/proverbs/8#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/8#v17)</sup> I love them that love me; and those that seek me early shall find me.

**[John 14:15](/bible/john/14#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/john/14#v15)</sup> If ye love me, keep my commandments.

**[John 14:21](/bible/john/14#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/john/14#v21)</sup> He that hath my commandments, and keepeth them, he it is that loveth me: and he that loveth me shall be loved of my Father, and I will love him, and will manifest myself to him.
