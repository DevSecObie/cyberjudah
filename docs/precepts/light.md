**[Baruch 4:2](/bible/baruch/4#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/baruch/4#v2)</sup> Turn thee, O Jacob, and take hold of it: walk in the presence of the light thereof, that thou mayest be illuminated.

**[Romans 13:12](/bible/romans/13#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/romans/13#v12)</sup> The night is far spent, the day is at hand: let us therefore cast off the works of darkness, and let us put on the armour of light.

**[Ephesians 5:14](/bible/ephesians/5#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/ephesians/5#v14)</sup> Wherefore he saith, Awake thou that sleepest, and arise from the dead, and Christ shall give thee light.

**[Proverbs 6:23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)</sup> For the commandment is a lamp; and the law is light; and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:

**[Isaiah 8:20](/bible/isaiah/8#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/8#v20)</sup> To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.

**[Isaiah 60:1](/bible/isaiah/60#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/60#v1)</sup> Arise, shine; for thy light is come, and the glory of the Lord is risen upon thee.

**[Matthew 5:16](/bible/matthew/5#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/5#v16)</sup> Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.

**[Matthew 25:4](/bible/matthew/25#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/25#v4)</sup> But the wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps.

**[Psalms 119:105](/bible/psalms/119#v105)**

> <sup>[105](/bible/psalms/119#v105)</sup> Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.
