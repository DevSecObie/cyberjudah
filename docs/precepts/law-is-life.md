**[Baruch 3:9](/bible/baruch/3#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/baruch/3#v9)</sup> Hear, Israel, the commandments of life: give ear to understand wisdom.

**[Baruch 4:1](/bible/baruch/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/baruch/4#v1)</sup> This is the book of the commandments of God, and the law that endureth for ever: all they that keep it shall come to life; but such as leave it shall die.

**[Romans 8:2](/bible/romans/8#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/8#v2)</sup> For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus hath made me free from the law of sin and death.

**[Ephesians 4:18](/bible/ephesians/4#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/ephesians/4#v18)</sup> Having the understanding darkened, being alienated from the life of God through the ignorance that is in them, because of the blindness of their heart:

**[Proverbs 7:2](/bible/proverbs/7#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/7#v2)</sup> Keep my commandments, and live; and my law as the apple of thine eye.

**[2 Esdras 3:7](/bible/2-esdras/3#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-esdras/3#v7)</sup> And unto him thou gavest commandment to love thy way: which he transgressed, and immediately thou appointedst death in him and in his generations, of whom came nations, tribes, people, and kindreds, out of number.

**[2 Esdras 9:37](/bible/2-esdras/9#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/2-esdras/9#v37)</sup> Notwithstanding the law perisheth not, but remaineth in his force.

**[Sirach 17:11](/bible/sirach/17#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/17#v11)</sup> Beside this he gave them knowledge, and the law of life for an heritage.

**[Sirach 19:19](/bible/sirach/19#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/19#v19)</sup> The knowledge of the commandments of the Lord is the doctrine of life: and they that do things that please him shall receive the fruit of the tree of immortality.

**[Sirach 45:5](/bible/sirach/45#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/45#v5)</sup> He made him to hear his voice, and brought him into the dark cloud, and gave him commandments before his face, even the law of life and knowledge, that he might teach Jacob his covenants, and Israel his judgments.

**[John 14:6](/bible/john/14#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/john/14#v6)</sup> Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.
