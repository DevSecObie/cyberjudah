**[Genesis 18:18](/bible/genesis/18#v18)** · taught in [Genesis 17-20](/study/genesis/17-20)

> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/18#v18)</sup> Seeing that Abraham shall surely become a great and mighty nation, and all the nations of the earth shall be blessed in him?

**[Jeremiah 31:1](/bible/jeremiah/31#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/31#v1)</sup> At the same time, saith the Lord, will I be the God of all the families of Israel, and they shall be my people.

**[Sirach 44:21](/bible/sirach/44#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/sirach/44#v21)</sup> Therefore he assured him by an oath, that he would bless the nations in his seed, and that he would multiply him as the dust of the earth, and exalt his seed as the stars, and cause them to inherit from sea to sea, and from the river unto the utmost part of the land.

**[Acts 3:25](/bible/acts/3#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/acts/3#v25)</sup> Ye are the children of the prophets, and of the covenant which God made with our fathers, saying unto Abraham, And in thy seed shall all the kindreds of the earth be blessed.
