**[1 Peter 3:21](/bible/1-peter/3#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/1-peter/3#v21)</sup> The like figure whereunto even baptism doth also now save us (not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the answer of a good conscience toward God,) by the resurrection of Jesus Christ:

**[Job 14:4](/bible/job/14#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/job/14#v4)</sup> Who can bring a clean thing out of an unclean? not one.

**[Isaiah 1:16](/bible/isaiah/1#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/1#v16)</sup> Wash you, make you clean; put away the evil of your doings from before mine eyes; cease to do evil;

**[Sirach 34:4](/bible/sirach/34#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/34#v4)</sup> Of an unclean thing what can be cleansed? and from that thing which is false what truth can come?

**[John 15:3](/bible/john/15#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/john/15#v3)</sup> Now ye are clean through the word which I have spoken unto you.

**[Psalms 119:9](/bible/psalms/119#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/119#v9)</sup> Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? by taking heed thereto according to thy word.
