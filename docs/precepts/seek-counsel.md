**[Proverbs 11:14](/bible/proverbs/11#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/11#v14)</sup> Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.

**[Proverbs 15:22](/bible/proverbs/15#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/15#v22)</sup> Without counsel purposes are disappointed: but in the multitude of counsellors they are established.

**[Proverbs 20:18](/bible/proverbs/20#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/20#v18)</sup> Every purpose is established by counsel: and with good advice make war.

**[Proverbs 24:6](/bible/proverbs/24#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/24#v6)</sup> For by wise counsel thou shalt make thy war: and in multitude of counsellors there is safety.

**[Tobit 4:18](/bible/tobit/4#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/tobit/4#v18)</sup> Ask counsel of all that are wise, and despise not any counsel that is profitable.

**[Sirach 6:6](/bible/sirach/6#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/6#v6)</sup> Be in peace with many: nevertheless have but one counsellor of a thousand.

**[Sirach 32:19](/bible/sirach/32#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/32#v19)</sup> Do nothing without advice; and when thou hast once done, repent not.

**[Sirach 37:11](/bible/sirach/37#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/37#v11)</sup> Neither consult with a woman touching her of whom she is jealous; neither with a coward in matters of war; nor with a merchant concerning exchange; nor with a buyer of selling; nor with an envious man of thankfulness; nor with an unmerciful man touching kindness; nor with the slothful for any work; nor with an hireling for a year of finishing work; nor with an idle servant of much business: hearken not unto these in any matter of counsel.

**[Sirach 37:16](/bible/sirach/37#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/37#v16)</sup> Let reason go before every enterprize, and counsel before every action.

**[Sirach 40:25](/bible/sirach/40#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/40#v25)</sup> Gold and silver make the foot stand sure: but counsel is esteemed above them both.
