**[1 Peter 3:18](/bible/1-peter/3#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/1-peter/3#v18)</sup> For Christ also hath once suffered for sins, the just for the unjust, that he might bring us to God, being put to death in the flesh, but quickened by the Spirit:

**[1 Peter 4:5](/bible/1-peter/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-peter/4#v5)</sup> Who shall give account to him that is ready to judge the quick and the dead.

**[Ephesians 2:1](/bible/ephesians/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ephesians/2#v1)</sup> And you hath he quickened, who were dead in trespasses and sins;

**[Hebrews 4:12](/bible/hebrews/4#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/4#v12)</sup> For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.

**[John 5:21](/bible/john/5#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/john/5#v21)</sup> For as the Father raiseth up the dead, and quickeneth them; even so the Son quickeneth whom he will.

**[John 6:63](/bible/john/6#v63)**

> <sup>[63](/bible/john/6#v63)</sup> It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.

**[Psalms 119:50](/bible/psalms/119#v50)**

> <sup>[50](/bible/psalms/119#v50)</sup> This is my comfort in my affliction: for thy word hath quickened me.
