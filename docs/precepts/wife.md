**[Ephesians 5:22](/bible/ephesians/5#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/ephesians/5#v22)</sup> Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as unto the Lord.

**[1 Corinthians 11:9](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v9)</sup> Neither was the man created for the woman; but the woman for the man.

**[Proverbs 18:22](/bible/proverbs/18#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/18#v22)</sup> Whoso findeth a wife findeth a good thing, and obtaineth favour of the Lord.

**[Sirach 7:26](/bible/sirach/7#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/7#v26)</sup> Hast thou a wife after thy mind? forsake her not: but give not thyself over to a light woman.

**[Sirach 26:3](/bible/sirach/26#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/26#v3)</sup> A good wife is a good portion, which shall be given in the portion of them that fear the Lord.

**[Sirach 26:13](/bible/sirach/26#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/26#v13)</sup> The grace of a wife delighteth her husband, and her discretion will fatten his bones.

**[Sirach 26:16](/bible/sirach/26#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/26#v16)</sup> As the sun when it ariseth in the high heaven; so is the beauty of a good wife in the ordering of her house.

**[Sirach 36:24](/bible/sirach/36#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/36#v24)</sup> He that getteth a wife beginneth a possession, a help like unto himself, and a pillar of rest.
