**[Joel 2:32](/bible/joel/2#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/joel/2#v32)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that whosoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall be delivered: for in mount Zion and in Jerusalem shall be deliverance, as the Lord hath said, and in the remnant whom the Lord shall call.

**[John 3:16](/bible/john/3#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/john/3#v16)</sup> For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.

**[Acts 2:21](/bible/acts/2#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/acts/2#v21)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that whosoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall be saved.

**[Acts 10:43](/bible/acts/10#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/acts/10#v43)</sup> To him give all the prophets witness, that through his name whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins.

**[Acts 13:26](/bible/acts/13#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/acts/13#v26)</sup> Men and brethren, children of the stock of Abraham, and whosoever among you feareth God, to you is the word of this salvation sent.
