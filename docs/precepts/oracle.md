**[Romans 3:2](/bible/romans/3#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/3#v2)</sup> Much every way: chiefly, because that unto them were committed the oracles of God.

**[2 Samuel 16:23](/bible/2-samuel/16#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/2-samuel/16#v23)</sup> And the counsel of Ahithophel, which he counselled in those days, was as if a man had enquired at the oracle of God: so was all the counsel of Ahithophel both with David and with Absalom.

**[1 Peter 4:11](/bible/1-peter/4#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-peter/4#v11)</sup> If any man speak, let him speak as the oracles of God; if any man minister, let him do it as of the ability which God giveth: that God in all things may be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom be praise and dominion for ever and ever. Amen.

> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/33#v3)</sup> A man of understanding trusteth in the law; and the law is faithful unto him, as an oracle.
