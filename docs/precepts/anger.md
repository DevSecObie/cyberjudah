**[Ecclesiastes 7:9](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v9)</sup> Be not hasty in thy spirit to be angry: for anger resteth in the bosom of fools.

**[James 1:19](/bible/james/1#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/james/1#v19)</sup> Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath:

**[Psalms 37:8](/bible/psalms/37#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/37#v8)</sup> Cease from anger, and forsake wrath: fret not thyself in any wise to do evil.

**[Ephesians 4:26](/bible/ephesians/4#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/ephesians/4#v26)</sup> Be ye angry, and sin not: let not the sun go down upon your wrath:

**[Proverbs 14:17](/bible/proverbs/14#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/14#v17)</sup> He that is soon angry dealeth foolishly: and a man of wicked devices is hated.

**[Proverbs 14:29](/bible/proverbs/14#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/14#v29)</sup> He that is slow to wrath is of great understanding: but he that is hasty of spirit exalteth folly.

**[Proverbs 15:1](/bible/proverbs/15#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/15#v1)</sup> A soft answer turneth away wrath: but grievous words stir up anger.

**[Proverbs 16:32](/bible/proverbs/16#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/16#v32)</sup> He that is slow to anger is better than the mighty; and he that ruleth his spirit than he that taketh a city.

**[Matthew 5:22](/bible/matthew/5#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/5#v22)</sup> But I say unto you, That whosoever is angry with his brother without a cause shall be in danger of the judgment: and whosoever shall say to his brother, Raca, shall be in danger of the council: but whosoever shall say, Thou fool, shall be in danger of hell fire.
