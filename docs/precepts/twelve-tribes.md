**[James 1:1](/bible/james/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/james/1#v1)</sup> James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad, greeting.

**[1 Esdras 7:8](/bible/1-esdras/7#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-esdras/7#v8)</sup> And twelve goats for the sin of all Israel, according to the number of the chief of the tribes of Israel.

**[Revelation 21:12](/bible/revelation/21#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/21#v12)</sup> And had a wall great and high, and had twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels, and names written thereon, which are the names of the twelve tribes of the children of Israel:

**[1 Kings 18:31](/bible/1-kings/18#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/1-kings/18#v31)</sup> And Elijah took twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Jacob, unto whom the word of the Lord came, saying, Israel shall be thy name:
