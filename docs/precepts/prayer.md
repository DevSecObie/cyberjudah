**[1 John 5:14](/bible/1-john/5#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-john/5#v14)</sup> And this is the confidence that we have in him, that, if we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us:

**[1 Timothy 2:8](/bible/1-timothy/2#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-timothy/2#v8)</sup> I will therefore that men pray every where, lifting up holy hands, without wrath and doubting.

**[Philippians 4:6](/bible/philippians/4#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/philippians/4#v6)</sup> Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God.

**[James 4:3](/bible/james/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/james/4#v3)</sup> Ye ask, and receive not, because ye ask amiss, that ye may consume it upon your lusts.

**[James 5:16](/bible/james/5#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/james/5#v16)</sup> Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed. The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.

**[Daniel 6:10](/bible/daniel/6#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/daniel/6#v10)</sup> Now when Daniel knew that the writing was signed, he went into his house; and his windows being open in his chamber toward Jerusalem, he kneeled upon his knees three times a day, and prayed, and gave thanks before his God, as he did aforetime.

**[1 Thessalonians 5:17](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v17)</sup> Pray without ceasing.

**[Psalms 55:17](/bible/psalms/55#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/55#v17)</sup> Evening, and morning, and at noon, will I pray, and cry aloud: and he shall hear my voice.

**[1 Kings 8:22](/bible/1-kings/8#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/1-kings/8#v22)</sup> And Solomon stood before the altar of the Lord in the presence of all the congregation of Israel, and spread forth his hands toward heaven:

**[1 Kings 8:48](/bible/1-kings/8#v48)**

> <sup>[48](/bible/1-kings/8#v48)</sup> And so return unto thee with all their heart, and with all their soul, in the land of their enemies, which led them away captive, and pray unto thee toward their land, which thou gavest unto their fathers, the city which thou hast chosen, and the house which I have built for thy name:

**[Tobit 3:11](/bible/tobit/3#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/tobit/3#v11)</sup> Then she prayed toward the window, and said, Blessed art thou, O Lord my God, and thine holy and glorious name is blessed and honourable for ever: let all thy works praise thee for ever.

**[Tobit 12:8](/bible/tobit/12#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/tobit/12#v8)</sup> Prayer is good with fasting and alms and righteousness. A little with righteousness is better than much with unrighteousness. It is better to give alms than to lay up gold:

**[Mark 14:38](/bible/mark/14#v38)**

> <sup>[38](/bible/mark/14#v38)</sup> Watch ye and pray, lest ye enter into temptation. The spirit truly is ready, but the flesh is weak.

**[Sirach 7:14](/bible/sirach/7#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/7#v14)</sup> Use not many words in a multitude of elders, and make not much babbling when thou prayest.

**[Sirach 18:23](/bible/sirach/18#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/sirach/18#v23)</sup> Before thou prayest, prepare thyself; and be not as one that tempteth the Lord.

**[Sirach 33:4](/bible/sirach/33#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/33#v4)</sup> Prepare what to say, and so thou shalt be heard: and bind up instruction, and then make answer.

**[Matthew 6:5](/bible/matthew/6#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/6#v5)</sup> And when thou prayest, thou shalt not be as the hypocrites are: for they love to pray standing in the synagogues and in the corners of the streets, that they may be seen of men. Verily I say unto you, They have their reward.

**[Matthew 9:38](/bible/matthew/9#v38)**

> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/9#v38)</sup> Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he will send forth labourers into his harvest.

**[Matthew 14:23](/bible/matthew/14#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/14#v23)</sup> And when he had sent the multitudes away, he went up into a mountain apart to pray: and when the evening was come, he was there alone.

**[Matthew 21:22](/bible/matthew/21#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/21#v22)</sup> And all things, whatsoever ye shall ask in prayer, believing, ye shall receive.

**[Matthew 26:41](/bible/matthew/26#v41)**

> <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/26#v41)</sup> Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.

**[Luke 21:36](/bible/luke/21#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/luke/21#v36)</sup> Watch ye therefore, and pray always, that ye may be accounted worthy to escape all these things that shall come to pass, and to stand before the Son of man.
