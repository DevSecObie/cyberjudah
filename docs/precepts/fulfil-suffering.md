**[Matthew 5:17](/bible/matthew/5#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/5#v17)</sup> Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil.

**[Luke 24:44](/bible/luke/24#v44)**

> <sup>[44](/bible/luke/24#v44)</sup> And he said unto them, These are the words which I spake unto you, while I was yet with you, that all things must be fulfilled, which were written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the psalms, concerning me.

**[Acts 3:18](/bible/acts/3#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/acts/3#v18)</sup> But those things, which God before had shewed by the mouth of all his prophets, that Christ should suffer, he hath so fulfilled.
