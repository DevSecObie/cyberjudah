**[1 Timothy 1:5](/bible/1-timothy/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-timothy/1#v5)</sup> Now the end of the commandment is charity out of a pure heart, and of a good conscience, and of faith unfeigned:

**[1 Peter 3:16](/bible/1-peter/3#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/1-peter/3#v16)</sup> Having a good conscience; that, whereas they speak evil of you, as of evildoers, they may be ashamed that falsely accuse your good conversation in Christ.

**[1 Peter 3:21](/bible/1-peter/3#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/1-peter/3#v21)</sup> The like figure whereunto even baptism doth also now save us (not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the answer of a good conscience toward God,) by the resurrection of Jesus Christ:

**[Hebrews 13:18](/bible/hebrews/13#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/hebrews/13#v18)</sup> Pray for us: for we trust we have a good conscience, in all things willing to live honestly.
