**[Philippians 1:10](/bible/philippians/1#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/philippians/1#v10)</sup> That ye may approve things that are excellent; that ye may be sincere and without offence till the day of Christ;

**[1 Corinthians 8:13](/bible/1-corinthians/8#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-corinthians/8#v13)</sup> Wherefore, if meat make my brother to offend, I will eat no flesh while the world standeth, lest I make my brother to offend.

**[Mark 4:17](/bible/mark/4#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/mark/4#v17)</sup> And have no root in themselves, and so endure but for a time: afterward, when affliction or persecution ariseth for the word’s sake, immediately they are offended.

**[Mark 9:42](/bible/mark/9#v42)**

> <sup>[42](/bible/mark/9#v42)</sup> And whosoever shall offend one of these little ones that believe in me, it is better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and he were cast into the sea.

**[Sirach 18:27](/bible/sirach/18#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/sirach/18#v27)</sup> A wise man will fear in every thing, and in the day of sinning he will beware of offence: but a fool will not observe time.

**[Sirach 28:7](/bible/sirach/28#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/28#v7)</sup> Remember the commandments, and bear no malice to thy neighbour: remember the covenant of the Highest, and wink at ignorance.

**[Matthew 11:6](/bible/matthew/11#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/11#v6)</sup> And blessed is he, whosoever shall not be offended in me.

**[Matthew 13:21](/bible/matthew/13#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/13#v21)</sup> Yet hath he not root in himself, but dureth for a while: for when tribulation or persecution ariseth because of the word, by and by he is offended.

**[Matthew 18:7](/bible/matthew/18#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/18#v7)</sup> Woe unto the world because of offences! for it must needs be that offences come; but woe to that man by whom the offence cometh!

**[Matthew 24:10](/bible/matthew/24#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/24#v10)</sup> And then shall many be offended, and shall betray one another, and shall hate one another.

**[Luke 7:23](/bible/luke/7#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/luke/7#v23)</sup> And blessed is he, whosoever shall not be offended in me.

**[John 16:1](/bible/john/16#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/john/16#v1)</sup> These things have I spoken unto you, that ye should not be offended.

**[Acts 24:16](/bible/acts/24#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/acts/24#v16)</sup> And herein do I exercise myself, to have always a conscience void of offence toward God, and toward men.
