**[1 Timothy 5:20](/bible/1-timothy/5#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/1-timothy/5#v20)</sup> Them that sin rebuke before all, that others also may fear.

**[2 Timothy 3:16](/bible/2-timothy/3#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/2-timothy/3#v16)</sup> All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness:

**[Leviticus 19:17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)** · taught in [Leviticus 18-21](/study/leviticus/18-21)

> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him.

**[Galatians 6:1](/bible/galatians/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/galatians/6#v1)</sup> Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted.

**[Psalms 141:5](/bible/psalms/141#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/141#v5)</sup> Let the righteous smite me; it shall be a kindness: and let him reprove me; it shall be an excellent oil, which shall not break my head: for yet my prayer also shall be in their calamities.

**[Job 5:17](/bible/job/5#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/job/5#v17)</sup> Behold, happy is the man whom God correcteth: therefore despise not thou the chastening of the Almighty:

**[Proverbs 6:23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)</sup> For the commandment is a lamp; and the law is light; and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:

**[Proverbs 15:10](/bible/proverbs/15#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/15#v10)</sup> Correction is grievous unto him that forsaketh the way: and he that hateth reproof shall die.

**[Hebrews 12:9](/bible/hebrews/12#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/hebrews/12#v9)</sup> Furthermore we have had fathers of our flesh which corrected us, and we gave them reverence: shall we not much rather be in subjection unto the Father of spirits, and live?

**[Proverbs 27:5](/bible/proverbs/27#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/27#v5)</sup> Open rebuke is better than secret love.

**[Proverbs 29:1](/bible/proverbs/29#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/29#v1)</sup> He, that being often reproved hardeneth his neck, shall suddenly be destroyed, and that without remedy.

**[2 Esdras 14:13](/bible/2-esdras/14#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/2-esdras/14#v13)</sup> Now therefore set thine house in order, and reprove thy people, comfort such of them as be in trouble, and now renounce corruption,

**[Jeremiah 10:24](/bible/jeremiah/10#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/10#v24)</sup> O Lord, correct me, but with judgment; not in thine anger, lest thou bring me to nothing.

**[Sirach 16:12](/bible/sirach/16#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/16#v12)</sup> As his mercy is great, so is his correction also: he judgeth a man according to his works

**[Sirach 20:3](/bible/sirach/20#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/20#v3)</sup> How good is it, when thou art reproved, to shew repentance! for so shalt thou escape wilful sin.

**[Sirach 21:6](/bible/sirach/21#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/21#v6)</sup> He that hateth to be reproved is in the way of sinners: but he that feareth the Lord will repent from his heart.

**[Sirach 32:17](/bible/sirach/32#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/32#v17)</sup> A sinful man will not be reproved, but findeth an excuse according to his will.
