**[Colossians 2:16](/bible/colossians/2#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/colossians/2#v16)</sup> Let no man therefore judge you in meat, or in drink, or in respect of an holyday, or of the new moon, or of the sabbath days:

**[Numbers 28:9](/bible/numbers/28#v9)** (key) · taught in [Numbers 27-30](/study/numbers/27-30)

> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/28#v9)</sup> And on the sabbath day two lambs of the first year without spot, and two tenth deals of flour for a meat offering, mingled with oil, and the drink offering thereof:

**[Leviticus 23:37](/bible/leviticus/23#v37)** · taught in [Leviticus 22-25](/study/leviticus/22-25)

> <sup>[37](/bible/leviticus/23#v37)</sup> These are the feasts of the Lord, which ye shall proclaim to be holy convocations, to offer an offering made by fire unto the Lord, a burnt offering, and a meat offering, a sacrifice, and drink offerings, every thing upon his day:

**[Galatians 5:1](/bible/galatians/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/galatians/5#v1)</sup> Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free, and be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.

**[Psalms 50:5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)</sup> Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.

**[Hebrews 9:9](/bible/hebrews/9#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/hebrews/9#v9)</sup> Which was a figure for the time then present, in which were offered both gifts and sacrifices, that could not make him that did the service perfect, as pertaining to the conscience;

**[Hebrews 10:1](/bible/hebrews/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/10#v1)</sup> For the law having a shadow of good things to come, and not the very image of the things, can never with those sacrifices which they offered year by year continually make the comers thereunto perfect.

**[Hebrews 10:8](/bible/hebrews/10#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/hebrews/10#v8)</sup> Above when he said, Sacrifice and offering and burnt offerings and offering for sin thou wouldest not, neither hadst pleasure therein; which are offered by the law;

**[1 Esdras 7:8](/bible/1-esdras/7#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-esdras/7#v8)</sup> And twelve goats for the sin of all Israel, according to the number of the chief of the tribes of Israel.

**[Ezekiel 45:17](/bible/ezekiel/45#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/45#v17)</sup> And it shall be the prince’s part to give burnt offerings, and meat offerings, and drink offerings, in the feasts, and in the new moons, and in the sabbaths, in all solemnities of the house of Israel: he shall prepare the sin offering, and the meat offering, and the burnt offering, and the peace offerings, to make reconciliation for the house of Israel.

**[Matthew 12:7](/bible/matthew/12#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/12#v7)</sup> But if ye had known what this meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice, ye would not have condemned the guiltless.

**[John 1:29](/bible/john/1#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/john/1#v29)</sup> The next day John seeth Jesus coming unto him, and saith, Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world.

**[Acts 13:39](/bible/acts/13#v39)**

> <sup>[39](/bible/acts/13#v39)</sup> And by him all that believe are justified from all things, from which ye could not be justified by the law of Moses.

**[Acts 15:10](/bible/acts/15#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/acts/15#v10)</sup> Now therefore why tempt ye God, to put a yoke upon the neck of the disciples, which neither our fathers nor we were able to bear?
