**[Leviticus 19:18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18)** · taught in [Leviticus 18-21](/study/leviticus/18-21)

> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18)</sup> Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am the Lord.

**[Mark 12:31](/bible/mark/12#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/mark/12#v31)</sup> And the second is like, namely this, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself. There is none other commandment greater than these.

**[Matthew 5:43](/bible/matthew/5#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/matthew/5#v43)</sup> Ye have heard that it hath been said, Thou shalt love thy neighbour, and hate thine enemy.

**[Matthew 22:39](/bible/matthew/22#v39)**

> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/22#v39)</sup> And the second is like unto it, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

**[Luke 10:36](/bible/luke/10#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/luke/10#v36)</sup> Which now of these three, thinkest thou, was neighbour unto him that fell among the thieves?
