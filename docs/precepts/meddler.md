**[Proverbs 20:19](/bible/proverbs/20#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/20#v19)</sup> He that goeth about as a talebearer revealeth secrets: therefore meddle not with him that flattereth with his lips.

**[Proverbs 24:21](/bible/proverbs/24#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/24#v21)</sup> My son, fear thou the Lord and the king: and meddle not with them that are given to change:

**[Proverbs 26:17](/bible/proverbs/26#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/26#v17)</sup> He that passeth by, and meddleth with strife belonging not to him, is like one that taketh a dog by the ears.

**[2 Esdras 16:67](/bible/2-esdras/16#v67)**

> <sup>[67](/bible/2-esdras/16#v67)</sup> Behold, God himself is the judge, fear him: leave off from your sins, and forget your iniquities, to meddle no more with them for ever: so shall God lead you forth, and deliver you from all trouble.

**[Sirach 11:10](/bible/sirach/11#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/11#v10)</sup> My son, meddle not with many matters: for if thou meddle much, thou shalt not be innocent; and if thou follow after, thou shalt not obtain, neither shalt thou escape by fleeing.
