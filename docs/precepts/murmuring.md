**[Jude 1:16](/bible/jude/1#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jude/1#v16)</sup> These are murmurers, complainers, walking after their own lusts; and their mouth speaketh great swelling words, having men’s persons in admiration because of advantage.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 1:10](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v10)</sup> For the ear of jealousy heareth all things: and the noise of murmurings is not hid.

**[Philippians 2:14](/bible/philippians/2#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/philippians/2#v14)</sup> Do all things without murmurings and disputings:

**[Numbers 11:1](/bible/numbers/11#v1)** · taught in [Numbers 11-14](/study/numbers/11-14)

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/11#v1)</sup> And when the people complained, it displeased the Lord: and the Lord heard it; and his anger was kindled; and the fire of the Lord burnt among them, and consumed them that were in the uttermost parts of the camp.

**[1 Corinthians 10:10](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v10)</sup> Neither murmur ye, as some of them also murmured, and were destroyed of the destroyer.

**[Sirach 28:13](/bible/sirach/28#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/28#v13)</sup> Curse the whisperer and doubletongued: for such have destroyed many that were at peace.

**[John 6:43](/bible/john/6#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/john/6#v43)</sup> Jesus therefore answered and said unto them, Murmur not among yourselves.

**[Numbers 14:27](/bible/numbers/14#v27)** · taught in [Numbers 11-14](/study/numbers/11-14)

> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/14#v27)</sup> How long shall I bear with this evil congregation, which murmur against me? I have heard the murmurings of the children of Israel, which they murmur against me.
