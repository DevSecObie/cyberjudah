**[3 John 1:2](/bible/3-john/1#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/3-john/1#v2)</sup> Beloved, I wish above all things that thou mayest prosper and be in health, even as thy soul prospereth.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 1:3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v3)</sup> For froward thoughts separate from God: and his power, when it is tried, reproveth the unwise.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 2:14](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/2#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/2#v14)</sup> He was made to reprove our thoughts.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 9:15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v15)</sup> For the corruptible body presseth down the soul, and the earthy tabernacle weigheth down the mind that museth upon many things.

**[Philippians 4:7](/bible/philippians/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/philippians/4#v7)</sup> And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

**[Proverbs 12:25](/bible/proverbs/12#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/12#v25)</sup> Heaviness in the heart of man maketh it stoop: but a good word maketh it glad.

> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/26#v3)</sup> Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.

> <sup>[15](/bible/2-esdras/14#v15)</sup> And set aside the thoughts that are most heavy unto thee, and haste thee to flee from these times.

**[Sirach 40:2](/bible/sirach/40#v2)**
