**[Romans 12:20](/bible/romans/12#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/romans/12#v20)</sup> Therefore if thine enemy hunger, feed him; if he thirst, give him drink: for in so doing thou shalt heap coals of fire on his head.

**[Exodus 23:4](/bible/exodus/23#v4)** · taught in [Exodus 22-25](/study/exodus/22-25)

> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/23#v4)</sup> If thou meet thine enemy’s ox or his ass going astray, thou shalt surely bring it back to him again.

**[Deuteronomy 22:1](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v1)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 19-22](/study/deuteronomy/19-22)

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v1)</sup> Thou shalt not see thy brother’s ox or his sheep go astray, and hide thyself from them: thou shalt in any case bring them again unto thy brother.

> <sup>[29](/bible/1-samuel/18#v29)</sup> And Saul was yet the more afraid of David; and Saul became David’s enemy continually.

**[1 Samuel 24:19](/bible/1-samuel/24#v19)** · taught in [1 Samuel 24-27](/study/1-samuel/24-27)

> <sup>[19](/bible/1-samuel/24#v19)</sup> For if a man find his enemy, will he let him go well away? wherefore the Lord reward thee good for that thou hast done unto me this day.
