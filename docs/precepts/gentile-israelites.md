**[Romans 9:25](/bible/romans/9#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/romans/9#v25)</sup> As he saith also in Osee, I will call them my people, which were not my people; and her beloved, which was not beloved.

**[Hosea 1:10](/bible/hosea/1#v10)** (key)

> <sup>[10](/bible/hosea/1#v10)</sup> Yet the number of the children of Israel shall be as the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured nor numbered; and it shall come to pass, that in the place where it was said unto them, Ye are not my people, there it shall be said unto them, Ye are the sons of the living God.

**[Galatians 3:8](/bible/galatians/3#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/galatians/3#v8)</sup> And the scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the heathen through faith, preached before the gospel unto Abraham, saying, In thee shall all nations be blessed.

**[Ephesians 2:11](/bible/ephesians/2#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/ephesians/2#v11)</sup> Wherefore remember, that ye being in time past Gentiles in the flesh, who are called Uncircumcision by that which is called the Circumcision in the flesh made by hands;

**[1 Corinthians 12:2](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v2)</sup> Ye know that ye were Gentiles, carried away unto these dumb idols, even as ye were led.

**[Matthew 4:15](/bible/matthew/4#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/4#v15)</sup> The land of Zabulon, and the land of Nephthalim, by the way of the sea, beyond Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles;

**[2 Maccabees 6:9](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v9)</sup> And whoso would not conform themselves to the manners of the Gentiles should be put to death. Then might a man have seen the present misery.

**[John 7:35](/bible/john/7#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/john/7#v35)</sup> Then said the Jews among themselves, Whither will he go, that we shall not find him? will he go unto the dispersed among the Gentiles, and teach the Gentiles?

**[Acts 10:45](/bible/acts/10#v45)**

> <sup>[45](/bible/acts/10#v45)</sup> And they of the circumcision which believed were astonished, as many as came with Peter, because that on the Gentiles also was poured out the gift of the Holy Ghost.
