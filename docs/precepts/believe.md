**[Titus 3:8](/bible/titus/3#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/titus/3#v8)</sup> This is a faithful saying, and these things I will that thou affirm constantly, that they which have believed in God might be careful to maintain good works. These things are good and profitable unto men.

**[Sirach 32:24](/bible/sirach/32#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/32#v24)</sup> He that believeth in the Lord taketh heed to the commandment; and he that trusteth in him shall fare never the worse.

**[John 3:16](/bible/john/3#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/john/3#v16)</sup> For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.

**[John 7:38](/bible/john/7#v38)**

> <sup>[38](/bible/john/7#v38)</sup> He that believeth on me, as the scripture hath said, out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water.

**[Acts 21:20](/bible/acts/21#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/acts/21#v20)</sup> And when they heard it, they glorified the Lord, and said unto him, Thou seest, brother, how many thousands of Jews there are which believe; and they are all zealous of the law:

**[Acts 24:14](/bible/acts/24#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/acts/24#v14)</sup> But this I confess unto thee, that after the way which they call heresy, so worship I the God of my fathers, believing all things which are written in the law and in the prophets:
