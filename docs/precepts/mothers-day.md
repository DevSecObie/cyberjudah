**[Jeremiah 7:18](/bible/jeremiah/7#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/7#v18)</sup> The children gather wood, and the fathers kindle the fire, and the women knead their dough, to make cakes to the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings unto other gods, that they may provoke me to anger.

**[Jeremiah 44:17](/bible/jeremiah/44#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/44#v17)</sup> But we will certainly do whatsoever thing goeth forth out of our own mouth, to burn incense unto the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings unto her, as we have done, we, and our fathers, our kings, and our princes, in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem: for then had we plenty of victuals, and were well, and saw no evil.

**[Matthew 12:50](/bible/matthew/12#v50)**

> <sup>[50](/bible/matthew/12#v50)</sup> For whosoever shall do the will of my Father which is in heaven, the same is my brother, and sister, and mother.

**[Luke 11:27](/bible/luke/11#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/luke/11#v27)</sup> And it came to pass, as he spake these things, a certain woman of the company lifted up her voice, and said unto him, Blessed is the womb that bare thee, and the paps which thou hast sucked.

**[Acts 19:27](/bible/acts/19#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/acts/19#v27)</sup> So that not only this our craft is in danger to be set at nought; but also that the temple of the great goddess Diana should be despised, and her magnificence should be destroyed, whom all Asia and the world worshippeth.
