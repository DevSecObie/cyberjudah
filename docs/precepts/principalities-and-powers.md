**[Titus 3:1](/bible/titus/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/titus/3#v1)</sup> Put them in mind to be subject to principalities and powers, to obey magistrates, to be ready to every good work,

**[Colossians 2:15](/bible/colossians/2#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/colossians/2#v15)</sup> And having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it.

**[Romans 8:38](/bible/romans/8#v38)**

> <sup>[38](/bible/romans/8#v38)</sup> For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come,

**[Romans 13:1](/bible/romans/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/13#v1)</sup> Let every soul be subject unto the higher powers. For there is no power but of God: the powers that be are ordained of God.

**[Psalms 62:11](/bible/psalms/62#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/62#v11)</sup> God hath spoken once; twice have I heard this; that power belongeth unto God.

**[Ephesians 1:21](/bible/ephesians/1#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/ephesians/1#v21)</sup> Far above all principality, and power, and might, and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this world, but also in that which is to come:

**[Ephesians 6:12](/bible/ephesians/6#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/ephesians/6#v12)</sup> For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.
