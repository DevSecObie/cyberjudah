**[Obadiah 1:4](/bible/obadiah/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/obadiah/1#v4)</sup> Though thou exalt thyself as the eagle, and though thou set thy nest among the stars, thence will I bring thee down, saith the Lord.

**[Lamentations 4:19](/bible/lamentations/4#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/lamentations/4#v19)</sup> Our persecutors are swifter than the eagles of the heaven: they pursued us upon the mountains, they laid wait for us in the wilderness.

**[2 Esdras 12:11](/bible/2-esdras/12#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-esdras/12#v11)</sup> The eagle, whom thou sawest come up from the sea, is the kingdom which was seen in the vision of thy brother Daniel.
