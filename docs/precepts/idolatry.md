**[Colossians 3:5](/bible/colossians/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/colossians/3#v5)</sup> Mortify therefore your members which are upon the earth; fornication, uncleanness, inordinate affection, evil concupiscence, and covetousness, which is idolatry:

**[Exodus 20:3](/bible/exodus/20#v3)** · taught in [Exodus 18-21](/study/exodus/18-21)

> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/20#v3)</sup> Thou shalt have no other gods before me.

**[1 Kings 9:6](/bible/1-kings/9#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-kings/9#v6)</sup> But if ye shall at all turn from following me, ye or your children, and will not keep my commandments and my statutes which I have set before you, but go and serve other gods, and worship them:

**[1 Corinthians 10:14](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v14)</sup> Wherefore, my dearly beloved, flee from idolatry.

**[Ezekiel 14:3](/bible/ezekiel/14#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/14#v3)</sup> Son of man, these men have set up their idols in their heart, and put the stumblingblock of their iniquity before their face: should I be enquired of at all by them?

**[Revelation 22:15](/bible/revelation/22#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/revelation/22#v15)</sup> For without are dogs, and sorcerers, and whoremongers, and murderers, and idolaters, and whosoever loveth and maketh a lie.
