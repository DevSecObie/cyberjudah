**[Joel 3:19](/bible/joel/3#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/joel/3#v19)</sup> Egypt shall be a desolation, and Edom shall be a desolate wilderness, for the violence against the children of Judah, because they have shed innocent blood in their land.

**[Isaiah 66:16](/bible/isaiah/66#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/66#v16)</sup> For by fire and by his sword will the Lord plead with all flesh: and the slain of the Lord shall be many.

**[Jeremiah 25:15](/bible/jeremiah/25#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/25#v15)</sup> For thus saith the Lord God of Israel unto me; Take the wine cup of this fury at my hand, and cause all the nations, to whom I send thee, to drink it.

**[Jeremiah 25:33](/bible/jeremiah/25#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/jeremiah/25#v33)</sup> And the slain of the Lord shall be at that day from one end of the earth even unto the other end of the earth: they shall not be lamented, neither gathered, nor buried; they shall be dung upon the ground.
