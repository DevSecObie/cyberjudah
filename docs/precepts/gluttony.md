**[Proverbs 23:2](/bible/proverbs/23#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/23#v2)</sup> And put a knife to thy throat, if thou be a man given to appetite.

**[Proverbs 23:21](/bible/proverbs/23#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/23#v21)</sup> For the drunkard and the glutton shall come to poverty: and drowsiness shall clothe a man with rags.

**[Sirach 31:20](/bible/sirach/31#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/31#v20)</sup> Sound sleep cometh of moderate eating: he riseth early, and his wits are with him: but the pain of watching, and choler, and pangs of the belly, are with an unsatiable man.

**[Sirach 37:30](/bible/sirach/37#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/sirach/37#v30)</sup> For excess of meats bringeth sickness, and surfeiting will turn into choler.
