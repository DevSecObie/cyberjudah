**[Joel 2:12](/bible/joel/2#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/joel/2#v12)</sup> Therefore also now, saith the Lord, turn ye even to me with all your heart, and with fasting, and with weeping, and with mourning:

**[Jonah 3:7](/bible/jonah/3#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/jonah/3#v7)</sup> And he caused it to be proclaimed and published through Nineveh by the decree of the king and his nobles, saying, Let neither man nor beast, herd nor flock, taste any thing: let them not feed, nor drink water:

**[Leviticus 23:32](/bible/leviticus/23#v32)** · taught in [Leviticus 22-25](/study/leviticus/22-25)

> <sup>[32](/bible/leviticus/23#v32)</sup> It shall be unto you a sabbath of rest, and ye shall afflict your souls: in the ninth day of the month at even, from even unto even, shall ye celebrate your sabbath.

**[1 Corinthians 7:5](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v5)</sup> Defraud ye not one the other, except it be with consent for a time, that ye may give yourselves to fasting and prayer; and come together again, that Satan tempt you not for your incontinency.

**[Isaiah 58:6](/bible/isaiah/58#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/58#v6)</sup> Is not this the fast that I have chosen? to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke?

**[Tobit 12:8](/bible/tobit/12#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/tobit/12#v8)</sup> Prayer is good with fasting and alms and righteousness. A little with righteousness is better than much with unrighteousness. It is better to give alms than to lay up gold:

**[Mark 9:29](/bible/mark/9#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/mark/9#v29)</sup> And he said unto them, This kind can come forth by nothing, but by prayer and fasting.

**[2 Esdras 5:13](/bible/2-esdras/5#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/2-esdras/5#v13)</sup> To shew thee such tokens I have leave; and if thou wilt pray again, and weep as now, and fast even days, thou shalt hear yet greater things.

**[Sirach 34:26](/bible/sirach/34#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/34#v26)</sup> So is it with a man that fasteth for his sins, and goeth again, and doeth the same: who will hear his prayer? or what doth his humbling profit him?

**[Matthew 6:17](/bible/matthew/6#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/6#v17)</sup> But thou, when thou fastest, anoint thine head, and wash thy face;

**[Matthew 17:21](/bible/matthew/17#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/17#v21)</sup> Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.
