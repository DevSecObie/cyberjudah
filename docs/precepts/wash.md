**[Isaiah 1:16](/bible/isaiah/1#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/1#v16)</sup> Wash you, make you clean; put away the evil of your doings from before mine eyes; cease to do evil;

**[Jeremiah 2:22](/bible/jeremiah/2#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/2#v22)</sup> For though thou wash thee with nitre, and take thee much soap, yet thine iniquity is marked before me, saith the Lord God.

**[Jeremiah 4:14](/bible/jeremiah/4#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/4#v14)</sup> O Jerusalem, wash thine heart from wickedness, that thou mayest be saved. How long shall thy vain thoughts lodge within thee?
