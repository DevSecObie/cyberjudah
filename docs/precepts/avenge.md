**[Zephaniah 3:8](/bible/zephaniah/3#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/zephaniah/3#v8)</sup> Therefore wait ye upon me, saith the Lord, until the day that I rise up to the prey: for my determination is to gather the nations, that I may assemble the kingdoms, to pour upon them mine indignation, even all my fierce anger: for all the earth shall be devoured with the fire of my jealousy.

**[Romans 12:19](/bible/romans/12#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/romans/12#v19)</sup> Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves, but rather give place unto wrath: for it is written, Vengeance is mine; I will repay, saith the Lord.

**[Deuteronomy 30:7](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v7)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 27-30](/study/deuteronomy/27-30)

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v7)</sup> And the Lord thy God will put all these curses upon thine enemies, and on them that hate thee, which persecuted thee.

**[Leviticus 19:18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18)** · taught in [Leviticus 18-21](/study/leviticus/18-21)

> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18)</sup> Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am the Lord.

**[Psalms 149:7](/bible/psalms/149#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/149#v7)</sup> To execute vengeance upon the heathen, and punishments upon the people;

**[Isaiah 14:21](/bible/isaiah/14#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/14#v21)</sup> Prepare slaughter for his children for the iniquity of their fathers; that they do not rise, nor possess the land, nor fill the face of the world with cities.

**[Jeremiah 10:25](/bible/jeremiah/10#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/10#v25)</sup> Pour out thy fury upon the heathen that know thee not, and upon the families that call not on thy name: for they have eaten up Jacob, and devoured him, and consumed him, and have made his habitation desolate.

**[Jeremiah 30:16](/bible/jeremiah/30#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/30#v16)</sup> Therefore all they that devour thee shall be devoured; and all thine adversaries, every one of them, shall go into captivity; and they that spoil thee shall be a spoil, and all that prey upon thee will I give for a prey.

**[Jeremiah 46:10](/bible/jeremiah/46#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/46#v10)</sup> For this is the day of the Lord God of hosts, a day of vengeance, that he may avenge him of his adversaries: and the sword shall devour, and it shall be satiate and made drunk with their blood: for the Lord God of hosts hath a sacrifice in the north country by the river Euphrates.

**[Ezekiel 25:14](/bible/ezekiel/25#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/25#v14)</sup> And I will lay my vengeance upon Edom by the hand of my people Israel: and they shall do in Edom according to mine anger and according to my fury; and they shall know my vengeance, saith the Lord God.

**[Sirach 39:28](/bible/sirach/39#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/sirach/39#v28)</sup> There be spirits that are created for vengeance, which in their fury lay on sore strokes; in the time of destruction they pour out their force, and appease the wrath of him that made them.

**[2 Maccabees 1:28](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v28)</sup> Punish them that oppress us, and with pride do us wrong.

**[Luke 18:7](/bible/luke/18#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/luke/18#v7)</sup> And shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry day and night unto him, though he bear long with them?

**[Revelation 13:10](/bible/revelation/13#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/revelation/13#v10)</sup> He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity: he that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword. Here is the patience and the faith of the saints.
