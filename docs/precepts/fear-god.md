**[Psalms 34:7](/bible/psalms/34#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/34#v7)</sup> The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.

**[Proverbs 8:13](/bible/proverbs/8#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/8#v13)</sup> The fear of the Lord is to hate evil: pride, and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the froward mouth, do I hate.

**[2 Corinthians 7:1](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v1)</sup> Having therefore these promises, dearly beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all filthiness of the flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in the fear of God.

**[Sirach 2:15](/bible/sirach/2#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/2#v15)</sup> They that fear the Lord will not disobey his Word; and they that love him will keep his ways.

**[Sirach 19:24](/bible/sirach/19#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/19#v24)</sup> He that hath small understanding, and feareth God, is better than one that hath much wisdom, and transgresseth the law of the most High.

**[Sirach 34:13](/bible/sirach/34#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/34#v13)</sup> The spirit of those that fear the Lord shall live; for their hope is in him that saveth them.

**[Matthew 10:28](/bible/matthew/10#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/10#v28)</sup> And fear not them which kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul: but rather fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.

**[Luke 12:5](/bible/luke/12#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/luke/12#v5)</sup> But I will forewarn you whom ye shall fear: Fear him, which after he hath killed hath power to cast into hell; yea, I say unto you, Fear him.

**[Psalms 96:4](/bible/psalms/96#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/96#v4)</sup> For the Lord is great, and greatly to be praised: he is to be feared above all gods.
