**[Deuteronomy 6:5](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v5)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 3-6](/study/deuteronomy/3-6)

> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v5)</sup> And thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thine heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy might.

**[Leviticus 19:18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18)** · taught in [Leviticus 18-21](/study/leviticus/18-21)

> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18)</sup> Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am the Lord.

**[Mark 12:30](/bible/mark/12#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/mark/12#v30)</sup> And thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind, and with all thy strength: this is the first commandment.

**[Mark 12:31](/bible/mark/12#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/mark/12#v31)</sup> And the second is like, namely this, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself. There is none other commandment greater than these.

**[Matthew 22:37](/bible/matthew/22#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/22#v37)</sup> Jesus said unto him, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind.

**[Matthew 22:39](/bible/matthew/22#v39)**

> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/22#v39)</sup> And the second is like unto it, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

**[Luke 10:27](/bible/luke/10#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/luke/10#v27)</sup> And he answering said, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy strength, and with all thy mind; and thy neighbour as thyself.
