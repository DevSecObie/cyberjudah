**[Philippians 4:8](/bible/philippians/4#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/philippians/4#v8)</sup> Finally, brethren, whatsoever things are true, whatsoever things are honest, whatsoever things are just, whatsoever things are pure, whatsoever things are lovely, whatsoever things are of good report; if there be any virtue, and if there be any praise, think on these things.

**[Romans 12:17](/bible/romans/12#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/romans/12#v17)</sup> Recompense to no man evil for evil. Provide things honest in the sight of all men.

**[Romans 13:13](/bible/romans/13#v13)** (key)

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/13#v13)</sup> Let us walk honestly, as in the day; not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and envying.

**[1 Thessalonians 4:12](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v12)</sup> That ye may walk honestly toward them that are without, and that ye may have lack of nothing.

**[2 Corinthians 8:21](/bible/2-corinthians/8#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/2-corinthians/8#v21)</sup> Providing for honest things, not only in the sight of the Lord, but also in the sight of men.

**[2 Corinthians 13:7](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v7)</sup> Now I pray to God that ye do no evil; not that we should appear approved, but that ye should do that which is honest, though we be as reprobates.

**[Hebrews 13:18](/bible/hebrews/13#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/hebrews/13#v18)</sup> Pray for us: for we trust we have a good conscience, in all things willing to live honestly.

**[Sirach 22:9](/bible/sirach/22#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/22#v9)</sup> If children live honestly, and have wherewithal, they shall cover the baseness of their parents.

**[Sirach 29:14](/bible/sirach/29#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/29#v14)</sup> An honest man is surety for his neighbour: but he that is impudent will forsake him.

**[Acts 6:3](/bible/acts/6#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/acts/6#v3)</sup> Wherefore, brethren, look ye out among you seven men of honest report, full of the Holy Ghost and wisdom, whom we may appoint over this business.
