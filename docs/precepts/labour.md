**[Colossians 1:29](/bible/colossians/1#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/colossians/1#v29)</sup> Whereunto I also labour, striving according to his working, which worketh in me mightily.

**[Romans 16:12](/bible/romans/16#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/romans/16#v12)</sup> Salute Tryphena and Tryphosa, who labour in the Lord. Salute the beloved Persis, which laboured much in the Lord.

**[1 Thessalonians 1:3](/bible/1-thessalonians/1#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-thessalonians/1#v3)</sup> Remembering without ceasing your work of faith, and labour of love, and patience of hope in our Lord Jesus Christ, in the sight of God and our Father;

**[1 Corinthians 15:58](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v58)**

> <sup>[58](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v58)</sup> Therefore, my beloved brethren, be ye stedfast, unmoveable, always abounding in the work of the Lord, forasmuch as ye know that your labour is not in vain in the Lord.

**[Hebrews 6:10](/bible/hebrews/6#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/hebrews/6#v10)</sup> For God is not unrighteous to forget your work and labour of love, which ye have shewed toward his name, in that ye have ministered to the saints, and do minister.

**[Sirach 11:21](/bible/sirach/11#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/sirach/11#v21)</sup> Marvel not at the works of sinners; but trust in the Lord, and abide in thy labour: for it is an easy thing in the sight of the Lord on the sudden to make a poor man rich.

**[Sirach 24:34](/bible/sirach/24#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/sirach/24#v34)</sup> Behold that I have not laboured for myself only, but for all them that seek wisdom.

**[Sirach 33:17](/bible/sirach/33#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/33#v17)</sup> Consider that I laboured not for myself only, but for all them that seek learning.

**[Sirach 51:30](/bible/sirach/51#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/sirach/51#v30)</sup> Work your work betimes, and in his time he will give you your reward.

**[Acts 20:35](/bible/acts/20#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/acts/20#v35)</sup> I have shewed you all things, how that so labouring ye ought to support the weak, and to remember the words of the Lord Jesus, how he said, It is more blessed to give than to receive.

**[Revelation 14:13](/bible/revelation/14#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/revelation/14#v13)</sup> And I heard a voice from heaven saying unto me, Write, Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord from henceforth: Yea, saith the Spirit, that they may rest from their labours; and their works do follow them.
