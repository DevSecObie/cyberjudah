**[Susanna 1:23](/bible/susanna/1#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/susanna/1#v23)</sup> It is better for me to fall into your hands, and not do it, than to sin in the sight of the Lord.

**[2 Samuel 24:14](/bible/2-samuel/24#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-samuel/24#v14)</sup> And David said unto Gad, I am in a great strait: let us fall now into the hand of the Lord; for his mercies are great: and let me not fall into the hand of man.

**[Sirach 2:18](/bible/sirach/2#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/2#v18)</sup> Saying, We will fall into the hands of the Lord, and not into the hands of men: for as his majesty is, so is his mercy.
