**[2 Timothy 3:12](/bible/2-timothy/3#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/2-timothy/3#v12)</sup> Yea, and all that will live godly in Christ Jesus shall suffer persecution.

**[Psalms 10:2](/bible/psalms/10#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/10#v2)</sup> The wicked in his pride doth persecute the poor: let them be taken in the devices that they have imagined.

**[2 Corinthians 12:10](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v10)</sup> Therefore I take pleasure in infirmities, in reproaches, in necessities, in persecutions, in distresses for Christ’s sake: for when I am weak, then am I strong.

**[John 15:20](/bible/john/15#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/john/15#v20)</sup> Remember the word that I said unto you, The servant is not greater than his lord. If they have persecuted me, they will also persecute you; if they have kept my saying, they will keep yours also.

**[Revelation 12:13](/bible/revelation/12#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/revelation/12#v13)</sup> And when the dragon saw that he was cast unto the earth, he persecuted the woman which brought forth the man child.
