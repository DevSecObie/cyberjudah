**[Philippians 2:2](/bible/philippians/2#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/philippians/2#v2)</sup> Fulfil ye my joy, that ye be likeminded, having the same love, being of one accord, of one mind.

**[Romans 16:17](/bible/romans/16#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/romans/16#v17)</sup> Now I beseech you, brethren, mark them which cause divisions and offences contrary to the doctrine which ye have learned; and avoid them.

**[Psalms 133:1](/bible/psalms/133#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/133#v1)</sup> Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!

**[Ephesians 4:3](/bible/ephesians/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/ephesians/4#v3)</sup> Endeavouring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.

> <sup>[13](/bible/ephesians/4#v13)</sup> Till we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ:

**[1 Corinthians 1:10](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v10)</sup> Now I beseech you, brethren, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that ye all speak the same thing, and that there be no divisions among you; but that ye be perfectly joined together in the same mind and in the same judgment.

**[Isaiah 11:13](/bible/isaiah/11#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/11#v13)</sup> The envy also of Ephraim shall depart, and the adversaries of Judah shall be cut off: Ephraim shall not envy Judah, and Judah shall not vex Ephraim.

**[Mark 3:25](/bible/mark/3#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/mark/3#v25)</sup> And if a house be divided against itself, that house cannot stand.

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/25#v1)</sup> In three things I was beautified, and stood up beautiful both before God and men: the unity of brethren, the love of neighbours, a man and a wife that agree together.

**[Matthew 12:25](/bible/matthew/12#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/12#v25)</sup> And Jesus knew their thoughts, and said unto them, Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation; and every city or house divided against itself shall not stand:

**[Matthew 18:20](/bible/matthew/18#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/18#v20)</sup> For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them.
