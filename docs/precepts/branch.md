**[Romans 11:24](/bible/romans/11#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/romans/11#v24)</sup> For if thou wert cut out of the olive tree which is wild by nature, and wert graffed contrary to nature into a good olive tree: how much more shall these, which be the natural branches, be graffed into their own olive tree?

**[Isaiah 4:2](/bible/isaiah/4#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/4#v2)</sup> In that day shall the branch of the Lord be beautiful and glorious, and the fruit of the earth shall be excellent and comely for them that are escaped of Israel.

**[Jeremiah 23:5](/bible/jeremiah/23#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/23#v5)</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that I will raise unto David a righteous Branch, and a King shall reign and prosper, and shall execute judgment and justice in the earth.

**[John 15:5](/bible/john/15#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/john/15#v5)</sup> I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing.
