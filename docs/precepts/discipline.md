**[Wisdom of Solomon 1:5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v5)</sup> For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit, and remove from thoughts that are without understanding, and will not abide when unrighteousness cometh in.

**[Baruch 4:13](/bible/baruch/4#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/baruch/4#v13)</sup> They knew not his statutes, nor walked in the ways of his commandments, nor trod in the paths of discipline in his righteousness.

**[Job 36:10](/bible/job/36#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/job/36#v10)</sup> He openeth also their ear to discipline, and commandeth that they return from iniquity.

**[Sirach 18:14](/bible/sirach/18#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/18#v14)</sup> He hath mercy on them that receive discipline, and that diligently seek after his judgments.

**[Sirach 32:14](/bible/sirach/32#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/32#v14)</sup> Whoso feareth the Lord will receive his discipline; and they that seek him early shall find favour.
