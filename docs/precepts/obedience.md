**[Baruch 2:5](/bible/baruch/2#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/baruch/2#v5)</sup> Thus we were cast down, and not exalted, because we have sinned against the Lord our God, and have not been obedient unto his voice.

**[Exodus 23:22](/bible/exodus/23#v22)** · taught in [Exodus 22-25](/study/exodus/22-25)

> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/23#v22)</sup> But if thou shalt indeed obey his voice, and do all that I speak; then I will be an enemy unto thine enemies, and an adversary unto thine adversaries.

**[1 Samuel 15:22](/bible/1-samuel/15#v22)** · taught in [1 Samuel 12-15](/study/1-samuel/12-15)

> <sup>[22](/bible/1-samuel/15#v22)</sup> And Samuel said, Hath the Lord as great delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices, as in obeying the voice of the Lord? Behold, to obey is better than sacrifice, and to hearken than the fat of rams.

**[Ephesians 6:5](/bible/ephesians/6#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/ephesians/6#v5)</sup> Servants, be obedient to them that are your masters according to the flesh, with fear and trembling, in singleness of your heart, as unto Christ;

**[2 Corinthians 10:6](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v6)</sup> And having in a readiness to revenge all disobedience, when your obedience is fulfilled.

**[Hebrews 13:17](/bible/hebrews/13#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/hebrews/13#v17)</sup> Obey them that have the rule over you, and submit yourselves: for they watch for your souls, as they that must give account, that they may do it with joy, and not with grief: for that is unprofitable for you.

**[Isaiah 1:19](/bible/isaiah/1#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/1#v19)</sup> If ye be willing and obedient, ye shall eat the good of the land:

**[Acts 5:29](/bible/acts/5#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/acts/5#v29)</sup> Then Peter and the other apostles answered and said, We ought to obey God rather than men.
