**[Proverbs 12:25](/bible/proverbs/12#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/12#v25)</sup> Heaviness in the heart of man maketh it stoop: but a good word maketh it glad.

**[2 Esdras 10:24](/bible/2-esdras/10#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/2-esdras/10#v24)</sup> And therefore shake off thy great heaviness, and put away the multitude of sorrows, that the Mighty may be merciful unto thee again, and the Highest shall give thee rest and ease from thy labour.

**[Sirach 30:21](/bible/sirach/30#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/sirach/30#v21)</sup> Give not over thy mind to heaviness, and afflict not thyself in thine own counsel.

**[Sirach 38:18](/bible/sirach/38#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/38#v18)</sup> For of heaviness cometh death, and the heaviness of the heart breaketh strength.

**[Psalms 119:28](/bible/psalms/119#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/119#v28)</sup> My soul melteth for heaviness: strengthen thou me according unto thy word.
