**[Genesis 3:1](/bible/genesis/3#v1)** · taught in [Genesis 1-4](/study/genesis/1-4)

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/3#v1)</sup> Now the serpent was more subtil than any beast of the field which the Lord God had made. And he said unto the woman, Yea, hath God said, Ye shall not eat of every tree of the garden?

**[Ecclesiastes 3:18](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v18)</sup> I said in mine heart concerning the estate of the sons of men, that God might manifest them, and that they might see that they themselves are beasts.

> <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/7#v3)</sup> And four great beasts came up from the sea, diverse one from another.

> <sup>[29](/bible/2-esdras/8#v29)</sup> Let it not be thy will to destroy them which have lived like beasts; but to look upon them that have clearly taught thy law.

**[Sirach 12:13](/bible/sirach/12#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/12#v13)</sup> Who will pity a charmer that is bitten with a serpent, or any such as come nigh wild beasts?
