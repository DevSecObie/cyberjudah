**[Joel 3:6](/bible/joel/3#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/joel/3#v6)</sup> The children also of Judah and the children of Jerusalem have ye sold unto the Grecians, that ye might remove them far from their border.

**[Galatians 3:28](/bible/galatians/3#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/galatians/3#v28)</sup> There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither bond nor free, there is neither male nor female: for ye are all one in Christ Jesus.

**[2 Maccabees 4:15](/bible/2-maccabees/4#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/2-maccabees/4#v15)</sup> Not setting by the honours of their fathers, but liking the glory of the Grecians best of all.

**[2 Maccabees 6:6](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v6)</sup> Neither was it lawful for a man to keep sabbath days or ancient fasts, or to profess himself at all to be a Jew.

**[2 Maccabees 6:9](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v9)</sup> And whoso would not conform themselves to the manners of the Gentiles should be put to death. Then might a man have seen the present misery.
