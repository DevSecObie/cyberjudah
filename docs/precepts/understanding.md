**[Nehemiah 8:8](/bible/nehemiah/8#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/nehemiah/8#v8)</sup> So they read in the book in the law of God distinctly, and gave the sense, and caused them to understand the reading.

**[Psalms 111:10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)</sup> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do his commandments: his praise endureth for ever.

**[Job 28:28](/bible/job/28#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/job/28#v28)</sup> And unto man he said, Behold, the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom; and to depart from evil is understanding.

**[Job 32:8](/bible/job/32#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/job/32#v8)</sup> But there is a spirit in man: and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth them understanding.

**[Proverbs 4:7](/bible/proverbs/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/4#v7)</sup> Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.

**[Sirach 6:33](/bible/sirach/6#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/sirach/6#v33)</sup> If thou love to hear, thou shalt receive understanding: and if thou bow thine ear, thou shalt be wise,

**[Sirach 19:24](/bible/sirach/19#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/19#v24)</sup> He that hath small understanding, and feareth God, is better than one that hath much wisdom, and transgresseth the law of the most High.

**[Sirach 21:11](/bible/sirach/21#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/21#v11)</sup> He that keepeth the law of the Lord getteth the understanding thereof: and the perfection of the fear of the Lord is wisdom.

**[Sirach 33:3](/bible/sirach/33#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/33#v3)</sup> A man of understanding trusteth in the law; and the law is faithful unto him, as an oracle.

**[Psalms 119:34](/bible/psalms/119#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/psalms/119#v34)</sup> Give me understanding, and I shall keep thy law; yea, I shall observe it with my whole heart.

**[Psalms 119:73](/bible/psalms/119#v73)**

> <sup>[73](/bible/psalms/119#v73)</sup> Thy hands have made me and fashioned me: give me understanding, that I may learn thy commandments.
