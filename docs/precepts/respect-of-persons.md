**[Romans 2:11](/bible/romans/2#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/romans/2#v11)</sup> For there is no respect of persons with God.

**[James 2:9](/bible/james/2#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/james/2#v9)</sup> But if ye have respect to persons, ye commit sin, and are convinced of the law as transgressors.

**[Exodus 2:25](/bible/exodus/2#v25)** · taught in [Exodus 2-5](/study/exodus/2-5)

> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/2#v25)</sup> And God looked upon the children of Israel, and God had respect unto them.

**[Deuteronomy 1:17](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v17)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 1-2](/study/deuteronomy/1-2)

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v17)</sup> Ye shall not respect persons in judgment; but ye shall hear the small as well as the great; ye shall not be afraid of the face of man; for the judgment is God’s: and the cause that is too hard for you, bring it unto me, and I will hear it.

**[Leviticus 26:9](/bible/leviticus/26#v9)** · taught in [Leviticus 26-27](/study/leviticus/26-27)

> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/26#v9)</sup> For I will have respect unto you, and make you fruitful, and multiply you, and establish my covenant with you.

**[Sirach 4:22](/bible/sirach/4#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/4#v22)</sup> Accept no person against thy soul, and let not the reverence of any man cause thee to fall.

**[Sirach 8:14](/bible/sirach/8#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/8#v14)</sup> Go not to law with a judge; for they will judge for him according to his honour.

**[Sirach 10:23](/bible/sirach/10#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/sirach/10#v23)</sup> It is not meet to despise the poor man that hath understanding; neither is it convenient to magnify a sinful man.

**[Sirach 33:12](/bible/sirach/33#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/33#v12)</sup> Some of them hath he blessed and exalted and some of them he sanctified, and set near himself: but some of them hath he cursed and brought low, and turned out of their places.

**[Sirach 46:14](/bible/sirach/46#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/46#v14)</sup> By the law of the Lord he judged the congregation, and the Lord had respect unto Jacob.

**[Acts 10:34](/bible/acts/10#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/acts/10#v34)</sup> Then Peter opened his mouth, and said, Of a truth I perceive that God is no respecter of persons:
