**[Proverbs 22:15](/bible/proverbs/22#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/22#v15)</sup> Foolishness is bound in the heart of a child; but the rod of correction shall drive it far from him.

**[Proverbs 23:13](/bible/proverbs/23#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/23#v13)</sup> Withhold not correction from the child: for if thou beatest him with the rod, he shall not die.

**[Proverbs 23:14](/bible/proverbs/23#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/23#v14)</sup> Thou shalt beat him with the rod, and shalt deliver his soul from hell.

**[Sirach 30:1](/bible/sirach/30#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/30#v1)</sup> He that loveth his son causeth him oft to feel the rod, that he may have joy of him in the end.

**[Sirach 30:12](/bible/sirach/30#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/30#v12)</sup> Bow down his neck while he is young, and beat him on the sides while he is a child, lest he wax stubborn, and be disobedient unto thee, and so bring sorrow to thine heart.
