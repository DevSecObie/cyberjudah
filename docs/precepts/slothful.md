**[Ecclesiastes 10:18](/bible/ecclesiastes/10#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/ecclesiastes/10#v18)</sup> By much slothfulness the building decayeth; and through idleness of the hands the house droppeth through.

**[Judges 18:9](/bible/judges/18#v9)** · taught in [Judges 17-20](/study/judges/17-20)

> <sup>[9](/bible/judges/18#v9)</sup> And they said, Arise, that we may go up against them: for we have seen the land, and, behold, it is very good: and are ye still? be not slothful to go, and to enter to possess the land.

**[Proverbs 12:24](/bible/proverbs/12#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/12#v24)</sup> The hand of the diligent shall bear rule: but the slothful shall be under tribute.

**[Proverbs 19:15](/bible/proverbs/19#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/19#v15)</sup> Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep; and an idle soul shall suffer hunger.

**[Proverbs 21:25](/bible/proverbs/21#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/21#v25)</sup> The desire of the slothful killeth him; for his hands refuse to labour.

**[Proverbs 22:13](/bible/proverbs/22#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/22#v13)</sup> The slothful man saith, There is a lion without, I shall be slain in the streets.

**[Hebrews 6:12](/bible/hebrews/6#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/6#v12)</sup> That ye be not slothful, but followers of them who through faith and patience inherit the promises.

**[Proverbs 26:14](/bible/proverbs/26#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/26#v14)</sup> As the door turneth upon his hinges, so doth the slothful upon his bed.

**[Sirach 22:2](/bible/sirach/22#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/22#v2)</sup> A slothful man is compared to the filth of a dunghill: every man that takes it up will shake his hand.
