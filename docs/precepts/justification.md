**[Romans 2:13](/bible/romans/2#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/2#v13)</sup> (For not the hearers of the law are just before God, but the doers of the law shall be justified.

**[Galatians 2:16](/bible/galatians/2#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/galatians/2#v16)</sup> Knowing that a man is not justified by the works of the law, but by the faith of Jesus Christ, even we have believed in Jesus Christ, that we might be justified by the faith of Christ, and not by the works of the law: for by the works of the law shall no flesh be justified.

**[Galatians 3:8](/bible/galatians/3#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/galatians/3#v8)</sup> And the scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the heathen through faith, preached before the gospel unto Abraham, saying, In thee shall all nations be blessed.

**[Isaiah 45:25](/bible/isaiah/45#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/isaiah/45#v25)</sup> In the Lord shall all the seed of Israel be justified, and shall glory.

**[Acts 13:39](/bible/acts/13#v39)**

> <sup>[39](/bible/acts/13#v39)</sup> And by him all that believe are justified from all things, from which ye could not be justified by the law of Moses.
