**[Jude 1:4](/bible/jude/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/jude/1#v4)</sup> For there are certain men crept in unawares, who were before of old ordained to this condemnation, ungodly men, turning the grace of our God into lasciviousness, and denying the only Lord God, and our Lord Jesus Christ.

**[1 John 4:1](/bible/1-john/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-john/4#v1)</sup> Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world.

**[2 Timothy 4:10](/bible/2-timothy/4#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-timothy/4#v10)</sup> For Demas hath forsaken me, having loved this present world, and is departed unto Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia, Titus unto Dalmatia.

**[Galatians 2:4](/bible/galatians/2#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/galatians/2#v4)</sup> And that because of false brethren unawares brought in, who came in privily to spy out our liberty which we have in Christ Jesus, that they might bring us into bondage:

**[2 Peter 2:1](/bible/2-peter/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-peter/2#v1)</sup> But there were false prophets also among the people, even as there shall be false teachers among you, who privily shall bring in damnable heresies, even denying the Lord that bought them, and bring upon themselves swift destruction.
