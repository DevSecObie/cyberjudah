**[Ecclesiastes 7:3](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v3)</sup> Sorrow is better than laughter: for by the sadness of the countenance the heart is made better.

**[2 Corinthians 6:10](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v10)</sup> As sorrowful, yet alway rejoicing; as poor, yet making many rich; as having nothing, and yet possessing all things.

**[2 Corinthians 7:10](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v10)</sup> For godly sorrow worketh repentance to salvation not to be repented of: but the sorrow of the world worketh death.

**[Sirach 37:12](/bible/sirach/37#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/37#v12)</sup> But be continually with a godly man, whom thou knowest to keep the commandments of the Lord, whose, mind is according to thy mind, and will sorrow with thee, if thou shalt miscarry.
