**[Psalms 10:10](/bible/psalms/10#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/10#v10)</sup> He croucheth, and humbleth himself, that the poor may fall by his strong ones.

**[Psalms 28:3](/bible/psalms/28#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/28#v3)</sup> Draw me not away with the wicked, and with the workers of iniquity, which speak peace to their neighbours, but mischief is in their hearts.

**[Psalms 55:21](/bible/psalms/55#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/55#v21)</sup> The words of his mouth were smoother than butter, but war was in his heart: his words were softer than oil, yet were they drawn swords.

**[Psalms 58:3](/bible/psalms/58#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/58#v3)</sup> The wicked are estranged from the womb: they go astray as soon as they be born, speaking lies.

**[Sirach 12:10](/bible/sirach/12#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/12#v10)</sup> Never trust thine enemy: for like as iron rusteth, so is his wickedness.

**[Sirach 12:16](/bible/sirach/12#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/12#v16)</sup> An enemy speaketh sweetly with his lips, but in his heart he imagineth how to throw thee into a pit: he will weep with his eyes, but if he find opportunity, he will not be satisfied with blood.
