**[Deuteronomy 13:3](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v3)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 11-14](/study/deuteronomy/11-14)

> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v3)</sup> Thou shalt not hearken unto the words of that prophet, or that dreamer of dreams: for the Lord your God proveth you, to know whether ye love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

**[Zechariah 10:2](/bible/zechariah/10#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/zechariah/10#v2)</sup> For the idols have spoken vanity, and the diviners have seen a lie, and have told false dreams; they comfort in vain: therefore they went their way as a flock, they were troubled, because there was no shepherd.

**[Jeremiah 23:25](/bible/jeremiah/23#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/23#v25)</sup> I have heard what the prophets said, that prophesy lies in my name, saying, I have dreamed, I have dreamed.

**[Sirach 34:7](/bible/sirach/34#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/34#v7)</sup> For dreams have deceived many, and they have failed that put their trust in them.
