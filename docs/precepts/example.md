**[Judith 8:24](/bible/judith/8#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/judith/8#v24)</sup> Now therefore, O brethren, let us shew an example to our brethren, because their hearts depend upon us, and the sanctuary, and the house, and the altar, rest upon us.

**[1 Timothy 4:12](/bible/1-timothy/4#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-timothy/4#v12)</sup> Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.

**[1 Corinthians 10:11](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v11)</sup> Now all these things happened unto them for ensamples: and they are written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come.

**[1 Corinthians 11:1](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v1)</sup> Be ye followers of me, even as I also am of Christ.

**[Sirach 33:17](/bible/sirach/33#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/33#v17)</sup> Consider that I laboured not for myself only, but for all them that seek learning.

**[John 13:15](/bible/john/13#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/john/13#v15)</sup> For I have given you an example, that ye should do as I have done to you.
