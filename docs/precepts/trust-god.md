**[Psalms 20:7](/bible/psalms/20#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/20#v7)</sup> Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will remember the name of the Lord our God.

**[Proverbs 3:5](/bible/proverbs/3#v5)** (key)

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/3#v5)</sup> Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.

**[Isaiah 26:3](/bible/isaiah/26#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/26#v3)</sup> Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.

**[Isaiah 31:1](/bible/isaiah/31#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/31#v1)</sup> Woe to them that go down to Egypt for help; and stay on horses, and trust in chariots, because they are many; and in horsemen, because they are very strong; but they look not unto the Holy One of Israel, neither seek the Lord!

**[Sirach 2:6](/bible/sirach/2#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/2#v6)</sup> Believe in him, and he will help thee; order thy way aright, and trust in him.

**[Jeremiah 17:5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v5)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Cursed be the man that trusteth in man, and maketh flesh his arm, and whose heart departeth from the Lord.

**[Sirach 4:22](/bible/sirach/4#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/4#v22)</sup> Accept no person against thy soul, and let not the reverence of any man cause thee to fall.

**[Sirach 33:3](/bible/sirach/33#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/33#v3)</sup> A man of understanding trusteth in the law; and the law is faithful unto him, as an oracle.
