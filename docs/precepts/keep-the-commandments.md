**[Baruch 4:1](/bible/baruch/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/baruch/4#v1)</sup> This is the book of the commandments of God, and the law that endureth for ever: all they that keep it shall come to life; but such as leave it shall die.

**[Genesis 26:5](/bible/genesis/26#v5)** · taught in [Genesis 24-27](/study/genesis/24-27)

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/26#v5)</sup> Because that Abraham obeyed my voice, and kept my charge, my commandments, my statutes, and my laws.

**[Deuteronomy 6:17](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v17)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 3-6](/study/deuteronomy/3-6)

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v17)</sup> Ye shall diligently keep the commandments of the Lord your God, and his testimonies, and his statutes, which he hath commanded thee.

**[Deuteronomy 10:13](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v13)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 7-10](/study/deuteronomy/7-10)

> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v13)</sup> To keep the commandments of the Lord, and his statutes, which I command thee this day for thy good?

**[Psalms 111:10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)</sup> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do his commandments: his praise endureth for ever.

**[Proverbs 3:1](/bible/proverbs/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/3#v1)</sup> My son, forget not my law; but let thine heart keep my commandments:

**[Proverbs 4:4](/bible/proverbs/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/4#v4)</sup> He taught me also, and said unto me, Let thine heart retain my words: keep my commandments, and live.

**[Proverbs 7:2](/bible/proverbs/7#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/7#v2)</sup> Keep my commandments, and live; and my law as the apple of thine eye.

**[Proverbs 19:16](/bible/proverbs/19#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/19#v16)</sup> He that keepeth the commandment keepeth his own soul; but he that despiseth his ways shall die.

**[Proverbs 29:18](/bible/proverbs/29#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/29#v18)</sup> Where there is no vision, the people perish: but he that keepeth the law, happy is he.

**[Sirach 1:26](/bible/sirach/1#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/1#v26)</sup> If thou desire wisdom, keep the commandments, and the Lord shall give her unto thee.

**[Sirach 21:11](/bible/sirach/21#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/21#v11)</sup> He that keepeth the law of the Lord getteth the understanding thereof: and the perfection of the fear of the Lord is wisdom.

**[Matthew 5:17](/bible/matthew/5#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/5#v17)</sup> Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil.

**[Matthew 5:19](/bible/matthew/5#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/5#v19)</sup> Whosoever therefore shall break one of these least commandments, and shall teach men so, he shall be called the least in the kingdom of heaven: but whosoever shall do and teach them, the same shall be called great in the kingdom of heaven.

**[Matthew 19:17](/bible/matthew/19#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/19#v17)</sup> And he said unto him, Why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, that is, God: but if thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments.

**[2 Maccabees 4:17](/bible/2-maccabees/4#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/2-maccabees/4#v17)</sup> For it is not a light thing to do wickedly against the laws of God: but the time following shall declare these things.

**[John 14:15](/bible/john/14#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/john/14#v15)</sup> If ye love me, keep my commandments.

**[Revelation 12:17](/bible/revelation/12#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/revelation/12#v17)</sup> And the dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ.

**[Revelation 22:14](/bible/revelation/22#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/revelation/22#v14)</sup> Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the city.
