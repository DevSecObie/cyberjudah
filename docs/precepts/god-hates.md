**[Malachi 1:3](/bible/malachi/1#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/malachi/1#v3)</sup> And I hated Esau, and laid his mountains and his heritage waste for the dragons of the wilderness.

**[Romans 9:13](/bible/romans/9#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/9#v13)</sup> As it is written, Jacob have I loved, but Esau have I hated.

**[Psalms 5:5](/bible/psalms/5#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/5#v5)</sup> The foolish shall not stand in thy sight: thou hatest all workers of iniquity.

**[Psalms 11:5](/bible/psalms/11#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/11#v5)</sup> The Lord trieth the righteous: but the wicked and him that loveth violence his soul hateth.

**[Sirach 12:6](/bible/sirach/12#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/12#v6)</sup> For the most High hateth sinners, and will repay vengeance unto the ungodly, and keepeth them against the mighty day of their punishment.

**[Sirach 15:13](/bible/sirach/15#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/15#v13)</sup> The Lord hateth all abomination; and they that fear God love it not.
