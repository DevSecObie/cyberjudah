**[2 Timothy 2:15](/bible/2-timothy/2#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/2-timothy/2#v15)</sup> Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

**[1 Kings 2:2](/bible/1-kings/2#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-kings/2#v2)</sup> I go the way of all the earth: be thou strong therefore, and shew thyself a man;

**[2 Esdras 10:33](/bible/2-esdras/10#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/2-esdras/10#v33)</sup> And he said unto me, Stand up manfully, and I will advise thee.

**[1 Maccabees 2:64](/bible/1-maccabees/2#v64)**

> <sup>[64](/bible/1-maccabees/2#v64)</sup> Wherefore, ye my sons, be valiant and shew yourselves men in the behalf of the law; for by it shall ye obtain glory.

**[1 Maccabees 9:10](/bible/1-maccabees/9#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-maccabees/9#v10)</sup> Then Judas said, God forbid that I should do this thing, and flee away from them: if our time be come, let us die manfully for our brethren, and let us not stain our honour.
