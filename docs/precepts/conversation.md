**[1 Timothy 4:12](/bible/1-timothy/4#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-timothy/4#v12)</sup> Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.

**[Philippians 1:27](/bible/philippians/1#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/philippians/1#v27)</sup> Only let your conversation be as it becometh the gospel of Christ: that whether I come and see you, or else be absent, I may hear of your affairs, that ye stand fast in one spirit, with one mind striving together for the faith of the gospel;

**[James 3:13](/bible/james/3#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/james/3#v13)</sup> Who is a wise man and endued with knowledge among you? let him shew out of a good conversation his works with meekness of wisdom.

**[Psalms 50:23](/bible/psalms/50#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/50#v23)</sup> Whoso offereth praise glorifieth me: and to him that ordereth his conversation aright will I shew the salvation of God.

**[1 Peter 1:15](/bible/1-peter/1#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-peter/1#v15)</sup> But as he which hath called you is holy, so be ye holy in all manner of conversation;

**[Ephesians 4:29](/bible/ephesians/4#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/ephesians/4#v29)</sup> Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying, that it may minister grace unto the hearers.

**[1 Corinthians 15:33](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v33)</sup> Be not deceived: evil communications corrupt good manners.

**[Proverbs 4:24](/bible/proverbs/4#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/4#v24)</sup> Put away from thee a froward mouth, and perverse lips put far from thee.

**[Hebrews 13:5](/bible/hebrews/13#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/hebrews/13#v5)</sup> Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have: for he hath said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.

**[Proverbs 25:11](/bible/proverbs/25#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/25#v11)</sup> A word fitly spoken is like apples of gold in pictures of silver.

**[Sirach 6:35](/bible/sirach/6#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/sirach/6#v35)</sup> Be willing to hear every godly discourse; and let not the parables of understanding escape thee.

**[Sirach 9:15](/bible/sirach/9#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/9#v15)</sup> Let thy talk be with the wise, and all thy communication in the law of the most High.

**[Sirach 27:11](/bible/sirach/27#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/27#v11)</sup> The discourse of a godly man is always with wisdom; but a fool changeth as the moon.

**[Sirach 28:25](/bible/sirach/28#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/28#v25)</sup> And weigh thy words in a balance, and make a door and bar for thy mouth.

**[Sirach 32:8](/bible/sirach/32#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/32#v8)</sup> Let thy speech be short, comprehending much in few words; be as one that knoweth and yet holdeth his tongue.

**[Matthew 5:37](/bible/matthew/5#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/5#v37)</sup> But let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay: for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil.

**[Matthew 12:36](/bible/matthew/12#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/12#v36)</sup> But I say unto you, That every idle word that men shall speak, they shall give account thereof in the day of judgment.
