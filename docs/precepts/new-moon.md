> <sup>[16](/bible/colossians/2#v16)</sup> Let no man therefore judge you in meat, or in drink, or in respect of an holyday, or of the new moon, or of the sabbath days:

**[Epistle of Jeremiah 1:60](/bible/epistle-of-jeremiah/1#v60)**

> <sup>[60](/bible/epistle-of-jeremiah/1#v60)</sup> For sun, moon, and stars, being bright and sent to do their offices, are obedient.
