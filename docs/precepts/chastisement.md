**[Wisdom of Solomon 3:5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/3#v5)</sup> And having been a little chastised, they shall be greatly rewarded: for God proved them, and found them worthy for himself.

**[Deuteronomy 8:5](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v5)** (key) · taught in [Deuteronomy 7-10](/study/deuteronomy/7-10)

> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v5)</sup> Thou shalt also consider in thine heart, that, as a man chasteneth his son, so the Lord thy God chasteneth thee.

**[Psalms 94:12](/bible/psalms/94#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/94#v12)</sup> Blessed is the man whom thou chastenest, O Lord, and teachest him out of thy law;

**[Job 5:17](/bible/job/5#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/job/5#v17)</sup> Behold, happy is the man whom God correcteth: therefore despise not thou the chastening of the Almighty:

**[1 Corinthians 11:32](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v32)</sup> But when we are judged, we are chastened of the Lord, that we should not be condemned with the world.

**[Hebrews 12:7](/bible/hebrews/12#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/12#v7)</sup> If ye endure chastening, God dealeth with you as with sons; for what son is he whom the father chasteneth not?

**[2 Maccabees 7:33](/bible/2-maccabees/7#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/2-maccabees/7#v33)</sup> And though the living Lord be angry with us a little while for our chastening and correction, yet shall he be at one again with his servants.

**[Revelation 3:19](/bible/revelation/3#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/revelation/3#v19)</sup> As many as I love, I rebuke and chasten: be zealous therefore, and repent.
