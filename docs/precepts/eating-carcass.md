**[Deuteronomy 14:21](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v21)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 11-14](/study/deuteronomy/11-14)

> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v21)</sup> Ye shall not eat of any thing that dieth of itself: thou shalt give it unto the stranger that is in thy gates, that he may eat it; or thou mayest sell it unto an alien: for thou art an holy people unto the Lord thy God. Thou shalt not seethe a kid in his mother’s milk.

**[Leviticus 11:8](/bible/leviticus/11#v8)** · taught in [Leviticus 10-13](/study/leviticus/10-13)

> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/11#v8)</sup> Of their flesh shall ye not eat, and their carcase shall ye not touch; they are unclean to you.

**[Leviticus 22:8](/bible/leviticus/22#v8)** · taught in [Leviticus 22-25](/study/leviticus/22-25)

> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/22#v8)</sup> That which dieth of itself, or is torn with beasts, he shall not eat to defile himself therewith: I am the Lord.
