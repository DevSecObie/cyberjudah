**[Jeremiah 23:29](/bible/jeremiah/23#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/23#v29)</sup> Is not my word like as a fire? saith the Lord; and like a hammer that breaketh the rock in pieces?

> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/3#v11)</sup> I indeed baptize you with water unto repentance: but he that cometh after me is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear: he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire:

> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/5#v14)</sup> Wherefore thus saith the Lord God of hosts, Because ye speak this word, behold, I will make my words in thy mouth fire, and this people wood, and it shall devour them.
