**[Leviticus 25:46](/bible/leviticus/25#v46)** · taught in [Leviticus 22-25](/study/leviticus/22-25)

> <sup>[46](/bible/leviticus/25#v46)</sup> And ye shall take them as an inheritance for your children after you, to inherit them for a possession; they shall be your bondmen for ever: but over your brethren the children of Israel, ye shall not rule one over another with rigour.

**[Isaiah 14:2](/bible/isaiah/14#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/14#v2)</sup> And the people shall take them, and bring them to their place: and the house of Israel shall possess them in the land of the Lord for servants and handmaids: and they shall take them captives, whose captives they were; and they shall rule over their oppressors.

**[Isaiah 60:12](/bible/isaiah/60#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/60#v12)</sup> For the nation and kingdom that will not serve thee shall perish; yea, those nations shall be utterly wasted.

**[Revelation 13:10](/bible/revelation/13#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/revelation/13#v10)</sup> He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity: he that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword. Here is the patience and the faith of the saints.
