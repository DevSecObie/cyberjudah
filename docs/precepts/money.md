**[1 Timothy 6:10](/bible/1-timothy/6#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-timothy/6#v10)</sup> For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

**[Ecclesiastes 7:12](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v12)</sup> For wisdom is a defence, and money is a defence: but the excellency of knowledge is, that wisdom giveth life to them that have it.

**[Ecclesiastes 10:19](/bible/ecclesiastes/10#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/ecclesiastes/10#v19)</sup> A feast is made for laughter, and wine maketh merry: but money answereth all things.

**[James 5:3](/bible/james/5#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/james/5#v3)</sup> Your gold and silver is cankered; and the rust of them shall be a witness against you, and shall eat your flesh as it were fire. Ye have heaped treasure together for the last days.

**[Deuteronomy 8:18](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v18)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 7-10](/study/deuteronomy/7-10)

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v18)</sup> But thou shalt remember the Lord thy God: for it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth, that he may establish his covenant which he sware unto thy fathers, as it is this day.

**[Psalms 62:10](/bible/psalms/62#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/62#v10)</sup> Trust not in oppression, and become not vain in robbery: if riches increase, set not your heart upon them.

**[Proverbs 13:11](/bible/proverbs/13#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/13#v11)</sup> Wealth gotten by vanity shall be diminished: but he that gathereth by labour shall increase.

**[Proverbs 30:8](/bible/proverbs/30#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/30#v8)</sup> Remove far from me vanity and lies: give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with food convenient for me:

**[Sirach 13:21](/bible/sirach/13#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/sirach/13#v21)</sup> A rich man beginning to fall is held up of his friends: but a poor man being down is thrust away by his friends.

**[Sirach 13:24](/bible/sirach/13#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/13#v24)</sup> Riches are good unto him that hath no sin, and poverty is evil in the mouth of the ungodly.

**[Sirach 31:1](/bible/sirach/31#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/31#v1)</sup> Watching for riches consumeth the flesh, and the care thereof driveth away sleep.

**[Matthew 6:19](/bible/matthew/6#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/6#v19)</sup> Lay not up for yourselves treasures upon earth, where moth and rust doth corrupt, and where thieves break through and steal:
