**[Romans 8:1](/bible/romans/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/8#v1)</sup> There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.

**[2 Corinthians 11:2](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v2)</sup> For I am jealous over you with godly jealousy: for I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ.

> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/31#v4)</sup> Again I will build thee, and thou shalt be built, O virgin of Israel: thou shalt again be adorned with thy tabrets, and shalt go forth in the dances of them that make merry.

**[Matthew 25:7](/bible/matthew/25#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/25#v7)</sup> Then all those virgins arose, and trimmed their lamps.

**[Revelation 14:4](/bible/revelation/14#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/revelation/14#v4)</sup> These are they which were not defiled with women; for they are virgins. These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth. These were redeemed from among men, being the firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb.
