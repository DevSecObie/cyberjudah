**[Sirach 5:7](/bible/sirach/5#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/5#v7)</sup> Make no tarrying to turn to the Lord, and put not off from day to day: for suddenly shall the wrath of the Lord come forth, and in thy security thou shalt be destroyed, and perish in the day of vengeance.

**[Matthew 9:9](/bible/matthew/9#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/9#v9)</sup> And as Jesus passed forth from thence, he saw a man, named Matthew, sitting at the receipt of custom: and he saith unto him, Follow me. And he arose, and followed him.

**[Psalms 119:60](/bible/psalms/119#v60)**

> <sup>[60](/bible/psalms/119#v60)</sup> I made haste, and delayed not to keep thy commandments.
