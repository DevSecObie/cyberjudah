**[Zephaniah 3:9](/bible/zephaniah/3#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/zephaniah/3#v9)</sup> For then will I turn to the people a pure language, that they may all call upon the name of the Lord, to serve him with one consent.

**[Genesis 11:7](/bible/genesis/11#v7)** · taught in [Genesis 9-12](/study/genesis/9-12)

> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/11#v7)</sup> Go to, let us go down, and there confound their language, that they may not understand one another’s speech.

**[Genesis 11:9](/bible/genesis/11#v9)** · taught in [Genesis 9-12](/study/genesis/9-12)

> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/11#v9)</sup> Therefore is the name of it called Babel; because the Lord did there confound the language of all the earth: and from thence did the Lord scatter them abroad upon the face of all the earth.

**[1 Corinthians 14:21](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v21)</sup> In the law it is written, With men of other tongues and other lips will I speak unto this people; and yet for all that will they not hear me, saith the Lord.

**[Isaiah 28:11](/bible/isaiah/28#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/28#v11)</sup> For with stammering lips and another tongue will he speak to this people.

**[Acts 2:6](/bible/acts/2#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/acts/2#v6)</sup> Now when this was noised abroad, the multitude came together, and were confounded, because that every man heard them speak in his own language.

**[Revelation 7:9](/bible/revelation/7#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/revelation/7#v9)</sup> After this I beheld, and, lo, a great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues, stood before the throne, and before the Lamb, clothed with white robes, and palms in their hands;

**[Revelation 10:11](/bible/revelation/10#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/10#v11)</sup> And he said unto me, Thou must prophesy again before many peoples, and nations, and tongues, and kings.
