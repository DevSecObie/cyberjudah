**[Colossians 2:8](/bible/colossians/2#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/colossians/2#v8)</sup> Beware lest any man spoil you through philosophy and vain deceit, after the tradition of men, after the rudiments of the world, and not after Christ.

**[Mark 7:9](/bible/mark/7#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/mark/7#v9)</sup> And he said unto them, Full well ye reject the commandment of God, that ye may keep your own tradition.

**[Matthew 15:3](/bible/matthew/15#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/15#v3)</sup> But he answered and said unto them, Why do ye also transgress the commandment of God by your tradition?
