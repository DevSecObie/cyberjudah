**[Baruch 4:28](/bible/baruch/4#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/baruch/4#v28)</sup> For as it was your mind to go astray from God: so, being returned, seek him ten times more.

**[Zephaniah 2:3](/bible/zephaniah/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/zephaniah/2#v3)</sup> Seek ye the Lord, all ye meek of the earth, which have wrought his judgment; seek righteousness, seek meekness: it may be ye shall be hid in the day of the Lord’s anger.

**[Deuteronomy 4:29](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v29)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 3-6](/study/deuteronomy/3-6)

> <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v29)</sup> But if from thence thou shalt seek the Lord thy God, thou shalt find him, if thou seek him with all thy heart and with all thy soul.

**[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

**[Psalms 10:4](/bible/psalms/10#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/10#v4)</sup> The wicked, through the pride of his countenance, will not seek after God: God is not in all his thoughts.

**[2 Chronicles 12:14](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v14)</sup> And he did evil, because he prepared not his heart to seek the Lord.

**[2 Chronicles 14:4](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v4)</sup> And commanded Judah to seek the Lord God of their fathers, and to do the law and the commandment.

**[Proverbs 8:17](/bible/proverbs/8#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/8#v17)</sup> I love them that love me; and those that seek me early shall find me.

**[Hebrews 11:6](/bible/hebrews/11#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/11#v6)</sup> But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.

**[Isaiah 55:6](/bible/isaiah/55#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/55#v6)</sup> Seek ye the Lord while he may be found, call ye upon him while he is near:

**[Jeremiah 29:13](/bible/jeremiah/29#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/29#v13)</sup> And ye shall seek me, and find me, when ye shall search for me with all your heart.
