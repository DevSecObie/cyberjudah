**[Wisdom of Solomon 8:14](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v14)</sup> I shall set the people in order, and the nations shall be subject unto me.

**[Titus 1:5](/bible/titus/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/titus/1#v5)</sup> For this cause left I thee in Crete, that thou shouldest set in order the things that are wanting, and ordain elders in every city, as I had appointed thee:

**[Deuteronomy 1:15](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v15)** · taught in [Deuteronomy 1-2](/study/deuteronomy/1-2)

> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v15)</sup> So I took the chief of your tribes, wise men, and known, and made them heads over you, captains over thousands, and captains over hundreds, and captains over fifties, and captains over tens, and officers among your tribes.

**[2 Thessalonians 3:6](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v6)</sup> Now we command you, brethren, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that ye withdraw yourselves from every brother that walketh disorderly, and not after the tradition which he received of us.

**[Psalms 50:21](/bible/psalms/50#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/50#v21)</sup> These things hast thou done, and I kept silence; thou thoughtest that I was altogether such an one as thyself: but I will reprove thee, and set them in order before thine eyes.

**[1 Corinthians 11:3](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v3)</sup> But I would have you know, that the head of every man is Christ; and the head of the woman is the man; and the head of Christ is God.

**[1 Corinthians 14:40](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v40)**

> <sup>[40](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v40)</sup> Let all things be done decently and in order.

**[Sirach 2:6](/bible/sirach/2#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/2#v6)</sup> Believe in him, and he will help thee; order thy way aright, and trust in him.

**[Sirach 10:1](/bible/sirach/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/10#v1)</sup> A wise judge will instruct his people; and the government of a prudent man is well ordered.

**[Sirach 38:10](/bible/sirach/38#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/38#v10)</sup> Leave off from sin, and order thine hands aright, and cleanse thy heart from all wickedness.
