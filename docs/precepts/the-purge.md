**[Daniel 11:35](/bible/daniel/11#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/daniel/11#v35)</sup> And some of them of understanding shall fall, to try them, and to purge, and to make them white, even to the time of the end: because it is yet for a time appointed.

**[Isaiah 1:25](/bible/isaiah/1#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/isaiah/1#v25)</sup> And I will turn my hand upon thee, and purely purge away thy dross, and take away all thy tin:

**[Ezekiel 20:38](/bible/ezekiel/20#v38)**

> <sup>[38](/bible/ezekiel/20#v38)</sup> And I will purge out from among you the rebels, and them that transgress against me: I will bring them forth out of the country where they sojourn, and they shall not enter into the land of Israel: and ye shall know that I am the Lord.

**[Matthew 3:12](/bible/matthew/3#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/3#v12)</sup> Whose fan is in his hand, and he will throughly purge his floor, and gather his wheat into the garner; but he will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.

**[Luke 3:17](/bible/luke/3#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/luke/3#v17)</sup> Whose fan is in his hand, and he will throughly purge his floor, and will gather the wheat into his garner; but the chaff he will burn with fire unquenchable.
