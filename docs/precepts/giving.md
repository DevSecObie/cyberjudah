**[2 Corinthians 8:2](/bible/2-corinthians/8#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/2-corinthians/8#v2)</sup> How that in a great trial of affliction the abundance of their joy and their deep poverty abounded unto the riches of their liberality.

**[2 Corinthians 9:7](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v7)</sup> Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver.

**[Tobit 4:7](/bible/tobit/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/tobit/4#v7)</sup> Give alms of thy substance; and when thou givest alms, let not thine eye be envious, neither turn thy face from any poor, and the face of God shall not be turned away from thee.

**[Sirach 12:1](/bible/sirach/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/12#v1)</sup> When thou wilt do good know to whom thou doest it; so shalt thou be thanked for thy benefits.
