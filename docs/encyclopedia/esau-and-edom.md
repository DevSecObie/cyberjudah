The lineage teaching on Esau and his descendants, from the birthright through the kings of Edom.

## Genesis

**[Genesis 3:14](/bible/genesis/3#v14)**  taught in [Genesis 1-4](/study/genesis/1-4)

- Going on the belly and eating dust means the serpent was brought to a very low estate, made like a vagabond upon the earth. Cain became a vagabond upon the earth, and so did Esau.

**[Genesis 14:4-11](/bible/genesis/14#v4)**  taught in [Genesis 13-16](/study/genesis/13-16)

- The five kings served Chedorlaomer twelve years and rebelled in the thirteenth; the four kings defeated them and took the goods of Sodom and Gomorrah.
- The vale of Siddim was full of slimepits: bitumen, tar, petroleum. The same region is rich in oil to this day.

**[Genesis 22:15-18](/bible/genesis/22#v15)**  taught in [Genesis 21-24](/study/genesis/21-24)

- The seed possessing the gate of his enemies is part of the covenant: the seed of Jacob shall possess the remnant of Edom and of all the heathen, and Edom shall be a possession. That is why all nations cannot be inside this covenant; the covenant has Israel possessing their enemies.
- Precept **[Amos 9:11-12](/bible/amos/9#v11)**: The tabernacle of David is raised up, that they may possess the remnant of Edom and of all the heathen.
- Precept **[Numbers 24:17-18](/bible/numbers/24#v17)**: A Star out of Jacob and a Sceptre out of Israel; Edom shall be a possession, and Israel shall do valiantly.

**[Genesis 24:58-60](/bible/genesis/24#v58)**  taught in [Genesis 21-24](/study/genesis/21-24)

- The covenant narrows again here: it runs through Rebekah's children, and of her two sons it is Jacob who carries it, for Esau sold his birthright. The covenants in Genesis cannot be broken.

**[Genesis 25:22-23](/bible/genesis/25#v22)**  taught in [Genesis 24-27](/study/genesis/24-27)

- The whole scripture is good set against evil: the godly against the sinner, Jacob against Esau, righteousness against sin. Two powerful forces were being born into the earth, and the struggle began in the womb.

**[Genesis 25:24-26](/bible/genesis/25#v24)**  taught in [Genesis 24-27](/study/genesis/24-27)

- The first came out red, all over like an hairy garment, and they called his name Esau. The scriptures describe the man because a nation comes from him; this teaching identifies Esau's seed with the red, hairy, melanin-lacking people of the world today.
- Jacob's hand took hold on Esau's heel, and that is prophecy: Esau is the end of the world, and Jacob is the beginning of it that followeth. When Esau's rulership is done, Jacob comes into power; that is how we know the times.
- Precept **[2 Esdras 6:7-9](/bible/2-esdras/6#v7)**: Jacob's hand held first the heel of Esau; Esau is the end of the world and Jacob the beginning of it that followeth.

**[Genesis 25:27-28](/bible/genesis/25#v27)**  taught in [Genesis 24-27](/study/genesis/24-27)

- Esau was a cunning hunter, a man of the field; Jacob was a plain man, dwelling in tents. The Most High gives you the characteristics of the man so the nation can be known.
- Isaac loved Esau because he did eat of his venison, but Rebekah loved Jacob, because the Lord had told her the prophecy that the younger was the chosen.

**[Genesis 25:29-34](/bible/genesis/25#v29)**  taught in [Genesis 24-27](/study/genesis/24-27)

- Esau sold his birthright for red pottage; therefore was his name called Edom, red. He said, What profit shall this birthright do to me, and sware it away for a meal.
- Thus Esau despised his birthright. Jacob is called a supplanter, but the supplanting was of the Holy Ghost; Esau gave the birthright away himself.

**[Genesis 26:34-35](/bible/genesis/26#v34)**  taught in [Genesis 24-27](/study/genesis/24-27)

- Esau at forty took wives of the Hittites, and they were a grief of mind unto Isaac and to Rebekah. He went straight against the fathers' law of marrying within the kindred; that is the nature of the vessel.

**[Genesis 27:1-4](/bible/genesis/27#v1)**  taught in [Genesis 24-27](/study/genesis/24-27)

- Isaac's eyes were dim so that he could not see, and the Lord allowed that blindness for a purpose: so that the blessing could pass to Jacob. What looks like evil can be for good, as Joseph's slavery in Egypt was for good.

**[Genesis 27:5-13](/bible/genesis/27#v5)**  taught in [Genesis 24-27](/study/genesis/24-27)

- Rebekah heard and moved for Jacob because she carried the word from the Lord: the elder shall serve the younger. She knew the blessing belonged to Jacob by prophecy, and she took the curse upon herself: Upon me be thy curse, my son; only obey my voice.

**[Genesis 27:15-23](/bible/genesis/27#v15)**  taught in [Genesis 24-27](/study/genesis/24-27)

- Rebekah put Esau's raiment on Jacob and the skins of the kids of the goats upon his hands and neck, and Isaac discerned him not: the voice is Jacob's voice, but the hands are the hands of Esau.

**[Genesis 27:34-38](/bible/genesis/27#v34)**  taught in [Genesis 24-27](/study/genesis/24-27)

- Esau cried with a great and exceeding bitter cry and begged, Bless me, even me also, O my father. But he had already sold the birthright and never dealt rightly with Jacob about it; he wanted the blessing anyway.
- Esau found no place of repentance, though he sought it carefully with tears. He that despised the birthright for one morsel of meat was rejected when he would have inherited the blessing.
- Precept **[Hebrews 12:16-17](/bible/hebrews/12#v16)**: Esau, who for one morsel of meat sold his birthright, was rejected and found no place of repentance, though he sought it carefully with tears.

**[Genesis 27:39-40](/bible/genesis/27#v39)**  taught in [Genesis 24-27](/study/genesis/24-27)

- Esau's portion: the fatness of the earth and the dew of heaven from above, and by thy sword shalt thou live. Esau's kingdoms have gotten what they have through war; his living is by the sword, by ever more advanced weaponry, and the nations are overcome and awed by it.
- And it shall come to pass when thou shalt have the dominion, that thou shalt break his yoke from off thy neck: this is the time period now, where Esau has the dominion and rules first, and when his rule is done, Jacob comes into power.
- Precept **[Revelation 13:13-14](/bible/revelation/13#v13)**: He maketh fire come down from heaven in the sight of men and deceiveth them that dwell on the earth by those wonders; the sword Esau lives by.

**[Genesis 27:41-46](/bible/genesis/27#v41)**  taught in [Genesis 24-27](/study/genesis/24-27)

- Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing and purposed in his heart to slay him, the same hatred Cain had for Abel. Good against evil runs from the beginning of the book to the end.
- Rebekah sent Jacob to Laban her brother until Esau's fury turned away, and said she was weary of her life because of the daughters of Heth: if Jacob take a wife of the daughters of the land, what good shall my life do me? Marrying the sons and daughters within the kindred is a weighty matter to the mothers of Israel.

**[Genesis 28:5](/bible/genesis/28#v5)**  taught in [Genesis 28-31](/study/genesis/28-31)

- Jacob was sent to Laban, the son of Bethuel the Syrian, the brother of Rebekah. Rebekah herself was born to Bethuel, son of Milcah, the wife of Nahor, Abraham's brother; the family line was kept in place, generation after generation going back to their own kindred.

**[Genesis 28:6-9](/bible/genesis/28#v6)**  taught in [Genesis 28-31](/study/genesis/28-31)

- Esau saw that the daughters of Canaan pleased not Isaac his father, but instead of following Jacob's path he went to Ishmael and took Mahalath to add to the wives he had. He answered his father's grief by doing his own thing.
- Esau had already taken Hittite wives that were a grief of mind unto Isaac and Rebekah, and his generations show wives of the daughters of Canaan. He was hardheaded from the beginning; that is the spirit of the wicked, who hate instruction and cast the words of the Most High behind them.
- Precept **[Genesis 26:34-35](/bible/genesis/26#v34)**: Esau's Hittite wives were a grief of mind unto Isaac and Rebekah.
- Precept **[Genesis 36:2-3](/bible/genesis/36#v2)**: Esau's generations confirm he took wives of the daughters of Canaan and of Ishmael.
- Precept **[Psalms 50:16-17](/bible/psalms/50#v16)**: Unto the wicked God saith, Thou hatest instruction, and castest my words behind thee; that is Esau's spirit.

**[Genesis 32:3-7](/bible/genesis/32#v3)**  taught in [Genesis 32-35](/study/genesis/32-35)

- Jacob sent messengers to Esau, and they returned saying Esau was coming with four hundred men, and Jacob was greatly afraid and distressed.
- His fear was rightful: the last word he had of Esau was that Esau purposed in his heart to slay him once their father's mourning came. The hatred began at the blessing, and the scriptures show in Ezekiel that the hatred never ended.
- Precept **[Genesis 27:41-42](/bible/genesis/27#v41)**: Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing and said in his heart, I will slay my brother Jacob; that was the last report Jacob had.

**[Genesis 32:9-12](/bible/genesis/32#v9)**  taught in [Genesis 32-35](/study/genesis/32-35)

- Jacob prayed humbly: I am not worthy of the least of all the mercies; deliver me from the hand of my brother. He was not too proud to confess his fear to the Most High.
- He pleaded the covenant: thou saidst, I will make thy seed as the sand of the sea. That is the pattern for our prayers; plead the promise made to the fathers and ask the Most High to remember His covenant.

**[Genesis 32:13-20](/bible/genesis/32#v13)**  taught in [Genesis 32-35](/study/genesis/32-35)

- Jacob sent droves of livestock ahead as a present, spaced apart, each servant instructed what to say. He said, I will appease him with the present that goeth before me. He moved strategically to soften Esau before they met face to face.

**[Genesis 33:1-4](/bible/genesis/33#v1)**  taught in [Genesis 32-35](/study/genesis/32-35)

- Jacob bowed himself to the ground seven times as he came near, and Esau ran, embraced him, fell on his neck, and kissed him, and they wept.
- That embrace is not proof that the hatred was gone. Jacob had prayed for deliverance, and when a man's ways please the Lord, He maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him; the Most High put that peace on Esau in that moment.
- Precept **[Proverbs 16:7](/bible/proverbs/16#v7)**: When a man's ways please the Lord, He maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him; that is what happened with Esau.

**[Genesis 33:5-11](/bible/genesis/33#v5)**  taught in [Genesis 32-35](/study/genesis/32-35)

- Jacob presented the children which God graciously gave thy servant, and urged the present on Esau until he took it. Receiving the gift sealed the peace; Jacob wanted the assurance.

**[Genesis 33:12-16](/bible/genesis/33#v12)**  taught in [Genesis 32-35](/study/genesis/32-35)

- Esau offered to travel together and to leave some of his four hundred men, and Jacob declined both, pleading the tender children and the young flocks: I will lead on softly. He stayed wise and kept his distance, and Esau returned that day to Seir.

**[Genesis 35:1-4](/bible/genesis/35#v1)**  taught in [Genesis 32-35](/study/genesis/32-35)

- Before going up to Bethel, Jacob told his household, Put away the strange gods that are among you, and be clean. The strange gods were there because Rachel had stolen her father's images and hidden them, sitting on them when Laban searched.
- Idolatry hidden in the household must be put away before you can stand at Bethel. Rachel finally gave up the images, and Jacob hid them under the oak by Shechem.
- From Eve to Rachel the scriptures warn how sin enters through the household; of the woman came the beginning of sin. Sisters, stay in the spirit and guard against the serpent's beguiling as the mothers were beguiled.

**[Genesis 35:22](/bible/genesis/35#v22)**  taught in [Genesis 32-35](/study/genesis/32-35)

- The judgment followed him: forasmuch as he defiled his father's bed, his birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph, and the genealogy is not reckoned after the birthright. The firstborn lost the birthright over it.
- Precept **[1 Chronicles 5:1](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v1)**: Reuben was the firstborn, but because he defiled his father's bed his birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph.

**[Genesis 35:27-29](/bible/genesis/35#v27)**  taught in [Genesis 32-35](/study/genesis/32-35)

- Isaac gave up the ghost at an hundred and fourscore years, old and full of days, and his sons Esau and Jacob together buried him.

**[Genesis 36:1](/bible/genesis/36#v1)**  taught in [Genesis 36-39](/study/genesis/36-39)

- Now these are the generations of Esau, who is Edom. Esau is Edom, and this teaching identifies Edom with the people called the white man today.
- Esau came out of the womb red, all over like an hairy garment; his blood could be seen through his skin for lack of melanin, and his name Esau goes into wasted away. Jacob was not described, because Jacob looked like everyone from Adam down to his time; the man of melanin needed no description.
- Esau was called Edom, which means red, because he sold his birthright for that same red pottage.
- Precept **[Genesis 25:24-26](/bible/genesis/25#v24)**: The first came out red, all over like an hairy garment; the twins were two nations, polar opposites.
- Precept **[Genesis 25:30](/bible/genesis/25#v30)**: Feed me with that same red pottage; therefore was his name called Edom, which is red.

**[Genesis 36:2-5](/bible/genesis/36#v2)**  taught in [Genesis 36-39](/study/genesis/36-39)

- Esau took his wives of the daughters of Canaan and of Ishmael. He was the first of the Edomites; there were no women of his nation yet, so his lineage began through those wives. Genesis is Bereshith, the beginning of nations.

**[Genesis 36:6-8](/bible/genesis/36#v6)**  taught in [Genesis 36-39](/study/genesis/36-39)

- Esau went from the face of his brother because their riches were more than that they might dwell together, the same as with Abram and Lot in [Genesis 13](/bible/genesis/13). Esau was rich at this time, out of the house of Isaac and Abraham.
- Thus dwelt Esau in mount Seir. Mount Seir is the clefts of the rock; the vision of Obadiah speaks to Edom as thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, whose pride hath deceived him.
- Precept **[Obadiah 1:3](/bible/obadiah/1#v3)**: The pride of thine heart hath deceived thee, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock; that is Edom in mount Seir.

**[Genesis 36:9-11](/bible/genesis/36#v9)**  taught in [Genesis 36-39](/study/genesis/36-39)

- Esau is the father of the Edomites in mount Seir, and his grandson Teman, son of Eliphaz, is identified in this teaching with Germany; the reference work Edom and Germany lays that connection out.
- The prophecy over Teman stands: the wise men shall be destroyed out of Edom and understanding out of the mount of Esau, and the mighty men of Teman shall be dismayed. Much of the world's science and weapons technology has come out of Germany, and the word says that wisdom will be destroyed.
- Precept **[Obadiah 1:8-9](/bible/obadiah/1#v8)**: The wise men of Edom and the mighty men of Teman shall be destroyed, that every one of the mount of Esau may be cut off.

**[Genesis 36:12](/bible/genesis/36#v12)**  taught in [Genesis 36-39](/study/genesis/36-39)

- Timna was concubine to Eliphaz, and she bare Amalek. Amalek came of a concubine, a lesser wife, which is why the word bastard is studied here: in the scriptures it means a child of incest or one born outside the proper marriage.
- The word says a bastard shall dwell in Ashdod, and Ashdod is the area of modern day Tel Aviv; this teaching places the line of Amalek dwelling there today.

**[Genesis 36:15-19](/bible/genesis/36#v15)**  taught in [Genesis 36-39](/study/genesis/36-39)

- These were the dukes of the sons of Esau: dukes are the chiefs of their tribes, the leaders of the families of Edom.

**[Genesis 36:20-30](/bible/genesis/36#v20)**  taught in [Genesis 36-39](/study/genesis/36-39)

- The sons of Seir the Horite inhabited the land before Esau; the Horites were the original dwellers of mount Seir.
- The Lord destroyed the Horims from before the children of Esau, and they succeeded them and dwelt in their stead, as Deuteronomy records.

## Exodus

- The potter has power over the clay, of the same lump to make one vessel unto honour and another unto dishonour. Some nations are honourable, which are the Israelites, and some are dishonourable; Jacob have I loved, but Esau have I hated. The vessels of wrath were fitted to destruction, endured with much longsuffering so the Most High could show how powerful he is; that is why Esau had to be set on such a high level, so that when he brings these people down all will know God is truly all powerful.
- Precept **[Romans 9:13](/bible/romans/9#v13)**: Jacob have I loved, but Esau have I hated; Jacob is the honourable vessel and Esau the dishonourable.

**[Exodus 15:14-16](/bible/exodus/15#v14)**  taught in [Exodus 14-17](/study/exodus/14-17)

## Numbers

**[Numbers 20:14-17](/bible/numbers/20#v14)**  taught in [Numbers 19-22](/study/numbers/19-22)

- Moses sent messengers to the king of Edom, saying, Thus saith thy brother Israel. Israel rehearsed the travail: our fathers went down into Egypt, the Egyptians vexed us, we cried unto the Lord and he heard our voice and sent an angel and brought us forth.
- Edom is identified in this teaching with the people called the white man today, the Caucasians. This is Israel pleading with the brother nation: not sneaking in, not hiding, not touching the fields, the vineyards, or the water of the wells; only the king's highway, turning neither to the right hand nor to the left, until past the borders, to reach the promised land.

**[Numbers 20:18-21](/bible/numbers/20#v18)**  taught in [Numbers 19-22](/study/numbers/19-22)

- Edom answered, Thou shalt not pass by me, lest I come out against thee with the sword. Israel stayed humble: we will go by the highway, and if I and my cattle drink of thy water, then I will pay for it; I will only go through on my feet.
- Edom still refused and came out against Israel with much people and with a strong hand, and Israel turned away from him.
- Why so harsh against a people asking only to pass through? That hatred stands from something: Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing wherewith his father blessed him, and purposed in his heart to slay his brother. [Genesis 25](/bible/genesis/25) through 27 is the start of the two nations, and Israel was given the birthright and the kingdom while Esau was rejected.
- The hatred is perpetual, everlasting, never ending. The forefather's hatred showed in [Numbers 20](/bible/numbers/20) and it shows to this day in the nation of Edom still rejecting Israel; it will not stop until Christ stops it, and Israel's part is to repent and come back to the lost statutes and commandments.
- Precept **[Genesis 27:41](/bible/genesis/27#v41)**: Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing and said in his heart, then will I slay my brother Jacob; the refusal in Numbers 20 goes back to this.

**[Numbers 20:22-29](/bible/numbers/20#v22)**  taught in [Numbers 19-22](/study/numbers/19-22)

**[Numbers 21:4-5](/bible/numbers/21#v4)**  taught in [Numbers 19-22](/study/numbers/19-22)

- They journeyed from mount Hor by the way of the Red Sea to compass the land of Edom, and the soul of the people was much discouraged because of the way: since Edom refused passage, Israel had to go around, and the way was longer than they wanted it to be.
