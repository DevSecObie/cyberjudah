Every place the study notes teach the sabbath, from the seventh day of creation through the wilderness laws.

## Genesis

**[Genesis 2:1-3](/bible/genesis/2#v1)**  taught in [Genesis 1-4](/study/genesis/1-4)

- This is the first Sabbath that you read about in the scriptures. The Most High blessed the seventh day and sanctified it.

**[Genesis 3:16](/bible/genesis/3#v16)**  taught in [Genesis 1-4](/study/genesis/1-4)

- This is one of the first commandments given, alongside be fruitful and multiply, the Sabbath, and the commandment not to eat of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.

## Exodus

**[Exodus 5:6-9](/bible/exodus/5#v6)**  taught in [Exodus 2-5](/study/exodus/2-5)

- Let there more work be laid upon the men, that they may labour therein; and let them not regard vain words. The same tactic runs in this captivity: burdens are increased and extra wages dangled for working the Sabbath and the feast days, to wear the people down so they will not regard the words of the Most High.

**[Exodus 5:15-19](/bible/exodus/5#v15)**  taught in [Exodus 2-5](/study/exodus/2-5)

- Pharaoh answered, Ye are idle, ye are idle: therefore ye say, Let us go and do sacrifice to the LORD. The charge of idleness is the standing answer to any who would go serve God, whether it is the three days' journey or keeping the Sabbath and the new moons now.

**[Exodus 10:27-29](/bible/exodus/10#v27)**  taught in [Exodus 10-13](/study/exodus/10-13)

- The same delusion falls on Israel when the people go reprobate in their sins: believing the Sabbath begins when the sun comes up, that anything can be eaten on Passover, or that the leaven to be put away is only the heart. Sin blinds.

**[Exodus 12:3-5](/bible/exodus/12#v3)**  taught in [Exodus 10-13](/study/exodus/10-13)

- In the tenth day of the month every man was to take a lamb according to the house of his fathers; if the household was too little for the lamb, he and his neighbour next unto his house were to take it according to the number of the souls.
- The lamb was to be without blemish, a male of the first year, taken from the sheep or from the goats. This is a foreshadowing: the feast days and sacrifices are a shadow of things to come, but the body is of Christ.
- The lamb without blemish and without spot points to Christ, who was sinless, verily foreordained before the foundation of the world but manifest in these last times for the nation of Israel.
- Precept **[Colossians 2:16-17](/bible/colossians/2#v16)**: The meat, drink, holydays, and sabbaths are a shadow of things to come, but the body is of Christ; the Passover lamb was a precursor of him.

**[Exodus 12:15-17](/bible/exodus/12#v15)**  taught in [Exodus 10-13](/study/exodus/10-13)

- The first day and the seventh day are holy convocations; no manner of work is done in them save that which every man must eat, meaning the food may be cooked.

**[Exodus 13:5-10](/bible/exodus/13#v5)**  taught in [Exodus 10-13](/study/exodus/10-13)

- When the Lord brings Israel into the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, a land flowing with milk and honey, this service is kept in this month: seven days of unleavened bread, and in the seventh day a feast to the Lord, with no leaven seen in all thy quarters.

**[Exodus 16:22-30](/bible/exodus/16#v22)**  taught in [Exodus 14-17](/study/exodus/14-17)

- On the sixth day they gathered twice as much, and Moses said, Tomorrow is the rest of the holy sabbath unto the Lord: bake that which ye will bake today. This is the scripture we always use to show that we do not cook on the sabbath; everything is prepared friday before sundown, and the leftovers are eaten the next day.
- What was laid up did not stink, neither was there any worm therein, because the Lord had given the bread of two days on the sixth day; the same manna that bred worms on other nights was preserved for the sabbath.
- Some of the people still went out on the seventh day to gather and found none, and the Lord said, How long refuse ye to keep my commandments and my laws? The sabbath was already binding in the wilderness.
- Abide ye every man in his place on the seventh day is about not going out to gather food on the sabbath. Some foolish Israelites read verse 29 out of context and say you never have to leave your house or join a congregation on the sabbath; that is not what it says. The sabbath is a holy convocation, a large gathering, in all your dwellings, and we are to come together on it.
- Precept **[Leviticus 23:1-3](/bible/leviticus/23#v1)**: The seventh day is a sabbath of rest, an holy convocation in all your dwellings; Exodus 16:29 does not undo the gathering.

**[Exodus 20:8-11](/bible/exodus/20#v8)**  taught in [Exodus 18-21](/study/exodus/18-21)

- Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy. The word says remember because Israel would forget it: in this captivity the people were forced to serve other gods and their religions, and the Sabbath was lost through forced conversion.
- Look up the blue laws, also known as Sunday laws; they help give understanding of why the Bible says remember the Sabbath day.
- Precept **[Deuteronomy 28:64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)**: Scattered among all people, there shalt thou serve other gods, wood and stone; that is how the Sabbath was forgotten.

**[Exodus 23:10-11](/bible/exodus/23#v10)**  taught in [Exodus 22-25](/study/exodus/22-25)

- The seventh year ties into the count of the jubilee: seven sabbaths of years to the forty-ninth year, and in the fiftieth year no plowing of the land, which the law takes up later.

**[Exodus 23:12](/bible/exodus/23#v12)**  taught in [Exodus 22-25](/study/exodus/22-25)

- Six days do thy work, and on the seventh day rest, that thine ox and thine ass may rest, and the son of thy handmaid and the stranger may be refreshed. The stranger here can be another nation working for you: they too get their due rest on the sabbath.
- When the Lord sets Israel back up to rule, the nations will kick and scream at first, but once these laws are applied there will be righteousness reigning over the earth like they have never seen, and all will be refreshed on the sabbath, spiritually and physically.

**[Exodus 24:13-18](/bible/exodus/24#v13)**  taught in [Exodus 22-25](/study/exodus/22-25)

- Moses rose up with his minister Joshua and went up into the mount of God, leaving Aaron and Hur with the elders for any matters. A cloud covered the mount, the glory of the Lord abode upon Sinai six days, and on the seventh day he called unto Moses out of the midst of the cloud.

**[Exodus 30:10](/bible/exodus/30#v10)**  taught in [Exodus 30-33](/study/exodus/30-33)

- Aaron made an atonement upon the horns of it once in a year with the blood of the sin offering of atonements; it is most holy unto the Lord. This altar served the day of atonement.
- On the tenth day of the seventh month Israel afflicted their souls and did no work at all, and the priest made an atonement for the sanctuary, the tabernacle, the altar, the priests, and all the people of the congregation, to cleanse them from all their sins before the Lord; an everlasting statute, once a year.
- The priests went always into the first tabernacle accomplishing the service of God, but into the second went the high priest alone once every year, not without blood. Christ offered one sacrifice for sins for ever, so the day of atonement is still kept by afflicting the soul and doing no work, but without the animal sacrifice, because Christ took the place of the animal.
- Precept **[Leviticus 16:29-34](/bible/leviticus/16#v29)**: On the tenth day of the seventh month ye shall afflict your souls, and the priest makes an atonement to cleanse the children of Israel from all their sins, an everlasting statute.

**[Exodus 30:22-33](/bible/exodus/30#v22)**  taught in [Exodus 30-33](/study/exodus/30-33)

- The Most High gave specific orders down to the spices and the weights, and none could deviate from them or they would die. Our forefathers carried out that tedious, exact work, yet we complain about the smallest things set in place for order, the uniform, the new moon, and not cooking on the sabbath; the spirit has to get right.

**[Exodus 31:12-15](/bible/exodus/31#v12)**  taught in [Exodus 30-33](/study/exodus/30-33)

- Verily my sabbaths ye shall keep, for it is a sign between me and you throughout your generations, that ye may know that I am the LORD that doth sanctify you. Keeping the weekly sabbath and the feast days shows that Israel are his children and he is their God; the nations do not have this sabbath, and adopted Sunday worship is not the true sabbath given to the children of Israel.
- Every one that defileth the sabbath shall surely be put to death. The stoning was done away in Christ, but the judgment for breaking the sabbath still stands, and people still die on the sabbath day, Friday sundown to Saturday sundown; the Most High is justified, because it is his sign between him and his people.
- Six days may work be done, but in the seventh is the sabbath of rest, holy to the LORD. It is more than a day of rest and congregating; it is the sign of who Israel's God is, which is why saying the sabbath is done away takes away the very sign between Israel and God.
- From the questions: eating is allowed on the sabbath, but not cooking. What is baked and seethed is prepared the day before and laid up, so the sabbath meal is leftovers or simple food; no microwaving, no coffee pots, no kindling the cooking fire.
- There is no buying or selling on the sabbath or on the holy day, so gas and food are bought before sundown; no fishing either, for what could be called work opens the door to every man excusing his trade on the sabbath.
- The Lord gives no man license to sin, but Christ is Lord of the sabbath and brought grace for necessities out of our control, a blown tire or an empty tank on the way to keep the sabbath, or medicine when the body is in danger; it is lawful to do well on the sabbath days.
- Precept **[Ecclesiasticus 33:7-9](/bible/sirach/33#v7)**: Every day has the same sun, but by the knowledge of the Lord some days were made high days and hallowed; the sabbath is distinguished by God, not by man.
- Precept **[Exodus 35:3](/bible/exodus/35#v3)**: Kindle no fire throughout your habitations upon the sabbath day; this speaks to the cooking fire, not to warming the household.
- Precept **[Exodus 16:23](/bible/exodus/16#v23)**: Bake that which ye will bake to day, and that which remaineth is laid up until the morning; the sabbath food is cooked the day before.
- Precept **[Nehemiah 10:31](/bible/nehemiah/10#v31)**: If the people of the land bring ware or victuals on the sabbath day to sell, we would not buy it of them on the sabbath, or on the holy day.
- Precept **[Matthew 12:11-12](/bible/matthew/12#v11)**: A man is better than a sheep lifted out of the pit on the sabbath; wherefore it is lawful to do well on the sabbath days.
- Precept **[Matthew 12:8](/bible/matthew/12#v8)**: The Son of man is Lord even of the sabbath day; grace covers what is out of our control, never license to sin.

**[Exodus 31:16-17](/bible/exodus/31#v16)**  taught in [Exodus 30-33](/study/exodus/30-33)

- The children of Israel shall keep the sabbath, to observe the sabbath throughout their generations, for a perpetual covenant; it is a sign between the Most High and the children of Israel for ever, for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, and on the seventh day he rested, and was refreshed.
- Christians use [Mark 2:27](/bible/mark/2#v27) to say the sabbath is done away, but that is not what Christ said. The sabbath was made for man, the children of Israel; man was not put on the earth for the sabbath, the sabbath was put on the earth for man, and the Son of man is Lord of the sabbath because Christ instituted it. He rested for our sakes, that we should rest after the same similitude.
- Precept **[Mark 2:27-28](/bible/mark/2#v27)**: The sabbath was made for man, and not man for the sabbath; therefore the Son of man is Lord also of the sabbath.

**[Exodus 32:15-20](/bible/exodus/32#v15)**  taught in [Exodus 30-33](/study/exodus/30-33)

- He took the calf which they had made, burnt it in the fire, ground it to powder, strawed it upon the water, and made the children of Israel drink of it. Our forefathers were no joke about the law; Nehemiah likewise put hands on those who profaned the sabbath in his day.

**[Exodus 33:12-17](/bible/exodus/33#v12)**  taught in [Exodus 30-33](/study/exodus/30-33)

- The presence of the Most High in the midst is how Israel is known to be separated from all the nations, and it ties back to the sabbath sign: when Israel keeps the sabbath and gathers in his name, the Lord and Christ are in the midst.

**[Exodus 34:21](/bible/exodus/34#v21)**  taught in [Exodus 34-37](/study/exodus/34-37)

- Six days thou shalt work, but on the seventh day thou shalt rest: in earing time and in harvest thou shalt rest. Even when it was the time of harvest and gathering, Israel still rested on the seventh day.

**[Exodus 35:1-3](/bible/exodus/35#v1)**  taught in [Exodus 34-37](/study/exodus/34-37)

- Moses gathered all the congregation of the children of Israel: six days shall work be done, but on the seventh day there shall be to you an holy day, a sabbath of rest to the Lord; whosoever doeth work therein shall be put to death.
- Ye shall kindle no fire throughout your habitations upon the sabbath day: kindling fire goes to cooking. Bake that which ye will bake today, and seethe that ye will seethe, and lay up what remains to be kept unto the morning; you could not cook on the sabbath, so the day before is the prep day, and what you prepare that day is what you eat.
- Precept **[Exodus 16:23](/bible/exodus/16#v23)**: Bake that which ye will bake today, and lay up what remaineth to be kept until the morning; kindling fire on the sabbath goes to cooking, so the cooking was done on the preparation day.

**[Exodus 37:10-16](/bible/exodus/37#v10)**  taught in [Exodus 34-37](/study/exodus/34-37)

- This is the table of shewbread: on it Aaron and his sons placed twelve loaves of bread made from fine flour, and these twelve loaves represented the twelve tribes of Israel, a continual reminder of the everlasting promises, the covenant between the Most High and the children of Israel, and a memorial of His provision of food; the bread was eaten by Aaron and his sons and replaced every week on the sabbath.

## Leviticus

**[Leviticus 1:4-9](/bible/leviticus/1#v4)**  taught in [Leviticus 1](/study/leviticus/1)

- He put his hand upon the head of the burnt offering, and it was accepted for him to make an atonement for him; the bullock was killed before the Lord, the blood sprinkled round about upon the altar by the priests, and the parts laid in order upon the wood on the fire.
- Notice the fat is laid on the altar. Do not stumble when chapter 3 says not to eat fat: that deals with the fat of the sacrifice that the priest sent up in the offering, not the fat on a steak off your grill.
- The offering made by fire is a sweet savour unto the Lord. Christ walked in love and gave himself for us an offering and a sacrifice to God for a sweetsmelling savour: he fulfilled and replaced all these offerings, once and for all, in his death and resurrection.
- This is the context so much of Paul's letters sit in: he was convincing the congregations that they no longer had to sacrifice animals, because Pharisees were creeping in trying to turn them back to those things; therefore let no man judge you in meat or in drink.
- In our captivity, with no Levitical priesthood and no temple, keeping the commandments is the offering and the reasonable service: he that keepeth the law bringeth offerings enough, and we present our bodies a living sacrifice, sacrificing the lusts and pleasures of the flesh to follow the footsteps of Christ. And [Ezekiel 20](/bible/ezekiel/20) shows that when Israel is brought back through the wilderness, offerings will be required again in the holy mountain.
- Precept **[Colossians 2:16](/bible/colossians/2#v16)**: Let no man judge you in meat, or in drink; Paul defended the congregations from those pushing them back to the sacrifices.

**[Leviticus 4:1-3](/bible/leviticus/4#v1)**  taught in [Leviticus 2-5](/study/leviticus/2-5)

- The sin offering is for a soul that sins through ignorance against any of the commandments. You sin through ignorance when you get complacent: it was a double Sabbath and you forgot, and you bought or sold something you were not supposed to; an honest mistake. Even then it was not a verbal sorry; you literally brought an offering for your mistake.

**[Leviticus 4:22-26](/bible/leviticus/4#v22)**  taught in [Leviticus 2-5](/study/leviticus/2-5)

- The man found gathering sticks upon the Sabbath day shows the presumptuous side, immediately after that law was spoken. They did not kill him right away; they put him in ward, because it was not declared what should be done to him, and Moses got direction from the Lord. And the Lord said, the man shall be surely put to death, and all the congregation stoned him without the camp.
- Precept **[Numbers 15:32-36](/bible/numbers/15#v32)**: The man gathering sticks on the Sabbath was kept in ward until the mind of the Lord was declared, and the Lord said he shall surely be put to death.

**[Leviticus 6:6-7](/bible/leviticus/6#v6)**  taught in [Leviticus 6-9](/study/leviticus/6-9)

- The trespass offering was a ram without blemish, perfect as the Most High made it, and the priest made an atonement for him before the Lord and it was forgiven him. The sacrifice stood in your place for your sin.
- The whole law of sacrifice points to Christ, who came in the volume of the book. He is the offering without blemish and the atonement, and who has become the high priest is Jesus the Christ.
- Israel became the enemy of the Most High by sin. There are two kinds of enemies: the nations that are the natural enemies of the Most High, and Israel, who rebelled and vexed his holy spirit, and he was turned to be their enemy. Through the death of his Son the nation is reconciled, brought back together with its God.
- The letters of Paul were written to Israelites living in those lands and called by their names, Romans and Corinthians, the same way we are called American or Jamaican today; written today they would be the letter to the Jamaicans or the letter to the Puerto Ricans.
- Precept **[Ezekiel 45:17](/bible/ezekiel/45#v17)**: The sin offering, meat offering, burnt offering, and peace offerings were prepared to make reconciliation for the house of Israel; that is what reconciled means.

**[Leviticus 6:8-9](/bible/leviticus/6#v8)**  taught in [Leviticus 6-9](/study/leviticus/6-9)

- The burnt offering burned upon the altar all night unto the morning, and the fire of the altar was burning in it. When Exodus says kindle no fire on the sabbath day, that speaks of making food; the Levites were commanded to keep a fire burning on the altar continually, sabbath included.
- Christ used this very law on the Pharisees: have ye not read how that on the sabbath days the priests in the temple profane the sabbath, and are blameless? The priests had fire going in the temple every sabbath by commandment.
- Jeremiah carries the same word: I spake not unto your fathers concerning burnt offerings or sacrifices, but this thing commanded I them, obey my voice. Yet they hearkened not, but walked in the counsels of their evil heart and went backward and not forward; speak all these words unto them, but they will not hearken. That is seen every sabbath on the streets.
- Precept **[Exodus 35:3](/bible/exodus/35#v3)**: Kindle no fire throughout your habitations upon the sabbath day; that is fire for cooking, not the altar fire.
- Precept **[Matthew 12:5-7](/bible/matthew/12#v5)**: On the sabbath days the priests in the temple profane the sabbath and are blameless; if ye had known what this meaneth, I will have mercy and not sacrifice, ye would not have condemned the guiltless.

**[Leviticus 6:12-13](/bible/leviticus/6#v12)**  taught in [Leviticus 6-9](/study/leviticus/6-9)

- The fire upon the altar shall be burning in it; it shall not be put out. The priest burnt wood on it every morning, and that included the sabbath; the fire shall ever be burning upon the altar, and it shall never go out.
- This is exactly what Christ went into in Matthew 12: the priests profane the sabbath and are blameless, because the commanded altar fire never went out.

**[Leviticus 7:35-38](/bible/leviticus/7#v35)**  taught in [Leviticus 6-9](/study/leviticus/6-9)

- Let no man therefore judge you in meat or in drink: add the word offering, the meat offering and drink offering we just read in Leviticus. No man can condemn you because you are not sacrificing animals any more, or for how you observe a holyday, new moon, or sabbath pertaining to sacrifice, for those are a shadow of things to come, but the body is of Christ.
- Precept **[Colossians 2:14-17](/bible/colossians/2#v14)**: Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, nailing it to his cross; let no man therefore judge you in meat, or in drink, or of an holyday, or of the new moon, or of the sabbath days: which are a shadow of things to come; but the body is of Christ.

**[Leviticus 8:14-17](/bible/leviticus/8#v14)**  taught in [Leviticus 6-9](/study/leviticus/6-9)

- Aaron and his sons laid their hands upon the head of the bullock for the sin offering, and Moses put the blood upon the horns of the altar with his finger, purified the altar, and sanctified it, to make reconciliation upon it; reconciliation goes back to making reconciliation for the house of Israel.
- The fat, the caul, and the kidneys, the guts, were burnt upon the altar, but the bullock with his hide and flesh was burnt with fire without the camp. Christ's crucifixion lines up with the law of sacrifice: he was taken outside Jerusalem to the place called Golgotha and crucified there.
- Precept **[Ezekiel 45:17](/bible/ezekiel/45#v17)**: The sin offering and the burnt offering were prepared to make reconciliation for the house of Israel; the purified altar carried that reconciliation.

**[Leviticus 10:8-11](/bible/leviticus/10#v8)**  taught in [Leviticus 10-13](/study/leviticus/10-13)

- Some read this and think Aaron and his sons could never drink, but going precept upon precept, this speaks of when they went into the tabernacle of the congregation: they were at work doing the Lord's work, and there was no drinking on the job.
- Ezekiel lays out the priests' work rules. The priests the Levites, the sons of Zadok, kept the charge of the sanctuary; when they entered the gates of the inner court they were clocking in, clothed in linen garments and linen bonnets, girded with nothing that causeth sweat; and going out to the people in the utter court they put off the garments wherein they ministered and put on other garments, like taking off a work uniform.
- Neither shall any priest drink wine when they enter into the inner court; that is exactly what verse 9 speaks of.
- They had to be sober minded because they taught the people the difference between the holy and profane and caused them to discern between the unclean and the clean, and in controversy they stood in judgment, judging according to the judgments of the Most High.
- On the priests' hair: they were not to shave their heads nor suffer their locks to grow long, but only poll their heads. Polling the head means keeping the hair trimmed and maintained; you can grow it, but keep it up.
- Precept **[Ezekiel 44:23-24](/bible/ezekiel/44#v23)**: They teach the difference between the holy and profane and stand in judgment in controversy; the priest must be sober dealing with the people.

**[Leviticus 13:1-8](/bible/leviticus/13#v1)**  taught in [Leviticus 10-13](/study/leviticus/10-13)

- The plague of leprosy is discerned by the priest. When the hair in the plague is turned white and the plague in sight is deeper than the skin of the flesh, it is a plague of leprosy and the priest pronounces him unclean; if not, the priest shuts him up seven days, and seven days more if the plague is at a stay, and if the plague is somewhat dark and spread not, it is but a scab and he is pronounced clean; but a scab that spreads much abroad after his cleansing is a leprosy.

**[Leviticus 13:47-52](/bible/leviticus/13#v47)**  taught in [Leviticus 10-13](/study/leviticus/10-13)

- The plague of leprosy can be in a garment, woollen or linen, in the warp or woof or in any thing made of skin, greenish or reddish; it is shewed unto the priest and shut up seven days, and if the plague be spread in the garment it is a fretting leprosy: the garment is burnt in the fire. As with Miriam, the clothing wherein the plague is had to be dealt with.

**[Leviticus 14:8-9](/bible/leviticus/14#v8)**  taught in [Leviticus 14-15](/study/leviticus/14-15)

- On the seventh day he shaves all his hair off his head, his beard, and his eyebrows, washes his clothes and his flesh in water, and is clean.

**[Leviticus 14:33-42](/bible/leviticus/14#v33)**  taught in [Leviticus 14-15](/study/leviticus/14-15)

- If at the seventh day the plague be spread in the walls, the stones in which the plague is are taken away and cast into an unclean place without the city; the house is scraped within round about, the dust poured out without the city into an unclean place, and other stones and other mortar are put in their place.

**[Leviticus 18:4-6](/bible/leviticus/18#v4)**  taught in [Leviticus 18-21](/study/leviticus/18-21)

- The Ten Commandments are not the whole of the law but a summary. Under the Sabbath commandment sit sub-statutes such as no buying, no selling, no working; likewise under the law of adultery sit all the laws of this chapter, the ordinances of marriage and the forbidden beds.

**[Leviticus 19:1-3](/bible/leviticus/19#v1)**  taught in [Leviticus 18-21](/study/leviticus/18-21)

- Keep my sabbaths: the feast days are Sabbaths also. The Passover, the first and last days of the feast of Tabernacles, the day of atonement, and the memorial of blowing of trumpets are all Sabbaths; no servile work, no buying and selling, though cooking is permitted on the feast days.

**[Leviticus 19:30-32](/bible/leviticus/19#v30)**  taught in [Leviticus 18-21](/study/leviticus/18-21)

- Keep my sabbaths and reverence my sanctuary.

**[Leviticus 23:1-3](/bible/leviticus/23#v1)**  taught in [Leviticus 22-25](/study/leviticus/22-25)

- These are the feasts of the Lord, holy convocations which He Himself proclaimed. The Most High gave us holy days; a holiday not listed in this Bible and not ordained of the Most High has nothing holy about it. By the knowledge of the Lord the days were distinguished: some He made high days, as the Passover, Tabernacles, the new moon, and the Sabbath, and the days He did not name are ordinary days.
- Six days shall work be done, but the seventh day is the sabbath of rest, an holy convocation. No matter where your dwelling is, Israel, North America, London, or Australia, the Sabbath is still the Sabbath: Friday sundown to Saturday sundown in whatever time zone you are in.
- Keeping the Sabbath holy is more than no working. Ye shall kindle no fire throughout your habitations upon the sabbath day, so there is no cooking on the Sabbath; and if the people of the land bring ware or victuals on the sabbath day to sell, we would not buy it of them, so there is no buying and no selling.
- It is a holy convocation, which means a gathering. Keeping the Sabbath sitting at home with your gerbil or your hamster is not keeping the Sabbath; you have to come out of your comfort zone and congregate.
- Precept **[Exodus 35:1-3](/bible/exodus/35#v1)**: On the holy sabbath day whosoever doeth work shall be put to death, and ye shall kindle no fire throughout your habitations; no cooking on the Sabbath.
- Precept **[Nehemiah 10:31](/bible/nehemiah/10#v31)**: If the people of the land bring ware or victuals on the sabbath day to sell, we would not buy it of them; no buying and no selling on the Sabbath.

**[Leviticus 23:4-8](/bible/leviticus/23#v4)**  taught in [Leviticus 22-25](/study/leviticus/22-25)
