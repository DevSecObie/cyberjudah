The blessings and curses the law sets before Israel, and the teaching that reads the curses as the history of the people who are, in this teaching, Israel today: the so-called Blacks, Hispanics and Native Americans. Deuteronomy 28 is the chapter the recordings return to more than almost any other; the classes open it to answer why their people suffer as they do, and close it with the promise that the curses end when Israel keeps the commandments and Christ returns.

## Leviticus

**[Leviticus 26:17](/bible/leviticus/26#v17)**  taught in [ESCP 2.0 | America Lynchings And Black Mental Health](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E8s2FOluXeo&t=1211s)

- The same curses stand in Leviticus 26 as in Deuteronomy 28. "They that hate you shall reign over you": the teaching reads the terror, lynchings and killings their people have suffered as this verse fulfilled, and names disobedience as the cause.

**[Leviticus 26:40](/bible/leviticus/26#v40)**  taught in [GENERATIONAL CURSES](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q5Bdp3FLdO0&t=4743s)

- The way out of the curses begins with confession: "if they shall confess their iniquity, and the iniquity of their fathers". The classes call the curses generational because the sins of the fathers are carried until the people confess them and turn back to the law.
- Precept **[Leviticus 26:44](/bible/leviticus/26#v44)**: even in the land of their enemies God will not cast them away or break his covenant with them.

## Deuteronomy

**[Deuteronomy 28:1](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1)**  taught in [Re-Instruct Your Mind](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=85VexRXsWUI&t=7467s)

- The chapter opens with a condition: if Israel hearkens diligently to keep all the commandments, God will set them on high above all nations of the earth. The blessings and the curses are the two sides of one covenant.

**[Deuteronomy 28:15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)**  taught in [Passover Class: Exodus From America Modern Day Egypt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VyDEbsxgcas&t=2534s)

- "If thou wilt not hearken... all these curses shall come upon thee, and overtake thee." This is the verse the classes give when asked why their people are where they are: a covenant was made with a chosen people, and because they did not do what God commanded, the curses came.
- Keeping the commandments under the new covenant is the answer the teaching draws from it.

**[Deuteronomy 28:28](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v28)**  taught in [Religious Roots Of Injustice](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7wFyX3IvcEE&t=6634s)

- "Madness, and blindness, and astonishment of heart": read of the psychological damage done to the people, the derangement and dysfunction the classes see among them, and the blindness that keeps them from knowing who they are.

**[Deuteronomy 28:32](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v32)**  taught in [#IUIC | Truth Be Told: The Body Snatchers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=21F1jTEJ1EM&t=2460s)

- "Thy sons and thy daughters shall be given unto another people": read of children sold away in slavery, and today of children raised and taught by other nations and their institutions.

**[Deuteronomy 28:37](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v37)**  taught in [WHAT IS MY NAME..??](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Saste5H2BcE&t=629s)

- "An astonishment, a proverb, and a byword, among all nations": the teaching applies it to the names their people have been called by others, such as African American, in place of the name Israel.

**[Deuteronomy 28:43](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v43)**  taught in [ESCAPING THE PLANTATION 2.0 | JUDAH VS JEWISH: THE RESURRECTION OF THE GODS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-v_u3wllHMo&t=2570s)

- "The stranger that is within thee shall get up above thee very high; and thou shalt come down very low": read of their people at the bottom of society while other nations rise above them in their own land.

**[Deuteronomy 28:46](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v46)**  taught in [76 Year Old Learns Truth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FE8hILKrNhE&t=170s)

- The curses are "upon thee for a sign and for a wonder, and upon thy seed for ever". This is the verse behind the teaching that the curses are generational: they fall on the children because of the fathers' breaking of the law, and they identify the people they fall on.

**[Deuteronomy 28:48](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v48)**  taught in [Man Made Religions Are Not Of God](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1G2DPCCHjD0&t=346s)

- "Therefore shalt thou serve thine enemies... in hunger, and in thirst, and in nakedness... and he shall put a yoke of iron upon thy neck": read of slavery, the people sold into all four corners of the earth to serve their enemies, chained with iron.

**[Deuteronomy 28:49](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v49)**  taught in [Esau, Rome, & America: Do Edomites Still Exist?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hrsAyKK8LQk&t=2258s)

- "A nation... from far, from the end of the earth, as swift as the eagle flieth; a nation whose tongue thou shalt not understand": the teaching reads the eagle as the emblem of the European empires and the nation as the conquerors who came by sea.

**[Deuteronomy 28:64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)**  taught in [Christianity: Death of A People](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyZm629UjMk&t=571s)

- "The Lord shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other; and there thou shalt serve other gods": the scattering of Israel into the nations, and the religions of those nations, Christianity among them in this teaching, that the scattered people took up.

**[Deuteronomy 28:65](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v65)**  taught in [Gods Avengers Vs Gods Enemies](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QHB5Q_QEyNI&t=3313s)

- "Among these nations shalt thou find no ease": wherever the scattered people go, a trembling heart and sorrow of mind follow them, and the classes teach there is no rest for them until they gather together under God's law.

**[Deuteronomy 28:68](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v68)**  taught in [Passover Class: Exodus From America Modern Day Egypt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VyDEbsxgcas&t=3022s)

- "The Lord shall bring thee into Egypt again with ships... and there ye shall be sold unto your enemies for bondmen and bondwomen, and no man shall buy you." The classes hold that Egypt here stands for bondage and that Moses foresaw the transatlantic slave trade.
- The Passover teaching reads the exodus from Egypt as the pattern for a coming exodus of their people out of this bondage.
