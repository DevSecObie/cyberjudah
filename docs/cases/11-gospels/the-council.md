**Seeking false witness against Christ; condemning him by night; delivering him to the Gentiles; forbidding the apostles; stoning Stephen** · Temporal judgment · Gospels

Caiaphas had already ruled that it was expedient one man die for the people. The council sought false witness to put Jesus to death and found none that agreed; then the high priest adjured him and condemned him for his answer. They delivered him to Pilate, chose Barabbas, and afterward beat and forbade his apostles, and gnashed on Stephen and stoned him.

## The offense

Every rule of the court broken in one night: witnesses suborned, testimony not agreeing, a capital sentence at night by the accusers themselves, and the prisoner condemned by his own compelled confession. Stephen's charge to them: ye stiffnecked, which of the prophets have not your fathers persecuted? and ye have been now the betrayers and murderers of the Just One.

## The judgment

Christ told them the kingdom would be taken from them and their house left desolate; Paul says the wrath is come upon them to the uttermost. Peter to their faces: the stone which was set at nought of you builders. Within the generation the temple in which they sat was rubble.

## Laws broken

- [4E.1](/law/04-law-and-justice/4e#4E.1) You shall not bear false witness against another nor raise a false report.
- [4E.4](/law/04-law-and-justice/4e#4E.4) Do not join your hand with a wicked man to be an unrighteous witness.
- [4E.9](/law/04-law-and-justice/4e#4E.9) You shall not testify in a dispute for the sake of following the majority and thus pervert justice.
- [4E.10](/law/04-law-and-justice/4e#4E.10) At the testimony of two or three witnesses shall the fact of the matter be established.
- [4F.13](/law/04-law-and-justice/4f#4F.13) Doing injustice by justifying the wicked and condemning the righteous is an abomination and condemned by God.
- [4F.6](/law/04-law-and-justice/4f#4F.6) You shall keep far from a false matter.
- [8C.9](/law/08-government-civil-officers/8c#8C.9) The judge that justifies the wicked and condemns the just is an abomination to God.
- [10A.2](/law/10-capital-laws-offenses/10a#10A.2) Shedding of innocent blood is an abomination to God.
- [17K.5](/law/17-personal-character-traits/17k#17K.5) We are not to have hypocritical leaders because they ensnare the people.
