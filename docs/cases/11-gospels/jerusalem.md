**Killing the prophets and stoning them that are sent; not knowing the time of her visitation** · Captivity · Gospels

Christ wept over the city and told it what was coming: thine enemies shall cast a trench about thee, and lay thee even with the ground, and thy children within thee, because thou knewest not the time of thy visitation. He said not one stone of the temple would be left on another, and that this generation would see it.

## The offense

The blood of all the prophets shed from the foundation of the world, from Abel to Zacharias, required of this generation; and the rejection of the one who would have gathered her children as a hen her chickens. Daniel had said the people of the prince that shall come would destroy the city and the sanctuary.

## The judgment

There shall be great distress in the land, and wrath upon this people. And they shall fall by the edge of the sword, and shall be led away captive into all nations: and Jerusalem shall be trodden down of the Gentiles, until the times of the Gentiles be fulfilled. Deuteronomy 28 had described the siege in detail.
