**Condemning a man he had three times pronounced innocent** · Sentence not recorded · Gospels

Pilate found no fault in him, said so three times, was warned by his wife's dream, and offered the crowd Barabbas instead. When they threatened that he was not Caesar's friend, he washed his hands, said, I am innocent of the blood of this just person, and delivered Jesus to be crucified.

## The offense

The judge who justifies the wicked and condemns the just is an abomination; the judge who follows a multitude to do evil is forbidden by name. He knew, he said so, and he signed. Water does not answer for a sentence.

## The judgment

Scripture pronounces nothing on Pilate personally; Acts names him with Herod and the Gentiles and the people of Israel as gathered together against the Lord's holy child, to do whatsoever thy hand and thy counsel determined before to be done. Paul cites his court as the place where Christ witnessed a good confession.
