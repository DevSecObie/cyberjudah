**Insurrection and murder** · Spared · Gospels

A notable prisoner, lying bound with them that had made insurrection and had committed murder in the insurrection. Pilate offered the crowd a choice between him and Jesus; the chief priests moved them to ask for Barabbas. He was released and Jesus was crucified.

## The offense

Rebellion against the government and murder, both capital under the law of Moses and of Rome. Peter states the exchange to the men of Israel: ye denied the Holy One and the Just, and desired a murderer to be granted unto you.

## The judgment

Released, by the custom of the feast and the demand of the crowd. The guilty man walked free because the innocent was condemned in his place; the gospels leave the figure there and say no more of him.

## Laws broken

- [10A.4](/law/10-capital-laws-offenses/10a#10A.4) Those guilty of murder are to be put to death.
- [10G.3](/law/10-capital-laws-offenses/10g#10G.3) Rebellion and insurrection against the government is punishable by death.
- [4F.13](/law/04-law-and-justice/4f#4F.13) Doing injustice by justifying the wicked and condemning the righteous is an abomination and condemned by God.
- [8C.9](/law/08-government-civil-officers/8c#8C.9) The judge that justifies the wicked and condemns the just is an abomination to God.
