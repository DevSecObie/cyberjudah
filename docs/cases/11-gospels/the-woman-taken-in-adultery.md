**Adultery, in the very act** · Spared · Gospels

The scribes and Pharisees brought a woman taken in adultery and set her in the midst: Moses commanded that such should be stoned; what sayest thou? They said it to tempt him. He wrote on the ground, and then: he that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her.

## The offense

Hers was capital under Leviticus 20 and Deuteronomy 22, and the accusers were right about the statute. But the same statute requires the man, absent here, and Deuteronomy 17 requires the witnesses to cast the first stones on their own testimony. Christ put the witnesses to the test the law itself imposes.

## The judgment

Convicted by their own conscience, they went out one by one, beginning at the eldest. With no witness remaining there was no case: hath no man condemned thee? Neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more. Spared, and commanded.
