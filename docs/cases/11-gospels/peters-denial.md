**Denying Christ three times with cursing and swearing** · Reprieve · Gospels

Though I should die with thee, yet will I not deny thee. Before the cock crew twice he had denied him three times, the last with oaths and curses, to a maid and to bystanders in the high priest's courtyard. The Lord turned and looked upon Peter, and he went out and wept bitterly.

## The offense

Whosoever shall deny me before men, him will I also deny before my Father. Peter did it three times and sealed it with a false oath. The sin was foretold, and so was the mercy: I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not.

## The judgment

No sentence but the look and the tears. After the resurrection Christ asked him three times, lovest thou me? and three times restored him: feed my sheep. Peter's godly sorrow worked repentance; Judas' sorrow worked death.
