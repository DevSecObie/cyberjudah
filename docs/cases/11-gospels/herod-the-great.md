**Slaying all the male children of Bethlehem from two years old and under** · Sentence not recorded · Gospels

Mocked by the wise men who did not return to report the child, Herod was exceeding wroth and sent and slew all the children in Bethlehem and its coasts from two years old and under, according to the time he had inquired of them. Matthew hears Rachel weeping in Ramah.

## The offense

Innocent blood on a scale of a town, from a king's fear for his throne. The children's only crime was their age and their address.

## The judgment

Matthew records only that Herod died and Joseph brought the child back from Egypt. Scripture pronounces no sentence on him; secular history records a hideous death. The law's word on innocent blood stands over the case regardless.
