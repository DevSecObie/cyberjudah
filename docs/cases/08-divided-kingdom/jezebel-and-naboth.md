**Judicial murder to seize an inheritance** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom

Ahab coveted Naboth's vineyard; Naboth would not sell his fathers' inheritance. Jezebel arranged false witnesses to accuse him of blaspheming God and the king, had him stoned, and handed the land to Ahab.

## The offense

Covetousness executed through the machinery of law itself: a proclaimed fast, suborned witnesses, a capital charge, and a stoning, every step a mockery of the statutes it borrowed.

## The judgment

Elijah met Ahab in the stolen vineyard with a sentence on the whole house. Dogs would lick Ahab's blood and eat Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel. Both came to pass.
