**Polluting the house of the Lord; mocking the messengers of God; refusing the Sabbath and the land's sabbaths** · Captivity · Divided Kingdom

All the chief of the priests and the people transgressed very much after all the abominations of the heathen and polluted the house of the Lord. The Lord sent messengers because he had compassion; they mocked them, despised his words, and misused his prophets until there was no remedy.

## The offense

Ezekiel's catalogue of Jerusalem: princes shedding blood, fathers and mothers set light by, strangers oppressed, sabbaths profaned, incest, bribes for blood, usury, priests who put no difference between holy and profane, prophets daubing with untempered mortar. Jeremiah adds the Sabbath gates: bear no burden on the sabbath day, and they would not hear.

## The judgment

Nebuchadnezzar slew their young men in the sanctuary, burned the house of God, and carried the remnant to Babylon, to fulfil threescore and ten years until the land had enjoyed her sabbaths. Daniel and Baruch both confess it in the same words: the curse is poured upon us, as it is written in the law of Moses.
