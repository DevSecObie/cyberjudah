**Prophesying in the name of other gods** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom

Four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal cried from morning to noon and cut themselves with knives until the blood gushed out, and there was neither voice nor any to answer. Elijah's bullock, drenched three times, was consumed by fire from heaven with the water and the stones.

## The offense

Deuteronomy 13 and 18 are the statutes: the prophet who speaks in the name of other gods shall die, and the one who says, let us go after other gods, shall be put to death. They had done it in front of all Israel for years at the queen's table.

## The judgment

When the people fell on their faces and said, the Lord, he is the God, Elijah had the prophets taken down to the brook Kishon and slain there. Then the rain came.

## Laws broken

- [10F.3](/law/10-capital-laws-offenses/10f#10F.3) A preacher or a prophet who leads people to worship false gods is to be put to death, because he has counseled rebel against God.
- [10F.4](/law/10-capital-laws-offenses/10f#10F.4) Those who entice others to serve pagan gods and religions are to be put to death.
- [10F.1](/law/10-capital-laws-offenses/10f#10F.1) Anyone that has served or worshipped false or pagan gods shall be put to death.
- [20F.1](/law/20-churches-ministers/20f#20F.1) Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves.
