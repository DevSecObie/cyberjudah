**Rebuilding the high places; altars to the host of heaven in the temple; passing his sons through the fire; witchcraft; filling Jerusalem with innocent blood** · Captivity · Divided Kingdom

Hezekiah's son undid everything his father had done and more: altars for Baal, a grove, worship of all the host of heaven in both courts of the Lord's house, his children through the fire, observing times, enchantments, familiar spirits, wizards. He shed innocent blood until Jerusalem was filled from one end to another.

## The offense

The record says he seduced Judah to do more evil than the nations the Lord destroyed before them. Every capital offense against God in the handbook's tenth part appears in his reign. Judah's captivity, decades later, is charged to him by name.

## The judgment

The Lord brought the captains of the host of Assyria, who took Manasseh among the thorns, bound him with fetters, and carried him to Babylon. There he humbled himself greatly and prayed; the Prayer of Manasseh preserves the tradition of it. God brought him back to his kingdom and he took away the strange gods. But the sentence on Judah for his sins stood: the Lord would not pardon.
