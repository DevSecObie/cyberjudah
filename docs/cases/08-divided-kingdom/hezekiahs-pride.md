**Showing all his treasures to Babylon in the pride of his heart** · Reprieve · Divided Kingdom

Healed of a mortal sickness and given fifteen years, Hezekiah received envoys from Babylon and showed them the house of his precious things, the silver, the gold, the armor, everything. Isaiah asked what they had seen. Nothing hidden, said the king.

## The offense

Chronicles: Hezekiah rendered not again according to the benefit done unto him, for his heart was lifted up. God left him, to try him, that he might know all that was in his heart. A king glorying in his riches before the empire that would carry them away.

## The judgment

All that is in thine house shall be carried into Babylon, and of thy sons shall they take away, and they shall be eunuchs in the palace of the king of Babylon. Hezekiah humbled himself, and the wrath came not in his days: is it not good, if peace and truth be in my days?
