**Forsaking the Lord after Jehoiada's death; stoning Zechariah in the temple court** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom

The boy king saved by the priest Jehoiada did right all the days of Jehoiada. When the priest died, the princes of Judah came and Joash hearkened to them, left the house of the Lord, and served groves and idols. Jehoiada's son Zechariah rebuked him by the Spirit; the king had him stoned in the court of the temple.

## The offense

Apostasy the moment his mentor's restraint was gone, then the murder of that mentor's son for prophesying against it. Thus Joash the king remembered not the kindness which Jehoiada his father had done to him. Christ counts Zechariah's blood among the prophets' blood required of that generation.

## The judgment

Zechariah's dying word, the Lord look upon it and require it, was answered within the year: a small Syrian company defeated a very great host, and his own servants conspired against him for the blood of the sons of Jehoiada and slew him on his bed. He was not buried in the sepulchres of the kings.
