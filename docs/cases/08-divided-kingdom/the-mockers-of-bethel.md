**Mocking the prophet of the Lord** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom

As Elisha went up to Bethel, the seat of Jeroboam's calf, a company came out of the city mocking him: go up, thou bald head. He cursed them in the name of the Lord, and two she-bears came out of the wood and tore forty-two of them.

## The offense

Contempt for the office, in the city that had made contempt for the Lord its religion. Go up mocks Elijah's ascent, which the sons of the prophets had just witnessed. The Hebrew word covers youths, not infants; forty-two of them were a crowd, not a prank.

## The judgment

Leviticus warned that if Israel walked contrary to him, the Lord would send wild beasts among them which should rob them of their children. Forty-two were torn. Elisha went on to Carmel.
