**Breaking his oath to Nebuchadnezzar; stiffening his neck against the Lord's word by Jeremiah** · Captivity · Divided Kingdom

Sworn by God to serve Babylon, Zedekiah rebelled against Nebuchadnezzar and would not humble himself before Jeremiah speaking from the mouth of the Lord. Warned to the last that surrender would save the city and his life, he feared the Jews who had defected more than he feared the word.

## The offense

Ezekiel names the oath-breaking as the Lord's own quarrel: seeing he despised the oath by breaking the covenant, when, lo, he had given his hand. Chronicles adds that he stiffened his neck and hardened his heart from turning unto the Lord God of Israel.

## The judgment

Taken fleeing by night near Jericho, he watched his sons slain at Riblah, then had his eyes put out, and was carried in chains to Babylon, where he died in prison. The city and the temple burned behind him. Ezekiel's thou profane wicked prince of Israel, whose day is come.
