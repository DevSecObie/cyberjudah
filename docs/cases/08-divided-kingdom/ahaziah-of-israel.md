**Inquiring of Baal-zebub the god of Ekron** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom

Ahab's son fell through a lattice in Samaria and sent messengers to ask Baal-zebub whether he would recover. Elijah met them on the road: is it not because there is not a God in Israel that ye go to inquire of Baal-zebub? Two captains of fifty sent to arrest the prophet were consumed by fire.

## The offense

A king of Israel treating a Philistine idol as his oracle, and then sending soldiers against the man who told him so. The law forbids the ruler to consult mediums and spiritists, and forbids the people to listen to them.

## The judgment

Thou shalt not come down from that bed on which thou art gone up, but shalt surely die. So he died according to the word of the Lord which Elijah had spoken, and having no son, his brother reigned.
