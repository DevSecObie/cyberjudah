**Executing the house of Ahab, then keeping the calves of Jeroboam** · Temporal judgment · Divided Kingdom

Anointed to cut off the house of Ahab, Jehu killed Joram, Ahaziah, Jezebel, seventy sons of Ahab, and every worshipper of Baal in Samaria, and destroyed Baal out of Israel. But from the golden calves at Bethel and Dan Jehu departed not.

## The offense

Zeal for the Lord that stopped exactly where it became inconvenient to the throne. He took no heed to walk in the law of the Lord God of Israel with all his heart. Hosea adds that the blood of Jezreel would be visited on his house, judging the manner of his executions even where the sentence was God's.

## The judgment

Because he had done well in executing what was right, his sons sat on Israel's throne to the fourth generation. Because of the calves, the Lord began in those days to cut Israel short: Hazael smote them in all their coasts. His dynasty ended with Zechariah, murdered after six months.
