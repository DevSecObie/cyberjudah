**Prophesying a lie in the name of the Lord** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom

In the temple, before the priests and all the people, Hananiah declared that the Lord would break Babylon's yoke within two years and bring back the vessels and the captives. He broke the wooden yoke from Jeremiah's neck to prove it.

## The offense

Deuteronomy's test: the prophet who presumes to speak a word in my name which I have not commanded him, that prophet shall die. He made the people to trust in a lie and preached rebellion against the Lord, who had told Judah to serve Babylon.

## The judgment

Jeremiah: the Lord hath not sent thee. This year thou shalt die, because thou hast taught rebellion against the Lord. So Hananiah the prophet died the same year in the seventh month.
