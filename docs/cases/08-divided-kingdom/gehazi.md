**Greed and lying to a prophet** · Cursed · Divided Kingdom

Elisha refused payment from the healed Syrian Naaman. His servant Gehazi ran after Naaman, invented a story to collect silver and garments, then lied to Elisha's face about where he had been.

## The offense

Covetousness turned to fraud: he claimed the prophet had sent him, took the goods, hid them, and denied it. He traded the integrity of the prophetic office for money.

## The judgment

Elisha, knowing all of it, pronounced that Naaman's leprosy would cling to Gehazi and his seed forever. He went out from his presence a leper, white as snow.
