**Burning his children in the fire; copying the altar of Damascus; sacrificing to the gods that smote him** · Temporal judgment · Divided Kingdom

Ahaz walked in the ways of the kings of Israel, made molten images for Baalim, burnt his children in the fire after the abominations of the heathen, and hired Assyria against Syria with the temple's silver. Defeated, he sacrificed to the gods of Damascus because they had helped his enemies.

## The offense

Child sacrifice, the sin the Canaanites were destroyed for; a pagan altar pattern installed in the Lord's house; shutting up the temple doors; and the logic of a lost man, worship whoever is winning. Wisdom describes the Canaanites he imitated as merciless murderers of children.

## The judgment

The Lord delivered him into the hand of Syria and of Israel, who slew a hundred and twenty thousand in one day, and Assyria distressed him instead of strengthening him. In the time of his distress did he trespass yet more against the Lord: this is that king Ahaz. He was buried outside the kings' sepulchres.
