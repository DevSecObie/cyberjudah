**Two centuries of the sins of Jeroboam; secret sins; idols, high places, and children through the fire** · Captivity · Divided Kingdom

Second Kings 17 is the indictment of a nation: they feared other gods, walked in the statutes of the heathen and of the kings of Israel, built high places in every city, served idols the Lord had said they should not, made their sons and daughters pass through the fire, used divination and enchantments, and sold themselves to do evil.

## The offense

The Lord testified against Israel and against Judah by all the prophets and all the seers, and they hardened their necks like the necks of their fathers. Hosea's summary: swearing, lying, killing, stealing, adultery; my people are destroyed for lack of knowledge.

## The judgment

The Lord was very angry with Israel and removed them out of his sight: Assyria carried them away and placed them in Halah and Habor and the cities of the Medes. Deuteronomy had said it: the Lord shall scatter thee among all people. There was none left but the tribe of Judah only.
