**Devising mischief and giving wicked counsel in the city** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom

In vision Ezekiel saw twenty-five men at the east gate of the temple, princes of the people, Jaazaniah and Pelatiah among them, who said, it is not near; let us build houses: this city is the caldron, and we be the flesh. He was told to prophesy against them.

## The offense

Rulers who reassured the city against the prophets, treating Jerusalem as a safe pot for its own meat, while multiplying slain in its streets. The Lord: ye have not walked in my statutes, neither executed my judgments, but have done after the manners of the heathen.

## The judgment

As Ezekiel prophesied, Pelatiah the son of Benaiah died. The prophet fell on his face and cried out, wilt thou make a full end of the remnant of Israel?

## Laws broken

- [8A.9](/law/08-government-civil-officers/8a#8A.9) Irresponsible civil leaders are they who are either blind, ignorant, lazy, greedy, self-centered, drunkards, or act for their own gain.
- [8A.13](/law/08-government-civil-officers/8a#8A.13) Government officers are to execute judgment and righteousness, and deliver the spoiled out of the hand of the oppressor; they are to do no wrong, do no violence to the stranger, the fatherless, nor the widow, neither shed innocent blood in the land.
- [8A.19](/law/08-government-civil-officers/8a#8A.19) If a ruler hearken to lies, all his aides and ministers become wicked.
- [10A.2](/law/10-capital-laws-offenses/10a#10A.2) Shedding of innocent blood is an abomination to God.
- [2G.1](/law/02-relationship-to-god/2g#2G.1) We are not to learn or follow the ways, customs or abominations of the heathen or of any ungodly nation.
