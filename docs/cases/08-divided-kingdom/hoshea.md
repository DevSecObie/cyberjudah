<p class="casehead"><span class="badge badge--warning verdict">Captivity</span> <span class="charge">Doing evil in the sight of the Lord; conspiring with Egypt against Assyria</span><br/><span class="era">Divided Kingdom</span></p>

Hoshea the son of Elah, last king of the northern kingdom, did evil though not as the kings before him. He became tributary to Assyria, then conspired with So king of Egypt and withheld tribute. Shalmaneser shut him in prison, besieged Samaria three years, and carried Israel away to Assyria.

## The offense

Hoshea the son of Elah began to reign in the twelfth year of Ahaz and reigned nine years in Samaria. He did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, but not as the kings of Israel that were before him (2 Kings 17:1–2). The qualification does not excuse his reign; it measures how far the standard had fallen when the best that could be said of a king was that he was less evil than his predecessors.

Shalmaneser king of Assyria came against him, and Hoshea became his servant and paid tribute. But the king of Assyria found conspiracy in Hoshea, for he had sent messengers to So king of Egypt and brought no present as he had done year by year (2 Kings 17:3–4). Instead of turning to the Lord, Hoshea turned to Egypt, the very nation from which the Lord had brought Israel out.

## The judgment

The king of Assyria shut Hoshea up and bound him in prison. He came up throughout all the land and besieged Samaria three years. In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyria took Samaria and carried Israel away, placing them in Halah, in Habor by the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes (2 Kings 17:4–6).

The northern kingdom ceased to exist. The Lord removed Israel out of His sight, as He had said by all His servants the prophets (2 Kings 17:23). Every warning through every prophet sent to the northern kingdom was fulfilled in a single removal. The accumulated sin from Jeroboam through Hoshea was answered in captivity, and the tribes were scattered among the nations.
