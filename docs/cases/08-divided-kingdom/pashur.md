**Smiting the prophet and putting him in the stocks; prophesying lies** · Captivity · Divided Kingdom

Pashur, chief governor in the house of the Lord, heard Jeremiah prophesy the city's fall, struck him, and put him in the stocks at the high gate of Benjamin overnight. In the morning Jeremiah gave him a new name.

## The offense

A priest using his office to punish the word of God, while himself prophesying comfort that was a lie. The law reserves stripes for the wicked after judgment; he used them on the Lord's messenger before any.

## The judgment

The Lord hath not called thy name Pashur, but Magor-missabib, terror on every side. Thou shalt go into captivity to Babylon, and there thou shalt die and be buried, thou and all thy friends, to whom thou hast prophesied lies.
