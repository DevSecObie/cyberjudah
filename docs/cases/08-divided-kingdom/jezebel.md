**Killing the prophets of the Lord; promoting Baal; witchcrafts** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom

Ahab's Zidonian queen cut off the prophets of the Lord, fed four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal at her table, swore to kill Elijah, and stirred up her husband to every evil. Jehu named her whoredoms and witchcrafts as the reason there could be no peace.

## The offense

A foreign woman ruling a king of Israel into apostasy, with the machinery of state turned against the Lord's prophets. Revelation makes her name the type of the woman who calls herself a prophetess and teaches the servants of God to commit fornication and eat things sacrificed to idols.

## The judgment

Elijah pronounced it: the dogs shall eat Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel. Jehu had her eunuchs throw her from the window; horses trod her underfoot, and when they went to bury her they found no more than the skull, the feet, and the palms of her hands.
