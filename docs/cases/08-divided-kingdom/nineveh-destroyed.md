**A bloody city, full of lies and robbery, mistress of witchcrafts** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom

The repentance of Jonah's day did not last. Nahum's burden against Nineveh charges the city with blood, plunder, harlotries, and witchcrafts by which she sold nations; the Lord is slow to anger, and will not at all acquit the wicked.

## The offense

Assyria had been the rod of God's anger against Israel and then gloried in its own hand. Zephaniah names the heart of it: this is the rejoicing city that dwelt carelessly, that said in her heart, I am, and there is none beside me.

## The judgment

The gates of the rivers shall be opened, and the palace dissolved. Nineveh is laid waste; who will bemoan her? There is no healing of thy bruise. Tobit records hearing of its destruction by Nebuchadnezzar and Ahasuerus before he died, and rejoicing over it.
