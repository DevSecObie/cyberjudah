**Wickedness come up before the Lord** · Spared · Divided Kingdom

Yet forty days, and Nineveh shall be overthrown. The people believed God, proclaimed a fast, and put on sackcloth from the greatest to the least; the king rose from his throne, sat in ashes, and decreed that man and beast cry mightily unto God and turn from the violence in their hands.

## The offense

The charge is given in one line, their wickedness is come up before me, and Nahum a century later spells out what Assyria's wickedness was: a bloody city, full of lies and robbery, whose prey departeth not.

## The judgment

God saw their works, that they turned from their evil way, and repented of the evil that he had said he would do unto them. Jeremiah states the rule: if that nation turn from their evil, I will repent of the evil I thought to do. Christ: the men of Nineveh shall rise in judgment with this generation and condemn it.
