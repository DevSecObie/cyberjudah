**Insurrection: we have no part in David** · Put to death · United Monarchy

On the heels of Absalom's rebellion, Sheba the Benjamite blew a trumpet and cried, every man to his tents, O Israel, and all Israel went up after him. David judged him more dangerous than Absalom. He was besieged in Abel of Beth-maachah.

## The offense

Rebellion against the established government, immediately after the last one had been crushed at the cost of twenty thousand men. A man of Belial, the text calls him.

## The judgment

A wise woman of the city asked Joab why he would destroy a mother in Israel for one man; the people cut off Sheba's head and threw it over the wall, and Joab blew the trumpet and went home.
