**Treason against David; counsel of incest; suicide** · Put to death · United Monarchy

David's counselor, whose word was as if a man had inquired at the oracle of God, joined Absalom, advised him to take his father's concubines publicly, and asked for twelve thousand men to kill David that night. When Hushai's counsel was preferred, he went home, set his house in order, and hanged himself.

## The offense

Betrayal of the king he served, compounded by counsel that made the rebellion irreversible. Psalm 41 and Psalm 55 are read as David's lament over him: mine own familiar friend, in whom I trusted, which did eat of my bread. Peter cites the psalm of Judas.

## The judgment

The Lord had appointed to defeat the good counsel of Ahithophel, to the intent that the Lord might bring evil upon Absalom. He was buried in the sepulchre of his father, a self-executed sentence.
