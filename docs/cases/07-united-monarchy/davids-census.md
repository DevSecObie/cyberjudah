**Numbering Israel in pride; a plague on the nation** · Plague · United Monarchy

David commanded Joab to number Israel and Judah. Joab objected; the king's word prevailed. When the count came in, David's heart smote him: I have sinned greatly. Gad offered three judgments, and David chose to fall into the hand of the Lord.

## The offense

The narratives lay the provocation to God's anger against Israel and to Satan; the act is David's, and he owns it. A king measuring his strength in men, as if the Lord had not multiplied them and would not fight for them. Exodus required a ransom for every soul numbered, lest there be a plague among them.

## The judgment

Pestilence: seventy thousand men died from Dan to Beersheba in three days. The angel stood at Araunah's threshingfloor with drawn sword when the Lord said, it is enough. David bought the floor and built an altar; it became the temple site.
