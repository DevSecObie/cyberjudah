**Churlish refusal of hospitality; railing on the Lord's anointed** · Put to death · United Monarchy

David's men had guarded Nabal's shepherds all season. At sheep-shearing David asked for provision; Nabal, churlish and evil in his doings, answered, who is David? and sent them away. Abigail's wisdom stopped David's sword. The Lord did not stop his own.

## The offense

Ingratitude and contempt where the law commands open hands, delivered by a man drunk at his own feast. His wife's verdict: as his name is, so is he; Nabal is his name, and folly is with him.

## The judgment

Ten days after his heart died within him and became as a stone, the Lord smote Nabal, that he died. David blessed the Lord who had pleaded his cause and kept him from avenging himself.
