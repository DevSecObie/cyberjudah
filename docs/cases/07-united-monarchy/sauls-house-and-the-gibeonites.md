**Breaking Israel's sworn covenant by slaying the Gibeonites** · Put to death · United Monarchy

Three years of famine in David's reign were traced by inquiry of the Lord to Saul and his bloody house, because he slew the Gibeonites in his zeal for Israel, against the oath Joshua had sworn. David asked the Gibeonites what atonement they required.

## The offense

Joshua's oath had been sworn by the Lord God of Israel four centuries before. Saul's breach of it was blood-guilt on the land, and the land, as Numbers says, cannot be cleansed of blood but by the blood of him that shed it.

## The judgment

Seven of Saul's sons and grandsons were hanged before the Lord at Gibeah, sparing Mephibosheth for David's own oath to Jonathan. Rizpah kept the bodies from the birds until the rain came. Then David buried Saul's bones, and God was intreated for the land.
