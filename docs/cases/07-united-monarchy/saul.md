**Unlawful sacrifice; sparing what was devoted; consulting a medium** · Put to death · United Monarchy

A king undone by a pattern of partial obedience. He offered a sacrifice that was not his to offer, spared Agag and the best of the spoil against a direct command, and at the end sought a medium the law condemned.

## The offense

At Gilgal he would not wait for Samuel and offered the burnt offering himself. Sent to devote Amalek utterly, he kept the king and the best livestock and called it sacrifice. Finally, cut off from God, he consulted the witch of Endor.

## The judgment

The kingdom was torn from him and given to another. 'To obey is better than sacrifice.' He died by his own hand on Gilboa the next day, his sons with him.
