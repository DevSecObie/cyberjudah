**Despising the king in her heart for dancing before the ark** · Cursed · United Monarchy

As the ark came into the city of David with shouting and trumpets, Michal, Saul's daughter, watched from a window and despised David in her heart for leaping and dancing. She met him with contempt: how glorious was the king of Israel to day.

## The offense

Contempt for worship, delivered as contempt for a husband. David answered that it was before the Lord, who chose him over her father's house, and that he would be yet more vile than thus.

## The judgment

Therefore Michal the daughter of Saul had no child unto the day of her death. In Israel that was the end of a line; the house of Saul ended in her.
