**Touching the ark, transported unlawfully** · Put to death · United Monarchy

The ark was moved on a new cart rather than borne by the Levites as the law required. When the oxen stumbled, Uzzah put out his hand to steady it and died at once.

## The offense

The deeper fault was corporate: the ark was to be carried on staves by Kohathites, never touched, never carted. Uzzah's reach was the moment the accumulated carelessness came due.

## The judgment

God struck him for his error, and he died there beside the ark. David, first angry then afraid, left the ark three months until he learned to move it the way the law prescribed.
