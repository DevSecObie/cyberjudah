**Exalting himself to be king; asking for the king's concubine** · Put to death · United Monarchy

David's fourth son exalted himself, saying, I will be king, and gathered chariots, horsemen, Joab, and Abiathar for a coronation feast at En-rogel while David lay dying. Solomon was anointed the same day. Pardoned once, Adonijah then asked for Abishag the Shunammite.

## The offense

The narrator notes that his father had never displeased him at any time in saying, why hast thou done so. He usurped a throne the Lord had assigned to Solomon by name. The request for Abishag was, as Solomon read it, a second bid for the kingdom through the king's woman.

## The judgment

Solomon had spared him on condition of worthiness. The second offense was the end of it: Benaiah fell upon him and he died. Abiathar was banished and Joab executed in the same purge.
