**Murder of Onias the high priest under a sworn assurance** · Put to death · Second Temple (Apocrypha)

Onias, having exposed Menelaus' theft of the temple vessels, took sanctuary at Daphne. Menelaus persuaded the king's deputy Andronicus to lure him out; he came to Onias in deceit, gave him his right hand with oaths, and though suspected, persuaded him to leave the sanctuary, and shut him up immediately without regard of justice.

## The offense

A murder achieved by a false oath and the violation of asylum. Even the Greeks, the text says, were grieved for the unjust death of a man who had lived soberly and in virtue.

## The judgment

When Antiochus heard of it he was sorry in his mind, and sent for Andronicus, stripped him of his purple, rent his clothes, led him through the city, and slew him in the same place where he had committed impiety against Onias. Thus the Lord rewarded him his punishment, as he had deserved.
