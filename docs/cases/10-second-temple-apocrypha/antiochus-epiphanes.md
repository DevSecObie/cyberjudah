**Defiling the temple; forbidding the law; murdering the faithful** · Put to death · Second Temple (Apocrypha)

The Seleucid king outlawed the law of Moses, defiled the temple with an idol, and put to death those who kept the covenant. The books of Maccabees record both his persecution and his end.

## The offense

He forbade circumcision and the sabbaths, set up the abomination on the altar, forced sacrifice to idols, and killed mothers who circumcised their sons and families who kept the law in secret.

## The judgment

Struck with an incurable disease of the bowels, worms rising from the living body, he died in a strange land in bitter grief, by the account, confessing at the last that it was right to be subject to God.
