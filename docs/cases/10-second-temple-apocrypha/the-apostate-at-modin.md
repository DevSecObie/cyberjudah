**Sacrificing on the heathen altar at the king's command** · Put to death · Second Temple (Apocrypha)

The king's officers came to Modin to compel sacrifice and offered Mattathias silver, gold, and the title of king's friend to go first. He refused for himself and his sons. A Jew stepped forward in the sight of all to sacrifice on the altar according to the king's commandment.

## The offense

Deuteronomy 13 and 17: the man who serves other gods, or entices to it, in Israel shall be put to death, and the hand of the witnesses first upon him. He did it publicly, for reward, to lead the rest.

## The judgment

Mattathias was inflamed with zeal, ran upon him, and slew him at the altar, and the king's commissioner with him, and pulled down the altar. Thus dealt he zealously for the law of God, like as Phinees did unto Zambri. The revolt began there.
