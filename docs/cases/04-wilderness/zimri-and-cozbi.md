**Bringing a Midianite woman into the camp in the sight of the congregation** · Put to death · Wilderness

While the congregation wept at the tabernacle door over the plague, Zimri, a prince of Simeon, walked a Midianite princess past them into his tent. Phinehas followed with a javelin and thrust them both through.

## The offense

Open defiance of the judgment in progress: not hidden sin but sin paraded before Moses and the mourning assembly, by a leader, with the daughter of a chief of Midian. The offense was against the whole camp's standing before God.

## The judgment

Both died on the javelin, and the plague was stayed. God gave Phinehas a covenant of everlasting priesthood because he was zealous for his God and made an atonement. Sirach ranks him third in glory; the Maccabees claim him as father.
