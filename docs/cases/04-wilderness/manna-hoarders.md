**Keeping manna overnight; going out to gather on the Sabbath** · Temporal judgment · Wilderness

Told to gather only a day's portion, some kept manna until morning and it bred worms and stank. Told there would be none on the seventh day, some went out to gather anyway and found nothing.

## The offense

Distrust dressed as prudence. The first was hoarding against a promise of daily provision; the second was Sabbath-breaking against a double portion already given. Moses was angry at the one, and the Lord at the other: how long refuse ye to keep my commandments?

## The judgment

The hoarded bread rotted. The Sabbath gatherers went out and found nothing, and were rebuked. Small, exact, and recorded so the next test would be understood.
