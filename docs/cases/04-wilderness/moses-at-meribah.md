**Failing to honor God as holy before the people** · Reprieve · Wilderness

Told to speak to the rock so it would give water, Moses instead struck it twice and spoke rashly to the people. The water came, but so did a sentence: he would not enter the promised land.

## The offense

God said speak to the rock; Moses struck it and said, 'must we fetch you water?', taking to himself and Aaron what belonged to God, and disobeying the specific instruction in anger.

## The judgment

No death, but the leader who had carried the nation forty years was barred from the land for it. A case that measures how much is required of those entrusted with much.
