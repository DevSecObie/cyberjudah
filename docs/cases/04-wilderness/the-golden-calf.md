**Idolatry within weeks of the covenant** · Plague · Wilderness

While Moses was on the mountain receiving the law that forbade it, the people made a golden calf and worshipped it. The covenant was broken almost before the ink was dry.

## The offense

Impatient at Moses' delay, they pressed Aaron, who gathered their gold and cast a calf; they declared it the god that brought them out of Egypt and rose up to play. Aaron built it an altar and proclaimed a feast.

## The judgment

Moses ground the calf to powder and made them drink it. The Levites, called to the Lord's side, put about three thousand to the sword, and the Lord sent a plague on the people. Aaron was spared but not excused.
