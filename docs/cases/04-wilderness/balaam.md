**Divination for hire; counseling Moab to seduce Israel into idolatry** · Put to death · Wilderness

Hired by Balak to curse Israel, Balaam could only bless. So he taught Balak a surer way: draw Israel into fornication and the feasts of Baal-peor and let their own God judge them. It worked, and it killed him.

## The offense

A prophet who loved the wages of unrighteousness, pressed God for permission he had already been refused, and when he could not curse the people, arranged their sin. Christ names the doctrine of Balaam as still at work in the churches.

## The judgment

Balaam the son of Beor they slew with the sword when Israel went against Midian, together with the kings of Midian. Jude: they ran greedily after the error of Balaam for reward, and perished.
