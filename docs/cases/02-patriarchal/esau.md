**Despising his birthright; marrying Hittite wives; purposing to kill his brother** · Cursed · Patriarchal

The firstborn of Isaac sold his birthright for a single meal, took wives of the Canaanites to his parents' grief, and when the blessing went to Jacob resolved to kill him. The prophets and apostles treat him as the pattern of the profane man.

## The offense

Hebrews names it: a fornicator or profane person, as Esau, who for one morsel of meat sold his birthright. His marriages were a grief of mind to Isaac and Rebekah. His hatred of Jacob was murder in the heart.

## The judgment

He found no place of repentance, though he sought the blessing with tears. Malachi: I hated Esau, and laid his mountains and his heritage waste. Obadiah sentences his house for its violence against Jacob: the house of Esau shall be for stubble.
