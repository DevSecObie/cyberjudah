**Wickedness before the Lord; refusing to raise up seed to a dead brother** · Put to death · Patriarchal

Judah's firstborn Er was wicked in the sight of the Lord and the Lord slew him. His brother Onan, told to raise up seed to Er by Tamar, deliberately spilled his seed rather than give his brother an heir, and the Lord slew him also.

## The offense

Er's sin is unnamed; the verdict is not. Onan's is precise: he knew the seed would not be his, and each time he went in to Tamar he prevented conception. He took the wife but refused the duty, cheating the dead and the widow.

## The judgment

Both died at the Lord's hand, not by any court. The duty Onan refused was later written into the law of the kinsman: the firstborn she bears shall succeed in the name of his brother that is dead.
