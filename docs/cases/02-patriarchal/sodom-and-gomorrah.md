**Grievous sin; inhospitality and violence against strangers** · Put to death · Patriarchal

Cities whose sin was grave enough that its cry reached heaven. When two visitors lodged with Lot, the men of the city surrounded the house demanding them. Judgment fell the next morning.

## The offense

The prophets later name the fuller charge: pride, fullness of bread, careless ease, and no help for the poor and needy, a hardness that showed itself in the mob at Lot's door.

## The judgment

Fire and brimstone from the Lord out of heaven, overthrowing the cities and the plain. Lot's wife, looking back, became a pillar of salt. Held up ever after as the type of sudden ruin.
