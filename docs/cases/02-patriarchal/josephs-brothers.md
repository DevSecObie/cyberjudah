**Conspiracy to murder; selling a brother; deceiving their father with a bloodied coat** · Spared · Patriarchal

Ten brothers plotted to kill Joseph out of envy, then sold him to the Ishmaelites for twenty pieces of silver and dipped his coat in goat's blood so Jacob would think him dead. Twenty years later they stood before him in Egypt.

## The offense

Envy became conspiracy; conspiracy became man-stealing, which the law makes capital; and the cover-up was a lie that broke their father's heart for two decades. In Egypt they confessed among themselves: we are verily guilty concerning our brother.

## The judgment

The famine and Joseph's testing brought the guilt to the surface. Joseph forgave them: ye thought evil against me, but God meant it unto good. They were spared, and the reconciliation is the founding story of the twelve tribes.
