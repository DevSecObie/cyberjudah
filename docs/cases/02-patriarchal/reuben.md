**Lying with Bilhah, his father's concubine** · Cursed · Patriarchal

Jacob's firstborn went up to his father's bed and lay with Bilhah, and Israel heard of it. Nothing more is said until the deathbed, where it cost Reuben the birthright.

## The offense

The nakedness of thy father's wife shalt thou not uncover, later a capital offense in the law. Chronicles states the consequence plainly: because he defiled his father's bed, his birthright was given to the sons of Joseph.

## The judgment

Unstable as water, thou shalt not excel. The double portion went to Joseph, the rule to Judah. Reuben's tribe never produced a judge, prophet, or king.
