**Offering money for the gift of the Holy Ghost** · Reprieve · Apostolic

A sorcerer of Samaria who had long bewitched the people and given out that he was some great one, Simon believed and was baptized under Philip. When he saw that the Spirit was given through the apostles' hands he offered them money for the same power.

## The offense

Thy money perish with thee, because thou hast thought that the gift of God may be purchased with money. Peter names the state behind the offer: thy heart is not right in the sight of God; thou art in the gall of bitterness and in the bond of iniquity. A sorcerer's instinct, to buy power.

## The judgment

Repent therefore of this thy wickedness, and pray God, if perhaps the thought of thine heart may be forgiven thee. Simon asked Peter to pray for him that none of these things come upon him, and the record ends there. His name became the word for buying office in the church.
