**Exorcising in the name of Jesus whom Paul preacheth** · Temporal judgment · Apostolic

Vagabond Jewish exorcists at Ephesus, seven sons of a chief priest, took up the name of the Lord Jesus as a formula over those that had evil spirits. The spirit answered, Jesus I know, and Paul I know; but who are ye?

## The offense

The Name used as a spell by men who did not belong to it, in a city given to curious arts. Taking the name in vain in its most literal form.

## The judgment

The man in whom the evil spirit was leaped on them, overcame them, and prevailed against them, so that they fled out of that house naked and wounded. Fear fell on the city, the name was magnified, and the practitioners of curious arts burned their books, fifty thousand pieces of silver worth.
