**Calling herself a prophetess; teaching the servants of God to commit fornication and eat things sacrificed to idols** · Put to death · Apostolic

Christ's word to the angel of the church in Thyatira: thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants. He had given her space to repent of her fornication, and she repented not.

## The offense

The two things the apostles' council had bound on the Gentiles, abstinence from idol meats and from fornication, taught as liberty by a self-appointed prophetess, under a name that carried Ahab's queen with it. The church's toleration is charged against the church.

## The judgment

Behold, I will cast her into a bed, and them that commit adultery with her into great tribulation, except they repent of their deeds. And I will kill her children with death; and all the churches shall know that I am he which searcheth the reins and hearts.
