**Lying to the Holy Spirit about a gift** · Put to death · Apostolic

A couple sold property and gave part of the proceeds to the apostles while claiming it was the whole. The gift was never required; the lie was the offense. Each fell dead as it was exposed.

## The offense

They agreed together to keep back part of the price and present the rest as the full amount, a staged generosity meant to buy standing. Peter named it: not a lie to men but to God.

## The judgment

Ananias fell dead at Peter's word; Sapphira, questioned three hours later and holding to the same story, fell dead as well. Great fear came on the whole church.
