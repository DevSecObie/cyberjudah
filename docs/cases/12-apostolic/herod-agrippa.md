**Accepting worship due to God alone** · Put to death · Apostolic

Arrayed in royal robes, the king gave an oration and the crowd shouted that his was the voice of a god, not a man. He accepted the acclaim. The judgment was immediate and public.

## The offense

The sin was not the speech but the silence after it: he took to himself the honor owed to God, receiving worship he should have refused as Paul and Barnabas had refused it at Lystra.

## The judgment

Immediately an angel of the Lord struck him because he gave not God the glory; he was eaten of worms and died. The contrast follows at once: but the word of God grew and multiplied.
