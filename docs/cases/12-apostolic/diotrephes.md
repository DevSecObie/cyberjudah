**Loving the preeminence; prating against the apostle with malicious words; casting the brethren out of the church** · Sentence not recorded · Apostolic

John wrote to the church, but Diotrephes, who loveth to have the preeminence among them, received him not. He spoke against the apostle with malicious words, refused to receive the traveling brethren himself, forbade those who would, and cast them out of the church.

## The offense

Pride in office, slander of an apostle, refusal of hospitality to the brethren, and excommunication used as a weapon. The law's word on the slanderer and on hospitality to strangers both apply; so does Christ's on those who would be greatest.

## The judgment

Wherefore, if I come, I will remember his deeds which he doeth. The epistle records the charge and the intention; the sentence is not recorded. Beloved, follow not that which is evil, but that which is good.
