**Killing a man and boasting of it; claiming seventy-sevenfold vengeance** · Sentence not recorded · Primeval

Cain's descendant Lamech, first recorded polygamist, told his two wives he had killed a man for wounding him and a young man for bruising him, and claimed that if Cain were avenged sevenfold, Lamech would be seventy-sevenfold.

## The offense

A killing far out of proportion to the injury, then a song to celebrate it. Lamech takes the protection God extended to Cain and twists it into a license for private vengeance without limit.

## The judgment

Scripture records no sentence on Lamech. His line is not heard from after the Flood account opens; the whole world of Cain's descendants ends in Genesis 7.
