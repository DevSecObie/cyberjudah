**Murder of his brother Abel** · Cursed · Primeval

The first murder. Angry that his brother's offering was accepted and his own was not, Cain rose up in the field and killed Abel, then denied knowledge of it to God's face.

## The offense

Warned directly that sin crouched at his door and that he must master it, Cain instead led his brother out and struck him down. Asked where Abel was, he answered with a lie and a taunt: am I my brother's keeper?

## The judgment

Not death but a heavier exile: the ground would no longer yield to him, and he became a fugitive and a wanderer. God set a mark on him so that no one would kill him, extending the very mercy Cain had denied.
