**Attacking Israel's stragglers on the way out of Egypt** · Cursed · Egypt

Amalek came out against Israel at Rephidim and, as Deuteronomy records, smote the hindmost, the feeble and faint and weary, and feared not God. Israel prevailed while Moses' hands were held up.

## The offense

An unprovoked attack on a nation just out of bondage, aimed at those least able to defend themselves. No fear of God, no cause of war.

## The judgment

The Lord swore war with Amalek from generation to generation and commanded that his remembrance be blotted out from under heaven. Saul was sent to execute it and failed; the sentence passed to David and was finished under Hezekiah.
