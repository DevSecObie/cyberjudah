**Oppressing Israel; drowning their sons; refusing the Lord's command** · Plague · Egypt

The king who did not know Joseph enslaved Israel, ordered their male infants thrown into the river, and when Moses came in the Lord's name answered, Who is the Lord? He hardened his heart through ten plagues and drowned in the sea he pursued Israel into.

## The offense

Forced labor with rigor, infanticide by decree, contempt for the Name, and repeated broken promises: each time the plague lifted he hardened his heart and would not let the people go. Paul says he was raised up for this, that God's name might be declared in all the earth.

## The judgment

Ten plagues on Egypt ending in the death of every firstborn, Pharaoh's included. Then his army in the Red Sea: the waters returned and covered the chariots and horsemen; there remained not so much as one of them. Wisdom says a strange death.
