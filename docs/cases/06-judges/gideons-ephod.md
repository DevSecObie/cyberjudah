**Making an ephod from the spoil; Israel whoring after it** · Temporal judgment · Judges

Gideon refused the crown and said the Lord should rule, then asked for the golden earrings of the spoil and made an ephod of them and put it in Ophrah. All Israel went a whoring after it there, and it became a snare to Gideon and his house.

## The offense

The man who had thrown down his father's altar of Baal built a new object of worship out of victory. The text does not say he meant idolatry; it says what it became. The gold of Midian was the same material as the calf.

## The judgment

A snare unto Gideon and to his house. As soon as he died, Israel turned to Baal-berith, and his seventy sons were murdered by the son he had by a concubine in Shechem.
