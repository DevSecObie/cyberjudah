**Marrying a Philistine; going in to a harlot; betraying his Nazarite vow to Delilah** · Put to death · Judges

A Nazarite from the womb who repeatedly went down to the Philistines for women: a wife at Timnath against his parents' counsel, a harlot at Gaza, and Delilah in Sorek, to whom he told all his heart. He woke from her lap shaven, and the Lord had departed from him.

## The offense

Get her for me, for she pleaseth me well: the whole pattern in one sentence. Marriage with the uncircumcised, fornication, and the surrender of the sign of his consecration, each time with eyes that saw what they wanted. Hebrews still lists him among those who through faith waxed valiant.

## The judgment

The Philistines put out his eyes, bound him with fetters of brass, and set him grinding in the prison house. In the temple of Dagon he prayed once more, and the dead which he slew at his death were more than they which he slew in his life. He died with them.
