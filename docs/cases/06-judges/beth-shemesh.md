**Looking into the ark of the Lord** · Put to death · Judges

When the Philistines returned the ark on a cart, the men of Beth-shemesh rejoiced, offered the kine, and looked into the ark. The Lord smote them with a great slaughter, and they asked, who is able to stand before this holy Lord God?

## The offense

Even the Kohathites who carried the ark were forbidden to go in and see when the holy things were covered, lest they die. Farmers at harvest opened it for curiosity. The Levites of a priestly city should have known.

## The judgment

The text gives the number as fifty thousand and threescore and ten men, a figure long debated; the sentence is not. They sent the ark on to Kirjath-jearim and it stayed there twenty years.
