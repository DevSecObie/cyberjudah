**Sodomy, gang rape and murder of a stranger's concubine; a tribe that shielded the guilty** · Put to death · Judges

The men of Gibeah surrounded the house where a Levite lodged, demanding him; they were given his concubine and abused her all night until she died on the threshold. He cut her into twelve pieces and sent them through Israel. Benjamin refused to surrender the guilty, and Israel went to war.

## The offense

The crime of Sodom repeated in Israel, then compounded by the tribe: they would not hearken to the voice of their brethren to put away evil from Israel. Hosea names it twice as the depth of the nation's corruption: they have deeply corrupted themselves, as in the days of Gibeah.

## The judgment

After two defeats Israel destroyed Benjamin, the city, the cattle, and all that came to hand; six hundred men survived at the rock Rimmon, and the tribe was rebuilt from them. Israel wept at Bethel that a tribe was lacking.
