**Offering polluted bread and blemished beasts; being partial in the law** · Cursed · Exile and Return

After the return, the priests offered the blind, the lame, and the sick on the altar and said the table of the Lord was contemptible and its service a weariness. Offer it now unto thy governor, Malachi says; will he be pleased with thee? They also departed out of the way and caused many to stumble at the law.

## The offense

Leviticus forbids any offering with a blemish; they kept the best for themselves and the defective for God, while men of other nations feared his name more than they did. Priests are to keep knowledge and teach the law; these corrupted the covenant of Levi.

## The judgment

I will curse your blessings; yea, I have cursed them already. I will spread dung upon your faces, even the dung of your solemn feasts. Therefore have I also made you contemptible and base before all the people, according as ye have been partial in the law.

## Laws broken

- [20B.2](/law/20-churches-ministers/20b#20B.2) Ministers are not to profane the name of their God.
- [20A.7](/law/20-churches-ministers/20a#20A.7) A priest is to teach the people the difference between holy and profane and to discern between unclean and clean.
- [20A.9](/law/20-churches-ministers/20a#20A.9) Ministers are to teach the people all the laws and statutes of God.
- [4F.3](/law/04-law-and-justice/4f#4F.3) You are not to be partial towards anyone or a respecter of persons in exacting justice, whether they be poor or rich, small or great.
- [20E.3](/law/20-churches-ministers/20e#20E.3) We are to honor God by bringing the choice first fruits of our land into the house of God, so He may bring prosperity to us.
- [20F.4](/law/20-churches-ministers/20f#20F.4) Lying pastors and those that withhold the truth of God's word are cursed for causing national apostasy.
