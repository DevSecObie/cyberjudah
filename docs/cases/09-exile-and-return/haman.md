**Plotting the destruction of all the Jews; building a gallows for Mordecai** · Put to death · Exile and Return

Because one Jew would not bow to him, Haman sought to destroy all the Jews in the empire, bought the decree with ten thousand talents, cast lots for the day, and built a gallows fifty cubits high for Mordecai. Esther exposed him at her second banquet.

## The offense

Genocide from wounded pride, purchased by bribe on a false report to the king: their laws are diverse from all people, neither keep they the king's laws. The Agagite's name recalls Amalek, whose remembrance Israel was commanded to blot out.

## The judgment

So they hanged Haman on the gallows that he had prepared for Mordecai; his ten sons followed. Whoso diggeth a pit shall fall therein. The feast of Purim keeps the memory of the lot that turned.
