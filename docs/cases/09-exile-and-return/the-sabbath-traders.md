**Treading winepresses, bringing in sheaves, and selling fish and wares on the Sabbath** · Reprieve · Exile and Return

Nehemiah saw men of Judah treading winepresses and loading asses on the Sabbath, and Tyrian merchants selling fish in Jerusalem on the holy day. He testified against the nobles, shut the gates at dusk, set his own servants to guard them, and threatened to lay hands on the merchants who camped outside.

## The offense

Did not your fathers thus, and did not our God bring all this evil upon us, and upon this city? Yet ye bring more wrath upon Israel by profaning the sabbath. Jeremiah had named Sabbath burdens through those very gates as a cause of the city's burning.

## The judgment

The merchants lodged outside the wall once or twice; Nehemiah warned them, and from that time forth came they no more on the sabbath. He commanded the Levites to cleanse themselves and keep the gates. Remember me, O my God, concerning this also.
