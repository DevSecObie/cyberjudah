**Conspiring by law against an innocent man** · Put to death · Exile and Return

Finding no fault in Daniel except concerning the law of his God, the presidents and princes flattered Darius into signing a decree that no one might petition any god or man for thirty days, then caught Daniel praying. The king labored till sunset to deliver him and could not.

## The offense

A snare built from the machinery of law itself: a statute designed to make righteousness a crime, and a king manipulated into executing it. Deuteronomy's rule for a false witness is that what he thought to do to his brother be done to him.

## The judgment

Daniel came out of the den without a mark because he believed in his God. The king commanded those who had accused him, with their children and wives, cast into the den; the lions broke all their bones before they reached the bottom.
